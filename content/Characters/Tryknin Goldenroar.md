# Tryknin Goldenroar 
# Leonin Barbarian 

%% Tryknin was born into the Black Tide as the son of Gromon Spritroar, the last Dread Pirate King to wear the crown before Vask. His earliest memories are fragments: the roll of a deck under his paws, a hold that smelled of tar and salt, a wall of black flags snapping overhead, and his mother’s voice humming over him in the dark. He can’t recall her face anymore, or even the name she called him. Only the sound of it remains. He was eight when the lords turned on his father. Vask gathered the dread pirate lords and put the Tide’s stagnant rulers to the sword. Tryknin’s mother was killed in the coup. His father, bleeding and roaring, hauled him off the ship and into the dark water. The rest of that night is a blur of fire, shouting, and his father’s arms around him. They fled north into the Azure Plains Coalition, the very powers the Tide had raided for generations. Somewhere along the way, the Dread Pirate King died. Gromon Spritroar became Gaknin Goldenroar, a loud, cheerful lumberjack. His son became Tryknin. On quiet little Insul, where the Fiore family’s forests grow thick and no one presses a man about his past, they found something close to peace. Tryknin grew up swinging an axe beside his father and made friends on the island’s docks and in Uncle John’s. His father never speaks of the old life. Tryknin asked once, and the old king’s face went somewhere far away. The boy never asked again. Still, the temper that came with his mane has never gone away. When it takes him, he feels the roar of a crown his father buried, and every sailor’s whisper of “the King” makes his hackles rise. Tryknin fights to protect the peace his father built, and he has learned to fear the name Vask, who is still paying for word of a king who “died.” %%
