Rouge is a young tabaxi, with calico pattering, and a deformed left ear that looks almost like a horn. Her eyes are a deep red, giving her her name sake. Her father, [[Charles Bellball]] is a merchant, and ended up cheating on his wife, [[Tabatha Bellball]], with Rouge's biological mother. Returning home, with a bastard daughter in tow, Charles brought Rouge to [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Insul/index|Insul]].  This led Tabatha to leave Charles. He fled the island, leaving an infant rouge to be raised by Tabatha, leaving her only a golden necklace belonging to her biological mother.  Being raised by Tabatha, along side her half brother [[Shady Bellball]], she has long dreamed of leaving their little island to explore the world.
