[[Varek Herrera|Varek]] came to [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Insul/index|Insul]] out of a storm. When the [[Garrick Herrera|Herreras]] had barely unpacked their anvil, the sea gave up a wreck on the rocks below Harbour Cove, and among the splintered timbers lay a dragonborn boy of nine, half-drowned and clinging to driftwood. [[Mara Herrera]] carried him up from the shore. No one else from that boat was ever found, and the boy could not say where it had come from. That was ten years ago, and he is nineteen now.

Of what came before, he remembers almost nothing: the warmth of a great golden field, a vast shadow sliding across the sun, a deep voice saying a word he no longer knows. All that came ashore with him was a heavy medallion on a cord about his neck, cut with sharp, angular script that no one on Insul can read.

The Herreras raised him between the forge and the tide. [[Garrick Herrera|Garrick]] taught him to read the color of hot iron and the ring of a good blade. [[Mara Herrera|Mara]] taught him to fight on the wet cobbles at low tide, where a stumble cost him his footing and a lesson. He grew up among the island's children, and a lean [[Malkin Ratter|malkin ratter]] adopted him early. It sleeps on the forge's warm hearthstones and follows him wherever he goes, as though it knows something about him that he doesn't.
