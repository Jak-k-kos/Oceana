# Ledge Deer
Few islanders have ever seen a ledge deer, and fewer have seen one up close. They are small deer, standing no higher than a man's waist, russet-coated and white-throated, the stags crowned with short, curling antlers. They live where nothing else will: on the sheer cliffs and in the narrow ravines of the wilder islands, leaping from ledge to ledge across drops that would kill a goat. And unlike any other land beast in Oceana, they swim. At low tide they wade out to graze the weed from the rocks, and they will cross a narrow channel to reach a quiet islet, paddling with only their heads above the water.

They are all that remains of the great herds that once ran across the old world, shrunk by three thousand years of island life into something small enough to survive. The hunters of [[Ruthala]] take them rarely and with great ceremony, and on many islands it is considered a blessing to find a ledge deer drinking at a shrine at dawn.
