# DM Notes
- A captured or killed Hullbreaker is among the richest prizes the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]] army could bring home, and just the sort of thing the council sells.
- Riders use their own stat blocks (sahuagin, tritons and the like). The beast takes their orders.
- Its Ram is meant to rival a broadside. One Hullbreaker is a fight for a whole ship, not for one crew member.

# Hullbreaker
Sailors of the northern patrols have a saying: if you see sahuagin, fight; if you see a Hullbreaker, pray. Hullbreakers are the war-beasts of the sea folk empire, vast and slow-eyed things, half whale and half armored fish, bred in the deep for nothing but war. The sea folk grow living coral over their backs and brows in great ridged plates, until each one is less a beast than a reef that swims, and they lash coral towers to its spine, from which sahuagin and tritons ride to battle, hurling spears as their mount charges.

A Hullbreaker breaks a ship the way a hammer breaks a clay pot. It comes up from below, again and again, until the keel cracks and the hull splits wide, and then the sea folk swarm the wreck. They are seldom seen. When one is, it means the empire has come in earnest.

- **Saving Throws:** Str +12, Con +9
- **Senses:** Blindsight 120 ft. (echolocation), passive Perception 11
- **Languages:** None, but it understands simple commands in Aquan

# Traits
- **Water Breathing.** The Hullbreaker can breathe only underwater.
- **Siege Monster.** The Hullbreaker deals double damage to objects and structures, including ships.
- **War Towers.** Up to eight Medium riders can ride in the coral towers on its back. They have three-quarters cover, and they stay above the water unless the Hullbreaker dives.
- **Trained Mount.** On its turn, the Hullbreaker obeys the commands of its lead rider.
- **Hold Breath.** It can stay surfaced for 1 hour

## Actions
- **Multiattack.** The Hullbreaker makes one bite attack and one tail slam attack.
- **Bite.** Melee Weapon Attack: +12 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 30 (4d10 + 8) piercing damage.
- **Tail Slam.** Melee Weapon Attack: +12 to hit, reach 20 ft., one target. Hit: 26 (4d8 + 8) bludgeoning damage, and a creature on a ship's deck must succeed on a DC 18 Strength saving throw or be knocked overboard.
- **Ram (Recharge 5-6).** The Hullbreaker swims up to its speed in a straight line and strikes a ship from below. The ship takes 55 (10d10) bludgeoning damage, doubled by Siege Monster, and each creature on its deck must succeed on a DC 16 Dexterity saving throw or fall prone.

# Bonus Actions
- **Deep Call.** The Hullbreaker lets out a long groaning call that shakes the hull of every ship within 300 feet. Sea folk within a mile know exactly where it is.
- **Sound.** The Hullbreaker dives up to 60 feet straight down, carrying its sea folk riders under with it.
