# Saltback
Along the black rocks of the southern shores, the stones seem to be heaped with sleeping dragons. They are saltbacks: great grey-black lizards longer than a man is tall, crested from head to tail with a ridge of spines like broken shale. They spend their mornings sprawled in drowsy piles on the sun-hot rocks, and when the tide falls they slip into the shallows to graze the weed from the stones. Afterward they climb back out to bask, and every so often a saltback sneezes, snorting a fine white spray of salt from its nostrils. The rocks where they gather are crusted white with it.

Saltbacks are slow to anger and slower to move. A sailor can walk among them, if he does not mind being ignored. But a cornered saltback bites, and its bite is not soon forgotten. Islanders gather their eggs in the warm season, and in hard years the hunters of [[Ruthala]] take one for its tough, briny meat, never more than they need.
