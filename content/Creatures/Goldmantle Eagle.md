# Goldmantle Eagle
The goldmantle is the great eagle of the eastern [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/index|Kingdom]], a broad-winged hunter in brown and burnished gold, named for the bright feathers that lie across its shoulders and catch the sun like a new coin. Its wings spread wider than a tall man can reach, and its talons are long enough to lift a fat fish clean out of the swell. It nests on the high crags where no ladder goes, and comes down to hunt the water and the fields below, taking fish above all, and after them the rats, lizards and snakes of the terraces. Nothing else in the sky of Oceana hunts it, and it flies as though it knows.

It is a rare bird, even at home. An eagle of that size needs a great deal of sea to feed it, and a single island seldom keeps more than one pair. Most sailors go their whole lives without seeing a goldmantle, and those who do tend to remember the day.

It leaves other birds alone, or very nearly. The islands taught it to, much as they taught the [[Malkin Ratter|malkin ratter]]. For three thousand years a goldmantle that stooped on a henhouse or a messenger's perch was met with arrows, and a goldmantle that kept to fish and vermin was left to raise its young in peace. The eagles that remain are the children of the second kind. A goldmantle can take a bird, and now and then a hungry one does, but it is a rare thing, and islanders who see it happen speak of it for a season.

Fewer still have seen a tame one. A goldmantle is a proud bird and a patient one, and it gives its trust slowly, if it gives it at all. The few that are kept are kept to hunt, and to be looked at, by those with a purse deep enough to afford the vanity.
