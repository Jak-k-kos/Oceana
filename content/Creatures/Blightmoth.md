# Blightmoth
Blightmoths are dull brown moths, plain enough to vanish against the bark of any tree. Their caterpillars are another matter. Each Dife they hatch by the thousand in the orchards, and they eat everything: leaves, blossoms and the young fruit itself, boring into figs and mangoes and leaving them rotting on the branch. An orchard left to them can be stripped bare in a single summer. Only the warm islands know them, for the northern cold kills them.

The farmers' only true defense is the [[Gnatwing|gnatwings]], which hunt the moths by the thousand each night before they can lay their eggs. Old orchard-keepers tell of islands that drove off their bats, and of how their orchards were gone within three summers.
