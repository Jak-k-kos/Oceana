# Widowfin
The [[Locations/The Widow's Wrath/index|Widow's Wrath]] knows only two moods, and the widowfin has learned to live in both. It is a great ray, wide as a ship is long, smoke-grey above and pale as a drowned face below, with a long tail that ends in a barb like a boarding pike.

When the storm comes, the widowfins rise. They leap from the crests of waves the size of mountains and spread their fins upon the hurricane wind, gliding through the rain like sheets of torn sail. Lightning loves them. It strikes them again and again, and they drink it in and glow faintly blue in the dark, and sailors who have seen it say a widowfin skimming over their deck in the storm leaves the masts burning behind it.

When the calm comes, they sink. They lie motionless a few feet beneath the glassy surface, nothing but a shadow on the water, and they wait. A ship drifting in the dead calm is the only thing for leagues that moves, and a widowfin can feel every footstep on its deck through the still water. It is very patient.

- **Skills:** Stealth +6
- **Damage Immunities:** Lightning
- **Senses:** Blindsight 60 ft., tremorsense 120 ft. (through water, and through any hull touching the water), passive Perception 11
- **Languages:** None

# Traits
- **Hold Breath.** The widowfin can stay out of the water for 1 hour.
- **Storm-Ridden.** While a storm is blowing, the widowfin can fly, and its attacks deal an extra 9 (2d8) lightning damage.
- **Calm Hunter.** In a dead calm, a motionless widowfin beneath the surface looks like nothing more than a shadow, and it has advantage on Stealth checks.
- **Lightning Drinker.** Whenever the widowfin would take lightning damage, it regains that many hit points instead.

## Actions
- **Multiattack.** The widowfin makes one wing buffet attack and one tail barb attack.
- **Wing Buffet.** Melee Weapon Attack: +8 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 18 (3d8 + 5) bludgeoning damage, and a Large or smaller creature on deck is pushed 15 feet. A creature pushed past the rail goes overboard.
- **Tail Barb.** Melee Weapon Attack: +8 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. Hit: 16 (2d10 + 5) piercing damage, and the target must succeed on a DC 14 Constitution saving throw or take 10 (3d6) poison damage.
- **Rise From the Calm.** The widowfin bursts up beneath a ship or a creature within 10 feet of it. If it was hidden, it surprises its target. A Small ship takes 22 (4d10) bludgeoning damage, and each creature on its deck must succeed on a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw or fall prone.
- **Lightning Discharge (Recharge 5-6; storm only).** Each creature within 20 feet of the widowfin must make a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw, taking 33 (6d10) lightning damage on a failure, or half as much on a success. A wooden ship within 20 feet catches fire.

# Bonus Actions
- **Glide.** While flying, the widowfin moves up to half its fly speed without provoking opportunity attacks.
- **Sink.** While in water, the widowfin drops beneath the surface and goes still. If it doesn't move, it counts as hidden to creatures above the water.
