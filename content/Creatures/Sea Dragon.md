# DM Notes
- Sea dragons are beasts in all but name. They are clever the way gulls and dolphins are clever, quick to learn a boat, a voice or a trick, but they have no language and no thoughts of their own beyond the hunt, the nest and the flock.
- The flock comes first. A sea dragon that sees one of its own harpooned will turn on the boat that did it, and the rest of the bay with it.
- Delafina's egg trouble: [[Elio Marinas]] is taking eggs from the nesting rocks on the north shore and selling the hatchlings to fishing crews on other islands. A hatchling raised on a boat from the egg is gentle and easy to work with. One taken from a nest that has been robbed before is not, and the mothers of the north shore have started to watch the boats.
- Not everyone loves them. The harpoon gun was built for hunting sea monsters and sea dragons both, and on islands far from Delafina a sea dragon caught in the nets is still killed for its hide and venom. Selling sea dragon parts in Delafina, or to anyone who knows a Delafina fisherman, is asking for a knife in the dark.
- Stat blocks are built off the wyvern (MM, CR 6), traded some venom for a swim speed and a temper.

# Sea Dragon
When [[Aquilla|Aquilla's]] Wrath drowned the world, the wyverns of the high crags lost their mountains one by one, until there was nowhere left to roost but the sea itself. Most of them died. Those that lived learned to fold their wings and dive, to hunt the shoals instead of the goats, and to sleep on the rocks with the spray on their scales. Their wings grew broad and webbed, more fin than sail, and their tails grew long and strong for swimming. They have not forgotten the sky, and a sea dragon bursting from a wave and climbing into the wind is a sight no sailor forgets, but the water is their home now, and they glide through it like grey-green ribbons, sleek as eels and twice as quick.

They are not wise, nor cruel, nor kind. They are hungry, and they are patient, and they remember. Some eight hundred years ago the fishermen of [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Delafina/index|Delafina]] learned that a sea dragon will drive a shoal of mullet straight into a net if it knows it will be paid its share, and so began one of the strangest partnerships in all the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]]. When the mullet run, the fishermen beat the water with their oars, and the sea dragons come. The old folk of Delafina will tell you, quite seriously, that the dragons were never tamed at all.

Fishermen across the coalition now work alongside them, and a crew with a sea dragon of its own is counted lucky. But a sea dragon is still a wild thing with a stinger full of venom, and sailors who forget it do not often get the chance to forget it twice.

- **Skills:** Perception +4, Stealth +4
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 14
- **Languages:** None

# Traits
- **Hold Breath.** The sea dragon can hold its breath for 30 minutes.
- **Flock Hunter.** The sea dragon has advantage on attack rolls against a creature if at least one of its allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally isn't incapacitated.
- **Wronged Flock.** If the sea dragon sees another sea dragon reduced to 0 hit points, it has advantage on attack rolls until the end of its next turn, and it won't flee for the rest of the fight.

## Actions
- **Multiattack.** The sea dragon makes two attacks: one with its bite and one with its stinger. While swimming, it can use its tail lash in place of its bite.
- **Bite.** Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 11 (2d6 + 4) piercing damage.
- **Stinger.** Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 10 ft., one creature. Hit: 11 (2d6 + 4) piercing damage. The target must make a DC 14 Constitution saving throw, taking 17 (5d6) poison damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one.
- **Tail Lash.** Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 13 (2d8 + 4) bludgeoning damage, and a Medium or smaller creature must succeed on a DC 15 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone. A creature standing at a ship's rail is knocked overboard instead.
- **Breach (Recharge 5-6).** The sea dragon surges up from beneath a Small ship or a rowboat it is under. The ship takes 18 (4d8) bludgeoning damage, and each creature on its deck must succeed on a DC 14 Dexterity saving throw or fall prone. On a roll of 5 or less, a creature goes overboard.

# Bonus Actions
- **Dive.** While in water, the sea dragon plunges beneath the surface and swims up to half its swim speed without provoking opportunity attacks.

# Sea Dragon Hatchling
Hatchlings are long as a man's arm and all teeth and tail, and their stingers are still soft. A hatchling raised on a boat from the egg takes the crew for its flock and will follow that boat across half the sea.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Small | Dragon | Unaligned | 1/2 (100 XP) |

| AC | HP | Speed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 12 (natural armor) | 22 (5d6 + 5) | 20 ft., swim 40 ft., fly 20 ft. |

| Dex | Str | Con | Int | Wis | Cha |

| 14 (+2) | 12 (+1) | 13 (+1) | 3 (-4) | 12 (+1) | 6 (-2) |

- **Skills:** Perception +3, Stealth +4
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 13
- **Languages:** None

# Traits
- **Hold Breath.** The hatchling can hold its breath for 15 minutes.
- **Boat-Raised.** A hatchling raised aboard a ship from the egg treats its crew as its flock. It won't attack them, and it can be taught simple commands, such as "come", "stay" or "drive the fish", with a DC 12 Wisdom (Animal Handling) check.

## Actions
- **Bite.** Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 4 (1d4 + 2) piercing damage.
- **Stinger.** Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. Hit: 3 (1d4 + 2) piercing damage. The target must make a DC 11 Constitution saving throw, taking 5 (2d4) poison damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one.

# Bonus Actions
- **Dive.** While in water, the hatchling plunges beneath the surface and swims up to half its swim speed without provoking opportunity attacks.
