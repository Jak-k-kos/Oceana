# Creatures
When the waters rose, the world's creatures had to choose: cling to the shrinking land, or follow the flood into the deep. Most of those that chose the land are gone now. Those that chose the sea took on new shapes, and they have had more than two thousand years to grow strange. Here are the beasts and peoples that sailors of the islands have learned to fear, to hunt, and now and then to live beside.
