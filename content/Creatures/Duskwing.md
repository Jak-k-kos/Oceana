# Duskwing
At every sunset, the sea caves of the southern islands breathe out bats, led out through the dark by the clicking of the [[Gnatwing|gnatwings]]. Duskwings are fruit bats with fox-like faces and wings as wide as a man's outstretched arms, and they pour from their roosts in great dark streams to cross the water from island to island. They feed on fig and mango, on blossom and nectar.

Farmers curse them every harvest all the same, and net their trees against them. But no farmer nets them all. In every orchard a few trees are left bare for the bats, the duskwings' share, a custom as old as the orchards themselves. The duskwings carry pollen from blossom to blossom as they feed, and half the fruit of the south would never set without them. An island that starves its duskwings soon finds its trees barren, and its orchards overrun with [[Blightmoth|blightmoths]] besides, for wherever the duskwings fly, the gnatwings fly with them. Sailors watch their crossings closely. When the gnatwings smell a storm they will not fly, and the duskwings cannot leave the dark without them. So when the bats stay home at dusk, sailors stay in harbor.
