# Gnatwing
Watch a stream of duskwings crossing the water at dusk, and you will see that many carry passengers. Gnatwings are little insect-eating bats no bigger than a sparrow, and no gnatwing could ever cross the open sea alone. So they ride, clinging to the fur of the [[Duskwing|duskwings]] from island to island, and they pay their passage well. A duskwing is half blind in true darkness, and the deep sea caves where the colonies roost are black as pitch. The gnatwings fly ahead, clicking and chirping, and the duskwings follow the sound in and out of the dark.

In the orchards of the south the gnatwings hunt. They will eat any insect they can catch, but [[Blightmoth|blightmoths]] most of all, and they kill them by the thousand before the moths can lay their eggs. Where one bat lives, so does the other. Where one dies out, the other soon follows.
