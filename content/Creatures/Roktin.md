# Roktin
On [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Las Eridani/index|Las Eridani]], and nowhere else in all the world, the stones get up and walk. Roktins are small, five-legged creatures about the size of a cat, crusted over in rough grey stone. A resting roktin looks exactly like a rock until it unfolds its legs and ambles away. They wander the fields, orchards and granaries of the island at their own slow pace, and they eat everything that would eat the harvest: rats and mice, beetles and locusts, grubs and slugs.

No one knows where they came from. The islanders say they are [[Roktra|Roktra's]] gift to the valley, and no one has found a better answer. Their stony shells turn aside blades and arrows alike, so killing one is hard work, and no islander would ever try. Children are taught to step around them from the cradle, and visitors are warned in every tavern: many a careless kick at what looked like a stone has ended in a broken toe.
