# Ecology of Oceana

# The Drowned Highlands
Oceana was once the high country of a great land. When [[Aquilla's Wrath]] drowned the world, the lowlands sank first, down into the lightless deep, and still the sea climbed until only the mountains stood above it. Those mountains are the islands, and their drowned shoulders are the shallows. Between them the old valleys lie dark and deep, far below where any light can reach, but ships cross them every day, for they are still the highlands' own waters. Beyond the highlands' rim lies the Deep: the drowned lowlands, abyssal and black, where the [[Locations/Sea Folk Waters|Thalassine Dominion]] makes its home. No ship crosses the Deep.

# Warm Seas and the Cold North
Oceana lies beneath a high, hot sun, and most of its waters are warm the whole year round. Only along the far northern rim is it otherwise. There, cold water wells up out of the Deep and spills over the edge of the highlands, chilling a rocky band of islands far below what the sun alone would allow. Fog lies thick there for much of the year, and in winter the bays and harbors skin over with ice. The cold does not reach far. A few days' sail south, the fog burns away, the water turns turquoise, and the heat settles in. Southward still lie the true tropics: coral reefs, mangrove shores, and jungle-shadowed ravines where nothing has ever known frost.

# The Turning Year
Oceana's year turns through four seasons: Glas, the long cool season; Grandi, the green and rainy spring; Dife, the short blazing summer; and Fre, the season of harvest. In the warm heart of the region, Glas is merely mild. Only in the far north does it bite.

# The Land
Land is the rarest treasure in Oceana. When the waters rose, [[Roktra]] and [[Apiot]] turned their hands to what remained, and over three thousand years the stony mountaintops grew rich soil in their valleys and on their terraces. Some islands are still bare rock, but many are greener and more fertile than any mountaintop has a right to be. Every good acre was claimed long ago for terraces, pasture or orchard. What wildness remains lives at the edges: on cliffs too sheer to plow, in ravines too narrow to graze, and on islets too small to be worth the trouble.

# Trees and Green Things
Timber is precious everywhere. In the cool north grow oak, chestnut and pine, and the last great oak forests are cut with care for ship timber. Through the middle of the region the hills are silver-green with olive, fig, almond and cypress. In the south the forests turn to jungle: palms, broad-leafed hardwoods, towering shade trees and tangled rattan, with mangroves crowding the shores, and the great hardwoods of the south make ship timber as fine as any northern oak.

# Beasts of the Land
Three thousand years on islands have made the land's creatures small. [[Ledge Deer|Ledge deer]] and wild goats pick their way along cliffs no farmer wants. Half-wild pigs roam the forests, and the boars among them can grow fierce. Rodents are everywhere. In the stone walls and granaries of nearly every island live the [[Malkin Ratter|malkin ratters]], half-tame cats who keep the rats in check, and the warm southern islands ring each dawn with the drumming of [[Red-Throated Barrel Monkey|red-throated barrel monkeys]].

Lizards bask on every sunny stone. In the south they grow large, and the great [[Saltback|saltbacks]] heap themselves across the black rocks and graze the shallows. Snakes and spiders haunt the jungles, some of them deadly, and saltwater crocodiles lurk in the southern mangroves, swimming from one mangrove isle to the next. Frogs are rare, for fresh water is scarce, and few fish live in the islands' streams. Nearly all of Oceana's water life belongs to the sea.

# Working Beasts
Donkeys carry the loads of Oceana up its steep and narrow streets, and oxen pull its plows. Horses are a rare and costly marvel, kept only by the nobles of the Kingdom's great cities. Dogs are kept for work, not company: shepherds' dogs that run the flocks across the steep pastures. Every mouth on an island must earn its bread, and few can afford a dog that doesn't.

# The Small Things
Bees hum through every orchard and terrace, and without them the islands would starve. Beetles, moths, crickets and a thousand other small things fill the grass and the night air, and on them the birds and bats grow fat. In the warm south, at every dusk, the [[Duskwing|duskwings]] stream out from their caves to cross the water from island to island, and the little [[Gnatwing|gnatwings]] ride along with them, hunting the [[Blightmoth|blightmoths]] that would otherwise strip the orchards bare.

# The Birds
Where the land's beasts dwindled, the birds thrived. The sky above the endless sea belongs to them: gulls, terns, cormorants, and great wandering albatrosses that cross the region without landing. Seabirds nest by the hundred thousand on rocks too barren for anything else, and on islands like [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Terasaro/index|Terasaro]] their guano is gathered as the finest fertilizer an island farmer can buy. Malkins and rats never reach the great colonies on the sheer cliffs and bare rocks, and where malkins live, the rats that steal the eggs grow few. In the warm south, the jungles flash with parrots, macaws and lorikeets, loud as a market and twice as bright. Birds are [[Ruthala|Ruthala's]] messengers, and the islands still send word to one another on the wings of [[Knife-Wing Gull|knife-wing gulls]].

# The Shallows
Life in Oceana is richest on the drowned mountainsides, where sunlight still reaches the bottom. Under [[Apiot|Apiot's]] hand, old meadow and forest became gardens of the sea: kelp forests in the cold north, sea grass meadows across the middle, and coral reefs throughout the warm south. Shoals of mullet, sardine and mackerel feed the islands. Seals haul out on the northern rocks, sea turtles graze the southern meadows, and the [[Sea Dragon|sea dragons]] hunt among them all. Nearly every reef and shoal worth having is held by cannon, for the shallows feed the surface world.

# The Deep
Between the islands, the water turns from turquoise to ink, and great things sometimes rise out of the dark basins: whales, giant squid, and stranger beasts besides. Beyond the highlands' rim, the Deep belongs to the sea folk and to [[Caruilla]], and what lives there is known only from the things that sometimes come up out of it.
