- The swarm barely moves on its own and drifts with the current. It's a hazard to swimmers, divers, sailors gone overboard and anyone a [[Siren|siren]] drags under, not a monster that gives chase.

# Swarm of Lanternbells
On warm mornings in the shallows, the sea sometimes blooms like a garden. Thousands upon thousands of lanternbells drift in on the current, jellyfish no bigger than a child's fist, and every one a different color: rose and amber, violet and sea-green, gold and the deep blue of evening. They glow softly from within, so that at night a bloom lights the water like a festival of floating lanterns. Children wade out to the knee to look at them, and their mothers drag them back by the collar, for every one of those pretty bells trails a beard of threads finer than hair, and every thread is a needle full of fire. A bloom of lanternbells can numb a grown man stiff before he reaches the boat.

Yet the shallows feed on them too. Fishermen go out after a bloom in long gloves with fine-meshed nets and scoop them up by the bucketful. The colors do not last. As the bells are salted and dried, the glow fades and the colors bleed away, until all that's left are crisp, clear sheets like panes of glass, and that is why the markets sell them as glassleaf.

- **Damage Resistances:** Bludgeoning, piercing, slashing
- **Condition Immunities:** Charmed, frightened, grappled, paralyzed, prone, restrained, stunned
- **Senses:** Blindsight 30 ft. (blind beyond this radius), passive Perception 8
- **Languages:** None

# Traits
- **Swarm.** The swarm can occupy another creature's space and vice versa, and it can move through any opening large enough for a Tiny jellyfish. It can't regain hit points or gain temporary hit points.
- **Water Breathing.** The swarm can breathe only underwater.
- **Glowing Bloom.** The swarm sheds dim light in a 10-foot radius.
- **Long Threads.** The bells are easy to see, but their threads trail unseen around them. A creature in the water within 5 feet of the swarm counts as entering its space.
- **Adrift.** At the end of each of its turns, the swarm drifts 20 feet in the direction of the current.
- **Stinging Bloom.** A creature that enters the swarm's space for the first time on a turn, or starts its turn there, takes 10 (3d6) poison damage, or half as much if the swarm has half its hit points or fewer. It must also succeed on a DC 12 Constitution saving throw or be poisoned for 1 hour. If it fails by 5 or more, it is also paralyzed for 1 minute, repeating the save at the end of each of its turns. A paralyzed creature in the water can't swim and begins to sink.

## Actions
- **Stinging Threads.** Melee Weapon Attack: +0 to hit, reach 0 ft., one creature in the swarm's space. Hit: 2 (1d4) poison damage.
