# Knife-Wing Gull
The knife-wing gull is built for the open sky. It is long and lean, white below and storm-grey above, with narrow, swept-back wings that cut the wind like a blade. Where other gulls drift, a knife-wing flies straight and fast, ranging out over the water each day from island to island and shoal to shoal, often crossing several in a single day in search of food. Few birds in Oceana can fly so far, so fast, for so long.

That is why the druids of [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Esmerna/index|Esmerna]] chose them. The Vientos family's gift makes a knife-wing clever enough to carry a message wherever its owner commands, to a single ship at sea or a single house on an island it has never seen, and Esmerna knife-wings are now the most common messenger birds in Oceana. Harbormasters, merchant houses and the coalition army all keep them. To shoot one down is a crime on nearly every island, and an insult to [[Ruthala]], whose messengers they are.

A knife-wing is clever, but it is no mind reader. Sent with a poor description, or with only an island and a name, it does the sensible thing and lands at the harbormaster's perch. So every harbormaster in Oceana is something of a postmaster too, reading the name on the letter and pointing the bird up the right street, and an old harbormaster knows more about who writes to whom than anyone else on the island.
