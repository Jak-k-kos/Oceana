# Malkin Ratter
No one owns a malkin ratter, and no malkin ratter has ever agreed to be owned. They are lean, quiet cats, a little larger than any hearth cat, with brindled coats the color of weathered stone and ears tipped with dark tufts. They live wherever there is stone and mice: in the dry-stone walls of the terraces, in the rafters of granaries, and among the barrels in the holds of ships. Nearly every island in Oceana has its malkins, though few islanders could say how many.

They are half wild and half something else. Over three thousand years, the islands have made them so. A malkin that takes a hen is driven off or killed, and a malkin that keeps the mice from the grain is left in peace. Generation by generation, only the gentle ones were left to raise their kittens, and the long years have made them rat hunters to the bone. A malkin will now sun itself on a farmer's doorstep and let a child scratch its ears, then vanish for a week into the walls. Sailors count a malkin aboard as good luck, and islands that live off eggs and guano welcome them gladly, for a malkin kills the rats that would empty the nests. An island that loses its malkins soon loses half its harvest to the rats. Killing one without cause is thought to sour [[Ruthala|Ruthala's]] favor, and few will risk it.
