# Shops, Shells and Survival
The sea doesn't care how brave you are. It cares whether you brought enough water, whether the biscuit in your hold has turned, and whether you can pay the harbor master before the tide goes out. Out here, a full purse and a full barrel keep you alive as surely as a sharp cutlass. This is how sailors in Oceana eat, trade, heal, and get by from one port to the next.
