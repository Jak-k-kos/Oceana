# Diver's Broth
## Description
A thin, salty broth of fish heads and dried kelp, drunk by pearl divers before they go under. Divers swear it slows the heart and stretches the breath. Nobody else drinks it if they can help it.

**Difficulty:** Normal

**Cook time:** 1 hour

## Ingredients
1) 2 rations of fish
2) 1 bundle of dried kelp
3) 1 lb of salt
4) 2 water rations

Makes 2 servings.

## Effects
1) You can hold your breath for twice as long. (12-15)
2) Also +1 to Strength (Athletics) checks to swim. (16-19)
3) Also you gain a swimming speed equal to your walking speed. (20-21)
