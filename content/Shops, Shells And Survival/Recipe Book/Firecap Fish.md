# Firecap Fish
## Description
Raw fish cured in citrus and salt, then buried in sliced firecap peppers until the flesh turns white and the eyes water. It's easy to make and hard to make well. Too little pepper and it's just fish; too much and the eater spends the afternoon breathing through their mouth. Done right, it lights a fire that has a crew moving before the bell stops ringing.

**Difficulty:** Hard

**Cook time:** 1 hour

**Properties:** Perishable, Fishy

No fire needed.

## Ingredients
1) 2 rations of fish
2) 2 citrus
3) 1 lb of salt
4) 1 handful of firecap peppers

Makes 3 servings.

## Effects
1) Your speed increases by 5 ft. (17-18)
2) Also +1 to Dexterity saving throws. (19-20)
3) Also advantage on initiative rolls. (21)
