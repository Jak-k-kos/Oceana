# Lentil and Garlic Soup
## Description
A thick, peppery soup heavy with garlic. Every ship's surgeon swears by it, and every grandmother on every island makes it the moment someone coughs. Whether it's the garlic or the stubbornness, people who eat it seem to shake off sickness faster.

**Difficulty:** Normal

**Cook time:** 1 hour

**Properties:** Perishable, Rancid

## Ingredients
1) 2 rations of dried lentils
2) 1 ration of vegetables
3) 1 bundle of dried herbs

Makes 3 servings.

## Effects
1) +1 to saving throws against disease. (12-15)
2) Also +1 to Constitution saving throws. (16-19)
3) Also advantage on saving throws against disease and being poisoned. (20-21)
