# Honey Cakes
## Description
Small round cakes, soaked in warm honey and baked until the edges crack. They're made for festivals and homecomings, and a captain who shares them with the crew finds the crew a little quicker to listen.

**Difficulty:** Normal

**Cook time:** 1 hour

**Properties:** Perishable, Rancid

## Ingredients
1) 1 ration of flour
2) 2 lb of honey
3) 1 dozen eggs

Makes 3 servings.

## Effects
1) Add 1d4 to Command Crew Actions. (12-15)
2) Also +1 to Charisma checks. (16-19)
3) Also, once before the effect ends, you can use your reaction to add 1d4 to the roll of an ally within 30 ft who can hear you. (20-21)
