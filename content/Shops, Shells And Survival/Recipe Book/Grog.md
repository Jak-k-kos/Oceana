# Grog
## Description
Every sailor's first drink and most sailors' last. Watering the rum stretches it and keeps the crew on their feet, and the citrus keeps their teeth in their heads. A cup of grog after a bad watch makes the next one seem possible.

**Difficulty:** Easy

**Cook time:** 10 minutes

**Properties:** Perishable, Stagnant

Counts as a water ration.

## Ingredients
1) 4 water rations
2) 1 bottle of rum
3) 1 citrus
4) 1 lb of raw sugar

Makes 4 servings.

## Effects
1) +1 to saving throws against being frightened. (10-14)
2) Also advantage on saving throws against being frightened. (15-18)
3) Also +1 to Wisdom saving throws. (19-21)
