# Whaler's Iron Stew
## Description
A dark, heavy stew of smoked whale meat, salt beef and firecap peppers, cooked down for half a day until it's almost black. Whalers eat it before a hunt, because it's the last thing some of them will ever eat. Those who've survived a whale's thrashing swear the stew is what kept them breathing.

**Difficulty:** Master

**Cook time:** 5 hours

## Ingredients
1) 2 rations of smoked whale meat
2) 2 rations of salted beef
3) 1 handful of firecap peppers
4) 1 bottle of rum

Makes 4 servings.

## Effects
1) +1 to death saving throws. (18-19)
2) Advantage on death saving throws instead. (20)
3) Also, the first time you would drop to 0 hit points before the effect ends, you drop to 1 hit point instead. (21)
