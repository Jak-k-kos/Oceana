# Conch Chowder
## Description
Thick, creamy and peppery, full of chewy conch pounded tender with a mallet. Divers say it puts the bellow of the conch horn in your chest. Pirates say the same thing, and they mean something worse by it.

**Difficulty:** Normal

**Cook time:** 2 hours

## Ingredients
1) 2 rations of dried conch
2) 1 ration of vegetables
3) 1 pinch of pepper
4) 1 ration of ship's biscuit

Makes 4 servings.

## Effects
1) +1 to Charisma (Intimidation) checks. (12-15)
2) Also +1 to Strength saving throws. (16-19)
3) Also advantage on Charisma (Intimidation) checks. (20-21)
