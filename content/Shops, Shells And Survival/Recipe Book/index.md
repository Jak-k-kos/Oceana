Recipes are learned on your travels: taught, traded, found or bought. Each one you learn is added here.

# Cooking
- **What you need:** the recipe's ingredients, cook's utensils, and a fire or ship's galley. Each recipe lists its cook time, and you can cook during a short rest.
- **The roll:** roll 2d10 and add them together. You can stop there, or roll another d10 and add it to your total. Keep rolling one die at a time for as long as you like.
- **Going over 21 busts the dish.** A total of 22 or more always busts.
- **When you stop,** your total decides the meal's effect, using the ranges for its difficulty. Each higher effect includes the ones below it unless it says "instead."
- **A short or busted dish** is still perfectly edible. It counts as a ration but gives no effect.
- **Servings:** each serving counts as 1 food ration, or 1 water ration for drinks.
- **Cooked food is Perishable.** It needs to be eaten by the end of the next day.
- **One meal at a time:** you can only benefit from one meal's effect at a time. A new meal replaces the old effect.
- **Duration:** unless a recipe says otherwise, effects last until the end of your next short rest.

# Techniques
When you gain proficiency with cook's utensils, choose one technique. You can learn more as you go. Unless a technique says otherwise, you can only learn it once.

| Technique | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Light Touch | Once per cook, when you roll an extra die, you can roll a d6 instead of a d10. |
| Second Taste | Once per cook, reroll the last die you added, even if it busted the dish. You must use the new roll. |
| Steady Hand | Once per cook, after you stop rolling, add 1 to your total. |
| Taste As You Go | Twice per cook, roll your next die before adding it. If you don't like it, set it aside and stop with the total you had. |
| Save the Dish | Once per cook, when you bust, remove the last die you added and stop. |
| Signature Dish | Choose one recipe you know. You cook it using the next easier difficulty's ranges. You can learn this more than once, choosing a different recipe each time. |
| Big Pot | When you cook, the recipe makes half again as many servings, rounded down. |
| Make Do | You can cook a recipe with one of its ingredients missing. If you do, the dish can't give its third effect. |
