# Glassleaf Salad
## Description
Dried glassleaf soaked until it's clear and supple again, then tossed with sea fennel and sharp vinegar. It's cold and crunchy and tastes faintly of the sea. Riggers eat it before a long day aloft, and claim it settles the stomach and steadies the feet.

**Difficulty:** Normal

**Cook time:** 1 hour

**Properties:** Perishable, Fishy

## Ingredients
1) 2 rations of glassleaf
2) 1 bundle of sea fennel
3) 1 flask of vinegar

Makes 3 servings.

## Effects
1) Advantage on saving throws against being knocked prone. (12-15)
2) Also you gain a climbing speed equal to your walking speed. (16-19)
3) Also waves, rams and the ship's movement can't knock you prone or throw you overboard. (20-21)
