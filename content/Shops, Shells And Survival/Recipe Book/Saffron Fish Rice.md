# Saffron Fish Rice
## Description
Golden rice, slow-cooked in fish broth with a pinch of saffron worth more than the rest of the pot together. Too much saffron turns it bitter and too little wastes the coin, so few cooks dare it. Scholars and navigators prize it for keeping the mind clear through long nights over charts.

**Difficulty:** Hard

**Cook time:** 2 hours

## Ingredients
1) 2 rations of fish
2) 2 rations of rice
3) 1 pinch of saffron

Makes 4 servings.

## Effects
1) +1 to Intelligence and Wisdom checks. (17-18)
2) Also +1 to Intelligence and Wisdom saving throws. (19-20)
3) Also advantage on saving throws against being charmed. (21)
