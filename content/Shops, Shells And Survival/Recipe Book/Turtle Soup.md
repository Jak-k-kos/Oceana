# Turtle Soup
## Description
A rich, dark soup simmered for hours from sea turtle, white wine and herbs. Turtles are hard to catch and harder to butcher, and the soup is harder still to get right, so few have ever tasted a good bowl. Sailors say a good bowl puts iron in your blood. Some won't touch it at all, out of respect for [[Caruilla]].

**Difficulty:** Master

**Cook time:** 4 hours

## Ingredients
1) 2 rations of sea turtle
2) 1 bottle of white wine
3) 1 bundle of dried herbs
4) 1 ration of vegetables

Makes 4 servings.

## Effects
1) +1 to Constitution saving throws. (18-19)
2) Also gain 1d4 + 2 temporary hit points. (20)
3) Gain 2d4 + 4 temporary hit points instead, and advantage on Constitution saves to keep concentration. (21)
