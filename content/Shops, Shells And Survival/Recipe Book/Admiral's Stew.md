# Admiral's Stew
## Description
A stew of fresh fish and shellfish, cooked in white wine with citrus and a pinch of saffron. It's a captain's dish served at flagship tables, and one of the hardest stews a cook can attempt. Old officers swear it stiffens the spine.

**Difficulty:** Master

**Cook time:** 3 hours

**Properties:** Perishable, Fishy

## Ingredients
1) 2 rations of fish
2) 1 ration of shellfish
3) 1 bottle of white wine
4) 1 citrus
5) 1 pinch of saffron

Makes 4 servings.

## Effects
1) +1 to Dexterity and Constitution saving throws. (18-19)
2) Also +1 to AC. (20)
3) Also, once before the effect ends, you can use your reaction to give an attack against you disadvantage. (21)
