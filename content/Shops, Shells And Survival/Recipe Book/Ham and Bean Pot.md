# Ham and Bean Pot
## Description
Salted ham and beans, slow-baked under molasses until the beans fall apart and the ham falls off the bone. This is what the loading crews eat before a day of hauling cargo up the gangplank. Nothing else keeps a stevedore going until sundown.

**Difficulty:** Normal

**Cook time:** 2 hours

## Ingredients
1) 2 rations of salted ham
2) 2 rations of dried beans
3) 1 jar of molasses

Makes 4 servings.

## Effects
1) +1 to Strength checks to lift, push, pull or carry. (12-15)
2) Also +1 to Strength (Athletics) checks. (16-19)
3) Also advantage on checks and saving throws to avoid being grappled or shoved. (20-21)
