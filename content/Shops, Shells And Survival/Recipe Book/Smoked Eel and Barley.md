# Smoked Eel and Barley
## Description
Smoked eel, flaked into a pot of barley, slow-cooked thick and smoky. It's a navigator's supper, eaten late over charts and lanterns. Old navigators swear it helps them feel the stars through the clouds.

**Difficulty:** Normal

**Cook time:** 1 hour

**Properties:** Perishable, Fishy

## Ingredients
1) 2 rations of salted or smoked fish
2) 2 rations of barley

Makes 4 servings.

## Effects
1) +1 to checks made with navigator's tools. (12-15)
2) Also +1 to Intelligence (Investigation) checks. (16-19)
3) Also advantage on checks made with navigator's tools, and you always know which way is north. (20-21)
