# Fried Fish and Flatbread
## Description
The day's catch, fried hot in oil and folded into bread still blistered from the pan. It's the first thing a crew cooks when the lines come up full, and lookouts swear they see further with it in their bellies.

**Difficulty:** Easy

**Cook time:** 30 minutes

## Ingredients
1) 2 rations of fish
2) 1 ration of flour
3) 1 flask of olive oil

Makes 3 servings.

## Effects
1) +1 to Wisdom (Perception) checks. (10-14)
2) Also +1 to Wisdom saving throws. (15-18)
3) Also advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight. (19-21)
