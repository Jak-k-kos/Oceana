# Bosun's Biscuit
## Description
Ship's biscuit split, soaked in honey and toasted on the galley stove until it crackles. The bosun hands them out to whoever's on the pumps or the patching crew. It's not much, but it's sweet, and sweet is rare at sea.

**Difficulty:** Easy

**Cook time:** 10 minutes

**Properties:** Perishable, Rancid

## Ingredients
1) 2 rations of ship's biscuit
2) 1 lb of honey

Makes 2 servings.

## Effects
1) +1 to Repair action checks. (10-14)
2) Also +1 to Strength (Athletics) checks. (15-18)
3) Also advantage on Repair action checks. (19-21)
