# Tea
## Description
Dried leaves steeped in hot water, taken slow by navigators, clerks and anyone with a long night of charts ahead of them. Every island has its own blend and swears it's the best. A good pot settles the mind and sharpens it at the same time.

**Difficulty:** Easy

**Cook time:** 10 minutes

**Properties:** Perishable, Stagnant

A brew, not a meal. Doesn't count as a water ration. See [[Shops, Shells And Survival/Recipe Book/index#Brews|Brews]].

## Ingredients
1) 1 day's brew of tea leaves
2) 1 water ration

Makes 1 pot (4 mugs).

## Effects
1) +1 to Intelligence checks. (10-14)
2) Also +1 to Wisdom (Insight) checks. (15-18)
3) Also, once before the effect ends, you can give yourself advantage on an Intelligence check. (19-21)
