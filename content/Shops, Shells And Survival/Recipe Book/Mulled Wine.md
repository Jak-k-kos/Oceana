# Mulled Wine
## Description
Common wine warmed slowly with honey and a little pepper until the whole galley smells of it. It's for cold nights on watch and the long northern crossings, when the wind cuts straight through an oilskin.

**Difficulty:** Easy

**Cook time:** 20 minutes

**Properties:** Perishable, Sour

Counts as a water ration.

## Ingredients
1) 4 gallons of common wine
2) 1 lb of honey
3) 1 pinch of pepper

Makes 4 servings.

## Effects
1) +1 to saving throws against being poisoned. (10-14)
2) Also +1 to Charisma (Persuasion) checks. (15-18)
3) Also resistance to cold damage. (19-21)
