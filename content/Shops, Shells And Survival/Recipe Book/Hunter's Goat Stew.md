# Hunter's Goat Stew
## Description
Fresh goat braised with olives and red wine, the way the hill herders make it. It's a meal for people who spend the day reading tracks, wind and weather, and they swear it sharpens the senses for it.

**Difficulty:** Normal

**Cook time:** 2 hours

## Ingredients
1) 2 rations of goat
2) 1 jar of olives
3) 1 bottle of fine red wine

Makes 3 servings.

## Effects
1) +1 to Wisdom (Survival) checks. (12-15)
2) Also +1 to Intelligence (Nature) checks. (16-19)
3) Also advantage on Wisdom (Survival) checks to fish, forage or track. (20-21)
