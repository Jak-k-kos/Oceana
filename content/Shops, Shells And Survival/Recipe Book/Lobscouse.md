# Lobscouse
## Description
Salt beef, crushed biscuit and onion, stewed down into a brown hash thick enough to stand a spoon in. It's the oldest meal at sea and the plainest. It is also the reason sailors can haul on a line for hours.

**Difficulty:** Normal

**Cook time:** 1 hour

## Ingredients
1) 2 rations of salted beef
2) 1 ration of ship's biscuit
3) 1 ration of vegetables

Makes 4 servings.

## Effects
1) +1 to Strength checks. (12-15)
2) Also +1 to Strength saving throws. (16-19)
3) Also advantage on Sail Crew Actions. (20-21)
