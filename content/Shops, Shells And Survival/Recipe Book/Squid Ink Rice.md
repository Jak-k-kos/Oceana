# Squid Ink Rice
## Description
Rice cooked black in squid ink with strips of dried squid. It stains the teeth and the tongue for a day. Smugglers eat it before a night run, half for luck and half because a black grin doesn't flash in the dark.

**Difficulty:** Normal

**Cook time:** 1 hour

## Ingredients
1) 2 rations of rice
2) 1 ration of dried squid
3) 1 pot of squid ink

Makes 3 servings.

## Effects
1) +1 to Dexterity (Stealth) checks. (12-15)
2) Also +1 to Dexterity checks. (16-19)
3) Also advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks in dim light or darkness. (20-21)
