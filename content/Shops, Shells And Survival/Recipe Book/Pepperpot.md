# Pepperpot
## Description
A black, fiery stew of salt beef, vegetables and firecap peppers, kept going on the stove for days and topped up as it's eaten. Some ships' pots are said to have been simmering for years. Gun crews eat it before battle, for the heat they say keeps the fire from biting.

**Difficulty:** Normal

**Cook time:** 2 hours

**Properties:** Perishable, Fishy

## Ingredients
1) 2 rations of salted beef
2) 1 ration of vegetables
3) 1 handful of firecap peppers

Makes 3 servings.

## Effects
1) +1 to saving throws against fire. (12-15)
2) Also +1 to Constitution checks. (16-19)
3) Also resistance to fire damage. (20-21)
