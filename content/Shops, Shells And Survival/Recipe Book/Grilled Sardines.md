# Grilled Sardines
## Description
A string of sardines grilled whole over the coals, salted hard and eaten bones and all. Deckhands eat them standing at the rail, flicking the tails overboard. It's good food for quick, nimble fingers, which is why pickpockets love it too.

**Difficulty:** Easy

**Cook time:** 20 minutes

## Ingredients
1) 2 rations of fish
2) 1 lb of salt

Makes 2 servings.

## Effects
1) +1 to Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) checks. (10-14)
2) Also +1 to checks made with thieves' tools or navigator's tools. (15-18)
3) Also advantage on Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) checks. (19-21)
