# Captain's Supper
## Description
Salted ham, cave-aged cheese, amber slabs of roe and sweet dried figs, served with a bottle of the best red the purse can bear. This is the meal a captain lays out to close a deal, seal an alliance, or thank a crew that brought her home. Those who sit at such a table rise from it a little braver.

**Difficulty:** Master

**Cook time:** 3 hours

## Ingredients
1) 2 rations of salted ham
2) 1 lb of cave-aged cheese
3) 1 lb of dried mullet roe
4) 1 ration of dried figs
5) 1 bottle of fine red wine

Makes 4 servings.

## Effects
1) +1 to Charisma checks. (18-19)
2) Also +1 to all saving throws. (20)
3) Also, once before the effect ends, you can reroll a failed saving throw. (21)
