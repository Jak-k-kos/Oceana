# Fried Plantains
## Description
Ripe plantains sliced thick and fried in oil until the edges blacken and the middles turn soft and sweet, with a drizzle of molasses on top. They're dock food, sold from carts and eaten with fingers. Somehow there's always music wherever someone's frying them.

**Difficulty:** Easy

**Cook time:** 30 minutes

## Ingredients
1) 8 plantains
2) 1 flask of olive oil
3) 1 jar of molasses

Makes 2 servings.

## Effects
1) +1 to Charisma (Performance) checks. (10-14)
2) Also +1 to Dexterity (Acrobatics) checks. (15-18)
3) Also advantage on Charisma (Performance) checks. (19-21)
