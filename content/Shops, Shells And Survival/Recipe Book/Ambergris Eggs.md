# Ambergris Eggs
## Description
Soft eggs, scrambled slow in butter with a shaving of ambergris worth more than the eggs, the plate and the table together. It's an old aristocrat's breakfast that few have ever tasted. Those who have speak of a strange, lingering sharpness, as if the world comes into focus.

**Difficulty:** Master

**Cook time:** 1 hour

**Properties:** Perishable, Rancid

## Ingredients
1) 1 dozen eggs
2) 1 ounce of ambergris
3) 1 flask of olive oil
4) 1 pinch of pepper

Makes 2 servings.

## Effects
1) +1 to Charisma saving throws. (18-19)
2) Also +1 to all ability checks. (20)
3) Also, once before the effect ends, you can reroll a failed ability check. (21)
