# Pepper-Crusted Tuna
## Description
Thick cuts of fresh tuna rolled in cracked pepper and seared fast in a smoking-hot pan, raw and red in the middle. Gunnery crews claim it steadies the hand and sharpens the eye. Get the sear wrong and it's just expensive, tough fish.

**Difficulty:** Hard

**Cook time:** 1 hour

## Ingredients
1) 2 rations of fish
2) 1 pinch of pepper
3) 1 flask of olive oil
4) 1 citrus

Makes 2 servings.

## Effects
1) +1 to attack rolls with ship weapons. (17-18)
2) Also +1 to ranged attack rolls. (19-20)
3) Also your ranged attacks ignore half cover. (21)
