# Ink-Braised Cuttlefish
## Description
Whole cuttlefish braised slowly in their own black ink with wine and herbs until tender. It's tricky: too long and the meat turns to rubber, too short and the ink stays bitter. Scribes and mapmakers prize it, and claim the ink gets into their thoughts.

**Difficulty:** Hard

**Cook time:** 2 hours

**Properties:** Perishable, Fishy

## Ingredients
1) 2 rations of dried cuttlefish
2) 1 pot of squid ink
3) 1 bottle of white wine
4) 1 bundle of dried herbs

Makes 3 servings.

## Effects
1) +1 to Intelligence (Investigation) checks. (17-18)
2) Also +1 to Intelligence saving throws. (19-20)
3) Also advantage on Intelligence (Investigation) and Intelligence (History) checks. (21)
