# Surgeon's Broth
## Description
Bones boiled for hours with garlic and herbs until the broth turns golden. Every ship's surgeon keeps a pot of it going, and claims it steadies the hands as much as the stomach. The patients mostly just like that it's hot.

**Difficulty:** Easy

**Cook time:** 1 hour

**Properties:** Perishable, Fishy

## Ingredients
1) 1 ration of salted beef or fish
2) 1 ration of vegetables
3) 1 bundle of dried herbs

Makes 2 servings.

## Effects
1) +1 to Wisdom (Medicine) checks. (10-14)
2) Also +1 to checks made with a healer's kit or herbalism kit. (15-18)
3) Also advantage on Wisdom (Medicine) checks. (19-21)
