# Ship's Porridge
## Description
When the biscuit is too hard to bite, sailors crush it with a belaying pin and boil it down. A spoonful of molasses turns it from punishment into breakfast. Nobody has ever asked for seconds, but nobody has ever starved on it either.

**Difficulty:** Easy

**Cook time:** 30 minutes

## Ingredients
1) 2 rations of ship's biscuit
2) 1 jar of molasses
3) 2 water rations

Makes 2 servings.

## Effects
1) +1 to Constitution checks. (10-14)
2) Also +1 to Constitution saving throws. (15-18)
3) Also advantage on saving throws against exhaustion. (19-21)
