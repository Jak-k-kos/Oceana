# Coffee
## Description
Black, bitter and boiled hard in the galley before the morning watch. Beans come in by the sack from the hot islands, and a ship that runs out of them runs on short tempers. The first mug wakes you up. The second keeps your eyes on the horizon.

**Difficulty:** Easy

**Cook time:** 10 minutes

**Properties:** Perishable, Stagnant

A brew, not a meal. Doesn't count as a water ration. See [[Shops, Shells And Survival/Recipe Book/index#Brews|Brews]].

## Ingredients
1) 1 day's brew of coffee beans
2) 1 water ration

Makes 1 pot (4 mugs).

## Effects
1) +1 to initiative rolls. (10-14)
2) Also +1 to Wisdom (Perception) checks. (15-18)
3) Also advantage on saving throws against being put to sleep. (19-21)
