# Topman's Glazed Mullet
## Description
Mullet glazed in bitter-orange liqueur and honey, roasted until the skin crackles. It's the favorite of the topmen who work the highest yards. They say it keeps the feet light and the fall short.

**Difficulty:** Hard

**Cook time:** 1 hour

## Ingredients
1) 2 rations of fish
2) 1 bottle of bitter-orange liqueur
3) 1 lb of honey

Makes 3 servings.

## Effects
1) +1 to Dexterity (Acrobatics) checks. (17-18)
2) Also you take half damage from falling. (19-20)
3) Also advantage on Dexterity (Acrobatics) checks and on checks to escape a grapple. (21)
