# Stuffed Grape Leaves
## Description
Brined grape leaves rolled tight around rice, herbs and a little oil. They're small, neat and served cold on a platter. This is the food of merchants' tables, and of the deals made across them.

**Difficulty:** Normal

**Cook time:** 1 hour

**Properties:** Perishable, Rancid

## Ingredients
1) 1 jar of grape leaves
2) 1 ration of rice
3) 1 bundle of dried herbs
4) 1 flask of olive oil

Makes 2 servings.

## Effects
1) +1 to Charisma (Deception) checks. (12-15)
2) Also +1 to Charisma (Persuasion) checks. (16-19)
3) Also advantage on Charisma (Deception) checks. (20-21)
