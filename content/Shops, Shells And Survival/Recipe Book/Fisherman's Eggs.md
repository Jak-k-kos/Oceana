# Fisherman's Eggs
## Description
Eggs scrambled with flaked salt fish and a pinch of herbs, eaten standing up in the dark before the boats go out. Fishermen say it's the only breakfast that gets them awake before their eyes open.

**Difficulty:** Easy

**Cook time:** 30 minutes

**Properties:** Perishable, Fishy

## Ingredients
1) 1 dozen eggs
2) 1 ration of salted or smoked fish
3) 1 bundle of dried herbs

Makes 2 servings.

## Effects
1) +1 to initiative rolls. (10-14)
2) Also +1 to Wisdom (Insight) checks. (15-18)
3) Also advantage on Wisdom (Insight) checks. (19-21)
