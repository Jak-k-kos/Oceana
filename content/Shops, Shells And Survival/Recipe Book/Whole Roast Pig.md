# Whole Roast Pig
## Description
A whole pig, roasted all day over the coals, glazed with molasses and citrus. It's a feast for a whole crew, made for launches, victories and the night before a battle everyone knows is coming. A crew that eats together fights together.

**Difficulty:** Master

**Cook time:** 6 hours

## Ingredients
1) 1 pig (60 rations of meat)
2) 2 jars of molasses
3) 4 citrus
4) 1 bundle of dried herbs

Makes 60 servings.

## Effects
1) +1 to all Crew Action checks. (18-19)
2) Also advantage on saving throws against being frightened. (20)
3) Also, once before the effect ends, a Crew Action that fails can be treated as a success instead. (21)
