Shot, powder and the stores that keep a ship fighting and afloat. Market prices in shells (SH). What you actually pay is down to haggling, and prices run higher or lower from island to island.

Loose supplies don't count toward Tonnage until they add up to a full cargo unit. Every cargo unit takes up 1/5 ton of a ship's Tonnage.

# Ammunition
Each round includes its powder charge. See [[Ship Weapons]] for what each round does. The cannon and long gun fire the same rounds. A grappling hook can be hauled back aboard after a fight unless its chain was broken, a harpoon can often be cut from whatever it struck, and a boarding plank can be drawn back aboard once the fighting is done. Signal flares are fired from a signal hand cannon (see [[#Signals]]).

**Boarding plank.** 20 ft long and 10 ft wide. Placing it securely takes an action. AC 15, 22 HP, immune to poison and psychic damage, vulnerable to fire. Holds up to 800 lb.

**Naval mine.** A crew member primes it and drops it overboard with the Operate Crew Action. The next object to move into its space sets it off, dealing 28 (8d6) fire damage in a 10-foot radius. It also goes off if it drops to 0 hit points.
- Once placed, it's hidden. Spotting it takes a DC 15 Wisdom (Perception) check.
- Disarming it takes a DC 15 Dexterity check. A disarmed mine can be primed again.
- AC 10, 10 HP, immune to poison and psychic damage.

# Repair and Upkeep
| Item             | Single        | Price  | Cargo (1/5 ton) | Cargo Price |

| Emergency stores | 1 unit        | 950 SH | 5 units         | 4,275 SH    |
| Heavy cordage    | Coil (100 ft) | 140 SH | 10 coils        | 1,260 SH    |
| Spare anchor     | 1             | 950 SH | -               | -           |
| Fire sand        | Bucket        | 95 SH  | -               | -           |

**Repair stores.** Timber, pitch, oakum, nails and cordage, bundled for field repairs. Each Hull Die spent with the [[Damage and Repair#Repairing Damage|Repair]] action uses up 1 unit of repair stores. Without them, you can't make field repairs.

**Emergency stores.** Pre-cut patches, braced timbers and quick-setting pitch, made to be slapped over a hole in a hurry. On a successful Repair action, you can use 1 unit of emergency stores instead of spending a Hull Die. Roll one of the ship's Hull Dice and add the result to its Hull Points, but the die isn't spent.

A unit of repair stores is roughly a bucket of pitch, a bundle of oakum, a couple of planks, and some nails and cordage. The materials themselves are sold as [[Raw Goods#Timber and Shipbuilding|Raw Goods]]. A spare anchor takes up 1/5 ton on its own.

**Fire sand.** A bucket of sand. When a crew member fights a fire with it, they reduce the fire by 2d6 instead of 1d6, or 3d6 with [[Ship Upgrades#Fire Troughs\|Fire Troughs]]. One use.

# Fuel
| Item          | Single       | Price | Cargo (1/5 ton) | Cargo Price |
| ------------- | ------------ | ----- | --------------- | ----------- |
| Kelp charcoal | Sack (50 lb) | 24 SH | 8 sacks         | 173 SH      |
| Charcoal      | Sack (50 lb) | 95 SH | 8 sacks         | 684 SH      |

**Kelp charcoal.** Dried kelp, pressed into bricks and charred in slow kilns. It burns hot and long, and it's what feeds the furnaces of every fuel-powered ship in Oceana. It never spoils, but wet charcoal won't burn until it's dried out on deck for a day. See [[Basic Ship Rules#Properties|Fuel Powered]] for how much a ship burns.

**Charcoal.** Wood charcoal, made for forges. It burns hotter than kelp charcoal and feeds a ship's furnace just the same, sack for sack, but at four times the price few captains burn it unless it's all they have. Like kelp charcoal, it won't burn wet.
