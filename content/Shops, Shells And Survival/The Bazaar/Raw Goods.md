Trade goods and raw materials, bought by the merchant or the shipload. Prices swing more here than anywhere else in the Bazaar. Goods are cheapest where they're made, and dearest where they're needed. Market prices in shells (SH).

Loose goods don't count toward Tonnage until they add up to a full cargo unit. Every cargo unit takes up 1/5 ton of a ship's Tonnage.

# Gemstones
Most fine stones in Oceana come up from wrecks and drowned cities, not mines. Stones from the sea are easy to come by. Stones from the land are rarer than they've ever been.

# Salvage and Curios

| Value | Examples |
| --- | --- |

| 24,000 SH | A carved figurehead, a silver reliquary, an old navigator's instrument |
| 72,000 SH | A gold-chased sea chest, a drowned noble's signet ring |
| 240,000 SH | A drowned temple's idol, a jeweled ceremonial anchor |
| 720,000 SH | A crown of a nation lost to the flood |

**Monster parts.** Sea serpent hide, kraken beak, dragon turtle shell and other trophies of the hunt sell by the DM's ruling, often for thousands of shells.
