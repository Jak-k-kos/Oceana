**Rain canvas:** a canvas set out on a rainy day catches 1d4 gallons in light rain, 2d4 in medium rain, or 4d4 in heavy rain. A small ship can rig 1 canvas, a medium ship 2, a large ship 3, and a huge ship 4. Caught rainwater needs a barrel, cask or jug to go into. It's a new lot of water with a Keep die of d8, Hardy, Stagnant.
