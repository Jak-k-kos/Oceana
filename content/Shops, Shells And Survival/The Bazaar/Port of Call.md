Market prices in shells (SH). What you actually pay is down to haggling, and prices run higher or lower from island to island. Anything made or served for you costs more than buying the raw goods from [[Provisions]]. 
# Meals
Every port has somewhere to eat, from a dockside fish stall to a captain's dining hall. These are the going prices at the taverns, inns and cookhouses ashore. Each meal counts as 1 food ration.

| Meal | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Poor (bean pottage, fish heads, yesterday's bread) | 5 SH |
| Modest (fish stew and bread) | 10 SH |
| Comfortable (roast meat or fish, vegetables, a cup of wine) | 30 SH |
| Wealthy (several courses, good wine) | 80 SH |
| Aristocratic (a feast with imported spice and fine wine) | 200 SH |

## House Specialties
A wealthy or aristocratic meal can be a recipe from the [[Shops, Shells And Survival/Recipe Book/index|Recipe Book]], if the kitchen knows it. Wealthy kitchens cook Easy and Normal recipes, and aristocratic kitchens cook Hard and Master recipes. The kitchen's skill decides how good it turns out.
- **Price:** the meal's price, or twice the recipe's ingredient cost per serving, whichever is higher.
- The effect works the same as a meal you cook yourself, including only benefiting from one meal at a time.

# Drinks
Taverns, inns and dockside stalls pour everything from Hold Ale to lotus wine. A sailor fresh off a long voyage can fill a waterskin, drink a mug at the bar, or buy the house's finest bottle for the table. These are the going prices.

A skin fill is 1 gallon poured into your waterskin and counts as 1 water ration. Cups, mugs, bottles and pots don't.

# Harbor
Docking includes the harbor's night watch. While a ship is docked, the harbor is responsible for it.

| Service | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Docking, small ship | 20 SH per day |
| Docking, medium ship | 80 SH per day |
| Docking, large ship | 200 SH per day |
| Docking, huge ship | 500 SH per day |
| Anchoring in the harbor (no watch) | A quarter of the docking fee |
| Harbor pilot | 50 SH |
| Loading or unloading cargo | 1 SH per cargo unit |
| Warehouse storage | 1 SH per cargo unit per day |
| Dry dock materials | 1,000 SH per day |
| Shipwright | 300 SH per day |

Dry dock repairs follow the [[Damage and Repair#Long-Term Repairs|Long-Term Repairs]] rules. Materials are always needed. A shipwright is only needed if your own crew isn't doing the work.

# Passage and Freight
Fares are paid per hex of the planned route when you board, not the distance actually sailed. Detours, storms and calm days don't change the fare. Fares include food of poor-meal quality. The minimum fare is the price of 5 hexes.

| Fare | Price per hex |
| --- | --- |
| Deck passage | 2 SH |
| Hammock below decks | 3 SH |
| Shared cabin | 6 SH |
| Private cabin | 16 SH |
| Freight | 1 SH per cargo unit |
