Medicines, remedies and the tools of the healing trade. Their ingredients come from far-off islands and deep water, so none of it comes cheap. Market prices in shells (SH). What you actually pay is down to haggling, and prices run higher or lower from island to island.

**Using draughts.** Drinking or applying a draught on yourself takes a bonus action. Using one on another creature takes an action. You can be under the effects of only two draughts or tinctures at a time. If you take a third, the oldest one ends.

## Deadman's Draught
A black, bitter last resort, brewed for boarding parties and men with nothing left to lose. As it takes hold, the drinker's mind slips away, leaving only the fight. It lasts 3 rounds.

**For the duration:**
- You have advantage on melee weapon attack rolls.
- Your melee weapon attacks deal an extra 1d6 damage.
- Your melee weapon attacks score a critical hit on a 19 or 20.
- You have disadvantage on ranged attack rolls.
- Attack rolls against you have advantage.
- You're immune to being charmed and frightened.

**At 0 hit points:**
- Dropping to 0 hit points doesn't knock you unconscious. You keep fighting.
- You make a death saving throw at the start of each of your turns, and taking damage still counts as a failed death save.
- Failures pile up, but you can't die until the draught ends.

**When it ends, you fall unconscious no matter what:**
- If you have three or more failed death saves, you die.
- If you're at 0 hit points with fewer failures, you keep dying, with your failures carried over.
- If you're above 0 hit points, you're unconscious but stable, and you can't be woken for 1 hour.
- Either way, you gain 2 levels of exhaustion.

If you regain hit points while it's active, your death saves reset as normal.

# Poisons
Most islands sell poison openly, for rats, sharks and pests. Some ban the worst of it, and some ban all of it. Applying a poison takes an action. Poisons aren't draughts, and don't count toward your limit of two.
- **Contact:** smeared on an object, it stays potent until it's touched or washed off. A creature that touches it with bare skin suffers its effects.
- **Ingested:** a creature must swallow a whole dose to suffer its effects. A dose can be hidden in food or drink.
- **Inhaled:** a powder or gas. Blowing the powder or releasing the gas exposes each creature in a 5-foot cube, and the cloud clears right after. Holding your breath doesn't protect you.
- **Injury:** applied to a weapon, up to 3 pieces of ammunition or trap that deals piercing or slashing damage. It stays potent until it gets into a wound or is washed off.
