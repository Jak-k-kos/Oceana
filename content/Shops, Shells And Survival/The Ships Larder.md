# The Ship's Larder

Below the creaking deck, past the ladder worn smooth by a hundred hurried hands, there lies a dim and fragrant kingdom of barrels and crates. Here the ship keeps its hope: biscuit and salt beef, sweet water and sour ale, the last brave loaves of the last port's bakery. And here, too, the sea keeps its patient hunger, for every morning it reaches down into the dark and takes a little of what was stored.

This is the quartermaster's ledger for that quiet war. It knows every provision sold in [[Shops, Shells And Survival/The Bazaar/Provisions|Provisions]], its Keep Die and its nature, and it follows the rules of [[House Rules#Spoilage|Spoilage]] to the letter.

# How the Ledger Is Kept

**Stowing provisions.** Choose a kind of food or drink, whether it rides loose or as cargo, and how much. If it was bought already past its best, give it a lower Keep. Fresh meat and fish are marked to spoil at the end of the following day, just as the rules decree.

**When dawn breaks.** Press *Dawn Breaks* once each morning at sea. The ledger casts every Keep Die, steps down those that come up 1, rolls their Loss Dice, eats away at turning lots, and lets perishable things quietly give up their freshness. Every roll is written in the Quartermaster's Log, so no one need wonder where the bread went.

**When the hold is struck.** If the hold floods or the ship is badly wounded, press *The Hold Is Struck*, and every lot steps down a die together.

**Eating, drinking and opening.** *Eat* or *Drink* draws rations from a lot. *Open* pries the lid from a cargo unit and spills its contents into the loose stores at the same Keep. *Butcher* turns living beasts into fresh, perishable meat.

**The spoiled portion.** Spoiled food is not gone, only ruined. Cast it overboard, or eat it anyway, and the ledger will remind you of the saving throw and the sickness that waits within.

**Shared dice.** Tick the box to use the optional rule, where one roll of each Keep Die is shared by every lot upon it.

**Mistakes and mercies.** *Undo* unmakes the last deed. *Amend* lets the quartermaster correct a count by hand.

**Mistakes and mercies, shared.** When the whole crew keeps one ledger, *Undo* returns the hold to just before your own last deed, and so it may also sweep away whatever a shipmate did in the moments between.

# Sharing the Hold Among the Crew

Whoever opens this page, on whatever device, at whatever hour, looks upon the very same barrels and the very same crates. When one shipmate eats, the others see the ration gone; when the quartermaster greets the dawn, every page turns together. A small light in the ledger's corner tells whether it is bound to the shared hold or, should the winds of the world fail, kept only on the device in your hands.

Dawn is best greeted by one hand alone. Should two shipmates press it within moments of each other, the ledger will pause and ask whether a second day has truly passed.

A *Message in a Bottle* still serves as a keepsake: cork the hold at the close of a session, and it can be uncorked again should ever the ledger need mending.
