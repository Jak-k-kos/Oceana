# The Champion
Tarek was the champion of [[Arkaius]], chosen alongside his brother from among the people of the old city. Where [[Tyrus]] was quiet and patient, Tarek was bold and warm and restless, and the Weaver sent him wherever the world needed a steady hand. The world has long since forgotten his name. Tyrus never will.
%%

%%
# The Last Days
Only days before [[Arkaius]] fell, Tarek ordered one last panel carved upon the temple walls: the Weaver alone upon the peak, facing a darkness at the edge of the world. What he knew, or what Arkaius had told him, he never said, not even to his brother.
%%

%%
# His Death
Tarek died in the Temple of [[Arkaius]] on the same day as his god, before the shattered statue of the Weaver, his hand stretched toward his staff. His bones lie there still, in [[Locations/The Widow's Wrath/Rayskaya/The Sealed Temple|the Sealed Temple]]. [[Tyrus]] has never moved them.
