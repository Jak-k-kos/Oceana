Gender: Male
Appearance: A towering grey-skinned goliath, his body a map of branching lightning scars from brow to heel. His eyes are milky white, clouded as a blind man's, and yet they follow every movement with an ease no blind man could ever know. He wears simple storm-grey robes and walks the sun-warmed stone barefoot.
Who: The keeper of the twin temples upon the peak of [[Locations/The Widow's Wrath/Rayskaya/index|Rayskaya]]. Each day he tends the Temple of [[Welestia]] with quiet devotion. The sister temple beside it stands locked, and he will let no one cross its threshold. Of that temple he will not speak at all, not even to say whose it is.

# The Warden
Tyrus is the warden of the peak, a mysterious keeper who watches over the Temple of [[Welestia]] and the sealed temple beside it. Where he came from, and how long he has walked these steps alone, he does not say. He tends the temples with the patience of the tide, as though there were nothing else in all the world worth doing.

%%
# The Warden
Tyrus is the Warden of the Wedded Temple, and he has been so since before the [[Arcanus Collapse]]. For more than two and a half thousand years its stones, its rites and its doors have been his charge alone. Kingdoms have risen and drowned and empires have come and gone in that time, and through all of it he has never once set his duties aside.
%%

%%
# The Gift
It was [[Arkaius]] himself who granted Tyrus unending life, so that the place where [[Arkaius|the Weaver]] and [[Welestia|the Storm]] were wed would never want for a keeper. The years pass him by like water around a stone, and he does not age.
%%

%%
# The Offer
Long before [[Arkaius|the Weaver]] fell, [[Welestia]] offered Tyrus the mantle of her champion. He bowed his head and refused it. He wanted no glory and no power, only to remain the humble caretaker of the temple he loved.
%%

%%
# The Twins
Tyrus had a twin brother, [[Tarek]], who was the champion of [[Arkaius]]. The world has long since forgotten Tarek's name. Tyrus never will.
%%

%%
# The Scars
On the day [[Arkaius]] died, [[Welestia|Welestia's]] grief broke over the peak like the end of the world. Tyrus stood before the temples he was sworn to keep and took her lightning upon himself, strike after strike after strike. He endured. He was saved from death, but at the cost of the scars that ravage his body to this day.
%%

%%
# The Voice in the Stone
Now and then, in the hush of the Temple of [[Welestia]], the goddess comes to speak with her keeper, and Tyrus kneels before her to listen. In all the long ages since [[Arkaius]] fell, he has been the only mortal she will speak to. Kings have begged her for mercy and sailors have screamed her name into the gale, and she has answered none of them. She answers only the keeper who would not leave her side. That changed when [[Shady Bellball]] came before her. Now there are two.
%%

%%
# The Sealed Temple
The sister temple is the Temple of [[Arkaius]], and it was there that [[Tarek]] died, on the very same day as his god. His skeleton lies there still, clad in his armor. The walls, the bones and every object in the room are blackened and scorched by lightning. Once each week, as his duty demands, Tyrus goes in and sweeps the dust away. He will not remove the body, mend a single stone, or move so much as a candle from where it fell.
