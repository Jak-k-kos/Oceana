Truth: Peregrine means to court the princess the way his father closes a deal. Love has nothing to do with it; a husband can always keep a mistress, and plenty of nobles do. What he hasn't bought is the one thing that matters: Cordelia is coming to the Hall for a trident, not a husband.

Gender: Male
Appearance: A human youth of about twenty. Broad-faced and sun-browned from the fields, with his father's small, clever eyes, sandy hair cropped short for the drill yard, and a single gold ring he is not allowed to wear on the practice field and wears everywhere else
Who: The eldest son and heir of [[Duke Ambrose Nettle]], the Duke of the West, in his second year at the Hall of the Unbeaten on [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Sparte Torro/index|Sparte Torro]], where even the heir to a duchy must work the fields to earn his keep. He is a middling spear and a poor shot, but he is the finest quartermaster the Hall's instructors have seen in a decade, and given a fleet and a month he could keep it fed, armed and paid farther from port than anyone alive.
Rumors:
- He has run a market among the Kingdom students since his first week, in favours, field shifts and smuggled sweets
- He keeps a file on the princess as thick as a ship's manifest
- He lost his first duel at the Hall in under ten seconds, and bought the winner dinner that same night
known relationships:
