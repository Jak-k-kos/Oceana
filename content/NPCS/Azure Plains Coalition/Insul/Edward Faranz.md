Relationships:

- [[Silk Faranz]]: adopted younger brother. Edward came home from the frontier not long before Silk arrived, and the two found each other quickly, for both had seen dark places and neither would speak of them. They have been friends ever since.

He trained at the medical college on [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/San Lazara/index|San Lazara]]. As a student he was sent to the frontier at [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Del Rosa/index|Del Rosa]], then on to the Siege of Koras about ten years ago, where he saw bloody fighting. What he saw in that slaughter sent him home to Insul the moment his studies were done, and he has not left since.
