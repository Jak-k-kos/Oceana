Gender: Male
Appearance: A broad, heavy tabaxi in his middle forties. His smoke-grey coat is barred with darker stripes, the fur of his forearms is singed short and patchy from a lifetime of sparks, and a notch is missing from his left ear. He wears a scorched leather apron over everything.
Who: The blacksmith of [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Insul/index|Insul]]. He came from [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Koras/index|Koras]] on the southern frontier with his wife [[Mara Herrera|Mara]] some ten years ago, after the siege took their old forge, and since then he has kept the island in axe heads and saw blades, oyster knives and nails, with the occasional fine sword for the merchants of [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Belna/index|Belna]]. He is a quiet giant, slow to speak and slower to anger, and he raised the dragonborn foundling [[Varek Herrera|Varek]] as his own.
