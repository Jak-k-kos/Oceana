Gender: Male
Appearance: A stout, barrel-chested dwarf of middle years, with long curly red hair tied back with a leather cord and a great bushy red beard. Wooden beads of his own carving are braided into the beard, and it is usually dusted with sawdust and wood shavings. His forearms are freckled and thick as logs. He wears a leather carpenter's apron full of tiny chisels, and a half-finished puzzle box is usually tucked in one pocket. His brother looks exactly the same, down to the last bead.
Who: One of the identical Du'Pliss twins, lumberjacks and master carpenters whose puzzle boxes are famous across Insul. No one can tell the two of them apart either.
