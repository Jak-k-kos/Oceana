Gender: Female
Appearance: A voluptuous, striking woman who could almost pass for human, if not for the soft green blush that rises on her skin in sunlight. Her long waves of rose-gold hair are always crowned with fresh blossoms, and her eyes are the bright green of new leaves. Faint vine-like patterns curl along her shoulders and wrists. Wherever she goes, she carries the scent of a spring morning, and the whole room seems to warm when she enters.
Who: The mother of Dahlia, and the wife of Orchid. She works as the book keeper for their family's lumber company.
