Gender: Female
Appearance: A lean, wiry tabaxi in her early forties, with a sand-gold coat spotted in dark rosettes and a pale scar across her ribs where the fur never grew back right. Her sleeves are always rolled, and her bare feet are tough as boot leather.
Who: [[Garrick Herrera|Garrick's]] wife and partner at the forge. She stood on the walls of [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Koras/index|Koras]] when the [[Locations/The Black Tide/index|Black Tide]] came, and she has not forgotten how to fight. The folk of Harbour Cove long ago stopped staring at her and her son, sparring barefoot on the wet cobbles at low tide while the sea tries to take their feet.
