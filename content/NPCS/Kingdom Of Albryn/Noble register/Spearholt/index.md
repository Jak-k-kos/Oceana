---
title: Nobles of Spearholt
---
The South is a duchy at war, and its nobles have long since stopped pretending otherwise. Duke Roderick Thorne is a half-orc soldier who would rather stand on a quarterdeck than sit in a hall. He spends more of each year at the front than at home, and he has spent two years trying to pry the royal flagship out of Crownsreach and into the fight. His counts are of the same hard stamp. One is a one-eyed captain called home from the line when his brother died. One is a tiefling countess who keeps the Kingdom's eastern gate and knows to the shell how much smuggling she lets through. And one is the descendant of a god, who governs the broken mountain from the temples of Glenmoor. In Spearholt a lord is measured by the names their house has added to the Endless Roll, and almost every house has added one.
