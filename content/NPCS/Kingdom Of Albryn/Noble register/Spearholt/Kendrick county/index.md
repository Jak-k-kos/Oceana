---
title: Nobles of Kendrick
---
Kendrick's lords are farmers at heart, and the hot south has made them patient. Count Raymond is a heavy, slow-spoken half-orc who walks the paddy dykes himself and sets the rice tithe that feeds the southern fleet. His countess is homesick for the waterfall of her girlhood. Baroness Pickett climbs up onto a croc-watcher's stilt beside the children every planting season, and Baron Brockhurst of Gravenwall climbs the training Wall each year on his birthday to prove he still can. At Carrickfall the proud Viscount Lisburne guards his family's falls and mill rights, while the dwarf Baron Calloway swims the icy plunge pool every morning and grumbles about everything else. And Baron Tinsley of Kittiwake wears his island's loudest calico to the duke's own table, his hands stained like any dyer's.
