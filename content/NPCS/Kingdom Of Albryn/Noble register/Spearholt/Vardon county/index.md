---
title: Nobles of Vardon
---
The lords of Vardon govern the front itself, and almost every one of them has a name carved on the Endless Roll. Count Severin was a Hawksley captain until his elder brother died at sea and the duke called him home. He lost an eye on the line and kept his captain's temper, and the fleet trusts him because he was one of them. Viscountess Hallett runs the Prize Court, where lawyers fear her and captains court her. Viscount Grimshaw is an orc of a pure-blooded house, and he reads aloud every name the Roll gains. Beneath them, Baron Ordway still test-fires the mended cannon himself and shouts at everyone because he can no longer hear. Baronetess Ruddock reads the names of the dead each dusk before the funeral boats are lit. House Hawksley and House Ashcombe keep their halls on the two great fortresses, and their banners fly beside the count's.
