---
title: Nobles of Brightwell
---
Brightwell holds two masters on one island. Duke Roderick Thorne keeps his hall-fort above the olive presses of Thornehallow, though he is far more often at the front with the fleet than at home. His duchess, Adeline, runs the island in his absence with a steady hand, and their son Brennus sails on his father's flagship as the darling of the South. Beside them, and older than any of them, stands the blood of a god. Roktra himself fathered the first of House Brightwell, and Count Theron, a hundred and twelve years old and as unhurried as the mountain, is the latest in an unbroken chain of single children. He has never faced a rival, and he has never needed to raise his voice. Below them the county's lords are split by the Morrowcliff Company's gold. Viscount Rookwood leans toward its money, while Baron Fernley trims the old thorn hedges himself and leads the farmers against it. Viscountess Ashenden taxes the company and courts it in the same breath, and Baron Redgrave of Kinross, once deep in its debt, is now kept on the count's short leash. On Glenmoor old Baronet Vowell attends every rite of every temple and quietly mourns the island his family lost to the priests and to House Stavely.
