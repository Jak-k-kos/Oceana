---
title: Nobles of Sallis
---
The lords of Sallis keep the Kingdom's eastern door, and every one of them has been offered a bribe. Countess Theodosia is an elegant tiefling who reads like a closed book. She keeps the gate law, sells what her searchers seize, and is said to know to the shell how much of the Undermarket she allows to live. Viscount Harcourt runs the convoys and the market licences, and some say he is a little too friendly with the Reach merchants. Baron Crowther keeps the wall and the dusk gates, and no one has ever managed to buy him, though his clerks are another matter. Further out are gentler houses. Baronetess Pennant can read any signal flag at a glance, and every cadet at Flagstaff adores and fears her. Baronet Tallant fines any ship that enters his still harbour faster than a walk. And on Brackenfloat a high elf dowager born a Fairleigh still tends the lagoons, two hundred years after the scandal of her marriage was forgotten.
