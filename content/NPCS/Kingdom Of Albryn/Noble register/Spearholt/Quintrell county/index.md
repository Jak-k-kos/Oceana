---
title: Nobles of Quintrell
---
Quintrell is a county of craftsmen's lords, and the carpenters' guild matters as much as any house. Count Everard is courtly and long-winded but shrewd. He spends his days fighting the Admiralty over who pays the keepers of the reserve fleet, while his glamorous countess hosts every visiting Marquess and his lively second wife sits in on the carpenters' tally carvings. His youngest son has apprenticed himself at the Carpenters' Hall, and the court thinks it a scandal. Baroness Bancroft is an old dwarf who checks every joint herself and stands between the guild and the count. Viscount Galloway keeps the powder contracts and sleeps at the far end of his raft from the mills. On Fathom, Baron Kittredge drills with his whalers and veterans, and his old father still tells the founding story every time a new deck section is lashed on.
