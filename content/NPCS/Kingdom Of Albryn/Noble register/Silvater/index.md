---
title: Nobles of Silvater
---
The West is a duchy of merchants and middlemen, of ledgers kept in three hands and smiles that never quite reach the eyes. Its duke, Ambrose Nettle, is the most charming man at any table and the last one any noble would lend a knife to; his duchess, born a Marlowe, is thought the sharper of the pair. Beneath them the counts of the West keep the coalition's border, the toll-chains and the storm-road, and every one of them has learned that in Silvater a bargain is never quite finished.
