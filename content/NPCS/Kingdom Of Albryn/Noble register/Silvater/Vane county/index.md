---
title: Nobles of Vane
---
The raft-lords of Vane live with one eye always on the storm. Their houses are old and proud and a little reckless, as befits nobles whose floors are decks and whose neighbour is the Widow's Wrath. House Rennick keeps its hall on Widow's Berth beside the duchy's fleet, cold eyes counting every hull, while the lesser houses govern brewers and raftwrights, divers and navigators, and toast the Wrath at every feast in the hope that it will keep its distance one more year.
