---
title: Nobles of Calder
---
Calder is the West's workshop, and its nobles are as varied as its chimneys. Above the smoke of Pendra the duke keeps his deer park; below it, counts and viscounts, coopers' barons and bell-founders' baronets argue over contracts and casks. House Corbin keeps its hall among them, which is perhaps why so much of Calder's business is done with a wink. The paper of Gandell, the bells of Bellmoor and the masons of Southwick all answer to these houses, and every one of them knows exactly what its neighbour is worth.
