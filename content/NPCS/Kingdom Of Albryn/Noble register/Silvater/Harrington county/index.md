---
title: Nobles of Harrington
---
The northern isles of the West are a cool, windswept company, and House Harrington keeps them with a full table and a long memory. Here the coalition's cargoes come to heel behind the toll-chain of Greyhal, convicts wait in the old hulk for the black ships, and every autumn the seabirds pour down out of the north to blacken the cliffs of Wrenhold. The lords of Harrington are a sturdy, sea-weathered lot: goliaths who hang the skulls of drowned monsters in their halls, a dwarf who walks his elms each evening, and a count whose laugh can be heard across the harbor.
