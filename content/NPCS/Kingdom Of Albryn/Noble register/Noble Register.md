title: Noble Register
---
Every noble house of Albryn bears a crest, and the crest is the house itself made visible. It is painted on the prows of the ships the house owes its lord, stitched on the coats of its servants, pressed into the wax of its letters and carved above the door of its hall. When a house is raised to a higher seat or sent to a lesser one, its crest goes with it unchanged, for a crest belongs to the blood and not to the island. Only when a house dies, or is stripped of its name, does its crest pass from the world; whoever is raised in its place must take up a new one. To know the crests is to know who is speaking before a word is said.

# The Knightly Houses
The knightly houses hold no island and govern no one; their halls stand by royal grant on islands that keep their own lords. They are the realm's officers and its blades, and each is known less for what it does than for the kind of noble it takes in.
