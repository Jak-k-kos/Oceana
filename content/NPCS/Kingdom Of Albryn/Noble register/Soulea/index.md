---
title: Nobles of Soulea
---
The Heartland is the softest and the most dangerous court in the Kingdom. Duchess Seraphine Marlowe rules it with a song and a smile, and every feast she gives is a net cast wide: her musicians, her bath attendants and her barkeeps all have ears, and a secret whispered over Soulea wine has usually reached Marlholm by morning. Her counts are a mixed and glittering company: a scholar who forgets to govern, a drow who walks his mines in the dark, a countess richer than some dukes, and an old count dying slowly while his children circle. In Soulea a noble is judged by the table they keep, and by how much they know about everyone sitting at it.
