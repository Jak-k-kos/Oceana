---
title: Nobles of Duskwell
---
The counts of Duskwell have walked the dark beneath Ravendell since the Kingdom was young. House Duskwell is the last pure drow house in the realm, its count three centuries old and unsettling to meet, as much at home in the mine tunnels as in his own hall, and his heirs wait patiently through lifetimes that would bury a human house twice over. Around them the human and half-orc lords of the county are a plainer, blunter sort: Viscount Carrow, who has never told a pleasant lie in his life, a baroness who governs the forge valleys, and the genial half-orc Fairburn of Hollstour, one of the Duchess's favourites, who keeps the best table in the county and a second wife for company.
