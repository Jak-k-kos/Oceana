---
title: Nobles of Wyndham
---
Wyndham is a county of the mind and the senses, and its nobles are split cleanly between the two. On Lyceum the scholar-count Desmond loses himself in his books while his countess quietly does the governing, and the high elves of House Ellwood keep the lamps of the Athenaeum as they have for centuries. Across the water on Revelmoor, Viscount Belcourt holds court among his four wives and his gaming tables, everyone's friend and no one's confidant, and passes every whisper he hears up the river to Marlholm. Between them sit potters, ink-makers and dyers' lords, and the old hold of House Lackland on Brindle, where the Kingdom's fallen nobles come to start again.
