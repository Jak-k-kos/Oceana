---
title: Nobles of Ainsworth
---
Ainsworth lives in the Duchess's shadow and grows sleek in it. Marlholm is her own seat, and Count Hadrian has made himself one of the finest threads in her web: indulgent in manner, sharp in fact, and always watching how the county's ties run out into the realm. Viscountess Montrose licenses the pleasure houses and keeps the watch, and everyone knows whose ear she whispers into. Further out the houses grow sterner: a grim half-elf who guards the crown's forest as if every tree were his own child, a dwarven baroness of the black iron beaches, a goliath who commissions a new statue every year, and the sour, rich baronet of Kingsgate, who governs the realm's busiest gate in name while a Brassmoor marquess searches every hull that passes through it.
