---
title: Nobles of Whitmore
---
The countess of Whitmore is richer than some dukes, and she has never once let anyone forget it. Aurelia keeps two husbands at her table, a charming Valance lord for the dinners and a sharp-tongued younger man for her pleasure, and the pair are so perfectly civil to each other that the whole court finds it unnerving. Beneath her the orc Viscount Treadwell runs the muster with a voice like a breaking wave, the wood elves of House Penhaligon tend their lemon groves as they have for centuries, and the barons range from the best-dressed lord in the duchy to a half-orc baroness who eats firecap peppers raw at feasts to watch her guests' faces.
