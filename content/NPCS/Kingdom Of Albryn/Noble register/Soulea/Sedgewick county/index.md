---
title: Nobles of Sedgewick
---
The lords of Sedgewick are waiting for a death. Count Benedict lies coughing in his tower above Highmoor, and the whole county has quietly taken sides: some behind Lady Helena, who has run its courts for years, and some behind her charming, idle brother Julian, whose debts were paid the day he married a banker's daughter and whose name the fleet suddenly speaks with fondness. Viscount Aldane keeps the county running in all but name and says nothing, and the rest of the raft-lords, from the half-orc who flenses his own whales to the dwarf who tastes every vat of cider, watch the tower windows and count their friends.
