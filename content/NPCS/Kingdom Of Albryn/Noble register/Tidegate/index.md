---
title: Nobles of Tidegate
---
The North is ruled by the oldest and most patient duke in the realm. Duchess Aveline Caldwell is three hundred years old, a high elf who has buried four husbands, none of them elves, and borne children who are all, somehow, full elves. No one has ever dared ask her how. She writes the boy king's lessons with her own hand, and everyone knows that if it ever came to it, the navy would follow her before any other duke. Now she has taken a fifth betrothed, a restless lord of twenty-two, and the whole north is placing bets. Her counts are cut from the same cold cloth: a border lord who has given a son to the sea folk, a dwarven count who guards his granaries as a sacred trust, and her own daughter, an elf countess who reads the storms better than anyone alive.
