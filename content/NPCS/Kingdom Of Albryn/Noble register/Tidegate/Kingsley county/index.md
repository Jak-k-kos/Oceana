---
title: Nobles of Kingsley
---
Kingsley is the steadiest county in the realm, and it is proud of being dull. Duchess Aveline holds her court above the granaries of Everhold, and Count Anselm, a solemn dwarf of a hundred and sixty, guards those granaries as a sacred trust. His daughter Thyra leads House Stanway, whose hall is set into the old walls beside them. Around them the houses are as varied as the north allows. Viscount Pennington is proud and rich on Sterling silver, and Baron Hartwell of Rooksdale is a snob about who may buy his horses. On Queensholm the discreet Baronet Ainsley keeps the queen's tea garden and plays host to the dragonborn of House Brassmoor, whose councils meet in the old queen's tower, and his son's dragonborn bride is the pride of the island. Baron Tanner wears his island's boots to court, and Baronetess Breck, raised from the shipbreakers' yards, strips hulls beside her own crews.
