---
title: Nobles of Hallam
---
The lords of Hallam keep the Kingdom's northern door, and they have paid for it in blood. Count Gavin is a stern man who has given one son to a sea-folk raid and a younger one, of all things, to Duchess Aveline as her fifth husband. Every house in the county seems to have lost someone to the ice or the Deep. Old Baron Ribble, a dwarf-blooded whaler, refuses to step down. The goliath Gorricks of Breakholm keep a watch over the Deep that is older than the Kingdom, and Viscount Winterbourne, a Greaves captain called home to lead a house that had no one better, plays host to the scholars who come to puzzle over the frozen mere. They are a grave, close company, and at the Blaze each year they all cheer the baron of Hailsby, who races the knitters around the bonfire and has never once won.
