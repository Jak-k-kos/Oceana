---
title: Nobles of Rainsford
---
Rainsford is the youngest county in the north, and the most patient. Seventy years ago a hurricane took the last of the old counts, and the king raised Saelin, Duchess Aveline's elf daughter, to found a new house at Stormhaven. She is still there, and she reads the sky better than anyone alive. Her half-elf children grow up knowing they will almost certainly die before she does, and they serve the house rather than wait on it. Around her the lords of the county are storm-folk to a man. Baroness Blackmore stands on the seawall in every gale. Baronetess Mowbray has kept the dawn watch over Sealholm's seals for forty years, and the navy trusts any gull that carries her seal. Viscount Ferrars has married off all three of his sisters and is the north's finest matchmaker. On Whinmoor Baronet Hearne cuts gorse beside his cutters and keeps two wives, the second a goliath he married for love, and the island adores them both.
