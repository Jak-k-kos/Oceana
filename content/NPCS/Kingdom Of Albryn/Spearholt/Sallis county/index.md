---
title: Sallis County
---
The folk of Sallis live between the law and the fog. On Gellswall they search the Reach ships at the gate, sell their cleared goods in the market, and very quietly trade the rest in back rooms after dark. When the hulk gates bang shut at dusk, everyone outside waits for morning. Flagstaff's cadets race to read the practice signals before the bell, and its townsfolk are said to gossip in flags. Keelhull's crews drop and drag every anchor before it is sold, Brackenfloat's rakers wade their sweet-smelling false lagoons, and Breakwater's builders tow out booms to shelter rafts across the south. Foamhold's printers guard the copper chart plates with their lives, Seawall's fish farmers drill as a militia between harvests, and in Hollowbay's still ring the instrument makers grind lenses and tune chronometers in a calm found nowhere else in the realm.
