---
title: Duchy of Spearholt
---
The people of the South are hot-blooded, hard-working and used to living close to danger. They cast shot and mend cannon, sew up the wounded and bake biscuit for the fleet. They farm rice in paddies where crocodiles slide between the stalks, and they dive for conch in water too warm to cool them. Most families have someone on the front, and many have someone on the Endless Roll. They grieve loudly and laugh louder, and when the funeral boats go out burning at dusk, the whole raft falls silent to watch them pass.
