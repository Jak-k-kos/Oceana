---
title: Vardon County
---
The folk of Vardon are sailors and gunners, chandlers and surgeons, and every one of them lives within sight of the war. On Fort Resolve they fit out ships along Chandlers' Row and drink away prize money in the captains' taverns. Grimkeel's divers raise sunken guns from the wrecks and build the funeral boats for the fallen. Pitchhollow's fire-makers, half of them without eyebrows, keep every lamp behind glass, and Gunwall booms from dawn to dusk as every mended cannon is test-fired over the empty sea. Port Sentinel's nurses and surgeons take in the wounded beneath the great lamp tower. On Fort Endless the quartermasters stack biscuit and powder for the whole front. Grapnel's smiths forge cutlasses and boarding pikes, Slagraf casts shot over its black reef of slag, and Cinderhook's shark-fishers work decks still black from the raid that burned them. They are fierce, close and loyal, and there is not a family on the front that has not lit a funeral boat.
