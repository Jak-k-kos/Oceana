---
title: Kendrick County
---
The folk of Kendrick grow, grind and weave for the south. Southwold's rice farmers work terraces where crocodiles slide between the stalks, and their children clatter wooden clappers from the croc-watchers' stilts at planting. Dunlin's salt-rakers walk pans turned pink with brine shrimp beneath wheeling flocks of shorebirds. Gravenwall's newly drawn levies climb the Wall by day and in the dark, and the whole raft turns out to cheer them on their last night before the Reach patrol. Wavecrest's bakers taste every batch of biscuit for the tang of orange peel, and they panic in a dead calm. Carrickfall's mill hands spin cotton at the foot of the roaring falls. Kittiwake's dyers are stained to the elbows in blue, red and gold, and on Ebbsfell the husk-beaters keep a rhythm the whole island walks to.
