---
title: Brightwell County
---
The folk of Brightwell live on the bones of a broken mountain, and they never quite forget it. On Thornehallow the olive-pressers and pease farmers walk the green lanes between hedges taller than a man, and they glare across them at the company's miners, who leave their first ore for Roktra while the farmers pray to Apiot. Glenmoor teems with pilgrims, priests and the Seekers in their coloured cords, and its trench-diggers clear the magma's paths as a holy duty. Asterby's vintners and silk-reelers tend their vines in woven nests against the gales, and Farholm's kiln-keepers guard a fire that has never once gone out. Morrowcliff's sulfur-carriers climb the steaming vents with wet cloths across their faces, and Torholm's quarrymen cut the holystones that every sailor in the navy scrubs with on his knees. Before dawn Wescote's boatmen race for the Kingsgate quay. Weirheart's eel-keepers live on stilts above their weirs, and on Kinross the miners press an ear to the rock before every shift, listening for the fire beneath.
