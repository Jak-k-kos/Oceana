---
title: Quintrell County
---
The folk of Quintrell build and mend the ships everyone else sails. Fleetmoor's carpenters and apprentices ship out in teams, carve their tallies on the Hall's wall, and ring every bell in the reserve fleet at dawn. Fathom's veterans hunt sperm whales under guard and argue over old regiments in the taverns. Oarlock's block mills clack all day, and its children can whittle a pulley before they can read. In Sternhold the powder-mill hands go soft-footed in wooden shoes, and Sparwell's crews heel ships onto their sides and scrape their hulls clean. Shoalwell's divers bring up conch from the pale bank, and on Lowhelm, where the decks are awash at every high tide, folk go barefoot or in tall boots and dry sea cucumbers for the coalition's markets.
