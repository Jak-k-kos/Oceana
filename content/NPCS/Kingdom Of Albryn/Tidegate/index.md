---
title: Duchy of Tidegate
---
The folk of the North are made for the cold. They shear the greatest flocks in the Kingdom and knit their wool into jumpers so thick a wave runs off them. They cut ice, hunt seals and haul cod from black water, and when the fog rolls in they simply carry on by feel. They are a quiet, steady people, slow to trust a southerner and slower still to forget a kindness. Every hearth in Tidegate keeps a pot on the fire and a spare pair of mittens by the door, because in the north you never know who the sea will bring you.
