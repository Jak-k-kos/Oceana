---
title: Rainsford County
---
The folk of Rainsford live by the weather, and they can smell a storm a day before it comes. Stormhaven's sail-makers cut the navy's storm canvas in long lofts behind the seawall, and when a hurricane is coming the whole town turns out to make room for the ships running in. On Whinmoor the cutters bundle gorse that blooms nearly all year, and they swear that courting is only out of season when it isn't flowering. Kalbrid's gunsmiths turn out muskets without pause while the walruses bellow on the north strand. Sealholm's folk dry their cod on vast racks and never fish the selkies' corner of the sound. Greymantle's miners dig copper and tin beneath the fog, and Sorrel's children come home red-handed every autumn from the berry moors. Chalkwell's people carve new pictures into their quarry face each spring, and Sheerwell's cliffmen go over the edge on ropes for eggs and feathers, but never on a day the sheer well is silent.
