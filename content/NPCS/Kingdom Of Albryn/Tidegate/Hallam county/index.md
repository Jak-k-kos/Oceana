---
title: Hallam County
---
The folk of Hallam are shepherds and sealers, ice-cutters and knitters, and the cold is in their bones. Northumbra's flocks give the wool for every navy coat, and in Hailsby every kitchen is a knitting circle, its fastest hands racing around the bonfire on the night of the Blaze. On Frostholm the sealers open each hunt with a rite to Ruthala, and on Skelton the whalers carve bone into combs and buttons beneath gates made of whale ribs. Halyard ships its oysters alive in barrels of seawater, Frostmere's cutters work the rim of the mere while the ice heals itself behind them, Breakholm smokes its halibut beneath the watchtower, and on Gelby they boil fish skins into glue and swear they can't smell it. The sea folk are never far away, and every family keeps a sharp knife by the door.
