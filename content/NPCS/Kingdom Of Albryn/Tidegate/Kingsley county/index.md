---
title: Kingsley County
---
The folk of Kingsley feed the north, put boots on its sailors' feet, breed its horses and build its boats. Everhold's farmers bring in oats, barley and rye to granaries that have never once been empty, and Hensea's smokehouses hang a golden haze of kippers over the water. On Towbridge the breakers take old ships apart plank by plank, and Ferring's cobblers leave boots on every doorstep for the fleet to collect. Queensholm's pickers tend the prized Queen's tea, Sterling's silversmiths are the richest guild in the north, and Gillam's folk gather laver from the rocks at low tide. Rooksdale breeds the only horses in the Kingdom beneath its deafening rookeries, and Barrcove's shipwrights build clinker boats behind the sandbar and comb the beach for amber after every winter storm. They are patient, careful people, and they take a quiet pride in knowing that whatever happens, Kingsley will not go hungry.
