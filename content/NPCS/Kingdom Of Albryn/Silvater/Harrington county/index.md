---
title: Harrington County
---
The folk of Harrington are a hardy, unhurried people, salted by the north wind and sweetened by gorse honey. Customs clerks and chain-keepers, beekeepers and quarrymen, egg-gatherers dangling from the Wrenhold cliffs and fishwives with tongues sharp enough to gut a cod: they keep the county running while their lords keep its accounts, and every one of them has a tale about the night the black ship came early.
