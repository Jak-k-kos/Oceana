---
title: Calder County
---
The folk of Calder smell of smoke and sawdust, sugar and ink. They are coopers who can raise a barrel blindfolded, bell-founders half-deaf from their own art, paper-makers with blue-stained fingers and vintners who swear their wine is better than anything the coalition ever bottled. They work hard, drink well, and know to the shell what their labour is worth.
