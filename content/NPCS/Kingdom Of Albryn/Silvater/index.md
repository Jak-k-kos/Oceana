---
title: Duchy of Silvater
---
The West is a land of merchants, and its people grow up in their shadow. Every child learns early how to read a ledger, weigh a catch honestly and walk away from a bad bargain, for in Silvater the market is never far from the door. Yet for all their sharp eyes they are a warm people, quick to laugh and generous with a stranger's supper, and the same dockhand who haggled you down at noon will stand you a drink at dusk.
