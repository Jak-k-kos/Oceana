---
title: Vane County
---
The folk of Vane never quite trust solid ground. They are brewers and divers, raftwrights and navigators, net-menders and insurers who can price a sailor's life to the last shell, and they live with their eyes forever turned toward the Widow's Wrath. Every child on the rafts can swim before they can walk, and every tavern keeps one empty chair for those the storm has taken.
