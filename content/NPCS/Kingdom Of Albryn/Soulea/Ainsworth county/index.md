---
title: Ainsworth County
---
The folk of Ainsworth feed the Duchess's city, and a good many of them make their living from its pleasures. Marlholm's cane-cutters and mill-hands work the endless green fields beyond the walls while its players, cooks and courtesans keep the streets alight after dark. Culverton's farmers fill the city's larder and climb their dovecotes at dawn; Polwick's crab-keepers tend their clicking tanks; Pellow malts barley and gathers eiderdown. On Lyndhurst the foresters plant two trees for every one they fell, Ironhyth's smelters work the black sands, Merriby burns a cane field every night before harvest, Penhallow cuts white marble, and Mossby's bark-strippers move barefoot and silent through the cork oaks. Down on Kingsgate the taverns never empty and the boat-sellers row the long line of waiting ships, selling bread, beer and trinkets to bored crews.
