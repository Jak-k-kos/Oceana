---
title: Duskwell County
---
The folk of Duskwell are a hard-handed, plain-spoken people, and nearly every one of them works with something that bites back. On Ravendell they mine and smelt and forge; on Ottrey they hunt whales through the cold northern swells; on Greylag they split slate, and on Whitsan they knap flint until their hands are white to the elbow. Hollstour's farmers cure the navy's pork in smokehouses that scent half the county, Haddon's ropemakers walk their long terraces backwards from dawn to dusk, and on little Rushcolmbe the rush-cutters plait the matting and lights for every cottage in the duchy. They are blunt, loyal and quick to stand a round, and they look after their own, for in Duskwell everyone knows someone the work has taken.
