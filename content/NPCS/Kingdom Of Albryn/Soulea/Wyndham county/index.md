---
title: Wyndham County
---
The folk of Wyndham are teachers and tavern-keepers, scribes and stagehands. On Lyceum they cook for the students, mend the books and gossip about which young lord failed his examinations again; on Revelmoor they deal the cards, pour the wine and patch up sailors on shore leave, and they have seen every kind of fool the navy can produce. Both islands share a fondness for a good story and a long memory for a bad tip, and both agree, without ever saying so, that the other is a terrible influence on the young.
