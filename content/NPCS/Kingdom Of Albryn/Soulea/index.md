---
title: Duchy of Soulea
---
The people of the Heartland are the Kingdom's hosts. They grow its sugar and its apples, brew its cider, teach in its schools and pour the wine at its pleasure rafts, and they take a quiet pride in a guest well fed and a song well sung. Miners and scholars, cane-cutters and courtesans live side by side here, and most of them learned long ago that the Duchess hears everything, so they speak kindly, laugh often and keep their real troubles for the kitchen.
