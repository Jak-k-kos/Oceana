---
title: Whitmore County
---
The folk of Whitmore are salt-rakers and seamstresses, chandlers and pepper-growers, and nearly all of them smell of something. On Highwake they rake the great pans in the shelter of the standing wave and pray at Aquilla's temple where the water breaks. On Silkdeck the needles never stop, stitching every coat and hammock in the navy, and downwind on Tallowholm the candle-makers render fat and whale oil into light. Dimmock's pepper-pickers work with streaming eyes, Withy's weavers bend willow into traps and fenders, Tidwel's orchard hands come home sticky with marmalade, and the divers of Whitterby bring up the sponges that scrub every deck in the realm. They are cheerful, practical people, and they are fiercely proud of their countess, at least when anyone from another county is listening.
