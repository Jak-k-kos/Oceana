---
title: Sedgewick County
---
The folk of Sedgewick live on the open water and would not trade it for any shore. They are deep-water fishermen who come home salt-stiff with tuna and marlin, net-weavers working their great harps, beekeepers tending flowers on soil their great-grandparents shipped in by the barrel, and soap-makers who smell of roses from dawn to dusk. They know the count is dying, and they know whose ships are crowding the harbour; but the catch still has to come in, and the honey still has to be sold.
