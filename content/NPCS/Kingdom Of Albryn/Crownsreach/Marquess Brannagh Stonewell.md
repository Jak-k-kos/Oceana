Gender: Female
Appearance: A goliath of about thirty-five. Towering and grey-skinned, with dark lithoderm markings, close-cropped black hair, and a halberd that never leaves her reach
Who: The Marquess of the Palace Guard, and one of the very few commoners ever to reach that rank. A sergeant of the Crown Marquess's marines, she led the first squad through the palace gates on the night of the murders, after the palace knights had failed. When the old captain of the guard was dismissed in disgrace, the Crown Marquess pushed her through to command it in his place, over the grumbling of half the court.
