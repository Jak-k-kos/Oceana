DM Notes:
------------------------------------
species: Dragonborn (bronze)
Unique Trait: Never raises his voice and never repeats an order. He has never needed to do either
Motivation: A navy that answers to the crown and to no one else, and a Kingdom that never again needs his marines in its streets
Occupation: Crown Marquess of Albryn: commander of the entire royal navy and of the four High Marquesses who serve the dukes, keeper of the waters of Crownsreach, and a member of the inner council
Belief: Mercy is a luxury for those whose ships are not at sea
Accent: Deep and level, every word measured, never one wasted
Current Location: Aboard the royal flagship in the harbor of [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/Crownsreach/index|Crownsreach]], and at the inner council whenever it sits
Relationships: 
- The late King Alaric Albryn IV: raised him to the post for his skill, over the objections of every duke
- [[King Edwin Albryn]]: the boy king he serves
- [[Captain Sir Varek Brassmoor]]: his son and heir, a frigate captain at the front
- [[Duke Roderick Thorne]]: the one duke who truly respects him, and who keeps trying to get the flagship sent to the front
- [[Duke Ambrose Nettle]], [[Duchess Seraphine Marlowe]] and [[Duchess Aveline Caldwell]]: wary of him on the best of days
- [[Marquess Brannagh Stonewell]]: the marine sergeant he pushed through to Marquess of the Palace Guard

Truth: The night of the murders, the palace knights failed. Within the hour Tavrek had every ship connected to the killers sunk in the harbor, and his marines poured into Crownsreach in their hundreds to run the assassins down. The dukes have never forgotten how quickly he moved, or that he asked none of them first. Not one warship, cannon or pistol of the royal navy moves within the Kingdom without his leave, and not even the dukes can push past him when he refuses. Each of the three who are wary of him would like their own man in his post, but none will move against him alone, and they trust one another even less than they trust him.

Gender: Male
Appearance: A bronze dragonborn of about sixty. Tall and lean, his snout greyed with age and his eyes pale and unblinking, in an immaculate dark coat bearing the gold anchor and crown of the Crown Marquess
Who: The Crown Marquess of the [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/index|Kingdom]], a cold and ruthlessly efficient man, and the most capable commander the navy has had in a century. The late king pushed him into the post over the objections of every duke, for his skill alone, and he has never given any of them a reason to remove him. He keeps nearly every ship of the Home Fleet in constant rotation through the front lines against the [[Sea Folk Waters|sea folk]] and the [[Locations/The Black Tide/index|Black Tide]]; only the royal flagship, a few customs cutters and a handful of galleons stay behind at [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/Crownsreach/index|Crownsreach]], and even those crews are rotated out so they stay sharp. He is the head of House Brassmoor, the only dragonborn house among the nobility of Albryn, born of Queen Maelis's ill-fated dragon marriage centuries ago.
Rumors:
- Within the hour of the murders, every ship tied to the killers was sunk in the harbor, and a few say not all of them were guilty
- The disgraced captain of the palace guard and every one of his officers were put aboard a black ship in secret, bound for [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Isla Embra/index|Isla Embra]]
- The Duke of the South has been trying for two years to pry the flagship out of Crownsreach and into the fray
- He has been offered a county more than once, and has refused every time
- He has never once lost his temper, and the sailors find that more frightening than if he did
known relationships:
