species: Yuan-ti (pureblood)
Unique Trait: Wears a dozen pairs of spectacles on a brass chain and swaps them mid-sentence
Motivation: Make the royal dockyards the envy of the world, and leave [[Ostin]] a masterwork no champion before her could equal
Occupation: Chief Artificer of the Crown, master of the royal workshops, and champion of [[Ostin]]; a knighted commoner
Belief: Magic left a hole in the world, and brass and patience can fill it
Accent: Soft and precise, growing quicker and quicker the more excited she gets
Current Location: The royal workshops beside the dockyards of [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/Crownsreach/index|Crownsreach]]
Relationships: 
- [[King Edwin Albryn]]: she makes him clockwork toys, and is the one adult who talks to him about how things work rather than what to sign
- [[Duke Roderick Thorne]]: wants everything she builds sent south the moment it leaves her bench
- [[Hamish Kettleby]]: the master shipwright, who argues timber against steam with her daily

Truth: The coalition's offer is real, and she has not said no. A quiet agent of [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Ostrakia/index|Ostrakia]] has been courting her for a year with promises of a workshop of her own and no dukes squabbling over every design, and she is more tempted than she lets anyone see. The only thing keeping her in Crownsreach is the boy king.

Gender: Female
Appearance: A yuan-ti pureblood who looks about fifty. Tall and graceful, with faint bronze scales along her temples and wrists, slit-pupiled amber eyes and dark hair wound up with brass pins. She is nearly always in roughspun work clothes and a scorched leather apron, and only for official meetings as Chief Artificer does she put on silk, looking quietly furious about it
Who: The Chief Artificer of the Crown and the champion of [[Ostin]], a commoner knighted by the crown for her genius, whose workshops beside the royal dockyards are said to be the finest in the world. She built the engines and boilers of the [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/index|Kingdom]]'s steam warships, and the lamps of her workshops never go dark.
Rumors:
- One of her engines took the roof off a workshop, and she was back at the bench the same afternoon
- The coalition has offered her a fortune to cross the border
- She once told the Duke of the South to his face that Ostin outranks him
- She has built the boy king clockwork soldiers that march on their own
known relationships:
- [[King Edwin Albryn]]: her king, for whom she builds clockwork toys
- [[Ostin]]: her god, whose champion she is
