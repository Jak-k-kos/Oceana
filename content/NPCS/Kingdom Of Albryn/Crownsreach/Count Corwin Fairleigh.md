species: High Elf
Unique Trait: Keeps an old brass hourglass on his desk and turns it at every change of the tide, and has not missed a turning in three hundred years
Motivation: Keep the city running and the market rich, whichever duke is steering the king this week, and decide what becomes of his house before his time runs out
Occupation: Count of the City of Crownsreach, the only titled lord who serves on the island. He holds none of its land, but governs the city and the [[Grand Market]] in the crown's name
Belief: Kings come and go, but the tide comes in twice a day, and the harbor must be ready for it
Accent: Cool, precise and a little old-fashioned, the court speech of centuries past
Current Location: The Counting House above the [[Grand Market]] of [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/Crownsreach/index|Crownsreach]]
Relationships: 
- [[King Edwin Albryn]]: the seventh king he has served
- [[Ledger]]: his Keeper of the Quays
- [[Lucius Vantreese]]: whose bank he watches very closely

Truth: Corwin is three hundred and seventy, old even for an elf, and he has no heir. When he dies the countship of the city reverts to the crown, and whichever house he has married into would have the best claim to be raised in his place. Half the noble houses of the Kingdom have offered him a daughter, and the dukes press their own candidates harder every year. He has refused them all so far, but he knows he cannot refuse forever. As for the tunnels, no one has ever confirmed they exist.

Gender: Male
Appearance: A high elf who looks old even for his kind. Tall and thin but still straight-backed, his long hair gone pure white and his grey eyes sharp in a finely lined face, in a plain dark green coat beneath the gold chain of the city, leaning on a cane of black coral
Who: The Count of the City of [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/Crownsreach/index|Crownsreach]], and the only count in the [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/index|Kingdom]] without a county. He holds no land, for every acre of the island belongs to the king, but its streets, its harbor and the [[Grand Market]] answer to him, and he has governed them for longer than anyone at court has been alive. He is growing old now, and he has no heir.
Rumors:
- He has not left the island in over a hundred years
- Half the noble houses of the Kingdom have offered him a daughter, and he has turned every one away
- There are old imperial tunnels beneath the city, and he alone knows where they run, by heart
known relationships:
- [[King Edwin Albryn]]: his king
