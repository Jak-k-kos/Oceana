species: Yuan-ti (pureblood)
Unique Trait: Wears a dozen pairs of spectacles on a brass chain and swaps them mid-sentence
Motivation: Build a steam engine small and safe enough to drive an ordinary frigate, and outdo every champion of [[Ostin]] who came before her
Occupation: Chief Artificer of the Crown, master of the royal workshops, and champion of [[Ostin]]
Belief: Magic left a hole in the world, and brass and patience can fill it
Accent: Soft and precise, growing quicker and quicker the more excited she gets
Current Location: The royal workshops beside the dockyards of [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/Crownsreach/index|Crownsreach]]
Relationships: 
- [[King Edwin Albryn]]: she makes him clockwork toys, and is the one adult who talks to him about how things work rather than what to sign
- [[Duke Roderick Thorne]]: wants her engines at the front before they are ready
- [[Hamish Kettleby]]: the master shipwright, who argues timber against steam with her daily

Truth: Her frigate engine is nearly ready, and it is not safe. Two apprentices died in the last trial, and the dukes hushed it up, for every one of them wants the engine first.

Gender: Female
Appearance: A yuan-ti who looks about fifty. Tall and graceful, with faint bronze scales along her temples and wrists, slit-pupiled amber eyes, dark hair wound up with brass pins, and a scorched leather apron over a gown of fine silk
Who: The Chief Artificer of the Crown and the champion of [[Ostin]], whose workshops beside the royal dockyards are said to be the finest in the world. She built the engines and boilers of the [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/index|Kingdom's]] rare steam warships, and the lamps of her workshops never go dark.
Rumors:
- One of her engines took the roof off a workshop, and she was back at the bench the same afternoon
- The coalition has offered her a fortune to cross the border
- Ostin speaks to her in the hammer-fall of her forges
- She has built the boy king clockwork soldiers that march on their own
known relationships:
- [[King Edwin Albryn]]: her king, for whom she builds clockwork toys
- [[Ostin]]: her god, whose champion she is
