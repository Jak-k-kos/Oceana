Gender: Male
Appearance: Thirteen years old. Golden curls, a sharp little chin, sugar on his fingers, and a coat of royal blue cut down from a man's size that still swallows his hands
Who: The boy king of Albryn, crowned at eleven after his parents were murdered in the palace by assassins out of [[Locations/Smuggler's Reach/index|Smuggler's Reach]]. The killers died in the chase that followed, some say by their own hands rather than be taken, and no one has ever learned who paid them. The crown should by right have passed to his elder sister, [[Princess Cordelia Albryn]], but three of the four great dukes stood behind the boy, the Duchess of the North alone abstaining, and against the other three no one in the Kingdom was strong enough to object. Albryn's oldest law forbids a regent and holds that a king may bow to no master, not even a schoolmaster, so Edwin rules the whole realm in name yet never received the schooling every noble child begins at eleven or twelve. In truth, the dukes take turns steering him.
