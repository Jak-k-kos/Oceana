

God of fire. Patron of the hospitable, of tavern and inn keepers, and of all who keep a light burning for others.

## Titles

- The Hearthfather
- Keeper of the Flame
- The Open Door
- The Warm Welcome
- Lord of the Long Table

## The Gift of Fire  
Flaris shaped the fire of the world, and in doing so took on its aspect. Yet of all the faces fire can wear, it is the hearth that he loves best: the crackling fireplace, the lantern in the window, the galley stove and the warm bowl of stew. He is no god of the inferno, but of the merry hearth.

The sun is his symbol, the great hearth of the heavens, hung high to warm the whole world and dry the sailor's clothes. Beneath it, every fire lit in a home is a small piece of the same gift.

To Flaris, fire was always meant as a gift, and every gift carries a responsibility. When fire is turned to cruelty, when cities burn and ships are set ablaze, it grieves him deeply, but the blame lies with the hand that held the torch, not the god who gave the flame. Even then, fire has another gift to give. Ash makes rich soil, and from what burns, something new can grow.

## The Power of Restraint  
Flaris is gentle, merry and warm, but no one should mistake that warmth for weakness. Within him sleeps all the rage that fire can hold, and if it were ever loosed, little in the world could stand against it.

It never is. His true power is not in his fury but in his control. Any fool can start a wildfire. Only a master can keep a flame burning steady and bright through the longest night, and Flaris is master of his own fire above all else.

## Guest-Right  
Hospitality is Flaris's sacred law. Anyone who has shared your fire and your bread is your guest, and a guest is under Flaris's protection. To harm a guest who has eaten at your table, or a host who has opened their door to you, is among the gravest sins in the world. Even pirates and sworn enemies have been known to honor it, for no one wishes to learn what follows when the Hearthfather's patience runs out.

Guest-right binds even the gods. At Flaris's table, feuding gods may sit side by side, and his hall is the one place in the heavens where every god is welcome.

## The Open Doors of the Flood  
Through all the long centuries of Aquilla's Wrath, as lands drowned and peoples fled before the rising tides, it was Flaris's faithful who opened their doors. Every refugee found a fire to sit by and a bowl to eat from. It is why his worship burns so strongly on the crowded islands today, and why a closed door on a stormy night is thought to be a shameful thing.

## Worship  
Flaris is worshipped not in temples but at tables. Every shared meal is a prayer to him, and every open door an act of devotion.

- **Carrying the ember.** When a family moves to a new home, they carry an ember from the old hearth to light the new one, so the fire is never truly put out.
- **The first pour** of every drink is raised to Flaris.
- **The empty seat.** Many families keep an empty seat at their table, for Flaris or for any stranger who might need it.
- **Flaris's lantern.** Every ship carries one lantern for Flaris, lit at dusk and tended through the night, so that his light is never absent from the sea.
- **Lighthouses** are his holiest places, hearths for the ships at sea and fires that call the lost home.

### Champion  
If Flaris has a champion, they are hidden, perhaps the keeper of some great tavern, warm and bustling and open to all. But Flaris himself has never made much of the distinction. To him, every tavern owner and lighthouse keeper is a champion of his, down to the humble family who lets a stranger in from the storm for the night.

## Relationships

- **[[Aquilla]]**: Fire and water. Flaris holds the same warmth for [[Aquilla]] that he holds for all, but [[Aquilla]] has never cared much for him.
- **[[Fennara]]**: The hearth and the dice, two halves of every good tavern. They are fast friends, and the liveliest evenings in the heavens are spent at Flaris's table with [[Fennara]] dealing the cards.
- [[Lyandris]] The third seat at the tavern table. Where Flaris keeps the fire and [[Fennara]] deals the cards, [[Lyandris]] brings the song, and no evening at Flaris's table is complete without all three.
- **[[Koruk]]**: The dragons breathe Flaris's fire. Flaris respects their flame, though he cared far less for [[Koruk]] in the days when the dragon god was cruel.
- **[[Welestia]]**: When her storms drive sailors from the sea, it is Flaris's fires that take them in. He bears her no ill will. Someone has to keep the lights on.
- **[[Apiot]]**: The harvest and the hearth. Every feast at Apiot's Boon is cooked over his fire, and the two have always been easy friends.
