God of fortune and luck. Patron of merchants, adventurers and pirates.

## Titles

- Lord Fortune
- The Laughing Coin
- The Gambler
- The Two-Faced
- The Turning Coin

## The Turning Coin  
Fennara shaped the chance of the world: the fall of the dice, the turn of the card, the gust of wind that comes just in time, and the one that comes just too late. In doing so he took on its aspect, and he has been as fickle as fortune ever since.

He wears two faces, so the sailors say. Fair Fortune smiles upon the lucky, and Foul Fortune laughs at everyone else, and no one can say which face he will show until the coin has landed. Good luck and bad are both his gifts, and he hands them out as he pleases. He owes no one anything, and everyone knows it.

## The Charming Trickster  
Fennara is a playful trickster and a shameless flirt, all easy smiles, quick wit and twinkling mischief. He is charming to gods and mortals alike, and he knows it. Few can stay angry with him for long, though a great many have tried.

He may well be the most prayed-to god in the world. Sailors, merchants, gamblers, pirates and adventurers whisper his name a dozen times a day. Yet luck is no reward for faithful service, and so he is adored and resented in equal measure, which suits him perfectly.

## A Seat Among the Lesser  
Luck touches every roll of the dice, every voyage and every bargain struck, and Fennara could likely have claimed a seat among the upper gods had he wanted one. The upper gods are glad that he never has, for they would rather keep his chaos as far from their councils as they can. Fennara could not be happier with the arrangement.

He wants no part in the politics of the heavens. He wants only a good seat for the show. He watches the quarrels of the gods the way a gambler watches a game of cards, and it is said that he keeps wagers going on all of them: on [[Urset]]'s schemes, on [[Ellehwin]]'s search for her father's killer, and on how long [[Caruilla]] will keep asking for a seat she will never be given. 
## Worship  
Fennara's worship is everywhere and nowhere, woven into the small habits of daily life rather than kept in temples.

- **A coin in the street.** Tossing a coin into the street is an offering for luck, and the larger the coin, the greater the luck it is said to buy. Whoever finds one has found his favor, and passing good fortune along to another, whether a found coin, a kind turn or a lucky charm, is said to bring good fortune back in turn.
- **A coin flip** is a prayer. Two who settle a dispute with a flip are asking Fennara to decide, and refusing his answer is the worst of luck.
- **Whistling for wind** when the sails go slack is a call to him, though he does not always answer the way sailors hope.
- **Never say it can't get worse.** Fennara always takes it as a challenge.

His priests are gamblers, his shrines are dice tables in taverns and gambling dens, and his sermons are wagers.

### Champion  
Fennara's champion is hidden, and likely always will be. Whoever they are, they are said to roam the world fleecing the rich and emptying the gambling dens, seeking their fortune in other people's pockets. The greatest thieves of the age are often whispered to be his champion, and whenever a truly great heist is pulled off, someone always wonders whether Fennara's chosen had a hand in it.

## Relationships

- **[[Morvaine]]**: Fate and chance, the written and the rolled. Morvaine can hardly stand him, and Fennara adores her for it. Of all the gods, she is the one he finds most interesting, and he has never stopped trying to make her laugh.
- **[[Aquilla]]**: Sailors pray to them both, and [[Aquilla]] finds his worship beneath her. Fennara finds her dreadfully dull, and tells her so.
- **[[Ellehwin]]**: Law and chance make poor bedfellows, and [[Ellehwin]] has no love for a god whose champion robs the rich. Fennara thinks she could use a good game of cards.
- **[[Flaris]]**: The dice and the hearth, two halves of every good tavern. [[Flaris]] is one of the only people he would truly call a dear friend, and Fennara's favorite seat in all the heavens is at Flaris's table, cards in hand and cup never empty.
- [[Lyandris]]: Fennara is friendly with nearly everyone and close to almost no one, but [[Lyandris]] is the exception. He is the one god Fennara might truly call family, and the only one he would never bet against.
- **The Upper Gods**: They would rather keep his chaos as far from their councils as they can, and he would rather watch than sit at the table. Both sides are perfectly happy.
