Goddess of beasts. Patron of hunters.

## Titles

- The Huntress
- Mother of the Wild
- The Silent Stalker
- Keeper of the Hunter's Law
- She Who Runs

## The Wild Heart

Ruthala shaped the beasts of the world: the wolf and the deer, the bear and the hare, the hawk upon the wind and every creature that walks, creeps or flies across the land. In the shaping, she became the wild itself, and something of every beast she made still lives in her.

To look upon her, Ruthala seems calm. She is patient and quiet, still as a hunter in the long grass, and she can wait for days without a flicker of impatience. But it is a predator's calm. Beneath it, the wild is coiled tight, all the speed and hunger and fury of every beast she ever made, waiting for the moment to spring. Those who have seen that moment come do not forget it.

She is the goddess of the hunter and the hunted alike, the wolf and the deer both. To Ruthala, the hunt is not cruelty but balance: the oldest dance in the world, and the most sacred.

## The Shrinking Wild

Before the flood, Ruthala was a goddess of real strength, and many thought her power bordered on that of the upper gods. Great herds thundered across the open plains, vast forests teemed with life, and her domain stretched across every land in the world.

Then the waters rose. Through the long centuries of [[Aquilla's Wrath]], the wild was driven onto ever-smaller islands, crowded together and forced to compete with mortals for every acre of dry land. Whatever fled into the sea became [[Caruilla|Caruilla's]], and much of what remained did not survive. The herds of the open plains, the great forest predators and countless creatures besides vanished from the world entirely. Today they live on only in old stories, and in the bones that miners and divers sometimes drag up from the drowned places of the world.

With every beast that was lost, Ruthala grew weaker. What was once a mighty goddess is now a shadow of her former strength, and the wild she rules is smaller than it has ever been.

Yet not all is lost. Every so often, on some remote and forgotten island, a creature long thought extinct is seen again. Ruthala's faithful guard such places fiercely.

## Gifts of Kinship

Ruthala did not weather the flood entirely alone. During the long centuries of [[Aquilla's Wrath]], as she watched her wild drown, two gods offered her what little they could.

[[Koruk]], who had watched his own children drown, granted her a small claim upon the dragons. They are his and always will be, but a dragon is a beast as surely as any wolf, and so a part of each of them now answers to the Huntress as well.

[[Roktra]] granted her the same over the land-born peoples he shaped. They remain his, but in his slow and steady way, Roktra reminded the heavens of something mortals often forget: that man, for all his cities and his ships, is but a beast too.

Neither gift restored what the flood took from her, but both were given freely, and Ruthala has never forgotten it.

## The Sky

Not everything in Ruthala's domain diminished. When the land drowned, the birds thrived. The open skies above the endless sea became theirs, and the seabirds, gulls and great ocean-crossing wanderers flourish as never before. Birds have become Ruthala's favorites and her messengers, and those who know how to read their flight say it shows the will of the Huntress.

## A Lower Goddess

Ruthala was never quite strong enough to sit among the upper gods, and she never cared enough to try. The politics of the heavens hold no interest for her. She is rarely seen in the halls of the gods at all, preferring to run with her beasts in whatever wild places remain. Now, with the wild so diminished, the question of a higher seat is one she does not even consider.

## The Hunter's Law

With so little of the wild left, Ruthala's hunters keep a strict and ancient code, and it is the closest thing she has to a commandment:

- **Take only what you need.**
- **Use everything you take.**
- **Never kill the last of anything.**
- **Honor what you hunt.**

To break the Hunter's Law is the deepest insult that can be offered to Ruthala. A wasteful hunter wounds both halves of her domain at once, for the prey is as much her child as the predator.

## Rangers

When [[Urset]] drank the magic of the world dry, Ruthala did not simply accept it. The rare rangers who still wield magic owe it to her. Like [[Koruk]] with his dragons, she quietly helps them steal threads of power back from [[Urset|Urset's]] hoard, so that those who walk the last wild places of the world need not walk them powerless.

Even with her help, true arcane rangers are rare. Most rangers are simply hunters, trackers and wardens of the wild, as skilled as any in the world but with no more magic than the beasts they follow. Only a rare few ever learn to draw a thread of true power from the hoard.

## Worship

Ruthala is the patron of hunters, trappers and rangers, and of all who live by the wild.

- **Thanks for the kill.** Every hunter speaks a word of thanks over every kill.
- **The hunter's share.** A portion of every kill is left behind for the wild, so that the wild may feed itself as well.
- **The single trophy.** Hunters may keep a single tooth, claw or feather from their prey, worn not as a boast but as a mark of respect.
- **Reading the birds.** The flight of birds is watched closely for omens, for they are the messengers of the Huntress.

### Champion

Ruthala's champion is hidden, and few rumors ever surround them. Those that do all say the same thing: that the champion stalks those who break the Hunter's Law. Poachers who kill the last of a kind, hunters who slaughter for sport and leave the meat to rot, trappers who take far more than they need: some of them simply never come home. If the champion is real, they move through the wild like their goddess, silent, patient and unseen, until the moment they strike.

## Relationships

- **[[Apiot]]**: The mother and the hunter. They respect one another deeply, but they have never agreed on what nature is for. [[Apiot]] would protect every living thing; Ruthala knows that the wild must also hunt.
- **[[Caruilla]]**: An endless squabble over who owns the seals, the seabirds and the crabs upon the beach. It has gone on for ages, and neither will ever give an inch.
- **[[Urset]]**: She helps her rangers steal from his hoard, and he hates her for it. Ruthala does not much care.
- **[[Koruk]]**: Two gods who help their children steal back what [[Urset]] took. During the flood, he granted her a small claim upon his dragons, and Ruthala respects the dragon god now more than she ever did in the old days.
- **[[Roktra]]**: Every island he held above the waves is a refuge for her beasts, and he granted her a share in the land-born peoples besides. She owes him more than she has ever said aloud.
