God of technology and progress. Patron of craftsmen, researchers, scientists and artificers. The middle moon.

## Titles

- The Orange Moon
- The Tinker
- Lord of the Workshop
- The Guiding Hand
- The Middle Moon

## The Middle Moon  
Ostin is the second child of [[Arkaius]] and [[Welestia]], and the middle of the three moons. Like his sisters, [[Ellehwin]] and [[Winstra]], he belongs to neither the upper gods nor the lower. He is a moon, nothing more and nothing less.

His is the orange moon, and it makes its turn across the heavens every forty-two days. Within it rests a sliver of his father, one of the few threads of [[Arkaius]] that slipped through [[Urset]]'s grasp, and Ostin has put it to work, as he puts everything to work.

He is a methodical god, careful and precise in all he does, measuring twice and building once. Yet beneath that care burns a deep restlessness. Ostin is impatient with the world, impatient with the slow crawl of mortal progress, and impatient most of all with how long it has taken to get where it is.

## The Long Stillness  
Before the [[Arcanus Collapse]], the world had no need to invent. Magic lit its lamps, healed its sick and carried its burdens, and what learning existed was built upon it. When [[Urset]] drank the world's magic dry, nearly all of it fell with it.

For Ostin, the centuries that followed were a long and bitter wound. Through the dark age of [[Aquilla's Slumber]] and the long drowning of [[Aquilla's Wrath]], he watched mortals forget how to build what they once had, and then crawl, step by painful step, toward what they might build instead. More than two thousand years later, the world has come only a little way, and Ostin has felt every slow inch of it.

## The Guiding Hand  
Ostin cannot simply hand the world its inventions. The First Law binds him as it binds every god, and he can only push so hard. Instead, he guides. A spark of insight in a shipwright's mind, a sudden understanding in a scholar's late-night study, a flaw noticed just in time on a workbench: these are the marks of Ostin's hand.

The great ships of the age, the cannons upon their decks and every new craft that has spread across the islands are mortal-made things, built by mortal hands. Yet those who make them often say they are expressions of Ostin's insight, shaped by a guiding hand they could feel but never see.

## His Era  
Now the flood is over, and Ostin means to make the [[Aquilla's Mercy Era]] his own. Not out of greed, for he wants nothing for himself, but for all. He believes that this age, at long last, can be one of progress, and that the mortals of the world can build something greater than anything they lost. He has waited a very long time for it, and he does not intend to wait any longer.

## Artificers  
Artificers are Ostin's most devoted faithful, craftsmen like any other, only more magical in nature. Through the sliver of [[Arkaius]] that rests in his moon, Ostin grants them the power to weave a thread of magic into their work, blending craft and magic as only he can.

That power can only be granted beneath his full moon. No one becomes an artificer on any other night.

## The Orange Moon  
Roughly twice each year, the orange moon swells to fullness, and by long custom those nights belong to Ostin's faithful. No law demands it and no temple declares it, but it is beneath the full orange moon that new inventions are unveiled, new workshops are opened, new ship designs are first set upon the water, and new artificers receive their gift.

## Worship  
Ostin is the patron of craftsmen, researchers, scientists and artificers, and of all who believe the world can be made better than it is. His shrines stand in workshops, shipyards, forges and studies across the islands.

- **The first piece.** The first thing any new craftsman or apprentice makes is dedicated to Ostin.
- **The maker's mark.** Craftsmen carve a small orange moon onto their finest work as a mark of pride and a quiet prayer.
- **Prayers for insight.** Those who pray to Ostin ask not for luck but for understanding. Luck, his faithful say, is only progress no one has understood yet.

### Champion  
Ostin is one of the few gods who has always kept his champions in the open, and every one of them has spent their life pushing to rebuild the lost craft and learning of the world. Some succeeded beyond anyone's hopes, and their work is still in use today. Others aimed too high or too soon, and are remembered more for their failures than their triumphs. One is still honored for the great lenses that light the lighthouses of the islands; another is best remembered for an engine that took half of his workshop with it when it failed. Yet every one of them moved the world a little further forward than they found it.

His current champion is [[Chief Artificer Lady Beatrix Thorncast]] of the [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/index|Kingdom of Albryn]], whose workshops are said to be the finest in the world.

## Relationships

- **[[Ellehwin]]**: His elder sister, tradition to his change. They clash constantly, for she would keep the world as it is and he would remake it, but at the end of the day they have each other's backs.
- **[[Winstra]]**: His younger sister, always reaching for the unknown. Ostin builds the ships, and Winstra guides them where no one has sailed before. Of his two sisters, she is the one most likely to share his restlessness.
- **[[Arkaius]]**: His father, whose magic built the world Ostin is trying to rebuild without it. He carries a piece of him in his moon, and puts it to good use.
- **[[Welestia]]**: His mother, whose storms wreck the very ships he helps to build. He loves her, and he builds the ships stronger every year.
- **[[Urset]]**: The god who hates him most. Every invention that does what magic once did proves the world has no need of Urset's hoard. Ostin, for his part, pays him little mind. The future, he says, has no need of a hoarder.
- **[[Flaris]]**: Every forge in the world burns with Flaris's fire, and every craftsman's hearth with it too. The two have always worked hand in hand.
