Goddess of sea creatures. Patron of the sea folk.

## Titles

- Mother of the Sea Folk
- The Tidechild
- Maker of Monsters
- Little Sister of the Sea
- The Restless Current

## The Little Sister  
When [[Aquilla]] poured out the waters of the world, Caruilla looked upon them and wished only to be near her. So she filled them. She shaped the fish and the whales, the crabs and the coral-dwellers, every fin and shell and tentacle that swims beneath the waves, and in the shaping she took on their aspect and became their goddess. She did it, more than for any other reason, to stay close to the one she had chosen as her big sister.

Theirs is not a bond of blood, for no god but the moons was ever born of another. Caruilla simply decided that [[Aquilla]] was her sister, and she has never once stopped believing it. [[Aquilla]], for her part, has never quite managed to tell her otherwise. Caruilla wears the role proudly, and she has no wish to ever outgrow it.

## A Child at Heart  
Caruilla is a child at heart, and always has been. She is easily bored and easily distracted, delighted by one thing in the morning and forgetting it by nightfall. She is rarely cruel, save in the careless way that children are cruel, and her temper is more sulk than storm.

She makes sea monsters because she thinks they are wonderful. The krakens, the sea serpents, the great nameless beasts of the deep are her favorite creations, and she has never given much thought to what they do to the ships that cross their paths.

## Mother of the Sea Folk  
Of all she has made, Caruilla loves the sea folk most. She did not bear them, but she made them, and she is mother to them in every way that matters. They are the only mortals she truly cares for. Toward the land dwellers she holds no hatred, only indifference. The fishermen who haul her creatures up in their nets annoy her the way a gnat might, and little more.

## The Rising Tide  
As the waters climbed through [[Aquilla's Wrath]], Caruilla climbed with them. Every drowned mile became new seas for her creatures and new homes for her sea folk, and over the long centuries her domain swelled until it rivaled the greatest of the gods. Now she wants what she believes she has earned: a seat among the upper gods. The pantheon has not given it to her, and she has not stopped asking.

Whether Caruilla knew that her sea folk guarded Magnus's cauldron through the centuries, or whether she ever cared, no one can say. But when the surface dwellers fought their way through her children to destroy it, and then raised a festival in her sister's name to celebrate, Caruilla was furious. She has sought no vengeance, for she is far too easily distracted for that. But she has not forgiven them either, and her sea folk have noticed.

## Worship  
Among the sea folk, Caruilla's worship is fierce and nearly universal. She is the goddess most of them pray to, and for many the only one.

Above the waves, few worship her at all. Those who do are mostly pearl divers, whalers and coastal villages hoping to stay beneath her notice. At the tideline they leave small offerings of shells, trinkets and brightly colored things that might catch her eye, in hope that the monsters of the deep will pass them by.

### Champion  
Caruilla's champion is of the sea folk, for it could hardly be anyone else. Beneath the waves, her champion is known and revered. Above them, they are little more than a sailor's tale.

## Relationships

- **[[Aquilla]]**: Her chosen big sister, and the center of her world. Caruilla adores her, follows her everywhere, and tests her patience without end. [[Aquilla]] protects her fiercely and is utterly tired of her nonsense, and Caruilla would not have it any other way.
- **[[Apiot]]**: Caruilla would never admit that she likes [[Apiot]]'s reefs, but her creatures crowd into every one of them. She lets her sea folk grumble about the gardens all the same.
- **[[Ruthala]]**: An endless squabble over who owns the seals, the seabirds and the crabs upon the beach. It has gone on for ages, and neither will ever give an inch.
- **The Upper Gods**: She pesters them constantly for a seat among them. Most have learned to stop answering.
