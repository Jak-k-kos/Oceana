## Pre-Divinity
Urset was born Mortimer Greenbe in -107 PAC in Veygard, a nation long since lost to the waves, only remembered for the fact that Urset was born there. Marty was born the 8th Prince of the Veygard royal family. Due to rampant inbreeding of the nobility and his mother's rampant drug and alcohol use before his birth, he was born horribly deformed. barely able to walk or even complete simple tasks due to his deformities and shunned by his family and the other nobles, he spent his early life stuck under constant care of his servants in a hidden away portion of the castle. his involuntarily sedentary lifestyle led him to turn to books as his only source of entertainment. he found a particular interest in magic, leading him to spend nearly every hour of his day studying arcane texts. by the age of 15 he finally was able to become self sufficient, using magic to accommodate his basic needs. due to his unparalleled natural talent he became an arch mage at the age of 23, an unprecedented achievement even for the Pre Arcanus Collapse. He quickly became essential to the Veygard kingdom leading to his rise to power becoming the Head Magos at age 36. as his power grew, so did his greed, leading him to attempt a coup. at age 41, his father died leaving Mortimers eldest brother to take the throne leading to Urset (then Mortimer)
