God of dragons. Ruler of the Draconic Conclave. Patron of all things draconic.

## Titles

- The Wyrmfather
- Lord of the Conclave
- The Iron Claw
- The Bronze King
- The Leashed Fury (whispered, and never within earshot of a dragon)

## The Wyrmfather

From fire and stone and boundless pride, Koruk shaped the dragons of the world, the mightiest and most magnificent of all living things, and in the shaping he became them. Their hunger became his hunger, their fury his fury, their pride the very marrow of his bones. Every dragon that has ever spread its wings is his child, from the ancient wyrms who coil about the mountain peaks to the smallest pseudodragon dozing in a sunlit window.

In the elder days, there was no god more feared. Koruk was as savage as the chromatic dragons who basked in his favor, and his children darkened the skies in burning swarms, raiding and plundering wherever they pleased. Again and again he set himself against [[Arkaius]], and more than once he stretched his claws toward the throne of the gods itself.

His scales in those days were black as a burnt-out sky and red as the embers left behind. They are not so now. Through the long centuries of the flood, his fury cooled, and his scales cooled with it, fading slowly to burnished bronze and gold.

## The Calming

What gentled the Wyrmfather, no one can truly say, and Koruk has never deigned to tell. The world is left only with its whispers.

Some say it was grief for his children. As the waters climbed, the mountains drowned, and with them went the lairs, the hoards and the proud old dragons who would not abandon them. Others say it was the flood itself, for the long centuries of [[Aquilla's Wrath]] brought a sorrow that seemed to have no end. Wars raged as the sea folk tore the drowning lands from those who dwelt there, and whole civilizations faded into silence as the waters cut them off from the rest of the world. Some believe that even the heart of a dragon god could not stand unmoved before so much death.

And some say it was guilt. Koruk and [[Arkaius]] had clashed for ages, and few have forgotten how often the dragon god reached for the throne that [[Arkaius]] held. When the Weaver fell, many eyes turned to Koruk, and when he grew quiet in the years that followed, many took his silence for a confession. Whether the change was born of guilt or of grief, only Koruk knows. For all their battles, he had loved [[Arkaius]] as a brother. And when a mortal thief named [[Urset]] climbed into the seat where his brother had sat, it was salt pressed into an open wound.

Yet let no one mistake the Wyrmfather for a creature at peace. Koruk is not calm. He has been calmed, the way a forest fire is calmed: banked and smothered and held in check, but never put out. The old fury still smolders beneath his bronze scales, and the old hunger still stirs in the dark of his heart. He simply holds them close, in a grip of iron, and he holds his children the same way.

## The Draconic Conclave

Far across the waves lies the Draconic Conclave, the realm of the dragons. It is a single vast island crowned by a peak that pierces the clouds, and ringed by a scattering of smaller isles like broken pieces of a crown. It is a proud and violent land that keeps to itself, and few sailors who stray too near its shores ever return to tell of it. Countless dragons dwell there, though in the wider world they have grown rare.

Upon the highest peak sits the council of the eldest dragons, and Koruk binds it together with an iron claw. Once, the chromatic dragons sat closest to his favor and held the greater part of the council. No longer. The tides of power have turned, and the metallic dragons and a handful of gem dragons now stand above them. The gem dragons have always been his most beloved children, and they remain mighty beyond measure.

The great burning swarms of old are no more, for Koruk no longer permits his children to raid the world in numbers. Some raids still happen, for no leash is ever perfect, but they are few. He keeps his children close beneath his wing, under his protection and his heavy hand alike.

### Children Beyond the Conclave

- **Faerie dragons and pseudodragons** are the most common dragons in the wider world. They are small enough to slip away from the Conclave unnoticed, and harmless enough that Koruk does not demand their return. They cause mischief, never chaos, and most fly home to the Conclave in time to breed in its sheltered havens.
- **Dragon turtles** are his children by right, though [[Caruilla]] has long claimed them as her own, and so they dwell in a gray tide between the two. Most choose to make their home within the Conclave, but some still roam the open waters.
- **Sea serpents** are no dragons at all, and belong to [[Caruilla]] alone.

### Dragonborn and Kobolds

Dragonborn and kobolds are the only peoples of human shape who dwell within the Conclave. They hold no seat of power there, living out their lives as a kind of middle folk in the long shadows of the dragons they serve. Some have left the Conclave and made new homes among the islands, carrying the worship of the Wyrmfather with them.

## Dragons and Magic

To Koruk, [[Urset]] is a pale and pitiful imitation of a dragon's might: a hoarder without the strength to guard his hoard. When [[Urset]] drank the magic of the world dry, Koruk refused to bow to it. The dragons hold their magic still because the Wyrmfather helps them steal it back from [[Urset]]'s hoard, and for this [[Urset]] counts him among his bitterest enemies.

Mortal sorcerers with dragon's blood in their veins are possible, but they are rare beyond rare, and their magic, too, is stolen from [[Urset]]'s hoard.

## Worship

Few mortals truly worship Koruk. Most prayers sent his way are pleas: that his children remain within the Conclave, and leave their ships and cities in peace.

Those who do worship him are mostly dragonborn and kobolds who have left the Conclave behind. Across the islands they have raised small churches and dragon cults, praying to him for fortune and for the favor of the dragons. Images of the old Koruk, black-scaled and terrible, are rare now, for most of his ancient temples lie drowned beneath the waves.

### Champion

Koruk's champion is one of the few known openly: the Chief Councilor of the Conclave, a great amethyst dragon who sits at the head of the council of the eldest. Beyond the Conclave's shores, no one knows their name.

## Relationships

- **[[Arkaius]]**: His rival, and his brother in all but blood. They warred for ages, and Koruk never once told him how much he loved him. It is the regret he carries above all others.
- **[[Ellehwin]]**: Once he reached for her father's throne. Now he stands among her staunchest supporters, an atonement to the friend he wishes he had loved more openly.
- **[[Urset]]**: A thief upon his brother's throne, and a pale shadow of a dragon's might. Koruk helps his children steal back what [[Urset]] hoards, and [[Urset]] counts him among his bitterest enemies.
- **[[Caruilla]]**: An endless squabble over the dragon turtles. Koruk bears it with far more patience than he would have once.
- **[[Flaris]]**: The dragons breathe [[Flaris]]'s fire. [[Flaris]] never cared for the old Koruk, but he has come to respect the new one.
- **[[Ruthala]]**: A dragon is a beast as surely as any wolf. After the flood, Koruk granted the Huntress a small claim upon his children, for he knew too well what it was to watch them drown.
