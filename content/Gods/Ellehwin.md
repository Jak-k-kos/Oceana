Goddess of law and order. Patron of judges, lawkeepers, legislators and leaders. The eldest moon.

## Titles

- The Green Moon
- The Lawkeeper
- Warden of the First Law
- Eldest of the Moons
- The Bound Judge

## The Eldest Moon  
Ellehwin is the firstborn of [[Arkaius]] and [[Welestia]], daughter of the eye and the storm, and the eldest of the three moons. She and her siblings, [[Ostin]] and [[Winstra]], are the only gods ever born of gods, and they belong to neither the upper gods nor the lower. They are the moons, nothing more and nothing less.

Her moon is the green one, and the slowest of the three. It takes ninety-two days to make its turn across the heavens, patient and unhurried, much like the goddess herself. Within it rests a sliver of her father, one of the few threads of [[Arkaius]] that slipped through [[Urset]]'s grasp.

## The Weight of the Throne  
When [[Arkaius]] fell, the gods needed someone to sit at the head of their halls, and none of the mighty would bow to another. In the end they chose Ellehwin. She favored no one, she was fair to all, and of every god she was the only one all the others would suffer to sit there.

Nor was she chosen for weakness. Ellehwin is law, and law runs through every people that has ever gathered together: every village and city-state, every ship's crew, every court of the sea folk beneath the waves. Wherever mortals live together, her domain is already there. Where other gods rule by might, she rules by law. Her father's First Law, that no god may act upon the world beyond their own domain, is the foundation of her throne, and she keeps it to the letter.

She is stern and confident, tough but fair, and she does her best to do what is right. But she is always beholden to the law, even when the law asks more of her than she wishes to give.

## The Law That Binds  
Ellehwin believes the law exists to protect, and that every law should be an act of justice. That belief was tested as no god's has ever been.

Through all the long centuries of [[Aquilla's Wrath]], Ellehwin watched the world drown and could do nothing. The cauldron lay in [[Aquilla]]'s waters, beyond Ellehwin's domain, and so it was beyond her reach. Neither could she force [[Aquilla]] to act, for the law asks the gods only to keep within their bounds, not to do good within them. The law her father made to protect the world was the very thing that held her back while it sank. It is a wound she carries still.

## Her First Judgment  
Her first act as head of the pantheon was to pass judgment on [[Urset]]. After [[Welestia]]'s wrath had burned and crippled him, he was too broken to be counted among the upper gods, and Ellehwin sentenced him to the lower Halls of Divinity. [[Urset]] has never forgiven her. To him, the girl who took his throne is the same one who cast him down.

## Her Mother's Daughter  
Ellehwin's bond with her mother has always been strained, even at the best of times, for [[Welestia]] is chaos incarnate and her daughter is order itself. Yet between them runs a deep and abiding love, on both sides.

That love is what makes the truth so unbearable. As keeper of the law, Ellehwin has sought her father's killer since the day he fell. Each thread that leads back to her mother breaks her heart anew. Of all the gods, [[Welestia]] is both the most powerful and the least stable, and in Ellehwin's eyes she remains the most likely suspect. Ellehwin prays, in the quiet of her own halls, that she will never find evidence enough to pass judgment on her own mother.

## The Green Moon  
Once in each long turn, the green moon swells to fullness. For four days it rises toward full, for three days it shines full, and for four days it falls away again.

No law commands it, and no temple has made it a holy day, but across the islands it has become custom to save the weightiest matters for those days. Oaths are sworn, judgments are given, and bargains are sealed beneath the full green moon. An oath sworn in its light is held to be the most binding of all, and it is said that one who breaks such an oath draws Ellehwin's eye.

On rare occasions, more than an eye is drawn. A mortal who swears a true oath beneath the full green moon may find that oath answered with power. Such oath-sworn are the rare paladins of the world who are not champions. Their power is a pale thing beside a champion's, but it is real, and it lasts only as long as the oath is kept.

### Temples  
Ellehwin's temples are rarely buildings of their own. Most are a single hall within a magistrate's court or council house, where the work of law and the worship of its goddess sit side by side. It is here that oaths are sworn beneath the full green moon.

Her priests are mediators more than lawmen. They help people settle their disputes before those disputes ever reach a judge, seeking a fair agreement rather than a winner and a loser. Mediation is heavily encouraged before any case is brought to trial, and most magistrates will send a dispute to Ellehwin's priests first. Some matters, however, cannot wait, and when justice must be swift, the courts may act without them.
## Worship  
Ellehwin is the patron of judges, lawkeepers, legislators and leaders, and of all who believe that the law should protect the weak. Her shrines stand in courthouses and council halls, and few rulers take their seats without first seeking her blessing.

She will grant a measure of her power to mortals, but only within the bounds of her laws, and those who receive it are expected to uphold them.

### Champion  
Ellehwin's champion is hidden. Whoever they are, they are said to fight injustice at every turn, wherever they find it, caring more for justice done than for wounds healed.

## Relationships

- **[[Welestia]]**: Her mother, chaos to her order. Their relationship has always been strained, but the love between them runs deep, and Ellehwin dreads the day her search for the truth might lead to her mother's door.
- **[[Arkaius]]**: Her father, whose law she keeps and whose throne she holds. She has never stopped searching for the one who killed him.
- **[[Ostin]]**: Her younger brother, change to her tradition. They clash constantly, but at the end of the day they have each other's backs.
- **[[Winstra]]**: Her youngest sibling, drawn always toward the unknown and the unexplored, where Ellehwin would rather keep to what is known and settled. They argue often, but they stand together when it matters.
- **[[Urset]]**: The usurper of her father's power, and the god she cast down. He hates her for the throne he believes was his, and she hates him in turn for stealing her father's soul.
- **[[Aquilla]]**: [[Aquilla]] broke no law, and so Ellehwin could do nothing. Their dealings remain cold and correct, and Ellehwin has never forgiven her.
