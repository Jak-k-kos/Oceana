Goddess of storms. Patron of none. Scourge of the skies.

## Titles

- The Storm
- The Widow
- Scourge of the Skies
- Mother of Moons
- The Fury That Was Once Gentle

## The Storm Herself  
Welestia shaped the skies of the world: the wind and the rain, the thunder and the lightning, the hurricane and the gentle summer breeze. In the shaping, she became the storm itself, and the storm has never once been still.

Her hair is living lightning, crackling and writhing about her like a crown of storms, and her eyes are pure white, each one a ball of lightning trapped behind her gaze. Few mortals have ever seen her and lived to describe it. Fewer still would wish to.

She is the mightiest of all the gods, and the most feared. Her domain reaches across every sky in the world, and there is no sea, no shore and no mountaintop that her storms cannot touch.

## The Dead Wind  
Welestia's storms are feared across every sea, but sailors fear her stillness just as much. When she withholds her winds entirely, the sea goes flat and silent, the sails hang limp, and a ship can drift for days or weeks without moving an inch toward home. Water runs low, food runs out, and crews go mad beneath an empty sky. Sailors call it the dead wind, and they will tell you that it has claimed as many ships as any hurricane. A ship trapped on a windless sea is as surely doomed as one torn apart by a storm. It only takes longer to die.

## The Eye of the Storm  
Welestia was never gentle by nature. The hurricanes are older than her husband's death, and many have always whispered that she was a little mad, and they were not entirely wrong.

Yet for untold ages, she was loved, and she loved in return. She wed [[Arkaius]], and theirs was the only marriage the heavens have ever known. He was the eye of her storm, the stillness at its heart, and for love of him she held her fury in check. In those days she sent soft rains to the fields and fair winds to the sails, and every harvest of the old world belonged as much to her as to [[Apiot]]. Together, she and [[Arkaius]] brought three children into the world: the moons [[Ellehwin]], [[Ostin]] and [[Winstra]], the only gods ever born of gods.

## The Widow's Grief  
When [[Arkaius]] died, the eye of the storm died with him.

What followed was a grief so vast that it became the weather of the world. The gentle rains turned to downpours, the fair winds to gales, and the hurricanes grew mightier and more frequent with every passing year. Many say that no mere grief could have birthed such fury, and to this day she bears more blame for her husband's death than any other god. Whether she cares what they say, no one knows.

When the mortal mage who had stolen her husband's soul rose as [[Urset]] and tried to claim her as his bride, he learned what had been held back through all the ages of her marriage. Her answer left him burned and crippled, cast down into the lower Halls of Divinity, and it was only the first storm of a fury that has lasted more than two and a half thousand years.

## The Turning Wind  
Yet even the longest storm must one day change.

Of late, sailors and fishermen have begun to notice something they never thought they would see. Welestia's storms have calmed, if only a little. The hurricanes come less often, the gales blow a little less fiercely, and here and there, a fair wind fills the sails where once there would have been only rain. No one knows why. Some say her grief is finally beginning to fade. Others say it is only the calm before something worse.

Only one place remains unchanged. The [[Widow's Wrath]] is as deadly as it has ever been

## The Prospect of Change  
Welestia is the fury of nature, and she is feared by nearly everyone who has ever lived beneath her sky. Yet she is more than destruction. A storm tears down what is old and rotten, scours the land clean and leaves the air fresh behind it. Welestia is change itself: change that cannot be stopped, bargained with or controlled.

## Patron of None  
Welestia is the patron of no one, and almost no one worships her. Most prayers sent her way are pleas to be spared. Sailors lash charms to their masts against her storms, and every island town knows that when her hurricanes come, it is [[Roktra|Roktra's]] churches they must run to.

Only the desperate truly pray to her: those who have lost everything, those trapped in lives they cannot bear, and those who want nothing more than for the storm to come and tear it all down so they can begin again.

She has no temples, no festivals and no priests.

## The Widow's Wrath  
Somewhere out upon the open sea lies a stretch of water that no captain will willingly cross. The sailors call it the Widow's Wrath.

There is no middle ground within the Wrath. It knows only two moods, and both are deadly. Either the storms rage without pause, hurricanes tearing at the sea while lightning strikes the waves day and night, or the wind dies entirely and the sea lies flat and silent beneath an empty sky, trapping any ship within it in a dead calm that may never break. There is never anything in between.

Worse still, the Wrath hides itself. From the outside, its waters can look no different from any other stretch of sea, and many a ship has sailed into it on a fair wind without ever knowing, until the sky darkened or the sails fell slack and it was already far too late. Wise captains chart their courses carefully and give it the widest berth they can, for by the time you know you are in its clutches, there is no escaping them. Ships that sail into the Widow's Wrath are rarely seen again.

No one knows why Welestia's fury gathers there more fiercely than anywhere else in the world. No one has ever lived to find out.

## Rayskaya  
At the heart of the [[Widow's Wrath]], hidden deep within its storms and dead calms, lies the island of Rayskaya. Rayskaya sits in the very eye of the storm: the calm at the center of Welestia's fury, and the one place in all her domain that lightning never touches. Beyond its shores the Wrath either rages or lies deathly still, but on Rayskaya the skies are clear, the breezes are gentle and the island is lush and green. 

Upon the highest peak above the island's cove stands the last temple of Welestia, a weathered place of dark stone with a great statue of the goddess at its heart. When she chooses to speak to the mortal world, it is through this statue that she speaks.

The temple is kept by a single monk, his body covered in branching lightning scars. He may well be the last true follower Welestia has left.

### Champion  
No one knows whether Welestia has a champion, and no one would dare to ask her. Most assume she has had none since the death of [[Arkaius]], and that the goddess who cares for no one would never choose one.

## The Champion  
Welestia chose no champion in all the long ages since the death of [[Arkaius]]. That has changed. Her champion is now Shady Bellball, who came before her statue on Rayskaya and gave up their soul in exchange for the power to save someone they loved. Shady is her sword of change upon the mortal plane, the first champion the goddess of storms has chosen in more than two and a half thousand years.

## Relationships

- **[[Arkaius]]**: Her husband, and the eye of her storm. She has mourned him for more than two and a half thousand years.
- **[[Ellehwin]]**: Her eldest daughter, order to her chaos. Their relationship has always been strained, but the love between them runs deep on both sides.
- **[[Ostin]]**: Her son, whose ships her storms so often wreck. He loves her anyway, and builds his ships stronger every year.
- **[[Winstra]]**: Her youngest, whose stars her storms so often hide. Winstra loves her all the same, and always makes sure the stars are waiting when the clouds clear.
- **[[Urset]]**: He tried to make her his bride, and she burned and crippled him for it. He hates her above all others, and wants her still. She has never given him a second thought.
- **[[Apiot]]**: Once her closest partner, the rain to her harvest. Since the death of [[Arkaius]], her storms have driven them apart.
- **[[Aquilla]]**: Her winds whip up [[Aquilla|Aquilla's]] waves. They have become allies without ever meaning to.
- **[[Roktra]]**: When her hurricanes come, it is his churches that shelter the mortal world from her. Neither of them has ever spoken of it.
