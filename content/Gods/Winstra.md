Goddess of stars and dreams. Patron of travelers, explorers, mapmakers and dreamers. The youngest moon.

## Titles

- The Purple Moon
- The Starmaker
- The Guiding Star
- Lady of Half-Made Dreams
- The Youngest Moon

## The Youngest Moon  
Winstra is the youngest child of [[Arkaius]] and [[Welestia]], and the last of the three moons. Like her siblings, [[Ellehwin]] and [[Ostin]], she belongs to neither the upper gods nor the lower. She is a moon, nothing more and nothing less.

Hers is the purple moon, the swiftest of the three, racing across the heavens every eighteen days and never in the same place two nights running. It suits her. Winstra is curious and playful, restless and bright-eyed, her head always full of half-made dreams. She is far more likely to be found wandering the edges of the world than sitting in the halls of the gods, and her eldest sister has long since given up trying to make her stay.

Within her moon rests a sliver of her father, one of the few threads of [[Arkaius]] that slipped through [[Urset|Urset's]] grasp.

## The Starmaker  
Long ago, the night sky was dark. [[Flaris]] had made the sun to warm the world by day, but when it set, the world was left in blackness, and any traveler caught out beneath it was lost.

Winstra could not bear it. With [[Flaris|Flaris's]] blessing, she took the heart of the sun into her hands and remade it a thousand times over, scattering the pieces across the heavens until the whole night sky was filled with light. Every star in the sky is a little piece of the sun, hung by Winstra so that no traveler would ever again be truly lost in the dark.

## The Hidden Reach  
Few realize what that means. Nearly every god's domain ends somewhere within the world. Winstra's does not. The stars stretch farther than any mortal can imagine, out into the endless dark beyond the sky, and every one of them is hers. Of all the gods, her reach is perhaps the greatest.

Winstra does not speak of it, and few of the other gods ever stop to consider it. To them, she is only the youngest moon, curious and playful and endlessly distracted. She seems quite content to keep it that way.

## The Constellations  
Winstra is still drawing in the sky. When a great deed is done in the world, she honors it the only way a goddess of stars can: she arranges the stars into a new constellation, so that the deed will be remembered for as long as there is a sky to see it.

Sailors and navigators learn the constellations as their charts, and the oldest of them tell the stories of ages long past. The newest is barely a season old. Shortly after the seas stopped rising, sailors began to notice a pattern in the stars that had never been there before, and it is now known across the islands as **[[The Tidebreakers]]**.

## The Dreamer  
The stars guide sailors through the dark, and dreams guide everyone else. Winstra is the patron of dreamers of every kind: the child who longs to sail past the edge of the map, the young captain with nothing but a leaky ship and a great ambition, the inventor scribbling plans late into the night. It is said that every great dream has a little of her starlight in it, and that those who dream boldly enough sometimes find the stars themselves seem to point the way.

## The Purple Moon  
Winstra's moon comes full more often than either of her siblings', five or six times every year. Sailors hold that there is no luckier time to begin a long voyage than beneath the full purple moon, and many captains will wait days in harbor for it before setting out into the unknown.

## The Wayfinders  
Winstra's church is known as the Wayfinders, and it is as much a guild as a faith. Its halls are filled with maps and star charts, and its priests are mapmakers, navigators and explorers, who spend their lives charting the drowned world and teaching others to find their way across it.

Their halls are also a guiding star to the weary. Any traveler who is lost, stranded or far from home may come to a Wayfinder hall and be given a meal, a place to rest, and directions home. Many a sailor who lost their ship has found their way back to their family through a Wayfinder's charts.

Since the end of the flood, the Wayfinders have never been busier. The drowned world is waiting to be rediscovered: new routes, new islands and seas no one has charted in more than two thousand years. If the new age belongs to [[Ostin]] in progress, it belongs just as much to Winstra in exploration.

## Worship  
Winstra is the patron of travelers, explorers, mapmakers and dreamers.

- **The first star.** Before any long journey, travelers speak a short prayer to the first star of the night.
- **Blessed charts.** Every navigator keeps a star chart blessed in Winstra's name in their cabin.
- **The mapmaker's mark.** Mapmakers draw a small purple moon in the corner of every chart they make.
- **Sailing under the purple moon.** Long voyages are begun beneath the full purple moon whenever possible.

### Champion  
Winstra's champion is hidden, though most likely not by design. Whoever they are, they are far more likely to be off exploring the unknown edges of the world than doing anything the rest of the world would notice.

## Relationships

- **[[Ellehwin]]**: Her eldest sister, the settled and the known to her wandering and unknown. They argue often, and [[Ellehwin]] despairs of ever getting her to sit still, but they stand together when it matters.
- **[[Ostin]]**: Her brother, and the sibling most likely to share her restlessness. He builds the ships, and she guides them where no one has sailed before.
- **[[Flaris]]**: It was with his blessing that she took the heart of the sun to make the stars. The warm hearth and the guiding light have been fast friends ever since.
- **[[Welestia]]**: Her mother, whose storms hide the stars that travelers need most. Winstra loves her all the same, and always makes sure the stars are waiting when the clouds clear.
- **[[Urset]]**: He hates all three moons for holding what he could not keep, and Winstra least of all seems to notice.
