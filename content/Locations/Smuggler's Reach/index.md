# Smuggler's Reach
Far in the southeast, where the fog banks lie thick upon the water and the charts give up, lies Smuggler's Reach. It is not a nation, not anymore. Once it was part of the [[Locations/The Black Tide/index|Black Tide]]; now it is a smattering of pirate bands and little island city-states, each with its own captain, council or crime lord, and bound together by nothing but a love of profit and a hatred of anyone who would rule them.

# Cut Off
During [[The Southern Reclamation]], the [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/index|Kingdom's]] advance drove a wedge through the Black Tide's waters, and the Reach was left cut off from its king. For years it kept faith with a throne that could not reach it and could not help it. Some seventy-five years ago, it stopped bothering. It broke away from the Black Tide and has never answered to anyone since.

# The Trade
The Reach lives by two trades. Its raiders prey on small and lonely ships, the merchants and fishing boats too slow to run and too poor to fight. And its smugglers carry anything to anyone, slipping goods past the customs boats of the Kingdom and selling them in its markets without a shell of tax. Every crown official in the Kingdom curses the Reach, and a fair few of them drink its brandy.

# No Safe Harbor
No one has ever ruled the Reach, and few have seriously tried. Its havens change hands from season to season, its captains feud as often as they drink together, and an alliance in the Reach rarely outlasts the voyage it was made for. The Kingdom could crush it, or so its admirals like to say, but taking the Reach would mean chasing a hundred feuding captains through the fog for years. Every ship and every shell spent there would be one not spent against the Black Tide, and so the Kingdom contents itself with customs boats, and with shipping every smuggler and pirate it catches off to the coalition's prison islands, for the Kingdom rarely bothers with the gallows. Lately the Reach's havens have suffered the sea folk's raids as badly as anyone's.
