# The Black Tide
Somewhere beyond the southern edge of every chart lie the islands of the Black Tide, and few who have found them have ever sailed home to say where. It is a nation of pirates: the terror of the southern seas, the old enemy of the [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/index|Kingdom]] and the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]] alike, and the only power in the south that bows to no crown but its own.

# The Code of the Tide
The Black Tide was born in 1875, when one captain bound the pirate lords of the south into one fleet beneath one flag ([[The Founding of the Black Tide]]). It was meant to be a nation without nobles. No one would rule by birth or by wealth. The finest captains would be raised to dread pirate lords, the lords would choose the finest among them to be the Dread Pirate King, and every hand aboard every ship, from the king to the lowest deckhand, was owed a fair share of all the Tide took and all it made. To the pirates who swore to it, it was freedom. To everyone else, it was the most dangerous idea on the seas.

# The Rot and the Retreat
It did not last. Some two hundred years ago, a Dread Pirate King handed his crown to his son, and the lords let him. The rot set in slowly, king after king, until the Black Tide could no longer hold what it had won. For nearly a century and a half the Kingdom and the coalition drove it back across the south ([[The Southern Reclamation]]), and far in the east [[Locations/Smuggler's Reach/index|Smuggler's Reach]] broke away entirely. The last of the failing kings was [[Dread Pirate King Gromon Spritroar|Gromon Spritroar]], feared across the southern seas and losing ground all the same.

# The King
Ten years ago, one of Gromon's own lords, [[Dread Pirate King Vask|Vask]], put the stagnant lords to the sword and took the crown. Gromon vanished and is believed dead. Within two years the retreat had stopped, and the Black Tide has not lost a mile of water since. Vask has rebuilt it into something disciplined and dangerous, and it is said he means to take back the whole of the south it once held. Some sailors whisper that [[Urset]] himself stands behind him.

# The Border
Between the Black Tide and the Kingdom lies a no-man's-land of constant skirmishes, water that no one can truly claim. To the north, the Black Tide presses against the coalition's southern islands, between the [[Locations/The Draconic Conclave/index|Draconic Conclave]] and the [[Locations/The Widow's Wrath/index|Widow's Wrath]], where the coalition's southern squadron stands guard and the frontier islands know siege all too well. Pirates taken there are sent to the coalition's prison islands for life, and their ships are stripped for their timbers and their iron.
