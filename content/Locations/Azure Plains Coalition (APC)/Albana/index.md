# Albana
Albana is an island of white stone, its hills made of fine marble pale as milk and quarried for the grandest temples and halls of the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]]. Yet its sculptors are the true treasure of the island. Every temple needs a statue of its god, for the gods can take hold of them as vessels, and the greatest of those statues are carved on Albana. Its workshops are full of half-finished gods: [[Flaris]] rising from a block of stone, [[Apiot|Apiot's]] face emerging beneath a chisel, [[Tarrakus]] waiting for his hands. Its smaller workshops turn out little figures of the gods by the hundred, especially of those with few great temples of their own, like [[Ostin]] and Flaris, and these find their way into tavern niches, workshop shelves, ship's cabins and small courthouses across the coalition. The sculptors work in a hushed reverence, and the islanders say a statue from Albana is one a god is glad to wear.
