# Nyassa
Nyassa is a small, low island on the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition's]] northern edge, ringed by old stone fish weirs that catch the tide twice a day. Its people live on what the weirs bring in, and they smoke most of it for sale: eel, mullet, and herring, packed in barrels for the neighbouring islands. The outermost weirs reach farther toward the [[Sea Folk Waters|sea folk waters]] than most islanders like, and Nyassans never work them after dark. In the island's sheltered lagoon lives a siren who left the sea folk long ago. She keeps to herself, helps mend the weirs from below, and never speaks of where she came from. The islanders have stopped asking.
