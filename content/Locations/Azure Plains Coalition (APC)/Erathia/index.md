# Erathia
Erathia is a tiny island with a long crescent of pale sand on its leeward side. Every summer, hundreds of sea turtles haul themselves up that beach to lay their eggs. The islanders take a share of the eggs, never all, and hunt the turtles at sea in the off-season. They sell turtle meat and soup to ships' galleys, and polish the mottled shells into tortoiseshell for combs, spectacle frames, and fine inlay. The rest of the year, Erathia is little more than a fishing village dozing in the sun.
