# Maya Jalld
Maya Jalld is a small island of red clay, and its people make one of the rarer goods in the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]]: fired brick. Fuel is too precious to fire brick for ordinary building, so most of the islands build in stone and timber. Maya Jalld's bricks go where nothing else will do: kilns, furnaces, forges, bread ovens, and the brick hearths of ships' galleys, which have to hold heat without burning through. Clay is dug from the island's pits, shaped in wooden moulds, and fired in long kilns that smoke day and night. Their pots and tiles are a sideline. The town's own houses are built entirely of rejects and seconds, so every wall on Maya Jalld is a patchwork of slightly wrong colours.
