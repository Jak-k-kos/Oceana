# Lithia
Lithia is a tiny, chalk-white island of limestone, and day and night its lime kilns breathe smoke into the sky. The stone is cut from quarries sunk deep into the island's heart and burned in squat stone kilns until it crumbles into quicklime, which is the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition's]] mortar, whitewash and plaster, and which tanners and sugar-boilers need besides. Lithia sends it out in sealed casks to every builder in the southeast. The island's stonecutters also turn the finest offcuts into small and lovely things: dice, tarrak pieces and counters, polished smooth and white, which find their way into every tavern in the coalition. The people of Lithia are pale with dust, and they cough a little, and they laugh a great deal.
