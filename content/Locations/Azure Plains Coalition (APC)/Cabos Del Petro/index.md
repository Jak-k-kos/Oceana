# Cabos Del Petro
Cabos Del Petro is a hard, grey cape of rock on the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition's]] far southwestern edge, where the cold, deep water teems with sharks. Its people hunt them from narrow boats with hooks and harpoons, and nothing is wasted. Sharkskin is dried into rough shagreen, prized for sword grips and by woodworkers as a polishing file. Livers are rendered into oil. Meat is salted and smoked, and fins are dried for soup. A stone lighthouse stands on the tip of the cape, and its lamp is the last coalition light ships see before dragon waters.
