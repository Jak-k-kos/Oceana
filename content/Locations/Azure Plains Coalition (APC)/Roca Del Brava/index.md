# Roca Del Brava
Roca Del Brava is a hard little island of grey stone, battered by rough water on its southern side. Its stone splits into thin, flat sheets of slate. The islanders quarry and split it into roofing slates, which last a lifetime and never burn: a precious thing in a world where fired tile is rare. Slate also makes writing tablets, and the island's finest grit makes the whetstones that keep every blade in the region sharp. The quarries ring with hammers from dawn to dusk, and slate roofs shine grey across the southwestern islands.
