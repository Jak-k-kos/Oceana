# Aspera
Aspera means "the rough one," and the island earns its name. It is a bare, windswept rock on the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition's]] northern edge, all gray stone and stubborn scrub, with a single sheltered harbor and a quarry that has been eating into its western cliffs for centuries. Aspera stone is hard, gray, and nearly impossible to wear down, and the island has always made its living cutting it: millstones for the grain islands, blocks for harbor walls and breakwaters, and paving stones for the streets of richer city-states. The quarry workers live in squat stone houses built from their own leftovers, and the whole town is the same color as the cliffs. Asperans are blunt, dusty, and proud of their stone. They like to say there isn't a harbor wall in the north that didn't start on Aspera.
