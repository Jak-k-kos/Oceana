# Cala Dora
Cala Dora is a tiny island with a single cove of golden sand, and in the warm light of evening the whole place seems to glow. That glow comes partly from its workshops. Cala Dora is an island of tinsmiths and lantern-makers, who cut, hammer, and solder sheet tin and brass into ship's lanterns, signal lamps, cooking pots, cups, plates, and every other small metal good a sailor needs. Their lanterns hang in ships, taverns, and houses across the southern [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]]. At night, every doorway on the island is lit by one of its own.
