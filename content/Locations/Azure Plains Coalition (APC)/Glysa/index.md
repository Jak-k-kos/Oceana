# Glysa
Glysa is a warm, sweet island of vineyards, though unlike those of [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Dalmora/index|Dalmora]], its vines are grown for eating rather than for wine. Its slopes are terraced from shore to summit in table grapes, purple and gold, and in late summer the whole island smells of ripe fruit and honey. Most of the harvest is dried into raisins on wide reed mats, or boiled down in copper pans into a thick, dark grape syrup that sweetens the bread, porridge and puddings of half the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]] at a fraction of the cost of sugar. The young vine leaves are picked in spring and packed in brine for wrapping rice and meat, and whatever grapes are left become a plain, honest table wine. The people of Glysa are known for sweet tempers to match their syrup, and they will press a handful of raisins on any visitor who lingers long enough.
