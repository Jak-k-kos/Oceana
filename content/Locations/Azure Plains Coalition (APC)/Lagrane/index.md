# Lagrane
Lagrane is a small, fortified island in the far southwest of the frontier, and it is the frontier's granary. Its fields grow barley and rye, and behind its stone walls stands a vast stone granary, older than anyone remembers, where the grain of the southwestern frontier is stored against siege, blockade and famine. When a frontier island is cut off, it is Lagrane's grain that keeps it alive, and so the granary is guarded like a treasury, its tallies kept to the last sack and its walls manned day and night. The people of Lagrane are dour, careful and suspicious of strangers, and they count everything twice.
