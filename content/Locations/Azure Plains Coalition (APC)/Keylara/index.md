# Keylara
Keylara is a green, rolling island whose hills are wooded with old cork oaks, their trunks stripped every nine years to a deep rust-red and slowly growing back. The cork is cut into bottle stoppers, net floats, buoys, and the cork jackets that keep sailors afloat when their ships do not. Between the groves, sheep graze the open hillsides, and the island's weaving houses turn their wool into warm, heavy cloth for coats, cloaks, and sailors' blankets across the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]]. Keylara is quiet, prosperous, and a little sleepy, and its people are proud that half the coalition's sailors owe their lives to its cork.
