# Nikalsa
Nikalsa is a small, rugged island with a mystery at its heart: gold. Generations ago its people sank a mine into the hills in search of the vein that must be feeding their streams, and they never found it. The mine stands abandoned now. The gold comes instead from a natural spring at the centre of the island, which bubbles up from somewhere deep and cold. Its water carries far more gold than any spring has a right to. The islanders pan its pools and the stream below it day and night, and the yield is steady and surprisingly rich. Nearly all of it goes under armed guard to the mint on [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Ostrakia/index|Ostrakia]], which strikes it into the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition's]] highest coins. Nikalsa is watched closely, guarded well, and treated by everyone with a mix of envy and suspicion.
