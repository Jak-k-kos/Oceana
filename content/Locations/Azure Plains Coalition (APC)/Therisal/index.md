# Therisal
Therisal is a great, rambling island of green hills and rich valleys, and its coast is cut by four great bays. Each bay shelters a busy port town, which makes Therisal one of the best-harboured islands in the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]]. Its hills are planted in mulberry groves, and its silk houses are full of trays of feeding silkworms. Its weavers turn the thread into fine cloth and silk rope that the wealthy of the coalition and the [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/index|Kingdom]] pay dearly for. The valleys between are some of the widest farmland in the south: wheat, barley, and vegetables in long golden fields, and fat cattle in the meadows. Therisal feeds a great many of its neighbours. Its merchants trade through all four harbors, and much of what they sell passes through the markets of nearby [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Kalisanta/index|Kalisanta]]. The town of Therisal sits on the largest bay. It is a lively mix of silk merchants, farmers, sailors, and weavers, and on festival days its streets are hung with coloured silk from end to end.
