# Kalinst
Kalinst is a small, smoky island on the frontier, and from its foundry comes the iron that the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition's]] cannons spit at their enemies. Iron bar comes in from [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Insal Fargo/index|Insal Fargo]] and lead from [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Isla Embra/index|Isla Embra]], and in its long foundry, lined with firebrick from [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Maya Jalld/index|Maya Jalld]], the founders of Kalinst cast cannonballs by the ton, along with chain shot, grapeshot and the small lead balls for every musket and pistol in the southern coalition. Its stocks are kept close to the frontier on purpose, so that the southern squadron and the island guards never run short, and an island that makes shot on the [[Locations/The Black Tide/index|Black Tide]] border is a prize worth raiding. The foundry stands behind thick walls with cannon of its own upon them, and more than one pirate fleet has learned that Kalinst fires back. The furnaces run on kelp charcoal and never quite go cold, and the whole island is stained a little grey by their smoke.
