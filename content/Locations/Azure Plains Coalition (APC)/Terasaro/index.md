# Terasaro
Terasaro is a steep rock barely two miles across, and its cliffs are white with tens of thousands of nesting seabirds. The few families who live here climb the cliffs on ropes to gather eggs in the spring and scrape the guano from the ledges the rest of the year. They bag the guano and sell it as fertilizer to farming islands across the northwest. With so little land to go around, good fertilizer is worth paying for, and Terasaro sells all the guano it can gather. The island stinks, and the people who live on it stopped noticing long ago.
