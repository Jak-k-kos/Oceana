# Palsna
Palsna is a small, scrubby island that belongs, as far as anyone can tell, to its chickens. They scratch in every yard, roost on every wall, and wander the roads in squawking flocks. The poultry families of Palsna send their birds across the southeastern [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]], live in wicker cages, fresh-killed, or salted down in barrels, and their eggs go out by the basket to every galley and kitchen within a day's sail. The roosters of Palsna are famously loud, and sailors joke that they can hear the island coming before they see it, though that is not entirely true.
