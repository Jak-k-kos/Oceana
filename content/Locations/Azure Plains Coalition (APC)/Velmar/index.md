# Velmar
Velmar is a tiny, flat island almost entirely given over to salt pans. Seawater is let into shallow basins and left for the sun to take, and the salt is raked up white and glittering. It goes to the fish-curers and kitchens of the southern islands, which find it far easier to buy salt here than to sail it all the way from the north. The few families on Velmar work the pans from spring to autumn and spend the winter mending their dikes.
