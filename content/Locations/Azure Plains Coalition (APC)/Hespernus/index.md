# Hespernus
Hespernus is a small, plain island on the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition's]] northern edge whose people make an honest living from the sea. Its fishing fleet works the cold northern waters, and its rendering sheds boil fish and the occasional whale into oil that sells across the islands. It is known for one thing above all else: its evening bells. Every night, as the sun touches the sea, its bell-ringers ring a peal that carries across the northern islands, and fishermen on the water stop to listen. Since the great bell cracked last winter, the evening peal sounds wrong, and the islanders wince every night.
