# DM Notes
- Leader: [[Rosa Ventura]]
- Food: Coconut rice, grilled fish
- Trade: Sailcloth
- extra: Map #5. Size: Small. Government: Village elder. The elder's knees predict storms with uncanny accuracy. Captains pay to consult her before long voyages, and she gives the money to the weavers' widows. Some islanders think she's touched by [[Welestia]]; she thinks that's nonsense.
%%
Isla Brisa is the windiest island in the north, and its people have made the most of it. Dozens of small windmills crowd its ridges, their canvas sails turning day and night to drive the looms below. The island weaves the coalition's best sailcloth: tight, light, and stubborn against tearing. The village is low, built behind stone windbreaks, and every doorway has a little bell that never stops ringing. Visitors find it noisy and charming; islanders can't sleep anywhere quiet.
