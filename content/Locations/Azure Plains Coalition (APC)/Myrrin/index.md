# Myrrin
Myrrin is a long, bare island, and its people do not light fires. Its workshops lie scattered far apart, each one sunk half into the earth and roofed with turf, so that if one should ever go up in smoke it takes none of the others with it. This is where the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition's]] ships get the things that burn and break. The powder-wrights of Myrrin take gunpowder from [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Brisaulna/index|Brisaulna]] and pack it into mortar shells, burst shells, smoke bombs, signal flares and naval mines, and they mix the fire sand and salamander fuel that the coalition's gunners fear and love in equal measure. A bell hangs at every workshop door, and the people of Myrrin speak softly, move slowly, and count their fingers every evening with a certain grim good humour.
