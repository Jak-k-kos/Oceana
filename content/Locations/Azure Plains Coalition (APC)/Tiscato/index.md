# Tiscato
Tiscato is a small, pale island that looks from the sea as though it has been dusted with flour, for its hills are made of fine white clay, soft as soap. Dug from long pits and dried in the sun, it becomes the fuller's earth that the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition's]] wool and cloth depend upon. The weavers of [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Keylara/index|Keylara]] and [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Halk/index|Halk]] scour their cloth with it to draw out the grease, soap-boilers mix it into their soaps, and every housewife and ship's steward in the coalition keeps a crock of it for lifting stains from canvas and linen. The finest white clay is set aside for Tiscato's pipe-makers, who roll and fire it into the long clay pipes that every sailor, dockhand and grandmother smokes of an evening. The whole island is white: the roads, the houses, and the boots of everyone who walks them.
