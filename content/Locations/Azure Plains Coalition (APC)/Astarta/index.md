# Astarta
Astarta is a round, green island, and from dawn to dusk it rings like a bell, for it is the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition's]] island of coopers. Its workshops crowd the harbor, hammers driving hoops onto barrels from first light until the lamps are lit, and its oak casks carry everything wet that the coalition eats and drinks: ships' water, salt fish, wine and brandy. Timber is far too precious to waste, so the staves are bought from the forests of [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Corvealna/index|Corvealna]] and [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Theronada/index|Theronada]] and shaved by hand, and the iron hoops come by the cartload from [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Insal Fargo/index|Insal Fargo]]. Nothing is thrown away. The splinters and offcuts too small for anything else come by the shipload from the breaking yards of [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Marsal/index|Marsal]], and in long workshops behind the harbor they are shaved thin, glued in crossed layers, and pressed into sheets of plywood. From these come Astarta's cheaper barrels, light and sturdy, for flour, biscuit, beans and powder, and anything else that must be kept dry, while the sheets themselves are sold for crates, sea chests and cabinets to anyone who cannot afford good timber. [[Old Tobiah|Old]] casks come home to Astarta to be broken down and built again, and some of the island's barrels are older than their owners. Every cask is branded with its maker's mark, and a good Astarta mark is trusted in every port of the coalition.
