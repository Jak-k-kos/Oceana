# Salitra
Salitra is a small, rugged island riddled with caves, and the caves are full of bats. For centuries their droppings have piled up in the dark, and the people of Salitra dig them out and leach them in great stone vats until the saltpeter crystallises, white and bitter. That saltpeter goes north to the powder mills of [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Brisaulna/index|Brisaulna]] and [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Myrrin/index|Myrrin]], and what is left of the guano feeds the fields of half the frontier. It is also exactly what the [[Locations/The Black Tide/index|Black Tide]] needs to fill its own powder barrels, and so Salitra has been raided, blockaded and besieged more times than anyone can count. The town is walled from shore to shore, its cisterns are always full and its storehouses always stocked, every islander over twelve can load a musket, and the very caves that make the island rich have sheltered its people through more sieges than its oldest grandmothers can remember. Supply ships run in beneath the guns of the southern squadron, and the people of Salitra are grim, stubborn, and very hard to frighten.
