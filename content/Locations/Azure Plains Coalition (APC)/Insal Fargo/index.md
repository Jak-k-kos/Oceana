# Insal Fargo
Insal Fargo is a broad, smoky island near the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition's]] northern edge where ore becomes metal. It has an old iron mine of its own. Its rich seams were worked out generations ago, and it still gives up enough to feed a furnace or two. Most of the ore comes in by ship from [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Phaedra/index|Phaedra]], and kelp charcoal from [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Aquillis Marin/index|Aquillis Marin]]. Just as much of the island's living comes from scrap. Broken anchors, burst cannon, old nails, and rusted fittings come in from every island in the region, and Insal Fargo melts them down and sends them back out as good metal. Along the shore, tall stone furnaces roar day and night, turning rock and rubbish into bars of iron, copper, and steel for the smiths, foundries, and shipyards of the coalition. Smoke hangs over the island like a second sky, and the glow of the furnaces lights the clouds at night. Fishermen use it as a lighthouse.
