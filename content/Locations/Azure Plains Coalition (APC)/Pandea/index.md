# Pandea
Pandea sits on the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition's]] far eastern corner, and it is the main door for trade from the eastern islands of the [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/index|Kingdom]]. [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Ledas/index|Ledas]] lies closer to them, but Ledas forges the coalition's armour and is kept under tight lock and key, so Kingdom merchants make the longer journey to Pandea instead. Its harbor is the busiest legal market on the eastern border, where Kingdom traders buy coalition coffee, kelp charcoal and silk and sell Kingdom wine, wool and steel under coalition law and in the open air, every day of the year but the [[Night of Names]]. Pandea feeds itself and its visitors from its eel weirs, and its smoked eel is sold on both sides of the border. Coalition and Kingdom accents mingle in its taverns, and no one asks too many questions about where a man is from, only whether his coin is good.
