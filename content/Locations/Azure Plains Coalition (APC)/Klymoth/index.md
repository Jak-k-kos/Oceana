# Klymoth
Klymoth is a small, dark island whose hills are threaded with seams of lodestone, the black stone that pulls iron to it as if by magic. Its people mine the lodestone and use it to magnetize compass needles. In the island's workshops, careful hands turn brass, glass, and lodestone into compasses, spyglasses, and navigators' instruments for the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition's]] fleets. Navigators across the coalition swear by a Klymoth instrument, and a good one is handed down from captain to captain.
