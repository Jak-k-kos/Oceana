# Allum Crya
Allum Crya is a tiny, pale rock made of a soft white stone that the islanders roast, soak, and boil down into alum crystals. Dyers need alum to fix their colours, tanners use it to cure leather, and healers use it to stop bleeding. A small island of alum-works supplies a surprising share of the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]]. The works fill the air with steam and a sharp, sour smell, and the island's few families are pale with stone dust.
