# Alverde
Alverde is a small, low island ringed by shallow, sunlit lagoons, and those lagoons are planted like gardens. Its people farm seaweed on long ropes strung between stakes: kelp, sea lettuce, and the fine red weeds that boil down into a clear, firm jelly. The harvest is dried on racks across every roof and beach. It is sold across the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]] as dried kelp for soups and sailors' rations, and as cakes of sea jelly that cooks set their sweets with and apothecaries use for their salves. At low tide, the whole island seems to wear a skirt of green and red.
