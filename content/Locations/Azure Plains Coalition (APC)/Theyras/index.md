# Theyras
Theyras is a good-sized, sunbaked island whose terraces blaze red and green with peppers. Black pepper vines climb poles on the shaded slopes. The fierce little firecap peppers grow on the sunny side, so hot that pickers wear gloves and wrap their faces. The peppers are dried on the rooftops, ground in stone mills, and shipped across the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]] to cooks, ship's galleys, and apothecaries. Peppers are the island's fortune, but Theyras has room to spare, and its lower fields grow onions, tomatoes, squash, and beans that fill the market boats of the neighbouring islands. The whole island tingles, and visitors sneeze for days.
