# Escal
Escal lies so close to the [[Locations/The Widow's Wrath/index|Widow's Wrath]] that the storm spills over it again and again, and lightning strikes its dunes more often than rain falls on most islands. Its people live low, in stone houses sunk half into the ground with iron rods on every roof to draw the strikes away, and the lightning is their living. Wherever it strikes the sand, it fuses it into twisted veins and beads of glass, and the waves tumble the pieces smooth into storm glass: pale and frosted, shot through with strange colours, and found nowhere else in the world. Jewelers and collectors across the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]] and beyond pay handsomely for it. A current that runs out of the heart of the Wrath carries the shattered glass of a thousand wrecks to Escal's shores as well, so ordinary sea glass washes up by the basketful, and now and then the storm lets go of a ship's broken carcass, which the islanders row out to strip of timber and iron before the current carries it away.
