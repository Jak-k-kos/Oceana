# Kyras Este
Kyras Este is the smaller, richer half of [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Kyras/index|Kyras]]. Its town is a tumble of whitewashed houses with blue shutters, and its warehouses smell permanently of oranges. The grove owners' villas line the ridge above town, and the council hall overlooks the channel so Este's councilors can keep an eye on [[Kyras Oeste|Oeste]].
