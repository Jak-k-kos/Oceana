# Kyras
Kyras is two small islands across a narrow channel on the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition's]] northern edge, where cold water wells up from the deep and the kelp grows thick and tall. The forests beneath its channel are among the richest in the north, and Kyras burns more kelp charcoal than any island in the coalition. Its fuel feeds hearths and forges, smithies and shipyards and the rare steamships, and the [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/index|Kingdom]] buys every sack it can get. Kyras [[Kyras Este|Este]], the eastern island, has the harbor, the kilns and the money. Kyras [[Kyras Oeste|Oeste]], to the west, has most of the kelp beds and most of the cutters. Each island keeps its own council, and the two agree on almost nothing. A single mayor, rowed back and forth across the channel every day, tries to hold them together. The one day both councils sit at the same table is [[Apiot's Boon]]. The first-cut kelp is offered to the [[Apiot|Green Mother]], and then the flat cutting boats of both isles race across the channel. The losing isle's council must serve the winners at the feast, and neither side has ever done it graciously.
