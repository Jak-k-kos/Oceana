- extra: Map #11. Size: Medium. Islands: Kyras [[Kyras Este|Este]] & Kyras [[Kyras Oeste|Oeste]]. Government: Twin councils, one per island, with a shared mayor. # Kyras
Kyras is two small islands across a narrow channel on the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition's]] northern edge, both covered in citrus groves. In spring the scent of orange blossom drifts miles out to sea, and sailors say you could find Kyras blindfolded. Kyras [[Kyras Este|Este]], the eastern island, has the harbor and the money. Kyras [[Kyras Oeste|Oeste]], to the west, has most of the groves and most of the workers. Each island keeps its own council, and the two agree on almost nothing. A single mayor, rowed back and forth across the channel every day, tries to hold them together. The one day both councils sit at the same table is [[Apiot's Boon]], when the first lemons are offered to the [[Apiot|Green Mother]] and the feast ends in a lemon fight that has broken more than one nose.
