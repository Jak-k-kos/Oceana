# Kyras Oeste
Kyras Oeste is greener, poorer, and prouder than its sister. Its groves cover nearly every hill, and its villages are full of pickers, pressers, and the peel-candiers who make [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Kyras/index|Kyras's]] famous sweets. Oeste's people work hard, drink bitter-orange liqueur, and complain loudly about [[Kyras Este|Este]].
