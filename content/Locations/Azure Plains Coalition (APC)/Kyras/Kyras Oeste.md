# Kyras Oeste
Kyras Oeste is wetter, poorer, and prouder than its sister. Its coves are crowded with flat cutting boats, its slopes with drying racks, and its villages with cutters and haulers. Oeste's people work hard, drink rough barley spirit, and complain loudly about [[Kyras Este|Este]].
