# Akatano
Akatano is a small, quiet island in the southwestern [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]], and one of the only places outside the [[Locations/The Draconic Conclave/index|Draconic Conclave]] where [[Koruk]] is openly worshipped. A few families of dragonborn and kobolds settled here generations ago, after leaving the Conclave behind, and raised a temple to the Wyrmfather on the island's high ground. The rest of the islanders came to see dragons as good luck, and over time the dragons came to see Akatano as safe. Faerie dragons and pseudodragons that slip out of the Conclave stop here to rest before heading out into the northern coalition, and now and then something larger comes to roost on the temple roof for a night. A dragon over the harbor is a blessing on Akatano: fishermen leave the best of the catch on the temple steps, and a child born on a night a dragon visits is said to be lucky for life. The village itself is small and simple, made up of fishermen, goatherds, and the temple families, who keep to their own traditions but have been part of the island for so long that nobody thinks of them as outsiders.
