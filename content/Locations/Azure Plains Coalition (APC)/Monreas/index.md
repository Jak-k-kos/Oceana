# Monreas
Monreas is a small, hilly island of dark, rich soil, and that soil grows potatoes: pale and purple, small and great, and sweet potatoes in the warm southern fields. Potatoes are filling and cheap, they grow where wheat will not, and kept cool and dark they last for months in a ship's hold, so Monreas sends them out by the sackful to every larder and galley in the southeast. Its farming families plant and dig the steep terraces, and in autumn the whole island turns out for the lifting, children and grandparents alike. What potatoes cannot be sold are fed to the island's pigs or distilled into a rough, clear potato spirit that the farmers swear is medicinal. No one else believes them, but everyone drinks it.
