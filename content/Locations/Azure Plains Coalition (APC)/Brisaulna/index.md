# Brisaulna
Brisaulna is a small, windswept island with dozens of white windmills crowding its ridges, their canvas sails turning almost all year. The wind here barely stops, and the island's mills grind and pulp whatever neighbouring islands send them: grain into flour, olives into paste, bark for tanners, pigment for dyers, and anything else that needs milling on an island too crowded to build its own. Flour is their largest trade. The second is gunpowder. A few dozen mills on the far end of the island are set aside for it, grinding sulfur and saltpeter from other islands into most of the powder the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]] uses. Nobody lives near that end.
