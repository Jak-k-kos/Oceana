# Korlia
Korlia is a round, hilly island whose slopes are terraced from the shore almost to the summit with old olive groves. Its presses make a heavy green oil that sells across the northwest. Divers work the reef for red coral, which the island's carvers turn into beads and charms, but olives are what Korlia lives on. Because it's the closest [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]] port to the [[Locations/The Draconic Conclave/index|Draconic Conclave]], it also sees a steady trickle of kobold and dragonborn trading ships coming to buy oil, wine, and cloth. That gives Korlia's harbor a stranger crowd than most islands its size, and the Korlians have grown used to scaled faces on their docks.
