# Atalyana
Atalyana is a long, low island in the southwestern [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]], and one of the busiest fishing ports in the region. Its fleet follows the great tuna runs that sweep through the southern waters every summer, and when the runs come in, the whole island turns out to meet them. The harbor fills with boats, the beaches fill with gutting tables, and the smokehouses along the shore burn day and night, packing smoked and salted tuna into jars and barrels that sell across the coalition. The rest of the year, Atalyana mends nets, repairs boats, and waits. The town of Atalyana smells of smoke and salt in every season, and its people are rough, cheerful, and superstitious, as fishing people tend to be.
