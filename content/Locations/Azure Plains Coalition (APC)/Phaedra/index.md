# Phaedra
Phaedra is a long, red-brown island about twelve miles across, and almost all of it is mine. Copper and iron have been dug here for centuries, and the pits, spoil heaps, and shaft-houses cover the island from end to end, leaving room only for the town and the docks. Phaedra doesn't make much from its metal. It ships ore to smiths, foundries, and the mint on [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Ostrakia/index|Ostrakia]], which buys most of its copper. The best seams now lie far below sea level, and the deep shafts flood constantly. Mule wheels and treadmills run the pumps day and night, and if they stop the lowest levels fill within hours. The water around the harbor runs green after rain, and the miners' cough is common.
