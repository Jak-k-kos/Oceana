# Ixia
Ixia is a small, rounded island whose terraces turn purple every autumn with saffron crocus. Each flower gives only three red threads, and the whole island turns out in the harvest weeks to pick them by hand before dawn. The threads are then dried over charcoal in the drying houses. Saffron is worth more than silver by weight, so a few acres keep the whole island comfortable. Ixians sell it to cooks, dyers, and healers across the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]]. The rest of the year, the island grows beans and keeps goats, and the drying houses are locked.
