# Ixia
Ixia is a small, rounded island whose terraces turn purple every autumn with saffron crocus. Each flower gives only three red threads, and the whole island turns out in the harvest weeks to pick them by hand before dawn. The threads are then dried over charcoal in the drying houses. Saffron is worth more than silver by weight, so a few acres keep the whole island comfortable. Ixians sell it to cooks, dyers, and healers across the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]]. Saffron alone could never feed the island, though, and the slopes too poor for crocus are planted in safflower, a hardy thistle with flowers of yellow and orange. Its seeds are pressed into a cheap, clean oil for cooking and lamps, and its petals give a bright yellow dye and a poor man's saffron that colours rice in every humble kitchen of the coalition. The rest of the year, the island grows beans and keeps goats, and the saffron drying houses stay locked.
