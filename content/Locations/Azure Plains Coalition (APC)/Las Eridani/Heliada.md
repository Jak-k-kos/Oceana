# Heliada
Heliada is a tiny, sun-scorched rock east of [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Las Eridani/index|Las Eridani]], too bare for anything to grow but lichen and dry grass, and it belongs to [[Morvaine]]. On its summit stands the greatest of her temples in all the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]], a long, low hall of grey stone, and every night a fire burns in an iron basket on its roof, so that the house of the dead also lights the grain ships safely into the bay. Young gravesmen from every corner of the coalition come here to learn their trade when there is no gravesman on their own island to apprentice under, and then sail out again to wherever they are needed. The acolytes come and go across the bay as their errands take them, but the high gravesman and the senior archivists seldom set foot off the rock, and some have not left it in years.

Beneath the hall lies the Master Archive, row upon row of ledgers in dry stone vaults. Every twenty-five years, the gravesman of each island sends a copy of their Book of Names to Heliada, and the archivists copy every name into the great ledgers by lamplight, year after year, so that even if an island's own book is lost to fire or flood, its dead will not be. The gravesmen say there is no larger record of the dead anywhere in the world, save Morvaine's own memory.
