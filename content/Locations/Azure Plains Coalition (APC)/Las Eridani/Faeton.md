# Faeton
Faeton is the little rocky islet off the western arm of [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Las Eridani/index|Las Eridani]], and its crooked harbor shelters the island's fishing boats from the worst of the weather. Its fishermen keep the valley supplied with fish in exchange for bread and cider, and on a calm evening their lanterns bob across the water like a second set of stars.
