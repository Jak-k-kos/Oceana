# Cygna
Cygna is a low, marshy islet at the mouth of the bay of [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Las Eridani/index|Las Eridani]], where the river's silt has gathered into beds of tall reeds. Every spring the wild white swans come there to nest, and the islanders, who believe the swans bring good harvests, never touch them or their eggs. Only when the swans have flown do the reed-cutters of Cygna go to work, harvesting the reeds for thatch and weaving them into the baskets the valley's farmers fill with apples and onions.
