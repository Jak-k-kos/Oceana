# Aurelia
Aurelia takes its name from its beaches, which glow golden in the morning light, and that sand is fine and pale and melts into the clearest glass in the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]]. Where the black glass of [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Melanta/index|Melanta]] is prized for its beauty, Aurelia's clear glass is prized for its use. Its glasshouses burn kelp charcoal from [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Aquillis Marin/index|Aquillis Marin]] and flux their sand with kelp ash from [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Mirada/index|Mirada]], and its glassblowers turn out bottles for brandy and vinegar, jars for preserves and pickles, vials for every apothecary in the coalition, window panes, lamp chimneys, and the delicate hourglasses that turn on every ship's deck to keep the watches. At night the furnaces glow through the glasshouse windows like a hundred small suns, and the island earns its name all over again.
