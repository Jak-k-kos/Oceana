# Belna
Belna is a long, thin sliver of an island, green from end to end, and it sits where the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition's]] waters meet the routes to the western raft cities of the [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/index|Kingdom]], which makes it the chief trading port between the two. Kingdom merchants come to Belna to trade under coalition law, and Belna grows fat on the customs it collects. Its narrow fields run in strips down to the water, planted in melons and watermelons, cucumbers, garlic and long rows of green beans, and what cannot be sold fresh is pickled in great crocks for long voyages. Its shipwrights mend the hulls of visiting merchantmen with lumber from neighbouring [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Insul/index|Insul]], and the cargo waiting to move in either direction is kept in the warehouses of [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Alhondra/index|Alhondra]], just across the channel.
