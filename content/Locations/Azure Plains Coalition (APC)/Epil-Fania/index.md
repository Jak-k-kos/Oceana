# Epil-Fania
Epil-Fania is a round, hilly island whose sheltered southern bay is lined with slipways and timber sheds. Its yards build most of the fishing boats, sloops, and small merchant ships in the northwest and repair a good share of the rest. Timber comes in from whichever islands are selling, sailcloth from [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Isla Brisa/index|Isla Brisa]], rope from wherever it's cheapest, and varnish from [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Isla Lentsco/index|Isla Lentsco]], and finished hulls go out. The hill farms behind the yards grow barley and keep pigs to feed the shipwrights. The island's great holiday is Launch Day at the end of winter, when everything built over the cold months goes into the water at once. Every hull is blessed with wine and fire, and the whole island gets drunk.
