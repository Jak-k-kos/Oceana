# Kalisanta
Kalisanta is a tiny island in the middle of the northwest, and almost every foot of it is wharf, warehouse, or counting house. It has no farms and grows nothing, and every mouthful of food is shipped in. What it has is a deep harbor in the centre of the region's sea lanes and centuries of trade. It is a free port where any [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]] ship can land goods without harbor duty, and the merchant houses based here buy, store, and resell the goods of the whole northwest: [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Dalmora/index|Dalmora]] wine, [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Dolsa Isla/index|Dolsa Isla]] rum, [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Korlia/index|Korlia]] oil, [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Delafina/index|Delafina]] roe, and [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Phaedra/index|Phaedra]] ore. The oil houses also refine fish oil, whale oil, and olive oil into lamp oil, and boil the dregs into hard olive-oil soap. The houses run the island through the Exchange, a pillared hall on the waterfront where prices are cried every morning.
