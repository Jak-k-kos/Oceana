# Lycrasto
Lycrasto is a towering island of sheer cliffs on the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition's]] northern edge, riddled with vast sea caves where hundreds of thousands of seabirds roost. For centuries those birds have left their droppings in the caves, layer upon layer, and the islanders mine it. Scraped from the cave floors and leached in great vats, it becomes saltpeter, the heart of gunpowder, and Lycrasto supplies a good share of the coalition's cannons with it. The work is dangerous. Miners are lowered into the caves on ropes, the air inside is choking, and every family has lost someone to a fall. The islanders also gather eggs and down from the cliffs on the side. Lycrastans are tough, quiet people who smell faintly of the caves no matter how often they wash.
