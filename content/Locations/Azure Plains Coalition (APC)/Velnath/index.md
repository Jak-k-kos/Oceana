# Velnath
Velnath is a quiet, misty island whose people live by the sea and the anvil. Its fishing boats work the cold banks offshore for cod and hake, which are salted and dried on racks along every wall in town, and its smithies take iron from [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Insal Fargo/index|Insal Fargo]] and turn out the small ironwork the southeastern islands need every day: nails and fish-hooks, hinges and hasps, knives and tools, and the iron fittings of sea chests and cargo holds. Yet Velnath is famous across the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]] for one thing above all, and that is its locks. Its master locksmiths guard their designs jealously, and their padlocks, strongroom locks and manacles are bought by archons, merchants and prison wardens alike, for a Velnath lock is said to be the one thing a thief in the coalition truly fears. The island is a hushed place, full of the tick and click of tiny files and springs, and its people are reputed to keep secrets as well as they keep doors.
