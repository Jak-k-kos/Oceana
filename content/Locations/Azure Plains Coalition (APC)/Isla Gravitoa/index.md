# Isla Gravitoa
Isla Gravitoa is a tiny, steep rock that sits over a shallow bank where squid, octopus, and cuttlefish gather every spring. The few families here catch them in baited pots, dry the meat, and draw off their ink. Squid and octopus ink make the everyday ink sold to clerks and ledger-keepers. Cuttlefish ink becomes sepia, the rich brown ink that mapmakers, scribes, and artists pay well for. A little drying house and a stone ink-works are nearly all there is to the island.
