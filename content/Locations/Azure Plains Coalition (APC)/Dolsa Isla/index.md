# Dolsa Isla
Dolsa Isla is green and humid, and its lower slopes are covered in sugar cane. During the harvest the island smells of boiling syrup, and the stone mills grind day and night. The cane is pressed and boiled into molasses, and most of the molasses goes into the island's rum houses. Dolsa rum is cheap, strong, and drunk in every port in the northwest. The mill families run the island, and they compete with each other as much as they cooperate. Above the cane fields, the mountain is still covered in old forest sacred to [[Apiot]], and the island's priests tend shrines there.
