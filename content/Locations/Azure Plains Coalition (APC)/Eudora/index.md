# Eudora
Eudora is a small, low, green island of market gardens and orchards that grows food for the islands that can't feed themselves. Its two villages fill boats every week with onions, garlic, cabbages, beans, eggs, and chickens, and send most of them to [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Kalisanta/index|Kalisanta]] and [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Phaedra/index|Phaedra]]. It isn't a rich island, but it is a busy one, and Eudoran farmers can always find a buyer.
