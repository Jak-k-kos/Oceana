# Cleia Medusca
Cleia Medusca is a tiny rock in the middle of great seasonal blooms of jellyfish, which turn the water around it pale and glassy for months at a time. The islanders net them from flat-bottomed boats, then salt and press them into thin, translucent sheets called glassleaf. Glassleaf keeps for a year and is eaten across the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]] as cheap, chewy food in soups and salads. The blooms also draw shoals of fish and sea turtles, so the island's few families eat well. Swimming is forbidden, and everyone on Cleia Medusca carries a few faded welts from careless days.
