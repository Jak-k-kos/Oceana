# Melanta
Melanta's beaches are black, made of fine volcanic sand that glitters in the sun, and its people have spent generations turning that sand into glass. Furnaces line the shore, chimneys trailing smoke, and the glassblowers work in open-sided sheds where visitors can watch. Melanta glass is dark and smoky, made into bottles, beads, lamp chimneys, and the window glass of rich homes across the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]]. At night the furnaces glow along the beach like a string of red lanterns. The island is hot, busy, and covered in a fine layer of black grit that gets into everything.
