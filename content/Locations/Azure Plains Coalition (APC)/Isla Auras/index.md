# Isla Auras
Isla Auras is a small, warm island where the smell of baking never leaves the air. Its long brick ovens burn day and night, baking ship's biscuit by the barrel: the hard, dry, square ration that keeps for a year and feeds every sailor at sea. Flour comes in from the grain islands nearby, and biscuit goes out to the fleets, merchantmen, and warships of the southern [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]]. Bakers on Isla Auras are proud, floury, and loud, and they will tell you that the coalition sails on their biscuit.
