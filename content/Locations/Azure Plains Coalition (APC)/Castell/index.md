# Castell
Castell was a hilltop before the flood, and an old castle still crowns its northern crag, grey and battered but never once taken. The Almenara family have been its castellans since before the water rose, and the island has grown up around them. Its two lobes are joined by a narrow neck of land, and the southern lobe is green with fields of wheat and barley and with oak woods where great herds of pigs root fat on acorns. Castell's pork, hams and sausages fill larders and ships' stores across the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]]. Beneath the castle an old mine still yields a little wealth in garnets, agates and the odd amethyst, polished by the island's jewelers and sold at a handsome price. Off the southern shore, the little islet of [[Torreta]] bears a lighthouse tower that has burned every night for a thousand years.
