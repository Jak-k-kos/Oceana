# Torreta
Torreta is a small stone islet off the southern shore of [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Castell/index|Castell]], crowned by a slender lighthouse tower as old as the castle itself. Its keepers have lit the lamp every night for a thousand years without missing one, and every ship in these waters knows its slow, steady light. The castellans pay for its oil, and keeping the Torreta light is counted among the highest honours the island can give.
