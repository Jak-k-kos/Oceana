# Escama
Escama is a narrow spur of rock off the northern shore of [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Drakosa/index|Drakosa]], crowned by a tall stone watchtower. Lookouts from the southern squadron keep watch there day and night over the northern and western approaches, and its beacon can carry a warning to the bay before a strange sail is even close.
