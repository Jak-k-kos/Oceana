# Drakosa
Drakosa is a great, broad island with its western side bitten out by a deep, round bay. From the sea the bay looks like an open jaw, its northern and southern headlands the teeth, and the islanders say that is how the island earned its name. That bay is one of the finest harbors in the south, and it is the home of the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]] army's southern squadron: sloops and frigates crowd its moorings, barracks and storehouses line the waterfront, and gun batteries crown both headlands at the bay's mouth. Beyond the harbor, the hills and valleys of Drakosa are some of the best farmland on the frontier, growing barley, millet, onions and beans and grazing goats and pigs, enough to feed the squadron and much of the frontier besides. The farmers and soldiers of Drakosa do not always love one another, but each knows full well that without the other, the frontier would starve or fall. A scatter of small islets rings the coast, and the largest of them, [[Escama]], stands off the northern shore, crowned by a watchtower.
