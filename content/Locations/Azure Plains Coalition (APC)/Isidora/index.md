# Isidora
Isidora is a small island of net-makers. In every doorway, under every awning, and along the quay, its people sit knotting nets: casting nets, drift nets, trawl nets, cargo nets, and boarding nets. They work them from linen and hemp thread bought from the islands to the north. Fishing fleets from across the southwest come to Isidora to buy new nets and have old ones mended, and the island keeps a small fleet of its own to test its work. Isidorans can knot a net blindfolded, and children learn the knots before they learn their letters.
