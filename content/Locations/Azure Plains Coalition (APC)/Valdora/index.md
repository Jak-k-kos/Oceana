# Valdora
Valdora is an island of soft, golden hills, and the hills are covered in sheep. Its shepherd families have grazed them there for longer than anyone remembers, and their mutton, fresh and salted, feeds ships' crews and island larders across the southern [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]], while their wool goes north to the weavers. In the greener valleys a few herds of cattle graze as well, kept for their milk rather than their meat; a cow on Valdora is butchered only when she is too old to give any more. The island's true treasure lies beneath its cliffs, which are honeycombed with cool sea caves. There the shepherds age their cheeses, sheep's milk and cow's milk alike, for months in the dark, each wheel sealed in wax. Valdora cheese is sharp and crumbly and keeps on the longest voyage, and sailors prize it above almost anything else in their stores.
