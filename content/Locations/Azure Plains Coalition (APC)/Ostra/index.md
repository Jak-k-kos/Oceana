# Ostra
Ostra is a small island of quiet lagoons where oysters and mussels are farmed on ropes and racks, feeding the southern islands fresh, smoked, and salted. Its true treasure lies deeper. In the lagoon beds grow great pen shells, which anchor themselves with fine golden threads. Ostran women dive for them, comb out the threads, and spin them into sea silk, the softest and rarest cloth in the islands, which shines like gold in the sun. Only a handful of Ostrans know the art, and a single scarf of sea silk can cost more than a ship.
