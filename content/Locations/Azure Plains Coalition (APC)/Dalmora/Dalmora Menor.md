# DM Notes
- Leader: [[Catalina Vintaros]] runs the Vintaros cellars; each family keeps its own caves
- Food: Cave-aged cheese, bread, wine
- Trade: Aged wine
- extra: The sea caves are a maze, and each family guards its own tunnels jealously. Some of the oldest caves were sealed generations ago, and no one remembers why.
%%
Dalmora Menor is a low, rocky island riddled with sea caves, and it is where Dalmoran wine goes to age. The caves stay cool and dark all year round, and each of the five families keeps its own maze of tunnels lined with casks, some of them older than anyone alive. Little lives on the surface besides the cellar-keepers' cottages, a small chapel to [[Apiot]], and the goats that crop the scrub. Visitors may tour the cellars by invitation only, and a bottle of old Menor reserve is one of the most valuable gifts in the coalition.
