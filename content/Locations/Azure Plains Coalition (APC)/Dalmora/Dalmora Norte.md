# DM Notes
- Leader: [[Gaspar Montalbán]]
- Food: Fish stew, hard bread, cheap wine
- Trade: Barrels, wine shipping
- extra: Norte is the Montalbán family's domain. Whoever controls its ships controls how Dalmoran wine reaches the rest of the world, which is why the other families court Gaspar so carefully.
%%
Dalmora Norte is the smallest of the three islands and the busiest. It holds the cooperages, where barrels are built and filled, and the moorings of Dalmora's wine fleet, which carries the islands' wine across the coalition. The air smells of sawdust and toasted oak, and the cooperages ring with hammers from dawn to dusk. Sailors waiting on their cargo fill the island's two rough taverns, and Norte has a reputation as the one place in Dalmora where a common sailor can get a drink without paying for the label.
