# DM Notes
- Leader: [[Aurelio Vintaros]]
- Food: Grilled fish, grape-leaf rolls, wine with everything

- extra: Map #9, 10. Size: Large. Islands: Dalmora Mayor, Dalmora Menor & Dalmora Norte. Government: Oligarchy: a council of the five vintner families, chaired by the richest. Teodora Gallis wants Aurelio's chair; Lydia Kouris holds the balance and enjoys it; young Nestor Arvelo is reckless and easily flattered; Gaspar Montalbán sides with whoever pays for his ships.

Dalmora is three islands and one great vineyard. Dalmora Mayor, the largest, is terraced from shore to summit with vines, and in late summer its hillsides turn purple with grapes. Across a narrow strait, Dalmora Menor is honeycombed with sea caves where the wine ages in the cool dark, and its cellars are said to hold vintages older than anyone alive. The little island of Dalmora Norte holds the cooperages and the wine fleet's moorings, where barrels are built, filled, and loaded. Five old vintner families have ruled the islands for as long as anyone remembers, and they treat politics like wine: patiently, with a great deal of tasting. Dalmoran red is drunk at every important table in the coalition, and Dalmora's celebration of [[Apiot's Boon]], when the first grapes are offered to the Green Mother and the old year's wine is poured freely for anyone who comes, is the best party in the north.
