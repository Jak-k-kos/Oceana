# DM Notes
- Leader: [[Aurelio Vintaros]] (as chair of the vintners' council)
- Food: Grilled fish, grape-leaf rolls, roast goat on feast days
- Trade: Grapes, wine
- extra: The five families' vineyards are divided by old stone walls, and boundary disputes are older than anyone alive. The council hall, the Casa del Consejo, sits on the summit so that no family's vines are higher than the council's.
%%
Dalmora Mayor is the largest of the three islands and the heart of the vineyards. From the harbor to the summit, its slopes are carved into terraces of vines, each walled section belonging to one of the five vintner families and marked with the family's colors on a painted post. The island's only town, Puerto Dalmora, crowds around the harbor below, full of taverns, tasting rooms, and the grand houses of the families. At the very top stands the Casa del Consejo, where the five families meet. In late summer the whole island turns out for the grape harvest, and the roads are jammed with carts and singing pickers.
