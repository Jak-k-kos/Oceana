# Kymaria
In early summer, Kymaria is blue. Its fields of flax flower all at once, and from the sea the island looks as though a piece of the sky has fallen onto the water. Kymaria is the island of oilskins: its people press the flax seed into linseed oil, boil it, and soak canvas and cloth in it until it turns stiff and gleaming and proof against any rain. The oiled cloth is hung on great wooden frames to dry, so that the whole island smells faintly of it, and is then cut and stitched into oilskin coats, hats, bags, rain canvas and chart cases, until nearly every sailor in the southern [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]] owes a dry back to Kymaria. Even the flax stalks are not wasted, but retted and sent north to the linen weavers of [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Halk/index|Halk]].
