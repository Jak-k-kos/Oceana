# Marsal
Marsal is a tiny island with a long, shelving beach where ships come to die. Wood is scarce in the islands, and an old hull is worth too much to let rot, so ships past saving are sailed or towed here to be broken up. Most of the pirate ships the council army takes from the [[Locations/The Black Tide/index|Black Tide]] end up here too. They are rarely worth the trouble of selling, so the army sends them to Marsal to be stripped, and each one is run up on the beach and broken, its black flag burned on the sand. The shipbreakers strip every hull down plank by plank and nail by nail. Sound timber goes to the shipyards and carpenters, iron and copper fittings go to the smelters of [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Insal Fargo/index|Insal Fargo]], and what's left of the wood goes for firewood. The beach is a graveyard of ribs and keels, and Marsal's people know the bones of every kind of ship that sails.
