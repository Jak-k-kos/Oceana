# Kastyaro
Kastyaro is a tiny rock crowned by an old stone fortress, and the fortress is now a foundry. Copper and iron ore come in by ship from [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Phaedra/index|Phaedra]], and kelp charcoal from [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Aquillis Marin/index|Aquillis Marin]]. Inside the thick walls the furnaces burn day and night, casting cannons, anchors, bells, and ship fittings for the islands around. The fortress walls hold the heat and the danger in, and the whole rock rings with hammering from dawn to dark.
