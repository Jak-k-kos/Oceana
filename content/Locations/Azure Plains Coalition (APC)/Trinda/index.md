# Trinda
Trinda is three low islands on the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition's]] northern edge, close enough together that they make one good stretch of farmland. Unlike the steep, rocky islands around them, the Trinda isles are flat and fertile, and nearly every inch of them is planted with wheat. Their grain feeds a good part of the northern coalition. The isles are linked by wooden platforms lashed together with rope: at low tide they rest on the sandbars and become a bridge, and at high tide they float, turning into a bobbing crosswalk that every child on Trinda can run across without getting wet. Each isle has an elder, and the three elders rule together from a round stone hall on the center isle. Through the long cool nights of Glas, the families of Trinda sit by their hearths weaving the year's straw into hats, baskets and little figures of every kind: sailors and sea dragons, fishwives and goats. Visitors buy the straw figures as luck charms for a new ship's hold, but far more of them end up in children's hands, and across the north, a Trinda straw doll is what a child finds waiting on their pillow when the cargo ships come home.
