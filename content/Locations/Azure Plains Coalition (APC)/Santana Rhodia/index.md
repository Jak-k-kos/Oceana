# Santana Rhodia
Santana Rhodia is a small, green island in the southwestern [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]], and its hillsides hum. Nearly every family on the island keeps bees, and the slopes are dotted with hives among the wild thyme and orange blossom. The islanders sell honey and beeswax across the southwestern coalition, and their candlemakers turn the wax into the candles that light temples, taverns, and ships' lanterns from island to island. The weeks before the [[Night of Names]] are the island's busiest of the year: families from all over the southwest sail in to buy the little lantern boats and wax candles they'll send out to sea with the names of their dead, and the harbor fills with mourners, buyers, and the smell of warm wax. The rest of the year, the town of Santana Rhodia is quiet, sweet-smelling, and a little sleepy.
