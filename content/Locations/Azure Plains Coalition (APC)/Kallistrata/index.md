# Kallistrata
Kallistrata is a great, ragged island whose coast folds inward into two deep, sheltered lagoons, still as glass and safe from any storm. Those lagoons hold the greatest shipyards in the southern [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]]. Their slipways ring with hammers, and frigates, merchantmen, and sloops rise from their keels in the shelter of the inland water. Around the yards grew a town of craftsmen. Ropewalks, sail lofts, and smithies crowd the shores, and Kallistrata's gunsmiths and bladesmiths turn out muskets, pistols, cutlasses, and boarding axes for the navies of the coalition. Beyond the yards, the island's rolling hills are farmed in olives and vines. Kallistrata is loud, busy, and smoky, and its people are proud that half the warships in the south were born here.
