# Cabo Letha
Cabo Letha is a small, hooked cape of rock, and it is almost always wrapped in fog. Beneath the fog lie great banks of shell and old dead reef, which the island's lime-burners dredge from flat boats and haul ashore by the boatload, and nearly the whole cape is given over to the work that follows. Heaps of shell stand in long grey rows across the slopes, the kiln yards smoke day and night, and in the warehouses by the harbor the finished lime is weighed, sacked and stacked to the rafters. It is the lime that binds every harbor wall and whitens every hill town in the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]], and the [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/index|Kingdom]] will buy all of it that anyone will sell. Between the heaps, the fog itself waters terraces of cabbages, kale and onions that thrive in the damp where nothing else will, and the islanders pickle them in great crocks and sell them to the ships that come for lime. Sailors give the cape a wide berth all the same. Ships go astray in its fog, and old sailors say that time runs strangely there, that men who stay too long forget what day it is, or where they were going. The lime-burners laugh at the stories, mostly.
