# Cabo Letha
Cabo Letha is a small, hooked cape of rock, and it is almost always wrapped in fog. Beneath the fog lie great beds of kelp, which the island's kelp-burners cut from flat boats and haul ashore by the boatload, and nearly the whole cape is given over to the work that follows. Racks of kelp stand drying in long grey rows across the slopes, the kiln yards smoke day and night, and in the warehouses by the harbor the finished kelp charcoal is weighed, sacked and stacked to the rafters. It is the fuel that warms the hearths of the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]] and drives its rare steamships, and the [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/index|Kingdom]] will buy all of it that anyone will sell. Between the racks, the fog itself waters terraces of cabbages, kale and onions that thrive in the damp where nothing else will, and the islanders pickle them in great crocks and sell them to the ships that come for charcoal. Sailors give the cape a wide berth all the same. Ships go astray in its fog, and old sailors say that time runs strangely there, that men who stay too long forget what day it is, or where they were going. The kelp-burners laugh at the stories, mostly.
