Korifas is a single sharp peak rising out of the northern chain, its slopes too steep for anything but goats and the hardy people who herd them. At its summit burns the Korifas beacon, a great iron fire basket that has been lit every night for two centuries to guide ships around the northern islands. To Korifans it is more than a lighthouse: it is [[Flaris]]'s lantern for the whole northern sea, and tending it is the island's prayer. The keeper's family speaks the evening blessing as the fire is lit, and every household keeps a coal from the beacon burning in its own hearth. A narrow path of switchbacks, worn smooth by generations of wood-carriers, climbs from the tiny harbor to the fire. The island's cheese is sharp, salty, and sold in wax-sealed wheels across the coalition. Korifans are quiet, stubborn people who measure time by the beacon: "since the fire was lit" is how they say forever.
