# Korifas
Korifas is a single sharp peak rising out of the northern chain, its slopes too steep for anything but goats. Its living lies below the waterline, where the cold sea is thick with kelp. The islanders cut it from small boats, dry it on the rocks, and bake it into charcoal in the kilns that crowd the tiny harbor, and Korifas charcoal warms hearths and feeds forges across the northwest. The best of every burning goes up the mountain. At its summit burns the Korifas beacon, a great iron fire basket that has been lit every night for two centuries to guide ships around the northern islands. To Korifans it is more than a lighthouse: it is [[Flaris|Flaris's]] lantern for the whole northern sea, and tending it is the island's prayer. The keeper's family speaks the evening blessing as the fire is lit, and every household keeps a coal from the beacon burning in its own hearth. A narrow path of switchbacks, worn smooth by generations of charcoal-carriers, climbs from the tiny harbor to the fire. The island's cheese is sharp, salty, and sold in wax-sealed wheels across the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]]. Korifans are quiet, stubborn people who measure time by the beacon: "since the fire was lit" is how they say forever.
