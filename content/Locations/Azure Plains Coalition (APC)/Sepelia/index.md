# Sepelia
Sepelia is a small, windswept island where the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition's]] frontier begins, and its people make the weapons that guard it. Timber is far too precious for the tall bows of old, so the bowyers of Sepelia build them the clever way: short composite bows of thin wood, horn from the cattle of [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Galtea/index|Galtea]] and sinew, glued and bound and curved until they strike as hard as any longbow. Its crossbow-makers fit steel prods to small, tough stocks, its fletchers turn the goose feathers of [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Athera/index|Athera]] into arrows and bolts by the thousand, and its smiths forge harpoons and the heavy bolts of the ship's ballista. Every crossbow on a coalition gun deck, and half the harpoons in the south, carry a Sepelia mark. Like every island on the [[Locations/The Black Tide/index|Black Tide]] border, Sepelia has weathered its share of raids, so its workshops stand inside a stout stone wall, and when the alarm bell rings, every islander takes up one of the bows they make. Between the workshops the islanders net fish from the reefs, and the children of Sepelia learn to shoot before they learn to swim.
