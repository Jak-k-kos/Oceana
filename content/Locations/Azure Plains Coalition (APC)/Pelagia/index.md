# DM Notes
- Leader: [[Marios Kalafatis]]
- Food: Grilled octopus with oregano, chickpea fritters, lobster on feast days
- Trade: Divers for hire (salvage, recovering lost anchors and cargo, scraping and patching hulls below the waterline), plus lobster and octopus from the deep traps
- extra: Map #1. Size: Medium. Government: Elected archon; the divers' guild holds half the council seats. Secret: below the deepest trap lines, the divers found the rooftops of a drowned town from before the flood, which they call Old Pelagia. The guild quietly salvages it (bronze, old coins, sealed jars) and sells the finds through middlemen in Kalisanta's markets. Since the flood stopped, the sea folk have started leaving warnings: cut lines, empty boats, and one diver brought back alive with a coral token in his mouth. The archon wants the salvage stopped before the sea folk do something worse. The guild refuses.

Pelagia sits alone on the far western edge of the coalition, a steep green hump of rock surrounded by deep, clear water. Its people are divers before they are anything else. Children learn to free-dive with a flat weighted stone before they can read, and a Pelagian who can't hold their breath through a slow count of two hundred is gently pitied. That skill is the island's living: Pelagian divers hire out across the coalition to recover lost anchors and sunken cargo, scrape and patch hulls below the waterline, and work the deep lobster and octopus traps no one else can reach. The harbor town of Kato Pelagia climbs the cliff in whitewashed steps, every doorway hung with a diving stone. Every spring the island hosts the Cold Tide Trials, named for one of [[Aquilla]]'s titles, where divers from across the coalition compete for depth and the honor of wearing the bronze diving stone for a year. Visitors are welcome, as long as they never whistle on the water.
