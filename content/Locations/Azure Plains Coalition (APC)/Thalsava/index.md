# DM Notes
- Leader: [[Phoebe Saltaro]]
- Food: Shellfish stew, flatbread
- Trade: Murex purple dye
- extra: Map #6. Size: Small. Government: Elected archon. The archon is lobbying the coalition council to make Thalsava purple the official coalition color, which would make her island rich. Rivals on other islands are trying to steal the dye recipe. The vats are also a good place to dissolve things that shouldn't be found.
%%
You smell Thalsava before you see it. The island's dye works crush tens of thousands of murex snails each season to make the deep purple dye the coalition is known for, and the stench carries for miles downwind. The dyers live in a village of purple-stained stone, and their hands, clothes, and even their hair are tinged violet. Thalsava purple is worth its weight in silver: archons, priests, and rich merchants across the islands wear it to show their rank. Visitors should stay upwind and never ask how the dye is made.
