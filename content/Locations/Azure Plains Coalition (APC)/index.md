# Azure Plains Coalition (APC)
The Azure Plains Coalition is not a nation, though outsiders often mistake it for one. It is a loose alliance of well over a hundred island city-states scattered across the northwestern shallows, each ruling itself in its own way. Some elect an archon, some bow to a hereditary don, some are ruled by a temple, a guild, or a council of old families, and a few are held by a single strong hand. What they share is a love of the sea, a stubborn pride in their own island, and a culture of whitewashed hill towns and old temples, sun-bright harbors, rum, and guitars in the plazas. Their faith is as varied as their people. [[Flaris]], [[Roktra]], [[Ruthala]], and [[Fennara]] are the most widely worshipped, but outside their own temples and shrines, few islanders pray to only one god. The coalition takes its name from the shallow turquoise waters between the islands, so bright and clear that sailors say you can see the old drowned plains shining below.

The coalition is older than the [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/index|Kingdom of Albryn]] as the world knows it today. More than fifteen hundred years ago, as the waters rose during [[Aquilla's Wrath]], a handful of island towns banded together against the sea folk and against the bandits who were quickly becoming pirates as the land disappeared beneath them. The alliance began small and grew, island by island, for centuries. It stopped growing only when the expanding [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/index|Kingdom]] reached its borders and there were no free city-states left to join.

# The Coalition Council
The coalition has no capital and no king. Its only shared body is the coalition council: nineteen councillors drawn from the most powerful and respected people in the islands, and by a custom older than anyone's memory, no island ever holds more than one seat. The council cannot tax an island, command its guards, or touch its laws. Its work is to keep the peace between the islands, keep the coalition army at sea, keep the black ships sailing, and see to the few laws that bind every member, and it pays for all of it from the ore of [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Isla Embra/index|Isla Embra]], its greatest source of wealth, and from the fees islands pay to have their quarrels heard. Even a corrupt council can do only so much harm, for the true strength of the coalition has always lain with the islands themselves, in their own guards and their own ships.

## The Councillors
Councillors are chosen by the council itself, and each serves a term of five years. The terms are staggered, so that every year a few seats come up for renewal. When a term ends, the sitting council votes on the seat: the holder keeps it unless a challenger wins an outright majority, and the holder never votes on their own seat. An empty seat is filled the same way.

## Sessions and Votes
The council meets twice a year, and every councillor must attend unless kept away by the gravest of reasons. The lesser session is held in the third week of Desca, when the winter seas make every crossing a hardship. The grand session is held in the second week of Calra, in the warmth of summer, timed so that every councillor may be home again for the [[Night of Names]]. Both are held in turn on one of the great islands or aboard a grand ship, and hosting a session is a great honour. When an emergency cannot wait, the council hears it by letter: each councillor sends a sealed and certified vote by council courier, and the same is done to confirm mediation panels between sessions. Thirteen votes cast, in person or by letter, are needed for any vote to count. Every vote is decided by simple majority unless the law says otherwise, and a tied vote always fails.

## Mediation
Every quarrel between islands must come to mediation before it can become a war. Five councillors are drawn by lot from those whose islands have no part in the dispute, and the council confirms them by simple majority; a councillor it rejects is replaced by a fresh draw, never by choice. The five decide by majority. An island that will not accept the ruling may appeal, but the full council will hear the appeal only if thirteen of the nineteen agree, and so most rulings stand.

## The High Admiral
Above the council's proceedings sits the High Admiral. The title is a relic of the coalition's first centuries, when the High Admirals led the founding fleets against the sea folk, and it long ago became the name of an office rather than a rank. Most High Admirals have come from the sea, but a few have been merchants or archons who never commanded a ship.

The High Admiral is the one office every island chooses. Once a decade, each member island casts a single vote. The votes are sealed and carried to the mint on [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Ostrakia/index|Ostrakia]] to be counted, and if no one wins a majority, the two strongest face one another in a second round. Should that round end tied, the council decides between them by majority, and should the council tie as well, the mint draws lots. The chosen High Admiral takes office a year after the vote, and spends at least the last two months of that year at the side of the sitting High Admiral, learning the office. A High Admiral may serve two terms and no more, and a councillor who stands for the office must first give up their seat. The present High Admiral, [[Severina Altamar]] of [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Isidora/index|Isidora]], is in the ninth year of her first term.

The High Admiral commands the coalition army and presides over every session, keeping order, giving the floor and keeping the rules of procedure, but never votes. Their powers are few and sharp:
- **Contempt.** The High Admiral may hold a councillor in contempt, but only for belligerence, filibustering or other plain abuse of the proceedings. A councillor in contempt may not speak or take part in debate, but never loses their vote, and thirteen of the nineteen may overrule the judgment.
- **Conflicts of interest.** When a councillor's own island, house or purse stands to gain directly from a vote, thirteen of the nineteen may bar that councillor from that single vote. The High Admiral must confirm that the bar is fair, and if they refuse, they must give their reasons before the council. Seventeen of the nineteen may overrule the refusal, and the councillor in question has no voice in it.
- **War.** The coalition is forever at war with the [[Locations/The Black Tide/index|Black Tide]] and the sea folk, and the High Admiral may defend the islands at any time. But no new offensive, and no war against any other power, may begin without a majority of the council, and on that question alone the High Admiral holds a veto. The islands' own guards and ships go to war only if their own islands send them.
- **Treaties.** The High Admiral treats with foreign powers, and the council ratifies by majority.

Should a High Admiral die or be unable to serve, the most senior fleet admiral presides in their place without a vote, and a new High Admiral is chosen within the year to serve a full term.

## No Confidence
Any councillor, or the High Admiral, may be put before every island in a vote of no confidence. The High Admiral may call one against a councillor, and thirteen of the nineteen may call one against a councillor or the High Admiral. The vote passes only if three fifths of all member islands agree, and a vote that fails may not be called against the same person again for a year.

## The Unmaking
Last of all, unused for more than a thousand years, is the Unmaking. Should the council itself grow rotten past saving, the High Admiral may ask the islands to dissolve it, but only with the unanimous agreement of the three fleet admirals, and three quarters of all member islands must agree. If they do, every councillor is removed at once. The High Admiral names nine new councillors, and those nine choose the other ten. The removed councillors may never again hold coalition office, and they answer for whatever they have done before a tribunal of archons. The High Admiral who called the Unmaking serves one more year to see the new council settled, then steps down, barred from coalition office for life, for no one may profit from unmaking the council.

## The Fleet Admirals
The coalition army is commanded by three fleet admirals: [[Rhea Kanellos]], admiral of the northern squadron on [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Argentum/index|Argentum]]; [[Ruk Valtor]], commodore of the southern squadron on [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Drakosa/index|Drakosa]]; and [[Malaquias Ferro]], warden of [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Isla Embra/index|Isla Embra]], whose mortar ships and customs cutters are the reserve of the southeast. When a fleet admiral's post falls empty, the remaining fleet admirals choose the successor, for they know best who is fit to command, and the council may reject their choice with thirteen of nineteen. The High Admiral has no say. Recruits for the south are trained on [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Lola Las Thea/index|Lola Las Thea]], and the condemned of the coalition and beyond are carried by the black ships to [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Sparte Torro/index|Sparte Torro]] and [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Isla Embra/index|Isla Embra]].

## The Courts
Every island judges its own people under its own laws, and the coalition never interferes. For the crimes no single island can claim, the coalition keeps a small corps of magistrates, who sail on circuit from island to island and sit permanently on Argentum and Drakosa. They try piracy and every crime on the open sea, breaches of guest-right and of [[Tarrakus|Tarrakus's]] Rules of War, desertion and draft-dodging, counterfeiting the shell, and crimes by the coalition's own officers, and they settle the prize money owed for captured pirates. A priest of Tarrakus advises on any case touching the Rules of War. Magistrates are chosen by their fellow magistrates, the council may reject a choice with thirteen of nineteen, and they serve for life. The powerful answer to their peers: when a councillor, a High Admiral, a fleet admiral or a magistrate stands accused, they are tried by a tribunal of archons drawn by lot from among the leaders of the islands.

## Joining and Leaving
An island joins the coalition by the vote of the council and a majority of the member islands. Any island may leave, and the council may cast one out, but either way it loses the protection of the coalition and of Tarrakus's Rules of War, and such an island rarely lasts long. Its neighbours take it, set up a sister government in its place, and bring it back into the fold, unless the [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/index|Kingdom]], or something worse, reaches it first.

# The Shared Laws
Only a few laws bind every member: answer the council's draft, honor [[flaris|Flaris's guest-right]], keep [[Tarrakus|Tarrakus's]] Rules of War when fighting a fellow island, never turn pirate, and bring every quarrel to mediation before it becomes a war. Beyond that, the islands keep each other in check far more than the council ever could, and an island that grows too bold soon finds its neighbors lined up against it. Now and then, when mediation fails entirely, two city-states do go to war. These wars are small and short, fought by rules everyone understands, and never as ruthless as the wars the islands fight against outsiders, let alone the long, bitter war between the [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/index|Kingdom]] and the [[Locations/The Black Tide/index|Black Tide]].

Every coalition ship and harbor flies the coalition's flag, a white ring of rope on an azure field, for the bond between the islands. The greatest city-states fly a banner of their own beneath it, but most islands are content with the ring alone.

The coalition's neighbors keep it honest. To the north, the [[Sea Folk Waters|sea folk waters]] press close, and the sea folk have grown restless since the flood stopped. To the southwest lie the dragons of the [[Locations/The Draconic Conclave/index|Draconic Conclave]]. To the south, the pirates of the [[Locations/The Black Tide/index|Black Tide]] prowl the waters between the Conclave and the deadly calms and storms of the [[Locations/The Widow's Wrath/index|Widow's Wrath]]. To the east and southeast stretches the [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/index|Kingdom of Albryn]], the coalition's greatest trading partner and its greatest worry, vast and orderly where the coalition is free and quarrelsome. Few islanders would trade their independence for the Kingdom's order, but more than a few city-states have begun to wonder what the coalition would do if the Kingdom ever decided to stop trading and start taking.
