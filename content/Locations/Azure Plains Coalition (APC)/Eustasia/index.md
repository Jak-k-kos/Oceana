# Eustasia
Eustasia is a small, sun-baked island at the edge of the [[Locations/The Draconic Conclave/index|Conclave]]'s waters, and its hillsides are planted in sesame. In late summer the tall stalks are cut and stood in sheaves to dry, until their pods split and the tiny seeds pour out. The seeds go by the sack to bakers across the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]], but most are pressed into sesame oil, good for cooking, lamps and soap alike, or ground into a thick paste that keeps for months, and the islanders boil that paste with honey into a sweet, crumbly halva that sailors carry in their pockets. Eustasia is a quiet island, and its people keep one eye on the western sky, for now and then dragons pass overhead, high and far, bound for places no one on Eustasia wishes to visit.
