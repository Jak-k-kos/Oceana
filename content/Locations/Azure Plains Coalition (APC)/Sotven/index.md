# Sotven
Sotven sits close behind the frontier, yet the [[Locations/The Widow's Wrath/index|Widow's Wrath]] stands between it and any pirate fleet, and the Wrath's endless winds drive its mills. Rows of heavy windmills line its ridges, and their shafts turn not only millstones but grindstones, drills and great trip-hammers that pound day and night. Here the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition's]] battered weapons come to be mended: musket locks filed and refitted, cutlasses and bayonets reground, boarding axes rehafted, gun carriages and cannon fittings repaired and swivel guns re-bored, then all of it sent back south ready to fight again, with the frontier's grain ground on the side. The island never sleeps, and from the sea at night you can hear the thump of its hammers long before you see its lamps.
