# Aquillis Marin
Aquillis Marin is a small, rocky island that sits on the edge of a cliff under the sea. Just offshore, the seabed drops away almost straight down into blue so deep it is nearly black, and the island's fishermen say [[Aquilla]] herself looks up from it. Above the water, the island climbs in green terraces of citrus: lemons, oranges, and the bitter oranges that go into liqueur. In spring the scent of orange blossom drifts far out over the drop, and sailors say you could find Aquillis Marin blindfolded. Its fruit and candied peel sail to every corner of the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]], and a crate of its lemons rides in half the galleys at sea. The islanders are devoted to Aquilla, and a small shrine to her stands above every landing.
