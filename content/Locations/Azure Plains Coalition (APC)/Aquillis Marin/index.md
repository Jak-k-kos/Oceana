# Aquillis Marin
Aquillis Marin is a low, rocky island that sits on the edge of a cliff under the sea. Just offshore, the seabed drops away almost straight down, and the deep water around the island holds vast kelp forests that stretch for miles in every direction. Wood is scarce in the islands, and coal and charcoal are rare. The islanders cut the kelp from boats, dry it in great brown heaps along the shore, and bake it in sealed kilns into kelp charcoal, which burns hot and long. They make it in enormous quantities. It fuels hearths, forges, and kitchens across the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]], and it is the fuel of the rare steamships and the even rarer steam-powered battleships. Kelp charcoal is valuable enough that the [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/index|Kingdom]] buys up all it can get. The islanders are devoted to [[Aquilla]], and a small shrine to her stands above every landing.
