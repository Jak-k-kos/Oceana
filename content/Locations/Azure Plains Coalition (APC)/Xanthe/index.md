# Xanthe
Xanthe means golden-haired, and in late summer the island is exactly that, its broad hills rippling with wheat from shore to summit, yellow as a child's curls. Xanthe is the breadbasket of the southern frontier. Its grain is milled on the island and baked in long brick ovens into bread for the garrisons and, above all, into ship's biscuit by the barrel: the hard, dry, all but everlasting bread that every frontier ship carries in its hold. Without Xanthe, the southern squadron would wait on biscuit from the far side of the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]]; with it, a frigate can be provisioned for a month's patrol in a single day. Like every island on the [[Locations/The Black Tide/index|Black Tide]] border, Xanthe keeps its walls manned and its granaries locked, and its people say that in a siege they will be the last on the frontier to starve.
