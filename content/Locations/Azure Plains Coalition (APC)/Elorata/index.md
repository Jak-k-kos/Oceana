# Elorata
Elorata is a small, rocky island on the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition's]] northern edge whose people live off the shallows. They farm oysters, clams, and mussels on ropes and racks in the sheltered coves, and the shellfish they raise feed a good share of the neighbouring islands, fresh, smoked, and salted. The oysters also make pearls. The finest wild pearls lie on the deep outer beds toward the [[Sea Folk Waters|sea folk waters]], and Elorata's free-divers work them in teams with a weighted stone and a basket. Its people are quiet and careful, and every diver wears a pearl on a cord for luck.
