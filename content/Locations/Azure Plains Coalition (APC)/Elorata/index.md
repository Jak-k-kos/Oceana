# Elorata
Elorata is a small, rocky island on the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition's]] northern edge whose people live off the shallows. They farm oysters, clams, and mussels on ropes and racks in the sheltered coves, and the shellfish they raise feed a good share of the neighbouring islands, fresh, smoked, and salted. The kelp was a pest at first. It grew so thick in the coves that it tangled the mussel ropes, and the islanders cut it back only to save their shellfish. When kelp charcoal became the fuel of the whole coalition, Elorata built kilns of its own, and now the cuttings that were once thrown back to the sea leave the island as charcoal by the boatload. The oysters also make pearls. The finest wild pearls lie on the deep outer beds toward the [[Sea Folk Waters|sea folk waters]], and Elorata's free-divers work them in teams with a weighted stone and a basket. Its people are quiet and careful, and every diver wears a pearl on a cord for luck.
