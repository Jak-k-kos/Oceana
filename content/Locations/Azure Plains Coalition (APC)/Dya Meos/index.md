# Dya Meos
Dya Meos is a pair of matching islands, so alike in shape that sailors call them the Mirror Isles, and its people hold twins to be sacred. When twins are born on Dya Meos, they are destined from birth to rule the islands together, and they are raised for it from the cradle. When no living pair is old enough to rule, the islanders choose a steward to govern until the next twins come of age. The islands live by their kilns. Dya Meos has mastered the art of making earthenware in great quantity: storage jars, plates, bowls, and pots, fired by the thousand and shipped across the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]] to apothecaries, kitchens, merchants, and anyone else who needs something to keep things in. It has made the islands comfortably wealthy, if not grand. The potters' finest work, tall jars painted with scenes of the old world before the flood, is made only in small numbers and sold to the highest bidder. The twins hold court together on a barge anchored in the strait between the two islands, so that they always rule from the middle.
