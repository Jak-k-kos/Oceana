# Pergma
Pergma is a tiny, quiet island of stationers and bookbinders. Paper comes from [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Velsanta Dorma/index|Velsanta Dorma]], leather from [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Fok Tara/index|Fok Tara]] and beeswax from the hives of the south, and in its workshops they become the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition's]] logbooks, ledgers and blank books, sealed with Pergma's red sealing wax. Every ship in the coalition keeps its log in a Pergma book, every merchant his accounts, and half the gravesmen's Books of Names are bound there. The island's card-makers cut and paint decks of playing cards on small printing presses, for no other way could one little island turn out enough for every forecastle and tavern in the coalition. Pergma smells of leather, glue and hot wax, and its people are tidy, precise, and very fond of a good story.
