# Kalyps
Kalyps is a small, quiet island terraced from shore to summit in beans, lentils, chickpeas, and fava. Dried pulses keep almost forever, and Kalyps sends them out by the barrel to ships' stores and island larders across the region. It isn't a rich island, but no one on Kalyps goes hungry, and its neighbours lean on it in lean years.
