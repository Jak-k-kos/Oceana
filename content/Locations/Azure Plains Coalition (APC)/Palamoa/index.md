# Palamoa
Palamoa is a low, warm island whose forests are made of palms: climbing palms that twist and coil through the groves and throw out long, tough canes thicker than a man's thumb. That is rattan, and in a [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]] where timber is too precious to waste on chairs and crates, it is very nearly as valuable as wood. Palamoa's cane-cutters harvest it, and its weavers turn it into chairs, tables and beds, light cargo baskets, fish and crab traps, ship's fenders, and the woven screens and gabions that shore up harbor walls. Half the coalition sits on Palamoa rattan, and half its fishermen set Palamoa traps. The fronds of its fan palms become thatch for roofs across the south, and the islanders tap the palms for a sweet brown sugar that they boil down into cakes. Every house on Palamoa is furnished in its own cane, from the cradles to the funeral biers.
