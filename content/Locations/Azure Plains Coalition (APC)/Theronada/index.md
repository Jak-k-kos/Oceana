# Theronada
Theronada is a Y-shaped island of three long, thin arms around a forested central ridge, and each of the three sheltered bays between the arms has its own village. The ridge is one of the last great oak forests in the northwest. The islanders cut it carefully for ship timber and burn the offcuts into charcoal, which they sell to smiths and shipyards across the region. Herds of half-wild pigs fatten on the acorns, and the island's salted ham sells well. Theronadans are hunters too. Every autumn the three villages come together for the boar hunt in honour of [[Ruthala]], and the best hunter of the year is feasted by the whole island.
