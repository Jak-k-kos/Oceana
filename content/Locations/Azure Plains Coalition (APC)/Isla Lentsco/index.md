# Isla Lentsco
Isla Lentsco is a small, bare island where the northwest's boiling work gets done. The kettle-houses take in raw pine resin, pitch, and oil from across the islands and cook them down into the tar, pitch, varnish, and lacquer used for sealing ship hulls, protecting rigging, and finishing woodwork in houses. The work is dangerous, and most islands are happy to have it done somewhere else. Smoke hangs over the island day and night, and sailors can smell Lentsco long before they see it.
