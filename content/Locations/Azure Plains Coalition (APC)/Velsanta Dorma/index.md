# Velsanta Dorma
Velsanta Dorma is a great sprawling island of rolling hills, wide valleys, and ragged peninsulas reaching into the sea like fingers. It is one of the largest islands in the eastern [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]]. Its valleys are golden with barley and green with tall hemp, and both feed the trades the island is known for. The hemp is retted, broken, and spun into rope and coarse canvas. Old rope comes back to the island to be picked into oakum for caulking ships, and the rags and fibres go to the paper mills along the island's rivers. The barley goes to the breweries, and Velsanta Dorma brews most of the beer and ale drunk in the coalition, from the light ale of the taverns to the dark, strong hold ale that keeps for months in a ship's belly. The town of Velsanta Dorma smells of malt and wet hemp, and on brewing days the whole harbor is warm with it.
