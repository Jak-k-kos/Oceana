# Esmerna
Esmerna is a tiny green rock where a dozen families raise and train birds of every kind, from hawks and falcons to bright tropical parrots. Their best sellers are knife-wing gulls, which can fly great distances fast. The head family, the Vientos, has druidic magic, and they use it to give every bird raised on Esmerna the intelligence to carry messages wherever its owner commands. An Esmerna bird can find a single ship at sea or a single building on an island it has never seen. Most people believe the birds understand Common from the day they hatch, and for all practical purposes they might as well. Being clever, the birds have a lot of personality, and they won't tolerate abuse. A well-cared-for Esmerna bird is worth its weight in gold, and archons, merchant houses, and the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]] army all keep them.
