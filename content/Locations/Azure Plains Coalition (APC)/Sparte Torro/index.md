# Sparte Torro
Sparte Torro is a long, fertile island in the northwest, and every native-born Sparte is a soldier. From the age of seven, children drill with spear, musket, and cannon, and by adulthood every one of them can stand in a shield line or crew a gun battery. Officers are given a bronze trident when they earn command, and they carry it for life. The island's wide valleys are farmed in grain, olives, and vines, and its hills carry flocks and pigs. All of it is worked by slaves: convicts from across the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]] and beyond, and captured [[Locations/The Black Tide/index|Black Tide]] pirates, bought for their passage or for the prize money owed to the crews who took them. A deputy of Sparte Torro rides aboard every black ship that carries the condemned, choosing the prisoners its fields can use, and those it passes over go on to [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Isla Embra/index|Isla Embra]]. Sparte Torro serves as the prison of the coalition, and the endless supply of convict labour feeds the largest standing military population in the coalition.

Many outsiders also come to settle, because any child born on the island may train and grow up a full Sparte. The settlers themselves can't bear arms or hold office, but they are treated well and serve as overseers on the estates. The children of the slaves are taken from their parents and raised in the temples, and they grow up as loyal Spartes who never knew any other home. The wealthiest Sparte families keep their private estates on a small islet off the northwest coast.

The island is dotted with temples to [[Tarrakus]], and the greatest of them, the Hall of the Unbeaten, stands at the heart of the town of Sparte Torro beside the harbor. It is the largest temple to Tarrakus in Oceana and one of the most prestigious war colleges anywhere. Sparte military families send their children to study there, and so, for a handsome price, do the powerful of other nations. Many of the highest-ranking officers in the [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/index|Kingdom]] and the coalition passed through its halls, and it is rumored that a couple of the current dread pirate lords who serve under the Black Tide's [[Dread Pirate King Vask|dread pirate king]] did too. The temples are among the few places on the island where outsiders may take up weapons, and only for training. Even the wealthiest foreign students must work the fields to earn their keep.
