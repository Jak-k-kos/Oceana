# Seriphea
Seriphea is a small, rocky island of fermenters and distillers. Neighbouring islands send their surplus fruit, grain, cane, and honey here, and the island's still-houses turn it into brandy, spirits, vinegar, and pickles. Seriphean brandy is aged in old wine casks in the cool caves along the shore, and a good bottle sells for a lot of shells. The island smells of sour mash and woodsmoke, and nobody here drinks water when there's anything else to hand.
