# Thellesia
Thellesia is a small, rocky island where the seabed is a maze of reefs and crevices, perfect for crabs and lobsters. Its people set their crab traps among the rocks each evening and haul them up each dawn, and they keep the catch alive in stone holding-pens along the shore until the trading boats come. Between the traps, they net anchovies, which they pack in salt and press into barrels. Thellesia's lobsters grace the tables of archons across the region, and its salted anchovies go into every ship's galley.
