# Altas
Altas lies at the edge of the [[Locations/The Widow's Wrath/index|Widow's Wrath]], and the storm's endless rains water its fields so well that sugarcane grows taller than a man in a single season. Altas is the frontier's sugar island. Its cane is cut and crushed in windmills, boiled into molasses and raw sugar, and distilled into a dark, fiery rum that fills the ration casks of every ship in the southern squadron, for a sailor's tot of Altan rum is the one comfort the frontier never runs short of. Its molasses sweetens the frontier's bread and porridge, and its raw sugar goes north. The Wrath shelters Altas from the [[Locations/The Black Tide/index|Black Tide]] and soaks it to the bone in the same breath, and the islanders thank it for both.
