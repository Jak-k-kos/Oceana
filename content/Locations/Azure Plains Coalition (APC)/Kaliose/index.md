# Kaliose
Kaliose is a round, grassy island of open pasture, about a day's sail north of the waters of the [[Locations/The Draconic Conclave/index|Draconic Conclave]]. Its herders raise tough, short-legged cattle on the hills and sell salted beef, hides, tallow, and leather to the islands around them. Most of the ships in the northwest carry Kaliose beef in their holds. The herders are devoted to [[Ruthala]], and every herd has a small shrine at its watering hole.
