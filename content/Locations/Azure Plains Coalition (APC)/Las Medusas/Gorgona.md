# Gorgona
Gorgona is the long, curving main island of [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Las Medusas/index|Las Medusas]], and the heart of its trade. Its warm hillsides are planted from shore to summit in almond, hazelnut, and olive groves, and each spring the almond blossom turns the whole island pink and white. The town of Las Medusas sits in a sheltered bay on its inner curve, its streets smelling of toasted almonds and fresh-pressed oil.
