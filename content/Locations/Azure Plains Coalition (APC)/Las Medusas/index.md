# Las Medusas
Las Medusas is a scattering of four islands that trail across the sea like the tentacles of some great jellyfish, which is how they got their name. The largest, [[Gorgona]], is a long, curving island of warm hillsides covered in almond, hazelnut, and olive groves. Each spring the almond blossom turns the hills pink and white. Its nuts, nut oils, and almond cakes are sold across the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]]. To the south lies [[Esteno]], a quieter island of grazing sheep and goats. West of the main island, the little islet of [[Euriale]] shelters the fishing boats, and the thin sliver of [[La Serpiente]] to the east is home to the island's snake-catchers, who milk the venom of the sea snakes that bask on its rocks and sell it to apothecaries across the coalition. The people of Las Medusas are easygoing and hospitable, and every visitor is pressed to try the almond cakes.
