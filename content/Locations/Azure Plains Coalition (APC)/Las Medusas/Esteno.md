# Esteno
Esteno is the quiet southern island of [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Las Medusas/index|Las Medusas]], given over to grassy pastures where sheep and goats graze under the open sky. A few shepherd families live in stone cottages scattered across its slopes, and their cheese and wool cross the channel to [[Gorgona|Gorgona's]] market.
