# Euriale
Euriale is the little western islet of [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Las Medusas/index|Las Medusas]], a rocky crook of land whose sheltered cove is home to the islands' fishing boats. Its fishermen keep Las Medusas fed with fish, and their nets dry on every wall.
