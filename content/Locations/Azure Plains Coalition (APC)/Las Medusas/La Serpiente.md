# La Serpiente
La Serpiente is the thin eastern sliver of [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Las Medusas/index|Las Medusas]], little more than a spine of sun-warmed rock where sea snakes come ashore to bask. The island's snake-catchers live among them in a handful of stone huts, milking their venom with long tongs and steady hands, and selling it in tiny sealed vials to apothecaries across the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]].
