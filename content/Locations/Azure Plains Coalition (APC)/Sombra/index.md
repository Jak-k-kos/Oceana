# Sombra
Sombra is an island of deep, shadowed ravines on the edge of dragon waters. Its high cliffs and towering shade trees keep the valley floors in green twilight even at noon. That shade is exactly what cacao needs, and Sombra's ravines are planted thick with cacao trees growing beneath the canopy, with vanilla vines climbing their trunks. The pods are cracked, fermented, and dried, then shipped across the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]] and into the [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/index|Kingdom]] as cacao beans and cakes of bitter chocolate. On the main island, [[Umbral]], the ravines and the plantation houses crowd together under the trees. To the south lies the low islet of [[Penumbra]], where the beans are fermented in great wooden boxes and dried in the open sun. The whole island smells of chocolate and wet leaves.
