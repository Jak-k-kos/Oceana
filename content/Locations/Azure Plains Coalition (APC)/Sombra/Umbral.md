# Umbral
Umbral is the main island of [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Sombra/index|Sombra]], a maze of deep ravines and high cliffs roofed over by towering shade trees. In the green twilight beneath them grow the cacao trees, their pods hanging straight from the trunks like strange lanterns, with vanilla vines winding up between. The plantation houses perch on the ravine rims, and the town of Sombra clings to the harbor below, smelling of chocolate and rain.
