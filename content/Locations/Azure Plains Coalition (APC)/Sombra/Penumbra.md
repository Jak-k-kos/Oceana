# Penumbra
Penumbra is the low, sunny islet south of [[Umbral]], and it is everything its sister is not: flat, bright, and open to the sky. Here the cacao pods from the ravines are cracked open, their beans heaped in great wooden boxes to ferment, then spread across long drying floors to bake in the sun. Workers rake them over through the day, and the smell of sweet, sour chocolate drifts far out to sea.
