# Santa Quillis
Santa Quillis is a small, breezy island crowned by a whitewashed shrine to [[Aquilla]], and its people keep the frontier's ships under sail. Its fields grow hemp and flax, and in the long ropewalks and sail-lofts along the harbor, its sailmakers and ropers turn them into sailcloth, cordage and rigging. Most of their days are spent mending what the frontier has torn: canvas holed by grapeshot, shrouds cut by chain shot, halyards burned through, so that a ship limping north from a skirmish can be re-rigged and re-sailed in days rather than weeks. By custom, every new sail cut on the island is carried up to the shrine to be blessed by the priestess of Aquilla before it is bent to a yard, and frontier sailors swear that a blessed Santa Quillis sail has never once failed them in a fight.
