Santa Ismene is a flat, blinding-white island of salt pans, where seawater is let into shallow stone basins and left for the sun to take. From the water it looks like a scattering of mirrors, and on bright days sailors claim they can see its glare from three islands away. Above the pans stands the Temple of the Still Water, a squat whitewashed dome where the high priestess of [[Aquilla]] rules the island in the goddess's name. The island's salt is sold across the coalition, and local sellers hawk little pouches of "blessed salt" to sailors at the docks, a regional good-luck charm more than anything holy. Santa Ismene is rumored to have thrown the finest celebration of the first [[Aquilla's Mercy]] anywhere in the islands, and many are already planning to sail there for the next one to see if the stories are true. The rest of the year, life here is quiet, orderly, and very salty.
