# Vistar
Vistar is a lonely tooth of rock in the middle of the eastern fishing grounds, and its people live off the fish around it in more ways than one. Their own boats work the grounds, and they salt and smoke their catch for the neighbouring islands. Vistar's real craft, though, comes from what the fleets would otherwise throw back to the gulls. Its people take the swim bladders from every catch, their own and the fleets', and clean, dry, and boil them down into isinglass. It is a clear, strong glue that shipwrights use to seal and bind fine joinery and that brewers use to clear their ale. Racks of drying bladders rattle in the wind like strange pale lanterns. The island smells exactly as bad as you'd imagine, and its few families are among the least visited and best paid in the region.
