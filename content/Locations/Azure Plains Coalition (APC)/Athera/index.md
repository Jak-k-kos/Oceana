# Athera
Athera is a small, green island of marshes and meadows, and it belongs to the geese. Great white flocks graze its pastures and paddle its ponds, and their honking greets every boat long before it reaches the harbor. Athera's goose-keepers send nearly every part of the bird out into the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]]: goose meat, smoked and fresh, and jars of goose fat for the galleys; eggs by the basket; soft down for pillows and quilts; grey wing feathers for the fletchers of [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Sepelia/index|Sepelia]]; and long white quills, cut and trimmed, for every scribe, clerk and navigator in the coalition. The island's children herd the geese with long willow switches, and its people are known for being stubborn, loud and fiercely loyal, rather like their birds.
