# Wellfleet Oyster Farm
Across the fields east of the town of [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Insul/index|Insul]], the land opens onto a round, sheltered bay, deep and still and clear, and on its bed lie the Wellfleet oyster beds. The oysters grow too deep to be reached by wading, so the family works them by diving: from their short wooden dock on the bay's western shore they row out over the beds, slip over the side, and come up with baskets full. The Wellfleets, tabaxi oyster farmers every one, keep their home beside that dock, and from dawn to dusk at least one of them can be found out on the water or drying off on the boards. Their oysters are served at [[Uncle Johns|Uncle John's]], sold in the market, and traded to every merchant who stops at Harbour Cove, and the bay rings with the shouts of the Wellfleet children, who seem to spend as much time getting into mischief as they do diving the beds.
