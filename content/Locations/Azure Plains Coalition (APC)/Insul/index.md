# Insul
Insul is a small, green island in the southeastern [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]], and it is one of the few places in all the coalition where trees still grow tall and thick. Dense, fragrant forest covers the whole island from shore to shore, broken only where the town and its fields have been cleared, and the Fiore family, who tend it, keep it growing back faster than anyone can quite explain. In a world where timber is too precious to burn and too dear for houses, Insul's lumber is worth its weight in shells, and it goes out by the shipload to neighbouring [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Belna/index|Belna]] and to ports across the southeast.

The island's town crowds the shore of Harbour Cove on its western side, its neat grid of streets running back from the docks to the edge of the forest. Market Street runs through its heart, past the town hall and the temples of [[Lyandris]] and [[Roktra]]. The [[Fiore Lumber Company]] and its log yard stand at the town's northern edge, and the Fiore family manor sits in its walled gardens at the town's centre. Down on the waterfront, [[Uncle Johns|Uncle John's]] looks out over the cove, full every night of lumberjacks, oyster farmers and traveling merchants, and the gravesmen's house sits quietly at the water's edge. Beyond the town, fields run east to a deep, sheltered bay where the [[Wellfleet oyster farm|Wellfleet oyster beds]] lie.

Insul is a quiet place, far from the frontier and the sea folk alike, where everyone knows everyone and very little ever happens. Its people like it that way.

# Insul
Insul was once a small, green island of tall forests and a cheerful little town, famous across the southeast for its lumber. Now it is a ruin. When a lumber shipment failed to arrive, the merchants of [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Belna/index|Belna]] sent birds to ask why, and not one of them was answered. At last they sent a fluyt to see for themselves, and its crew found the docks of Harbour Cove charred and broken, the town burned to its stone footings, the temples and the town hall gutted, and the Wellfleet oyster beds untended. The streets and the ground about the town were littered with charred and mutilated bodies, and no one was left alive to say what had happened. It took the gravesmen of Belna many days to send them all to sea.

No one knows who did it, or why, and the rumours have no end. Some say it was the [[Locations/The Black Tide/index|Black Tide]], though it seems all but impossible for pirates to have sailed so deep into coalition waters unseen. Others blame a wayward dragon, or greedy mercenaries, or soldiers of the [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/index|Kingdom]], or even of the coalition itself. There is no evidence for any of it, and no one has ever claimed the deed. The forests still stand, already creeping back over the ashes, and the merchants of Belna, who depended on Insul's timber, have begun to wonder aloud who the island belongs to now.
