# Uncle John's
Uncle John's is the only pub on [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Insul/index|Insul]], and it sits right on the waterfront of Harbour Cove, close enough to the docks that a sailor can step from his gangplank to the bar in a dozen paces. It belongs to [[John Bellball]], but everyone on the island knows that it is truly run by [[Tabatha Bellball]], who keeps its books, its kitchen and its customers in line with a sharp eye and a sharper tongue. Every night it is full of lumberjacks, oyster farmers and traveling merchants, and nearly every scrap of news that reaches Insul from the wider world is first heard over a cup of its ale.
