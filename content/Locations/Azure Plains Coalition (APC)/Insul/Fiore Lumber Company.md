# Fiore Lumber Company
The Fiore Lumber Company is the heart of the trade of [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Insul/index|Insul]], and it employs nearly everyone on the island. It pays its workers well and treats them even better, which is rare enough anywhere in the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]] that some of the merchants who visit Insul refuse to believe it. Its offices stand in a great wooden hall at the northern edge of town, beside the log yard where timber felled in the forests that cover the island is stacked, sawn and readied for shipping. The [[Orchid Fiore|Fiore]] family manor sits in its walled gardens at the heart of the town, a short walk away.
