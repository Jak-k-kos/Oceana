# Odelna
Odelna is a large, green island group in the heart of the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]], and it grows tobacco. The fields of [[Odelna Mayor]] are planted in long rows of broadleaf tobacco, cured in great tall barns. The leaf is rolled into cigars in the town's rolling houses, where the cigar rollers are read to while they work. The long southern islet of [[Odelna Menor]] is planted in cotton, which is spun and woven on the island and sold as cloth. The tiny rock of [[Odelna Chica]] holds a lighthouse at the mouth of the main harbor. Odelna is old money. The great plantation families have run the island for generations, and their houses stand above the fields like small palaces.
