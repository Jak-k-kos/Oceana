# DM Notes
- Leader: [[Rodrigo Alcazar]], whose family owns most of the islet
- Food: Rice and beans, fish, cornbread
- Trade: Raw cotton
- extra: Most of Menor's cotton fields belong to the Alcazars, while the spinning sheds on [[Odelna Mayor|Mayor]] belong to the Navarros. Since the wedding, the Alcazars have stopped shipping cotton across the channel. The sheds are going idle, and the cloth trade is choking. Armed Alcazar retainers now guard the Menor docks.

# Odelna Menor
Odelna Menor is the long islet south of the main island, flat and hot, and covered in cotton fields that turn white at harvest. A few villages of field workers and overseers line its coast, and a single Alcazar manor stands on its one low hill. Every boat that crosses the channel to [[Odelna Mayor|Mayor]] carries bales of cotton.
