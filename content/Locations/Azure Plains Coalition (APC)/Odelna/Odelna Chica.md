# DM Notes
- Leader: The lighthouse keeper, appointed by the governor
- Food: Fish, and whatever comes in by boat
- Trade: None; the governor pays for its upkeep
- extra: The keeper has watched Alcazar boats crossing the channel at night with armed men aboard. He has told no one, because he isn't sure which family pays him.

# Odelna Chica
Odelna Chica is a tiny rock at the mouth of [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Odelna/index|Odelna's]] main harbor, with a stone lighthouse and a keeper's cottage. The keeper lights the lamp every night and logs every ship that enters the harbor.
