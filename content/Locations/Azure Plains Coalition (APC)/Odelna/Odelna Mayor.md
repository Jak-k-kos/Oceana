# Odelna Mayor
Odelna Mayor is the main island, rolling and green, and nearly all of it is planted in long rows of broadleaf tobacco. Tall curing barns stand at the edge of every field, and the great plantation houses look down on them from the hills. The town of [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Odelna/index|Odelna]] sits on the main harbor. It's a busy place of warehouses, rolling houses, and cafes, and the smell of curing leaf hangs over everything. In the rolling houses, rows of workers roll cigars at long tables while a lector reads to them from newspapers and novels. The spinning and weaving sheds that turn [[Odelna Menor|Menor's]] cotton into cloth stand along the waterfront.
