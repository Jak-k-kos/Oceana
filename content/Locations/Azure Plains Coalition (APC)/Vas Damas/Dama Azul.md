# Dama Azul
Dama Azul is the western sister of [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Vas Damas/index|Vas Damas]], home of the Anil dye house. Its fields are planted in indigo and woad, and its groves of oak and walnut give galls and husks for browns and blacks. Great stone vats line the shore, and the water at their outflows runs deep blue. The isle's dyers are blue to the elbows, and the house's indigo is the deepest in the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]].
