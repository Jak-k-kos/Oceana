# Dama Roja
Dama Roja is the eastern sister of [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Vas Damas/index|Vas Damas]], home of the Garance dye house. Its fields are planted in madder, weld, dyer's broom, and safflower, and at harvest the slopes glow red, gold, and orange. The isle's grinding sheds turn roots and flowers into powders and cakes of colour, and its dyers are stained crimson and saffron to the wrist.
