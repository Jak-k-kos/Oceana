# Vas Damas
Vas Damas is a pair of curving sister islands that face each other across a narrow channel, like two ladies turned in conversation. Each belongs to a dyeing house, and between them they make nearly all the everyday colour in the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]]. Both isles have the room to grow every plant a dyer needs. [[Dama Azul]], the western isle, grows the blues and the darks: indigo and woad steeped in great vats, oak galls and walnut husk for browns and blacks. [[Dama Roja]], the eastern isle, grows the warm colours: madder roots for reds, weld and dyer's broom for yellows, safflower for oranges and pinks. Between them they blend greens, purples, and every shade in between. Dyers on both islands carry the colours of their house to the elbows, and the channel between them runs a muddy rainbow where the waters meet.
