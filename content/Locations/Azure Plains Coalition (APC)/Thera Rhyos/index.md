# Thera Rhyos
Thera Rhyos is a great crescent of cliffs wrapped around a flooded caldera, and its town clings to the rim high above the sheltered water. The island is made of the ash and pumice of an old volcano. The islanders quarry the pumice for builders and polishers, and they grind the grey ash into a cement that sets hard even underwater. Harbor builders across the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]] use it for sea walls and moles. The ashy soil on the outer slopes grows tough vines, and their strong white wine is drunk across the eastern islands. Ships moor safely in the caldera bay, sheltered on almost every side, and Thera Rhyos does good business as a port.
