# Corvealna
Corvealna is a pair of islands on the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition's]] northern edge, covered in pine forests so thick that from the sea they look almost black. The islanders are lumberjacks and sawyers, and the whine of the great water-powered sawmill, fed by a stream off the mountain, carries across the strait. Corvealna pine is strong and resinous, prized for masts and ship timbers. The islanders are proud and competitive, and they consider [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Insul/index|Insul]] their great rival in the lumber trade. The kobold engineer who built the sawmill is a local hero, and the taverns serve a sharp pine-needle liquor that visitors should approach with caution.
