# Vela Blanca
From the sea, Vela Blanca looks like a ship under full sail: its hills are covered in tall white grass that ripples in the wind like canvas. The islanders have tended that grass for generations, clearing and replanting it on every slope, and they twist its long, tough fibers into rope. Vela Blanca rope is strong, light, and resists rot in salt water, and it rigs a good share of the ships in the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]]. The harbor itself is small and exposed, and in bad weather the fishing boats are hauled up the beach by hand, every islander on the ropes, which they never run short of.
