# Doraidas
Doraidas is a small island of whalers who hunt the great whales of the southern deeps: humpbacks, right whales, and the great sperm whales most of all. Its narrow harbor is crowded with whaleboats and try-pots, and the island smells of boiling blubber for half the year. From each whale the Doraidans take oil, meat, and bone. From the sperm whales they also take the precious spermaceti from the great head, which they press into clean-burning candles that light the finest halls in the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]]. Now and then they find a lump of ambergris, worth more than its weight in gold. The whalers are hard, brave, and superstitious, and they burn a candle for every whaler lost.
