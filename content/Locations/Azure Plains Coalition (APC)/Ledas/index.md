# Ledas
At night, Ledas glows. Its forges burn until dawn, and their light spills from open doors and across the harbor like embers in a hearth, for Ledas is the armory of the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]]. Iron and steel bars come from [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Insal Fargo/index|Insal Fargo]] and hides from [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Kaliose/index|Kaliose]], and its armourers hammer them into breastplates, helmets, shields, and coats of leather and brigandine: the plain, sturdy armour that island guards, ship's marines and the coalition army wear. The island keeps goats in its hills and nets fish from its coves, so its smiths are well fed and well respected. Every apprentice on Ledas spends a year at the bellows before being allowed anywhere near a hammer.
