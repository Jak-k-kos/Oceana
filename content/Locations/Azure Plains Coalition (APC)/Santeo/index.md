# Santeo
Santeo is a small, quiet island crowned by the Monastery of the Watching Sky, a cluster of white stone cells around an open-roofed observatory dedicated to [[Winstra]], patron of travelers. Below the monastery, on terraced hillsides, lies the town of Santeo, built by the people the monks have taken in. For as long as anyone remembers, the monastery has offered sanctuary to anyone who asks for it: the poor, the hunted, the widowed, the runaway, the lost. No one is asked where they came from. In return, they work the rice paddies that step down the hillsides and keep the town and monastery fed. The monks sleep by day and chart the stars by night, and they sell their charts to passing captains. Visitors are welcome after sundown, when the observatory roof is opened and anyone may climb up to look at the sky.
