# Del Rosa
Del Rosa is a green, sheltered island blooming with flowers and medicinal plants, poppies and lavender, feverfew and yarrow, comfrey, chamomile and a dozen more, grown in long terraced beds that sweeten the air for miles. Its healers, faithful of [[Apiot]], turn them into medicines by the barrel: salves and tinctures, bloodstop powder, poppy draughts for the dying and healer's kits for the living, and these go out to every ship and garrison on the frontier. The hospital of Del Rosa is nearly always full of [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]] sailors wounded in the fighting against the [[Locations/The Black Tide/index|Black Tide]], and its healers are so trusted that the coalition's larger warships contract them to sail aboard before a great battle, working beside the ship's surgeon on the bloodied orlop deck.
