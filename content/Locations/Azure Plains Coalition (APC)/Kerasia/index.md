# Kerasia
Kerasia is a small, gentle island covered in orchards. Every spring the cherry, plum, and apricot trees bloom so thickly that the island turns pink and white. The fruit is picked by hand each summer, and most of it is dried, preserved, or soaked into sweet cherry liqueur for the islands around. The old orchard wood is precious too: cherrywood is sold to furniture-makers and cabinet-makers for its fine red grain. The Kerasians have a saying, that you plant a cherry tree for your grandchildren, and they mean it.
