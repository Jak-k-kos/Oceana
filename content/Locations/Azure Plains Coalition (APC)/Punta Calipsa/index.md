# Punta Calipsa
Punta Calipsa is a lush hook of land on the edge of dragon waters, watered by cold springs that run down through orchards, grape arbours, and shaded gardens. Its people grow figs, grapes, pomegranates, quinces, and citrus. They sell dried figs, raisins, and jars of fruit preserves to every ship that puts in. Ships heading along the [[Locations/The Draconic Conclave/index|Conclave]] edge often wait here for a safe passage, and crews who stop for a night tend to stay a week. The island is best known for its lotus wine, a pale, sweet wine made from the lotus flowers in its spring-fed ponds. It is popular across the region, and sailors swear a cup of it makes you forget where you were going.
