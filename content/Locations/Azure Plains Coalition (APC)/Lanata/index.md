# Lanata
Lanata is a small, steamy island right on the edge of dragon waters, thick with plantain groves and leaning coconut palms. Its people grow plantains and coconuts for the islands to the north. They twist the coarse husk fibre of the coconut into coir, a tough rope and matting that doesn't rot in salt water, which ships use for fenders, mats, and cheap cordage. Dragons pass overhead now and then, and the Lanatans wave.
