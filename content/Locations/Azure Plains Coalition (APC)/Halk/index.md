# Halk
Halk is a quiet, sheltered island of flax fields and weaving houses. Every summer the fields turn blue with flax flowers, and every autumn the stalks are pulled, soaked in the retting ponds, beaten, and spun into thread. Halk's weavers turn the thread into linen for clothing, sheets, and bandages, and they sell their worn rags to papermakers. The island's master weavers also take commissions for tapestries, and many great halls in the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]] have one from Halk.
