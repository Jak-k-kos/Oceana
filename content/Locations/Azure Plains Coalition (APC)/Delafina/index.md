# Delafina
Delafina is a quiet fishing village on a shallow, sandy bay, and it was the first place in the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]] to learn to fish alongside the sea dragons, some eight hundred years ago. When the mullet run, the fishermen beat the water with their oars. The sea dragons drive the shoals into the nets and take their share of what slips free. Fishermen on other islands have since learned to do the same, but Delafina's are still the best at it, and young fishermen come from across the northwest to learn. The villagers name every sea dragon in the bay, and harming one is the worst crime on the island. Delafina salts and dries its mullet roe into hard amber slabs that sell well in [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Kalisanta/index|Kalisanta's]] markets.
