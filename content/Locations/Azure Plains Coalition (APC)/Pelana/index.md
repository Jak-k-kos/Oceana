# Pelana
Pelana is a tiny, sun-bleached rock rising from shallows the colour of pale jade, and beneath those shallows lie the finest sponge beds in the southern [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]]. Every morning the divers of Pelana slip over the sides of their little boats with a stone in one hand and a knife in the other, and rise again with their arms full of soft, golden sponges. These are trimmed, washed, and strung to dry on lines like strange fruit, then sent out by the bale to every gun deck in the coalition, where they swab the cannon between shots, and to surgeons, scrubbers, and bathhouses besides. On the island's cliffs, where nothing else will grow, sea fennel and capers cling to the stone, and the divers' families pick them and pickle them in jars to be sold up and down the coast.
