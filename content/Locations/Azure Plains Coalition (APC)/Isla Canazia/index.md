# Isla Canazia
Isla Canazia is a great gray island of ash and steam in the heart of the northwestern [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]], built around a sleeping volcano. Its wealth comes from two places. Along the shore, the black volcanic soil is astonishingly rich, and terraced farms climb the lower slopes with grain, vegetables, and vines, feeding a good share of the island's neighbors. Higher up, the steaming vents crust the rocks with yellow sulfur, which the islanders mine and sell to the powder-makers. The island's hot springs are famous too, and the largest of them has been turned into the Ashwater, a lavish bathhouse and retreat for the rich and powerful of the coalition, where archons, merchant princes, and even [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/index|Kingdom]] nobles come to soak, rest, and quietly make deals. The town of Isla Canazia sprawls along the harbor, and at its center stands the Great Hearth, a vast open hall built around a fire said never to have gone out. In keeping with [[Flaris|Flaris's]] law, anyone who comes to the Great Hearth is fed, no questions asked. Canazians are warm, loud, and proud of their mountain.
