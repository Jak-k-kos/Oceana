Cabo Del Thalos is a bare rock fortress at the northernmost tip of the coalition, the last coalition flag before the sea folk waters. It is not a true settlement. No one lives here by choice: the coalition council drafts soldiers from the city-states in turn to man it, making its garrison one of the very few military units that belongs to the coalition council rather than to any single island. Some fifty soldiers watch the dark water from a single stone tower and a ring of cannon emplacements. The posting is cold, lonely, and dreaded. Draftees call it "the Cape" and count the days until the supply boat brings their replacements. At night the garrison lights a green lamp at the top of the tower, the coalition's warning to every ship: beyond this point, you sail at your own risk.
