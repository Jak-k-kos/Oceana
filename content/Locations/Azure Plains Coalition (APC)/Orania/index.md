# Orania
Orania is the last rock of the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]] before the waters of the [[Locations/The Black Tide/index|Black Tide]], a tiny island of bare stone and seabird nests that rises from the sea like a raised fist. On its summit stands a beacon tower, and that tower is the reason anyone lives there at all. The keepers of Orania watch the southern horizon day and night, and when Black Tide sails appear, they light the beacon, and the warning is seen from the southern hills of [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Drakosa/index|Drakosa]] long before the raiders draw near. The rock has been raided more times than its keepers can count, so the tower is built like a fortress, its cisterns kept full and its door barred from within. A handful of families of pilots and lookouts live in its stone huts, supplied by the squadron. They know the reefs of the frontier better than anyone alive, and coalition ships hire them to guide them through the dangerous waters to the south.
