# Eramita
Eramita takes its name from the hermits who first came there to live in quiet, long before the flood, and whose stone cells still dot its cliffs. Somewhere along the way their quiet turned into music, for Eramita is the island of instrument-makers. Wood is far too dear to waste, so its luthiers build from whatever they can find: salvaged timber from [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Marsal/index|Marsal]], shell and horn, goat gut for strings and goatskin for drums. Their fiddles, lutes, drums, horns, whistles and squeezeboxes are played in every tavern and on every forecastle in the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]], and not all of it for pleasure, for Eramita's drums beat time for the gun crews, and its horns and bosun's pipes carry orders across a ship in battle. The island's goats give the strings and skins, and milk and cheese besides, and in the evenings every house on Eramita spills music into the street.
