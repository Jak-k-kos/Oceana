# Galtea
Galtea is a great, sprawling island of green pastures and rolling hills, and it is the dairy of the southeast. Herds of cattle graze its meadows from shore to summit, and every morning and evening the whole island turns out for the milking. The milk is churned into butter, salted and packed into casks that keep for months in a ship's hold, and pressed into great wheels of cheese. Galtea's beef, fresh and salted, feeds crews and larders across the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]], and its hides go west to the cobblers of [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Fok Tara/index|Fok Tara]]. In the river valleys, its farmers grow hay and oats for the herds and barley for bread. Galtea is a prosperous, comfortable island, and its people have broad shoulders, ruddy cheeks, and a love of feasting that the rest of the coalition envies. Off its northwestern shore, a scattering of bare rocks is home to nothing but seabirds.
