# Koras
Koras is a small, rocky island on the frontier, and its fishermen hunt the greatest fish of the southern sea. In summer the swordfish and the great bluefin tuna run past Koras in their hundreds, and the island's harpooners go out after them in narrow boats, standing in the bow with harpoons raised, waiting for a fin to break the water. The catch is salted and smoked and sent north to feed the frontier, and Koras tuna fetches a fine price in every market of the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]]. Koras is also remembered for its siege. Some ten years ago the [[Locations/The Black Tide/index|Black Tide]] came for the island in force, and for days its walls and beaches were fought over in some of the bloodiest fighting the frontier has ever known. The wounded were carried away to [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Del Rosa/index|Del Rosa]] by the shipload, and many who saw the Siege of Koras never spoke of it again. The walls have been rebuilt, the harpooners still go out each dawn, and swordfish bills hang over every door, but no one on the island has forgotten.
