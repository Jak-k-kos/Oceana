# Mirada
Mirada is a tiny, steep rock ringed by wide tidal flats, and the flats are thick with saltwort, a low, fleshy plant that drinks the sea. Its people cut the saltwort at low tide, dry it on the rocks, and burn it in long, low kilns. What comes out is barilla ash, a grey, bitter powder that every glassmaker and soapmaker in the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]] needs. It goes north to the glassworks of [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Melanta/index|Melanta]] and to the soap-boilers of [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Kalisanta/index|Kalisanta]] by the sackful. Smoke drifts off Mirada day and night, and sailors can smell its kilns long before they see the rock.
