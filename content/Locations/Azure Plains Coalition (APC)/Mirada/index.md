# Mirada
Mirada is a tiny, steep rock wrapped in kelp. The beds around it are thick and shallow, and its people cut the kelp from little boats, dry it on the rocks, and burn it in long, low kilns. What comes out is kelp ash, a grey, bitter powder that every glassmaker and soapmaker in the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]] needs. It goes north to the glassworks of [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Melanta/index|Melanta]] and to the soap-boilers of [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Kalisanta/index|Kalisanta]] by the sackful. Smoke drifts off Mirada day and night, and sailors can smell its kilns long before they see the rock.
