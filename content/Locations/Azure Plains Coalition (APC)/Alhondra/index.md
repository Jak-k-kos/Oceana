# Alhondra
Alhondra is a small, rocky islet across the channel from [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Belna/index|Belna]], and nearly every inch of it is covered in warehouses: long, low stone buildings with heavy doors, built shoulder to shoulder along a deep quay. Everything that passes between the western raft cities of the [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/index|Kingdom]] and the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]] waits here, sometimes for days and sometimes for months: [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Insul/index|Insul]] timber and Belna pickles, Kingdom wool and wine, coalition coffee and kelp charcoal. Each warehouse is let by the season and guarded day and night, and the keepers of Alhondra can tell you to the barrel what is stored where and to whom it belongs. A lighthouse on its northern point marks the channel, and the islet's few residents are clerks, porters and watchmen, every one of them paid by Belna.
