# San Lazara
San Lazara is a quiet, whitewashed island that is home to the finest medical college in the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]]. Young healers and surgeons come from every island to study in its lecture halls and wards, and they leave as the most sought-after doctors in the islands. A ship is lucky to have a surgeon who trained at San Lazara, and every captain knows it. The island's hillsides are terraced in medicinal herbs, and its ponds breed the leeches that apothecaries across the coalition buy by the jar. The whole island smells faintly of lavender and vinegar, and its students are always in a hurry.
