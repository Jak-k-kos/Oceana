# Caracona
Caracona is a tiny rock above a shallow bank thick with conch and shellfish. Its few families dive the bank and sell most of the catch dried and salted. Dried conch keeps for months, and ships across the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]] carry it as provisions. Nothing goes to waste. The empty shells are burned in the island's kilns into quicklime, which builders use for mortar and whitewash. The best shells are kept back for the carvers, who turn them into buttons, inlay, and cameos. That luxury trade brings in good money for a few families, but it is lime and dried conch that keep the island going.
