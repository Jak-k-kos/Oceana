# Perla
Perla is a tiny island that glows white on the horizon, for it is made almost entirely of chalk. Its cliffs gleam like a pearl in the sun, and its people quarry the soft white stone into blocks and powder. Builders mix it into whitewash. Tailors and shipwrights mark their work with it, polishers rub silver and brass bright with it, and apothecaries grind it into soothing draughts for sour stomachs. Everything on Perla is dusted white: the houses, the boats, the goats, and the quarrymen themselves, who look like ghosts at the end of every day.
