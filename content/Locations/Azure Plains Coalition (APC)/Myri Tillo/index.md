# Myri Tillo
Myri Tillo is a green, hilly island in the northwestern [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]], covered in wild herbs. Myrtle, thyme, sage, and rosemary grow so thickly on its slopes that the whole island smells of them, and passing sailors can catch the scent before they see land. Its people gather and dry the herbs and sell them to cooks, apothecaries, and healers across the coalition, and they distill the island's myrtle into a dark, bitter liqueur that is drunk at every celebration on the island and almost nowhere else. The town of Myri Tillo is a cluster of stone houses hung with drying bundles, and its market is mostly baskets of leaves. Life here is slow, fragrant, and a little sleepy.
