# Liritos
Liritos is a small island of terraced flower and herb fields, covered in lilies, jasmine, roses, and medicinal plants through the spring and summer. Its distillers work them in copper stills. Some of what they make is perfume and scented oil for the wealthy. Most of it is the island's real trade: tinctures, extracts, salves, distilled essences, and alchemical reagents, sold to apothecaries, healers, and alchemists across the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]]. The whole island smells sweet and a little medicinal, and visitors tend to leave with a headache.
