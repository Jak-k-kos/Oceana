# Fok Tara
Fok Tara is hardly more than a speck of black rock, but it smells, not unpleasantly, of leather and tallow, for it is an island of cobblers. Hides come in by the bale from the ranches of [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Kaliose/index|Kaliose]], and in the island's workshops they are cut, stitched and waxed into the sea boots, shoes, belts and powder pouches that sailors across the southern [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]] wear out by the thousand. A small colony of seals hauls out on the western beaches, and once a year, with prayers to [[Ruthala]] before and after, the cobblers take the few the colony can spare. Sealskin boots are the finest thing Fok Tara makes, supple and warm and utterly waterproof, and there are never enough of them. A sailor who has worn a pair will never want anything else.
