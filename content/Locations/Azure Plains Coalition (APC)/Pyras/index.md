# Pyras
Pyras is a long, narrow island, and its shores are one great boatyard from end to end. Its boatwrights build the small craft the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]] cannot do without: rowboats, skiffs, fishing dories, and the longboats and jolly boats that every ship carries on its deck. They build funeral boats too, simple and sturdy, by the hundred, and ship them to temples across the region that could never build enough on their own. The yards ring with saws and mallets from dawn to dusk, and the whole island smells of fresh shavings and hot pitch. At the far end of Pyras, a small temple of [[Morvaine]] stands at the water's edge, as quiet as the rest of the island is loud, and sailors say every boat Pyras builds carries a little of that quiet in it.
