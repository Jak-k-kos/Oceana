# Lola Las Thea
Lola Las Thea is a flat, sun-bleached island fringed with shallow salt pans, and its salt goes north to [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Drakosa/index|Drakosa]] and to every fishing island on the frontier. Nothing more useful will grow in its thin soil, so the island's broad interior has been given over to the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]] army. Recruits sent south come here first, and on its drill fields and mock gun decks they learn to load and run out a cannon, to work the sails and rigging, and to read every signal, order and drill the army knows, so that when they leave they can step aboard anything from a cutter to a frigate and take their place without a word of explanation. The island echoes with drums and bellowed orders from dawn to dusk, and the salt-rakers of Lola Las Thea long ago stopped looking up when the practice cannons fire.
