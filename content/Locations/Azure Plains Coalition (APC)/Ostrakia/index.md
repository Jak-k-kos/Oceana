Ostrakia is where the shell was born. Generations ago its mint struck the first shell coins, and they proved so reliable that they spread from island to island until they became the common coin of all Oceana. Today only a handful of places in the coalition and the Kingdom are licensed to mint shells, and every one of them answers to the master of the mint on Ostrakia. The island itself is small and fortified: its walls are thick, its harbor is chained at night, and every ship that docks is searched by the mint guard. Inside, the clatter of the coin presses never stops during working hours, and the workers are searched again on their way home. The island has no inns; visitors on business stay in the guardhouse and are escorted everywhere they go. For all that, Ostrakians are fiercely proud of their work: a shell from Ostrakia is a shell you can trust.
