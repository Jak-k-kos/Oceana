# Centros
Centros once had another name, though few remember it now. When it became the hundredth island to join the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]], its people were so proud that they renamed it Centros in honour of the day, and they have kept the anniversary as their great festival ever since. It is a tiny island, and every house on it is a workshop full of needles. Linen comes from [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Halk/index|Halk]], cotton from [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Odelna/index|Odelna]], wool from [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Keylara/index|Keylara]] and canvas from [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Isla Brisa/index|Isla Brisa]], and the tailors of Centros turn it all into the clothes the coalition actually works in: sailors' slops, common clothes, hammocks, sea bags, and the plain coats of the council army. By lamplight they sit in long rows, stitching and gossiping, and a Centros seam is said to outlast the cloth around it.
