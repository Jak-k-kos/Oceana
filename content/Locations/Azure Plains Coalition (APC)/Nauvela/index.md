The whalers have hunted the northern waters for generations, but since the flood stopped, [[Caruilla]]'s sea folk have started treating every hunt as an act of war. Nauvela is a cold, remote island on the far northern edge of the coalition, home to its whalers. Its harbor is crowded with small, sharp whaleboats, and its beaches are lined with try-works, the great iron pots where blubber is boiled down into oil. The smoke and smell hang over the island for most of the year. Nauvelan whale oil burns long and clean and lights lamps across the coalition, and the islanders carve whalebone into combs, tools, and scrimshaw that sailors collect. Whalers are a superstitious, tight-knit people: every boat carries a carved charm to [[Fennara]] and a lantern for [[Flaris]], and no Nauvelan will speak of a hunt before it is finished. The island's taverns are rough, its songs are long, and its people do not welcome questions about the sea folk.
