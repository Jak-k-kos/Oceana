# The Draconic Conclave
Southwest of the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]], across waters few captains will sail without good reason, lies the Draconic Conclave, the realm of the dragons. It is ruled by [[Koruk]], the Wyrmfather, through a council of the eldest dragons, and it bows to no crown and no council of the surface world.

The Conclave keeps to itself. Its dragons no longer raid the world in burning swarms as they did in the elder days, for Koruk keeps his children close beneath his wing. Yet the coalition's border islands live in its shadow all the same. Dragons pass overhead, envoys come calling, and every so often something larger than a fishing boat is lost near its waters and never found.

# Scales on the Docks
The dragons themselves rarely come to trade, but the dragonborn and kobolds of the Conclave do. Their ships put in at the nearest coalition ports, buying olive oil, wine and cloth and paying in gold, gems and the shed scales of dragons, and on islands like [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Korlia/index|Korlia]] scaled faces on the docks are nothing strange. It is a careful trade, conducted politely on both sides, for no one on the border wants to learn what an impolite one would cost.

Not everyone who leaves the Conclave means to go back. Now and then a family of dragonborn or kobolds slips across the border and asks for sanctuary, and the Conclave has a way of sending envoys, ever so politely, to ask for them back.

# Untouched by the Deep
Of all the powers of the world, the Conclave alone pays the sea folk little mind. A lone dragon might be dragged down by a horde from the deep, but since the flood Koruk has kept his children close to home, and to set foot upon the Conclave's shores would be to invite a far greater horde in return. Some dragons have even come to love the water, and so the seas around the Conclave are as dangerous for the sea folk as any shore is for a sailor.

# Old Bargains
The surface has tried to bind the Conclave before. Nearly a thousand years ago, Queen Maelis of Albryn took a dragon for her husband in hopes of winning its friendship, and the Conclave gave her nothing at all ([[The Dragon Queen's Usurpation]]). The Wyrmfather's children do not bargain for crowns.
