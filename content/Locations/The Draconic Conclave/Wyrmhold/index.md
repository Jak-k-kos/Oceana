- Leader: [[Koruk]], through the council of the eldest dragons on the Wyrmcrown
- Food: Grain from the endless fields, cattle, goats and sheep beyond counting, fruit and fish. The dragons take their share of the herds first.
- Trade: Little. The kobold and dragonborn captains trade gold, gems and shed dragon scales for oil, wine and cloth. The Conclave will not sell its grain or its herds, whatever price is offered.
- extra: Size: Huge. Government: The council of the eldest dragons, bound together by Koruk's iron claw. # Wyrmhold
Wyrmhold is the heart of the [[Locations/The Draconic Conclave/index|Draconic Conclave]]: a single vast island stretched long across the sea, greater than any island of the coalition or the Kingdom, and richer than any of them. Its lowlands roll on and on in endless fields of grain, its hills are dotted with herds beyond counting, and its rivers run fat with fish. In a drowned world where every acre is precious, Wyrmhold has more good land than the whole of the coalition put together. It must, for it feeds a nation of dragons.

At its heart rises the Wyrmcrown, a peak so tall it pierces the clouds, its slopes riddled with caverns large enough to swallow a ship whole, and around the island lies a scattering of smaller isles, like broken pieces of a crown. Upon the Wyrmcrown's summit sits the council of the eldest dragons, and it is said that [[Koruk]] himself watches over it.

# The Dragons
Countless dragons make their lairs in Wyrmhold's caverns and peaks, more than anywhere else in the world, for in the wider world they have grown rare. Once the chromatic dragons stood closest to the Wyrmfather's favor. No longer. The metallic dragons and a handful of gem dragons now hold the greater part of the council, and the chromatics brood in their caves over what they have lost. Dragon turtles bask in Wyrmhold's warm shallows, faerie dragons and pseudodragons nest in its sheltered valleys, and the waters around the island are the most dangerous in the world for anything that would come uninvited.

# The Middle Folk
Below the dragons live the dragonborn and the kobolds, the only peoples of mortal shape who dwell on Wyrmhold. They hold no seat of power. They work the endless fields and drive the great herds, tend the dragons' lairs and sail the trading ships, living out their lives in the long shadows of the great wyrms they serve. The harvest is never theirs to sell. Every hungry island along the border looks across the water at those golden fields, and every one of them knows better than to ask. Most of Wyrmhold's folk would call theirs an honored place. Some of those who have fled across the border would call it a gilded cage.
