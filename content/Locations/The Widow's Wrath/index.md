**Type:** Region of open sea **Ruled by:** [[Welestia]]

## The Widow's Wrath

Somewhere out upon the open sea lies a stretch of water that sailors speak of only in lowered voices. They call it the Widow's Wrath, after the goddess whose grief is said to have made it, and no captain in their right mind will sail into it willingly.

## Two Moods, Both Deadly

There is no middle ground within the Wrath. It knows only two moods.

In the first, the storms rage without pause. Hurricanes tear at the sea, waves rise like mountains, and lightning strikes the water day and night until the whole sky seems to burn. Ships caught in the storm are battered, broken and swallowed whole.

In the second, the wind simply dies. The sea goes flat and silent, the sails hang limp, and an empty sky stretches from one horizon to the other. A ship caught in the dead calm can drift for days or weeks without moving an inch, while its water runs out, its food runs low and its crew slowly loses its mind. It is a slower death than the storm, but no less certain.

There is never anything in between. No fair winds blow within the Wrath, and no gentle rains fall. It is fury or stillness, and nothing else.

## The Hidden Danger

What makes the Wrath truly dreaded is that it hides itself. From the outside, its waters look no different from any other stretch of sea. Ships have sailed into it on a fair wind and a clear sky without ever knowing, until the clouds came boiling up from nowhere, or the breeze died away to nothing, and it was already far too late.

Wise captains chart their courses with great care, and give the Wrath the widest berth they can. By the time a ship knows it is in the Widow's Wrath, it is already in its clutches, and there is no escaping them.

## Unchanged

Of late, sailors across the islands have noticed that [[Welestia|Welestia's]] storms are calming, if only a little. The Widow's Wrath has not changed at all. It is as deadly as it has ever been, and no one knows why.

## Ships Lost

Ships that sail into the Widow's Wrath are rarely seen again. Those few that do find their way back out tell of endless storms or endless stillness, and of drifting wrecks glimpsed on the horizon, ships that sailed in long ago and never sailed out. No one has ever crossed it, and no one has ever learned what lies at its heart.

## Rayskaya

At the heart of the Widow's Wrath lies the island of [[Rayskaya]], hidden deep within its storms and dead calms. It sits in the very eye of the storm, the one place in all of [[Welestia|Welestia's]] domain that lightning never touches. There, the skies are clear, the breezes are gentle and the island is lush and green. It is home to the last temple of [[Welestia]].
