# DM Notes
- Leader: None
- Food: Reef fish, crabs, octopus and shellfish
- Trade: None. Its worth is in salvage.
- extra: Beneath [[Locations/The Widow's Wrath/Rayskaya/The Cove|the cove]] of [[Locations/The Widow's Wrath/Rayskaya/index|Rayskaya]] and the waters around the island.
  - The name: No one in the world remembers this city, save [[Tyrus]]. Its name was Rayskaya, and when the water took it, Tyrus kept the old name for the island so that something of it would remain.
  - What it was: The city grew around the foot of the mountain, in the calm where [[Welestia|Welestia's]] storm first fell still, long before the [[Arcanus Collapse]].
  - The great temples: The temples open to everyone stood here, one to [[Welestia]] and one to [[Arkaius]], while the Wedded Temple on the peak was kept for high priests and people of importance. Both great temples lie in the deepest part of the drowned city now.
  - How it died: When [[Arkaius]] fell and [[Welestia|Welestia's]] grief became the [[Locations/The Widow's Wrath/index|Wrath]], the storm closed in around the island. The calm held only near the peak, and the lower city lay outside it. Most of its people fled. Those who stayed died in the storms that tore across the land before it flooded. The city was already empty when the water took it.
  - Depths: The upper streets near the cove lie 10 to 30 feet down, close enough to swim. The middle districts lie 30 to 80 feet down. The great temples lie deeper still, beyond 100 feet, where a diver needs a diving bell or something better.
  - What lives here: Sea creatures only: reef sharks, eels, octopus, and perhaps something larger in the temple district. The sea folk of the Wrath never come here, for the Wrath kills any who come too close.
  - Hooks (use or ignore):
    - The great Temple of [[Arkaius]] may still hold records from before the Collapse.
    - The great statue of [[Welestia]] stands somewhere in the deep. Whether she could ever take it up as a vessel is the DM's call.
    - The people who fled left their homes behind, and some of what they could not carry is still down there.

# The Drowned City
Beneath the glass-still water of [[Locations/The Widow's Wrath/Rayskaya/The Cove|the cove]] lies a city.

Its streets run down the drowned slopes in broad stone avenues, lined with columns and arches and the roofs of a thousand homes. Doorways gape open on empty rooms. Fountains stand dry beneath the sea. Steps climb to nowhere, and statues stand in the squares where they stood when the last of the people walked away. Coral has crept over all of it in a slow tide of color: pink and gold and violet crusting the rooftops, sea fans swaying in the windows, and anemones blooming in the cracks between the paving stones. Fish dart through the streets in glittering shoals, as though they had always lived there.

Near the cove, the city lies so close to the surface that a swimmer can touch the tops of its towers. Further out, it falls away into blue, and then into darkness. On the clearest days, far down at the very edge of sight, two great domes can just be made out on the seabed, side by side.

The world has long forgotten this city and the people who lived here. Only one soul still remembers: the monk who keeps the peak. Its name, [[Tyrus]] will tell you, was Rayskaya. When the sea took it, he gave that name to the island, so that something of the city would remain.
