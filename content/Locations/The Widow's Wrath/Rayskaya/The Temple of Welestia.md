# The Temple of Welestia
Upon the southern peak of [[Locations/The Widow's Wrath/Rayskaya/index|Rayskaya]], where the wind comes clean off the cove and the storm wall of the [[Locations/The Widow's Wrath/index|Wrath]] glimmers on every horizon, stands the Temple of [[Welestia]]. It is a small place, built of dark stone worn smooth by more years than anyone could count, its pillars carved as twisting columns of cloud and its roof open at the center to the sky.

Beneath that opening, at the temple's heart, stands the goddess herself, carved in stone. The statue towers over any mortal who enters. Her hair streams out behind her in a crown of forked and branching lightning, frozen mid-strike, and her blank white eyes gaze out over the cove as if waiting for something only she can see. At her feet sits a wide stone basin, always brimming with fresh rainwater.

Carvings run the length of the inner walls, a long procession of figures worked deep into the dark stone and softened by the ages.

A storm rages across the sky, alone, with nothing beneath it spared and nothing above it daring to come near.

A figure of light sits at a great spinning wheel, sending shining threads streaming up into the sky, and lightning forks down to strike them, shattering them into sparks.

A dragon and the storm do battle above a burning city while tiny figures flee below. The figure of light steps between them with his hands raised.

The figure stands before a great company of shining beings, his mouth open in speech. Among them, a woman with her hair streaming wild bows low, the only one who has bent her head.

The figure walks into the heart of the storm upon a lonely mountain, and where he stands, the storm falls still. Around the foot of that mountain, small figures build their homes in the calm.

He draws a single shining thread from his own breast and ties it into the storm's heart.

A man and a woman stand hand in hand, and around them a ring of shining beings have turned their backs, every one of them.

For the first time on all the walls, the storm lets fall a gentle rain, and green fields rise beneath it.

The storm howls across mountain and shore alike. Forests lie flattened in its wake, villages are scattered like chaff, and great waves hurl themselves against the cliffs. In its heart stands the figure of light with the shining thread drawn taut, unmoved, patient as the mountain beneath his feet.

Upon the mountaintop, the figure of light and a woman crowned in lightning stand hand in hand, with shining figures gathered above and a multitude kneeling below.

Three moons rise together, each holding something in its hands: a pair of scales, a hammer, a lantern.

Beneath three full moons, the figure of light and the woman dance upon the peak while three smaller figures whirl around them.

The figure of light and the storm hover side by side above two young goliaths, alike as reflections. The figure of light holds out a staff, and one goliath reaches up with both hands to take it. From the storm, a sword of forked lightning is held out to the other, but he kneels with his hands flat upon the ground and his head bowed and turned away.

The figure of light and the storm hold out a single key between them, and the young goliath who turned away the sword now takes it.

The last panel waits beside the covered walk to the sealed temple. The figure of light stands alone upon a peak, facing a darkness at the very edge of the world. The carving was never finished. The chisel strokes simply stop, mid-line, and the stone beyond lies smooth and bare.

Whatever tale the carvings tell has long since passed out of memory. No scholar in Oceana could tell you what they mean.

The temple is spotless. Not a leaf lies on its floor, and not a speck of dust gathers on the statue's shoulders. Beside it, joined to it by a short covered walk, stands a second temple of the same dark stone, [[Locations/The Widow's Wrath/Rayskaya/The Sealed Temple|its doors shut fast]].

Few in all of Oceana would believe this place exists. [[Welestia]], so the world says, has no temples and no priests. The world has never been to [[Locations/The Widow's Wrath/Rayskaya/index|Rayskaya]].

# The Story on the Walls
## The Lonely Storm
Before the Weaver came to her, [[Welestia]] raged across the skies alone, a fury with no stillness in it, and no god dared come near her.

## The Torn Threads
[[Arkaius]] sat at his spinning wheel and sent the first threads of magic streaming into the sky, and [[Welestia|Welestia's]] lightning tore them apart. For an age they went on like this, the patient weaver and the furious storm. He never once grew angry with her, and that, more than anything, was what she could not understand.

## The War Over the City
In those days [[Koruk]] was as savage as the chromatic dragons he ruled. When he and [[Welestia]] went to war in the sky, a mortal city burned beneath them. [[Arkaius]] stepped between them and stopped the fighting with his own hands.

## The First Law
From the ashes of that city came the First Law: no god may lay their hand upon the world beyond their own domain. When [[Arkaius]] spoke it before the gods, the proudest and wildest of them all was the first to bow. [[Welestia]] submitted to his law before any other god did, and the rest followed because she had.

## The Eye
[[Arkaius]] walked into the heart of her storm upon a lonely mountain. The lightning would not strike him and the winds would not touch him, and where he stood, the storm fell quiet for the first time since it was born. Mortals came to live in that calm, and around the foot of the mountain grew the city of [[Locations/The Widow's Wrath/Rayskaya/The Drowned City|Rayskaya]]. The mountain is this one. The first eye of her storm stood exactly where the eye stands now.

## The Thread
[[Arkaius]] drew a thread from his own soul and wove it into the heart of her storm, binding her calm to his life. It was his promise: so long as he lived, she would never rage alone again. That is why her grief became the weather of the world. When he died, the thread was cut, and nothing was left to hold her.

## The Gods Turn Away
The other gods opposed the match. Gods took mortal lovers and scattered their children across the world, and they did not wed. Least of all would they see the first among them bound to the wildest. Not one god would bless the union.

## The Gentle Rain
[[Welestia|Welestia's]] answer was a gentle rain, the first she had ever given freely to the world. She sent it softly over the fields of the world, and [[Apiot|Apiot's]] green rose beneath it. It was her gift to the world, in his name, and it was how the gods were won over, one by one.

## The Storm Breaks Loose
Even bound to him, [[Welestia]] was never tamed. Her fury still tore free over sea and land alike, ripping forests from the hillsides, casting villages to the wind and dashing ships against the rocks. Each time, [[Arkaius]] stood in the heart of it with the thread pulled taut, a steady hand until she calmed. He never asked her to be other than she was, only to come back to him.

## The Wedding
They were wed upon this very peak, with the gods gathered above and the faithful of the city kneeling on the slopes below.

## The Moons
From their union rose the three moons, each born with a gift from their father. [[Ellehwin]] received the scales, for law and order. [[Ostin]] received the hammer, for making. [[Winstra]] received the lantern, to light the way for wanderers.

## The First Dance
On the first night all three moons stood full together, the family danced upon this peak: the Weaver and the Storm at the center, and their children whirling around them. It was the first [[The Siblings' Dance|Siblings' Dance]], long before mortals gave it that name. The mourning cults of [[Arkaius]] believe the Dance is the one night he can still hear them. Few would ever guess that the night was his long before it was theirs.

## The Twins
[[Arkaius]] and [[Welestia]] chose two brothers of the city for their service. To [[Tarek]], Arkaius gave a staff, and made him his champion. Welestia offered the other brother, [[Tyrus]], a sword of lightning and the mantle of her champion. He refused it. All he wanted was to tend the place where the two of them had been wed. Arkaius granted both brothers unending youth, so that neither would ever grow old in his service, and for all the long ages that followed, the Weaver's champion and the Warden of the Wedded Temple never aged a day.

## The Key
So the Weaver and the Storm offered him a key together, and made him Warden of the Wedded Temple. He accepted it, and he has kept it ever since. If the party looks closely at the young face in the stone, they may see the man who keeps the temple now, without his scars.

## The Unfinished Panel
[[Tarek]] ordered the last panel carved only days before [[Arkaius]] died. It shows the Weaver alone on this peak, looking out at something dark at the edge of the world. What Tarek knew, or what Arkaius had told him, to make him order it, no one can say. Neither of them ever spoke of it, not even to [[Tyrus]]. The carver was still at work when the day of their deaths came, and the chisel never touched the stone again.
