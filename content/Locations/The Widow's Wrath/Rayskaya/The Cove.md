# DM Notes
- Leader: None
- Food: Reef fish, crabs and shellfish from the shallows
- Trade: None
- extra: In the hollow of [[Locations/The Widow's Wrath/Rayskaya/index|Rayskaya's]] crescent, between the long northern arm and the southern lobe, opening to the east.
  - The dock: [[Tyrus]] built the jetty on the southern shore, right below the peak, in preparation for [[Shady Bellball|Shady]] and companions. [[Welestia]] told him they would come. It holds a single Medium or smaller ship. Large and Huge ships have to drop anchor in the cove.
  - No harbor services: There are no docking fees, no harbor watch and no storage. [[Tyrus]] does not keep watch over the ships.
  - Fresh water: A spring runs down the peak and spills into the cove beside the jetty. The party can refill their water here for free.
  - Anchoring: Anchors dropped in the cove often catch on the drowned rooftops below. Flavor only, with no extra rules.

# The Cove
Cradled in the curve of [[Locations/The Widow's Wrath/Rayskaya/index|Rayskaya's]] crescent, between the long reaching arm of the north and the green swell of the southern peak, lies the cove. Its mouth opens to the east, toward the distant flicker of the storm wall, but within it the water lies as still as a held breath. No swell rolls in from the open sea, and no wind ruffles the surface. Sailors who have spent their lives fighting the waves find the quiet here strange, almost holy.

The water is clear as glass. Look over the side, and [[Locations/The Widow's Wrath/Rayskaya/The Drowned City|the drowned city]] stares back from below: broad streets, toppled columns and the roofs of a thousand homes, all furred with coral and shimmering with darting fish. In places the old towers rise so near the surface that a keel might brush their crowns, and an anchor let fall here as often as not comes to rest on someone's long-forgotten rooftop.

On the southern shore, beneath the shadow of the peak, a single jetty reaches out into the water. It is plainly new. Its stones are freshly fitted and its timbers still pale, the only work on the island not softened by the ages. It is just long enough for one good-sized ship to tie up alongside. Beside it, a spring tumbles down the rocks from the heights above and spills into the cove, cold and sweet.

From the end of the jetty, a stair of old, worn steps climbs the slope toward [[Locations/The Widow's Wrath/Rayskaya/The Temple of Welestia|the temples on the peak]].
