# The Sealed Temple
Beside [[Locations/The Widow's Wrath/Rayskaya/The Temple of Welestia|the Temple of Welestia]], joined to it by a short covered walk, stands its twin: the same dark stone, the same cloud-carved pillars, the same patient weathering of ages. Yet where its sister stands open to the sky, this temple is shut tight.

Its doors are great slabs of dark stone bound in green-black bronze, and they are always locked. Above them, a symbol was once carved into the lintel, but the years have worn it away to a few soft curves that no one could read. Along the threshold, if one looks closely, there is a faint dark stain, as if something had once scorched the stone from the other side.

The monk who keeps the peak will not open these doors for anyone. Ask him whose temple it is, and he will only turn back to his work.

# Within the Sealed Temple
The air within is cool and still, and smells faintly of old smoke. No leaf, no dust, no cobweb mars the floor. Someone keeps this place swept clean. And yet every surface is black.

Lightning has scorched the walls in great branching scars. Beneath the burns, the same carvings that grace the temple next door run the length of the walls, blackened almost beyond seeing. The pillars are charred from floor to ceiling. Candlesticks lie toppled where they fell, a brazier rests on its side, and a carved stone wheel has been split clean in two. Nothing has been set right. Nothing has been moved.

At the temple's heart, where a great statue once stood, there is only a broken plinth and a field of shattered stone. Among the rubble lies a skeleton in armor, old and fine and burned as dark as the stone around it. One blackened hand still reaches toward a staff that lies just beyond its fingers.

# The Brother's Rest
This is the Temple of [[Arkaius]], the Weaver's half of the Wedded Temple, and the place where his champion died.

On the day [[Arkaius]] fell, his champion [[Tarek]] fell here too, before the statue of his god. What struck them down, no one living can say. When [[Tyrus]] came through these doors with his own skin still smoking from the storm outside, he found the statue of the Weaver shattered across the floor and his brother lying among the pieces, his hand stretched toward the staff [[Arkaius]] had given him.

Tyrus has left everything just as he found it. The broken wheel, the toppled candles, the scattered stone of the god's own likeness. Once a week he comes in alone and sweeps away the dust, as his duty demands. He has never moved his brother. He has never mended a single stone. To set it right, he says, would be to say that it is over, and he is not ready to say so.
