# DM Notes
- Leader: None. [[Tyrus]] keeps the peak, and the rest is the party's to claim.
- Food: Figs, citrus and grapes gone wild on the old terraces; reef fish from the cove
- Trade: None
- extra: In the eye of the [[Locations/The Widow's Wrath/index|Widow's Wrath]]. Size: About 30 miles north to south and 25 east to west, a hooked crescent opening to the east, with two small lagoons tucked into its western spine. Government: None.
  - The blessing: Any ship blessed by [[Shady Bellball]] crosses the Wrath untouched, for good, until Shady revokes the blessing. Every other ship meets the Wrath as normal.
  - The Wrath's secret: The [[Locations/The Widow's Wrath/index|Widow's Wrath]] exists to protect this island. Rayskaya sits in the eye because it is what the storm is guarding.
  - The sea folk: The sea folk of the Wrath don't come here. The Wrath kills any of them who come too close.
  - The dock: [[Tyrus]] built the island's only dock, a jetty big enough for a single Medium ship, in preparation for [[Shady Bellball|Shady]] and companions. See [[Locations/The Widow's Wrath/Rayskaya/The Cove|The Cove]].

# Rayskaya
In the heart of the [[Locations/The Widow's Wrath/index|Widow's Wrath]], where the storms break against the sky like waves against a sea wall, there lies a circle of perfect calm. The clouds there part to a sky of clear and endless blue, and beneath it lies Rayskaya.

The island curls upon the sea like a cupped hand. Its broad western spine runs north to south, its long northern arm reaches out across the water like a beckoning finger, and its southern shore swells up into the island's single peak. Cradled between them lies [[Locations/The Widow's Wrath/Rayskaya/The Cove|the cove]], still as glass. Jungle spills down every slope in tumbling waves of leaf and vine, and beneath the green lie [[Locations/The Widow's Wrath/Rayskaya/The Terraces|the old stone terraces]] of a people long gone, heavy now with wild figs, bright citrus and tangled grapevines. Tucked into the western spine, two small lagoons gleam like dropped coins among the trees.

The water of the cove is so clear that a sailor leaning over the rail can see [[Locations/The Widow's Wrath/Rayskaya/The Drowned City|the drowned streets]] far below, their rooftops, columns and broad stone avenues crusted in coral and alive with darting fish. A single jetty juts out from the southern shore, the only sign of a living hand on all the water.

Atop the southern peak, looking down over the cove, stand two small temples of dark weathered stone. [[Locations/The Widow's Wrath/Rayskaya/The Temple of Welestia|One]] belongs to [[Welestia]], the Storm. [[Locations/The Widow's Wrath/Rayskaya/The Sealed Temple|The other]] stands locked and silent. A lone monk keeps them both.

On every horizon the storm wall of the Wrath churns and flickers, endless and furious. Here at its heart, no lightning falls.
