# Hallam County
Hallam is the northern edge of the Kingdom, where the fog rolls in off the Deep and the harbours freeze solid in winter. Northumbra is its bastion, a great island of sheep-downs and weaving towns, its sea fort facing the dark water where the sea folk dwell, and the northern squadron riding at anchor below the walls. Around it lie Frostholm's winter ice, Skelton's bone yards with their arches of whale rib, and the sheltered sound of Halyard with its oyster beds and willow stakes.

Further north the islands grow colder and stranger. Frostmere's ice lasts far longer than any ice should, and beneath its glass-clear heart lies a structure from the Golden Age that no one can reach. Breakholm is the northernmost rock, its goliath watchtower turned forever toward the Deep. Hailsby's knitters click away through the long nights, and the smell of Gelby's glue carries a mile downwind.

### Barony of Skelton
- [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/Tidegate/Hallam county/Skelton/index|Skelton]] (baron's seat): whaling and the bone yards beneath arches of whale rib
- [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/Tidegate/Hallam county/Halyard/index|Halyard]] (baronet): cold-water oysters shipped alive, and a little peat
