# The Duchy of Tidegate
Tidegate is the Kingdom's cold crown, a duchy of fog and wool and grey water, where the harbours freeze in winter and the wind comes down off the Deep with nothing to stop it. Northumbra's sea fort watches the edge of the sea folk's waters, and its northern squadron rides at anchor beneath walls that have never been breached. Everhold, the hold that has never fallen, keeps a full year of grain behind its walls against famine or siege, and the Duchess's court sits above the granaries, unhurried and watchful.

Further out the islands grow wilder. Frostmere's ice lasts far longer than any ice should, and something old lies unreachable beneath it. Breakholm's goliaths keep the watch over the Deep, and away to the east Stormhaven raises its great seawall against the hurricanes. When the storms come, a hundred hulls at a time run in behind that wall to wait them out.
