# Kingsley County
Kingsley is the north's breadbasket and its unbroken heart. Everhold is the hold that has never fallen. Its walls ring the great granaries that always keep a full year's grain against famine or siege, and the Duchess's court sits above them, unhurried and watchful. Its fields of oats, barley and rye roll away beneath the fog. Close by lie the smokehouses of Hensea, the long beach of Towbridge where the bones of ships lie in rows, and Ferring's leather-workers. Queensholm's tea garden grows around the tower where Queen Maelis lived out her days, and from there the funeral boats of House Brassmoor go out burning past the point.

Beyond them lies Sterling, with its silver mines and smithies, the north's richest island. Gillam's rocks are draped in dulse and laver, Rooksdale echoes with its rookeries at dusk, and Barrcove's boatyards shelter behind the sandbar, where amber washes up after the winter storms.

- [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/Tidegate/Kingsley county/Towbridge/index|Towbridge]] (baronet): shipbreaking; timber to the yards and iron to the forges

- [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/Tidegate/Kingsley county/Ferring/index|Ferring]] (baron's seat): boots and leather goods for the northern fleet
- [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/Tidegate/Kingsley county/Queensholm/index|Queensholm]] (baronet): the Queen's tea garden, and House Brassmoor's hall in Queen Maelis's tower
