# Rainsford County
Rainsford lies at the far eastern edge of the north, where the hurricanes strike first and no island can see another across the grey water. Stormhaven is its harbour of refuge. A great curving seawall encloses a deep anchorage where a hundred hulls at a time can ride out a gale, the storm watch reads the sky from the headland, and when it sees a hurricane coming it looses gulls south and west to warn the realm. Whinmoor glows gold with gorse, Kalbrid booms with test-fired muskets, and on Sealholm the seals crowd the rocks while the selkies keep their own corner of the sound.

Further out, Greymantle's spoil heaps sit beneath a mantle of fog, and Sorrel turns red every autumn with its berries. Chalkwell gleams bone-white even in fog, its quarry face carved over with pictures that change every year. Last of all is Sheerwell, whose seabird cliffs drop straight to the sea, and at their foot the sheer well roars and spouts like a whale whenever a storm comes in.
