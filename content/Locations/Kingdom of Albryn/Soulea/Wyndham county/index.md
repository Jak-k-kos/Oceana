# Wyndham County
Wyndham is where the Kingdom comes to learn and where it comes to forget. Lyceum rises green and terraced from the sea, crowned by the halls of the college and the great dome of Alaric's Athenaeum, where the lamps never go out and every noble child of the realm must sooner or later pass its examinations. Around it lie the dairies and dog kennels of Brindle, the mirror-ringed kilns of Welxy, which flash like a scattered handful of coins in the sun, and the lamp-boats of Hythe, hunting squid by night for the scholars' ink.

To the south rides Revelmoor, the navy's liberty port and the Kingdom's second city of pleasure, all theatres, gaming houses and steaming bathhouses, its lanterns burning until dawn. Beyond it lie the tar-boilers of Amberhold, forever watching for fire, and Bluelark, where the Dyers' Guild colours every coat and signal flag in the fleet.
