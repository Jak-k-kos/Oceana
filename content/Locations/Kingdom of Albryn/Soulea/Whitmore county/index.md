# Whitmore County
Whitmore is moored around a wonder. Highwake rides at anchor above a drowned summit just beneath the waves, where the current breaks into a standing wave that never falls, and in its shelter acres of salt pans glitter white across the outer decks. The count's hall rises three tiers above them, Aquilla's great temple stands where the wake breaks, and the duchy's fleets gather in the calm water behind.

Around it ride the working rafts: Silkdeck, the navy's sewing raft, where sea silk is spun from giant pen shells, and Tallowholm, moored well downwind. To the south lie the pepper fields of Dimmock under their eye-watering harvest smoke, Withy's willow beds, the bright citrus terraces of Tidwel, and Whitterby, where divers vanish beneath the waves and come up with their arms full of sponges.
