# Sedgewick County
Sedgewick is a county of rafts, a floating fleet of towns riding the deep water beyond any island's shelter. At its heart rides Highmoor, great enough to hold a fish market that feeds half the Heartland, its dry docks and sail lofts crowding the edges and a single lantern-lit street glowing behind the stalls at night. Around it the smaller rafts keep their own trades: Penfloat's ducks and geese, Harpstra's tall net-looms, the cider and vinegar of Cideraft, and the guarded counting house of Mintra, where every shell in the duchy is struck. Further off, Vellum's flensing ramps run red with the catches too great for anyone else, while Honeyfloat's bees and Roseslip's roses scent the air downwind, and the floating gardens of Mallowmere drift around their lagoon.

## Viscounty of Aldane
### Barony of Highmoor
- [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/Soulea/Sedgewick county/Highmoor/index|Highmoor]] (count's seat): the deep-water fleet and the greatest fish market of the open water; dry docks, chandlers and sail lofts
- [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/Soulea/Sedgewick county/Penfloat/index|Penfloat]] (baronet): ducks and geese in floating pens; eggs, down and quills
### Barony of Harpstra
- [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/Soulea/Sedgewick county/Harpstra/index|Harpstra]] (baron's seat): nets and lines woven on the tall harp-looms
- [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/Soulea/Sedgewick county/Cideraft/index|Cideraft]] (baronet): cider and pickling vinegar
- [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/Soulea/Sedgewick county/Mintra/index|Mintra]] (baronet): Soulea's mint and assay house

## Viscounty of Vesey
### Barony of Vellum
- [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/Soulea/Sedgewick county/Vellum/index|Vellum]] (viscount's seat): the rendering port for great catches, and the duchy's record house
- [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/Soulea/Sedgewick county/Honeyfloat/index|Honeyfloat]] (baronet): bees and honey on shipped-in soil; mead and candles
- [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/Soulea/Sedgewick county/Roseslip/index|Roseslip]] (baronet): rose-scented soap and solar stills
### Barony of Mallowmere
- [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/Soulea/Sedgewick county/Mallowmere/index|Mallowmere]] (baron's seat): floating gardens around a lagoon, and Baron Lisle's marshmallows
- [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/Soulea/Sedgewick county/Sparrowholm/index|Sparrowholm]] (baronet): fish sauce and pickles, and salting for other rafts
