# Ainsworth County
Ainsworth is the Duchess's own county and the Kingdom's first city of pleasure. Marlholm's streets climb toward her palace in a glitter of lanterns, music and the smell of fresh cane juice, and beyond its walls the cane rolls green to every horizon, the crushing mills sending sugar and molasses across the sea. Around it lie the barley and eiderdown of Pellow, Culverton's hundreds of round dovecotes, and Polwick's rock-cut tanks clicking with lobsters at low tide, while Lyndhurst's crown forest stands guarded for the navy's keels.

To the south the islands grow stranger: Ironhyth's black iron-sand beaches, where compasses spin wild for a mile around, Merriby's burning nights, the great white marble bowl of Penhallow where the players perform each summer, and mossy Mossby with its cork oaks. Last of all is Kingsgate, the gate of the Grand Market, its slopes crowded with storehouses and its waters with the long, patient line of ships waiting to be searched before they may sail on to Crownsreach.
