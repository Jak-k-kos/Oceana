# Duskwell County
Duskwell is the iron heart of the Heartland. Ravendell's valleys glow day and night with the Kingdom's greatest forges and steelworks, its sky smudged black with charcoal smoke and wheeling with ravens, and beneath it all the mines run deeper than anyone but the drow would care to follow. Around it lie quieter islands: the rush-beds of Rushcolmbe, the whaling station of Ottrey where the last sea otters swim untouched, and the grey slate quarries of Greylag.

Further south the air smells of woodsmoke and bacon on Hollstour, where the navy's salt pork is cured by the barrel, the ropewalks of Haddon stretch along its terraces, and the chalk cliffs of Whitsan give up the flints that spark every musket in the realm.

## Viscounty of Carrow
### Barony of Ravendell
- [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/Soulea/Duskwell county/Ravendell/index|Ravendell]] (count's seat): the Kingdom's greatest iron mines, forges and steelworks
- [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/Soulea/Duskwell county/Rushcolmbe/index|Rushcolmbe]] (baronet): rushlights, matting and baskets
### Barony of Ottrey
- [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/Soulea/Duskwell county/Ottrey/index|Ottrey]] (baron's seat): whaling, and the last untouched sea otters
- [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/Soulea/Duskwell county/Greylag/index|Greylag]] (baronet): grey slate for roofs, floors and writing slates

- [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/Soulea/Duskwell county/Haddon/index|Haddon]] (baron's seat): hemp rope from the Ropemakers' Guild's ropewalks
- [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/Soulea/Duskwell county/Whitsan/index|Whitsan]] (baronet): gunflints, whiting and chalk
