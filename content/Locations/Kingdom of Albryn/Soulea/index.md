# The Duchy of Soulea
Soulea is the Kingdom's heartland in every sense: the richest of the four duchies in land, the warmest in welcome, and the one every road and sea-lane seems to pass through on its way somewhere else. Cane fields roll green across Marlholm, the great raft of Highmoor rides at anchor like a city that has never once needed the shore, and on Lyceum the Kingdom's noble children learn their letters beneath the old lamps of the Athenaeum.

Further out, the forges of Ravendell glow in their valleys above the drow mines, Highwake keeps the duchy's fleets, and Revelmoor's lanterns burn all night across the water, where the pleasure houses never close and every second guest is listening.
