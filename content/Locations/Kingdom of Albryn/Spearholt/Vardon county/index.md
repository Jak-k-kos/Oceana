# Vardon County
Vardon is the Black Tide front, a long line of fortress rafts and war-workshops strung across hot, dangerous water. At its head rides Fort Resolve. Its gun platforms and chain booms ring the Anchorage, the Prize Court, the Hawksley Quarter and the crowded stalls of Chandlers' Row, and along its outer ring the figureheads of captured pirate ships stand facing the enemy. Behind it work Grimkeel's salvage hulks, Pitchhollow moored far downwind in its fire-walled cells, Gunwall's booming test-fires, and Port Sentinel, whose great lamp marks the safe channel home.

At the southern end, where the front meets the sea folk line, rides Fort Endless. Its magazine hulks are moored well apart, and around its outer walls runs the Endless Roll, the carved names of every marine killed on the front, still growing. Close by lie Grapnel's forges, Slagraf's foundry above its black reef of slag, and Cinderhook, rebuilt on its own charred hulks, where the sharks gather thickest after a battle.
