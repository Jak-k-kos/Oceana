# Sallis County
Sallis is the Kingdom's eastern door, set where the fog of Smuggler's Reach rolls in from the east and the sea folk line closes in from the south. Gellswall is a city in six parts behind its famous wall of chained hulks, built centuries ago by a long-dead Count Gell. There is the Reach Gate where ships are searched, the Reach Market, and the Undermarket everyone knows of and no one admits to. Merchants gather on the Convoy Roads to sail east under escort, and the Eastern Squadron rides at anchor ready to hunt pirates. Near it lies Flagstaff, its signal mast fluttering from dawn to dusk, and Keelhull, where anchors are tested by being dropped and dragged.

Further out lie the county's stranger works. Brackenfloat's sunken green shallows look like floating land from a distance. Breakwater rides behind three rings of its own booms, and Foamhold guards every chart plate in the Kingdom. Seawall's walled fish farms double as fortresses, and last of all Hollowbay sits in its ring of sheltering hulks, the stillest water in the realm.
