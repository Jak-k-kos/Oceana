# Kendrick County
Kendrick is the hot, green south, a county of mangrove coasts, steaming fields and crocodile-haunted water. Southwold is its breadbasket, its rice paddies climbing in terraces from the shore and its mangroves cut in turn for the south's own charcoal. Dunlin's salt pans shimmer pink along the flats, and out near the fog of the Reach, Gravenwall rides at anchor around the great Wall, a warship's hull standing tall on the deck for levies to climb. Wavecrest's mills float on the swell with windmills turning above them, grinding flour for the fleet's biscuit.

Further out, the river of Carrickfall pours off the cliffs in a great fall, a mist rainbow hanging over the mills at its foot every sunny morning. Kittiwake's streams run blue, red or gold by the week beneath cliffs crowded with nesting kittiwakes, and Ebbsfell's palms crown the fell above beaches strewn with coconut husks.
