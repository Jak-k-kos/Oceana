# Quintrell County
Quintrell lies in the hot open water close to the sea folk line, a county of shipwrights and reserve hulls. Fleetmoor is its heart. There the Carpenters' Hall trains the masters every ship needs, Bellrow's taverns crowd the quays, and the Ordinary stretches away in long, quiet rows of warships with their masts struck, waiting for war. A squadron rotated from Fort Resolve watches the sea folk line, and a ring of old gun hulks guards the approach. Out at the edge of the deep, Fathom grows a little every year as new deck sections are lashed on for veterans, and Oarlock's mills turn out blocks to one pattern for the whole fleet.

Further off, Sternhold's powder mills ride in sealed cells well apart from the town, and Sparwell's slips always hold a ship laid over on her side for scraping and sheathing. Shoalwell rides above a pale sand bank where children wade among the conch beds at low water, and Lowhelm rides so low that the sea washes across its decks at every high tide.
