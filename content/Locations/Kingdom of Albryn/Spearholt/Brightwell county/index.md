# Brightwell County
Brightwell is the broken mountain. In the flood, Roktra heaved a great peak up against the tide and it tore itself apart, and the four great pieces of its rim still stand around a drowned crater so warm and deep that sailors simply call it "inside". Thornehallow is the largest, rich with ages of weathered ash, its terraced olive groves and pease fields threaded through with the first Thorne's hedges, now grown into green walls. Glenmoor is the most alive: magma runs through its trenches, Roktra's temple stands on stilts above the opening, and a temple to every god in the pantheon crowds the streets around it. Morrowcliff oozes and smokes and gives up sulfur, ash, lead and gold, and Kinross lies dead and cold, its cliffs cut open to show the mountain's banded heart.

Around them lie the smaller pieces the blast threw out. Asterby keeps its smoky wine and its silk-houses, and Farholm glows red at night with its undying kilns. Torholm is made of rock so light it floats, Wescote is Crownsreach's garden, and Weirheart drowns and drains with every high tide, its weirs teeming with eels.
