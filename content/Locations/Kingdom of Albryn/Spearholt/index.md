# The Duchy of Spearholt
Spearholt is the Kingdom's hot southern shield, a duchy of fortress rafts, steaming islands and warm, dangerous water. Along its western edge the Black Tide front stretches from the shadow of the Widow's Wrath down to the sea folk line. There, Fort Resolve and Fort Endless ride at anchor with their gun platforms and chain booms, and the figureheads of captured pirate ships stare out at the enemy. To the east lies Gellswall, the realm's eastern door, its wall of chained hulks closing every dusk against the fog of Smuggler's Reach.

Between them are the working islands that keep the war fed and armed: Fleetmoor's carpenters and its silent rows of reserve warships, Southwold's terraced rice paddies and mangrove coasts, the thundering waterfall of Carrickfall, and at the duchy's heart, the broken mountain of Brightwell, where a god once raised a peak against the flood.
