# DM Notes
- Leader: None of its own. [[Count Corwin Fairleigh]] governs it as part of the city, and the palace guard of [[Marquess Brannagh Stonewell]] keeps its gates
- Food: The finest tables in the Kingdom: roast swan, spiced wine, sugar-work and coalition coffee
- Trade: None. It is manors, walled gardens and the private bank of House [[Lucius Vantreese|Vantreese]]
- extra: All four dukes keep manors here, [[Duchess Seraphine Marlowe|Seraphine's]] the grandest and [[Duchess Aveline Caldwell|Aveline's]] the plainest, along with nearly every count and viscount and wealthy commoner families such as House Vantreese. The Highwards gates close at dusk to anyone without a manor's token.

# Highwards
The Highwards climb the hill of [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/Crownsreach/index|Crownsreach]] between the clamour of the [[Grand Market]] and the blue-veined walls of the [[The Azure Palace|Azure Palace]], a district of white stone manors, walled gardens and quiet, lamp-lit streets. It is the grandest noble quarter in the [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/index|Kingdom]]. Each of the four great dukes keeps a house here, and so does nearly every count and viscount who hopes to be remembered at court, along with a few commoner families rich enough to buy a place among them. In the evenings the Highwards glitter with feasts and music, and more of the Kingdom's business is settled across their dinner tables than in any hall of the palace. Twice each year, when the outer council gathers at the palace, every manor on the hill is thrown open and the whole district celebrates: in spring around [[Apiot's Promise]], with music and blossoms for the year to come, and again in autumn, when the realm's taxes and tributes come up the hill in a procession of carts and ceremony. Its gates close at dusk, and after that no one climbs the hill without a reason and a token to prove it.
