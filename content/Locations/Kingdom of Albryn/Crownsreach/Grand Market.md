# DM Notes
- Leader: [[Count Corwin Fairleigh]], with [[Ledger]] as his Keeper of the Quays
- Food: Every port's cooking at once: smoked eel, coalition coffee, pepperpot, sugared nuts and mutton pies
- Trade: Everything. Crown customs are taken on every cargo, and the house of [[Lucius Vantreese|Vantreese]] lends against half of them
- extra: Now and then a [[Locations/The Draconic Conclave/index|Conclave]] ship docks here, or a trader from one of the lawful islands of [[Locations/Smuggler's Reach/index|Smuggler's Reach]], allowed in so long as it breaks no law and is not a known pirate. Any dispute on the quays goes to Ledger first, and to the count's Counting House if it cannot be settled there.

# Grand Market
The Grand Market spreads along the harbor of [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/Crownsreach/index|Crownsreach]] in a tangle of quays, warehouses and canvas stalls that never truly closes. It is one of the busiest harbors in the known world. Coalition coffee and kelp charcoal change hands beside Kingdom wool and steel, ships from every corner of Oceana tie up hull to hull, criers shout prices in a dozen accents, cranes swing cargo over the heads of the crowd, and the crown's customs men take their share of everything that comes ashore. Above it all stands the Counting House, where the Count of the City keeps his ledgers. It is said that anything in the world can be bought in the Grand Market, if you know whom to ask and are willing to pay.
