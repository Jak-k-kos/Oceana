# DM Notes
- Leader: [[King Edwin Albryn]]
- Food: Mutton pie, salt fish, brown bread, strong tea, ale, and something from every port in Oceana in the market stalls
- Trade: The royal dockyards, the greatest shipbuilders in Oceana; the Grand Market, one of the busiest trade hubs in the world; and wool and mutton from the crown estates
- extra: Map #62. Size: Large. Government: The crown alone. No lord holds land beneath the king here; the crown estates are run by royal stewards, though most powerful nobles keep a manor in the Highwards. Now and then a [[Locations/The Draconic Conclave/index|Conclave]] ship docks at the Grand Market, or a trader from one of the honest islands of [[Locations/Smuggler's Reach/index|Smuggler's Reach]], allowed in so long as it breaks no law and is not a known pirate. The Royal Mint strikes the Kingdom's shells under licence from [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Ostrakia/index|Ostrakia]]. Like the rest of the Kingdom, Crownsreach keeps no great prison: its convicts wait in the harbor gaol until the black ships carry them to [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Isla Embra/index|Isla Embra]] or [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Sparte Torro/index|Sparte Torro]].

# Crownsreach
Crownsreach rises from the heart of the [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/index|Kingdom of Albryn]] like a crown set down upon the sea, all pale stone and slate roofs stepping up from the harbor to the palace on the hill. It is the only island in the realm that belongs to the king alone, and the great duchies ring its waters on every side. Its dockyards are the greatest in Oceana, their slipways never empty, and in the deepest berths lie the Kingdom's rare steam warships, black-hulled and patient. Below the yards sprawls the Grand Market, one of the busiest harbors in the known world, where ships from every corner of Oceana tie up side by side and coalition coffee changes hands beside Kingdom wool and steel. Above the market climb the Highwards, the grandest noble district in the realm, where nearly every duke, count and viscount of consequence keeps a manor, along with a handful of commoner families rich enough to buy their way up the hill. Beyond the city walls the rest of the island rolls away in the crown's own estates, green sheep downs and orchards and wheat fields worked by tenant farmers under the king's stewards, for no lord holds an acre here but the king. And at the very top of the hill stands the palace, where [[King Edwin Albryn]], thirteen years old, rules a realm he has never been taught to govern.
