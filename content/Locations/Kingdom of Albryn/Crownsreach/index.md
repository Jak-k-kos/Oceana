# DM Notes
- Leader: [[King Edwin Albryn]]; the city and the Grand Market are governed in the crown's name by [[Count Corwin Fairleigh]]
- Food: Mutton pie, salt fish, brown bread, strong tea, ale, and something from every port in Oceana in the market stalls
- Trade: The royal dockyards, the greatest shipbuilders in Oceana; the [[Grand Market]], one of the busiest trade hubs in the world; wool and mutton from the crown estates
- extra: Unnumbered. Size: Large. Government: The crown. Count Fairleigh is the only titled lord who serves on the island, and he holds none of its land; the crown estates are run by [[Bram Hollowell]], Steward of the Crown Estates, and most powerful nobles keep a manor in the [[Highwards]]. History: the island was the seat of the Azure Empire, and when the empire fell in 1325, [[The Fall of the Azure Empire|Alaric Albryn]] was crowned in its throne room; the kings of Albryn have ruled from the [[The Azure Palace|Azure Palace]] ever since. In the [[Locations/The Black Tide/index|Black Tide]]'s heyday Crownsreach was a border island and was nearly lost more than once, held by the skin of the Kingdom's teeth until [[The Southern Reclamation]] pushed the border far to the south. Home Fleet: nearly every ship of the Home Fleet is rotated through the front lines against the [[Sea Folk Waters|sea folk]] and the Black Tide, for even a weakened Tide takes every hull the Kingdom has; only the royal flagship, the finest steam warship in the realm, stays at Crownsreach with a handful of customs cutters and a few galleons, and even those are rotated out so the crews stay sharp. Councils: the inner council (the king, the four dukes and [[Crown Marquess Tavrek Brassmoor|the Crown Marquess]]) sits at the palace for every official decree. The outer council, which adds the High Marquesses and every count, gathers here twice a year: in spring around [[Apiot's Promise]], and in autumn after the harvests, when the realm's taxes and tributes are delivered. The Royal Mint strikes the Kingdom's shells under licence from [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Ostrakia/index|Ostrakia]]. Crownsreach keeps no great prison: its convicts wait in the harbor gaol until crown prison ships carry them to the border, where the coalition's black ships make pre-arranged pickups at a handful of Kingdom border islands and raft cities, bound for [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Isla Embra/index|Isla Embra]] or [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Sparte Torro/index|Sparte Torro]].

# Crownsreach
Crownsreach rises from the heart of the [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/index|Kingdom of Albryn]] like a crown set down upon the sea, all pale stone and slate roofs stepping up from the harbor to the palace on the hill. It was the seat of the Azure Empire before there was ever an Albryn, and when the empire fell, the first king of Albryn was crowned in its throne room. In the long years when the [[Locations/The Black Tide/index|Black Tide]] was at its height, the island stood on the very edge of the border and was nearly lost more than once, and the old scars of those sieges still pock its sea walls. Now the border lies far to the south, and Crownsreach is the only island in the realm that belongs to the king alone, with the great duchies ringing its waters on every side.

Its dockyards are the greatest in Oceana, their slipways never empty, and the Kingdom's rare steam warships are fitted out in the deepest berths, their tall funnels breathing lazy smoke over the harbor. Below the yards sprawls the [[Grand Market]], one of the busiest harbors in the known world. Above it climb the [[Highwards]], where nearly every noble of consequence keeps a manor. And at the summit stands the [[The Azure Palace|Azure Palace]], its pale walls veined in vivid blue, where [[King Edwin Albryn]], thirteen years old, rules a realm he has never been taught to govern. Beyond the city walls the rest of the island rolls away in the crown's own estates, green sheep downs and orchards and wheat fields, for no lord holds an acre here but the king.
