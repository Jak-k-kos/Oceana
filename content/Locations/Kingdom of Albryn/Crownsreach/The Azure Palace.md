# DM Notes
- Leader: [[King Edwin Albryn]]; the palace guard answers to [[Marquess Brannagh Stonewell]]
- Food: The royal kitchens: anything the king asks for, and he asks often
- Trade: None. It is the seat of the crown and of the inner council
- extra: Built by the Azure Empire and held by Albryn since its founding. The king and queen were murdered here two years ago, when the palace knights failed and the Crown Marquess's marines had to storm the city to finish what the knights could not. The great gates are kept open by day as a show of the founding law. Edwin's rooms are the most guarded in the realm.

# The Azure Palace
The Azure Palace crowns the hill of [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/Crownsreach/index|Crownsreach]], and there is no other building like it in all of Oceana. Its walls were raised by the Azure Empire in pale grey stone, and every joint between those stones was laid in mortar mixed with ground lapis, so that the whole palace is netted in veins of vivid blue that glow like the sea at noon and darken to deep indigo at dusk. It was from this palace that the empire shut its gates against the drowning tribes, and it was in its throne room that those same tribes crowned [[The Fall of the Azure Empire|Alaric Albryn]] their first king. By old custom its great gates now stand open from dawn to dusk, in memory of the gates that were once closed. Somewhere within, in rooms too large for him, the boy king [[King Edwin Albryn]] eats his sugared almonds and signs what he is given.
