# The Duchy of Silvater
Silvater is the Kingdom's western shore and its open hand to the coalition, a duchy that grew rich by standing in the doorway. Its northern isles are cool and green and smell of oats and woodsmoke; its middle county is all chimneys and foundries, ringing with bells; and its southern waters belong to the rafts, moored in the shadow of the Widow's Wrath, where the storm has turned and turned for longer than anyone remembers.

Everything that passes between the coalition and the Kingdom seems to pass through Silvater sooner or later, and a little of it always stays behind. Its harbors are crowded, its markets loud, and its lords forever counting.
