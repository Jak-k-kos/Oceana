# Calder County
Calder smokes and rings and hammers from dawn to dusk. Pendra's valleys are lined with refineries and distilleries, the sheds of Pellam turn out barrels by the thousand, and when the wind is right the whole county hums with the bells of Bellmoor. Yet there are gentler corners too: the vineyards of Padstow on their sunny slopes, the physic gardens of Cress Hallow, and the salt meadows of Ebbing where the lambs graze on grass the spring tides have kissed.

## Viscounty of Thackeray
### Barony of Pendra
- [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/Silvater/Calder county/Pendra/index|Pendra]] (duke's seat, count's seat): manufactories, refineries and distilleries beneath the duke's estate; House Corbin's hall
### Barony of Pellam
- [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/Silvater/Calder county/Ebbing/index|Ebbing]] (baronet): salt-marsh lamb and cockle beds
- [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/Silvater/Calder county/Pellam/index|Pellam]] (baron's seat): the cooperage of the Kingdom
### Barony of Padstow
- [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/Silvater/Calder county/Padstow/index|Padstow]] (baron's seat): cool-climate vineyards and a prized island wine
- [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/Silvater/Calder county/Bellmoor/index|Bellmoor]] (baronet): the great foundry, famous for its bells

## Viscounty of McGibbon
### Barony of Gandell
- [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/Silvater/Calder county/Bramnby/index|Bramnby]] (baronet): cattle, a tannery, leather and salt beef

### Barony of Southwick
- [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/Silvater/Calder county/Southwick/index|Southwick]] (baron's seat): the masons' hall and school of the West
- [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/Silvater/Calder county/Dunholt/index|Dunholt]] (baronet): dunes and glassworks
