# Vane County
Vane is a county of rafts, a floating realm moored between Belna and the endless storm of the Widow's Wrath. Widow's Berth sprawls across the water like a city that forgot to stop at the shore: fleet anchorage, insurance exchange, market and mending docks all lashed together. Around it ride the lesser rafts: Cloudwake with its dark ales, Stormedge with its forest of windmills, Driftholm moored above a drowned city far older than any empire, and Kelark, where the navigators learn to read the Wrath's moods.

## Viscounty of Sallow
### Barony of Widow's Berth
- [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/Silvater/Vane county/Widow's Berth/index|Widow's Berth]] (count's seat): the Squadron Roads, the Widows' Exchange, the Gate Market and the Mending Docks; House Rennick's hall
### Barony of Cloudwake
- [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/Silvater/Vane county/Cloudwake/index|Cloudwake]] (baron's seat): the brewers of the rafts, hold ale and dark ale
- [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/Silvater/Vane county/Stormedge/index|Stormedge]] (baronet): windmills and ship's biscuit, and salvage from the storm
### Barony of Ropewalk
- [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/Silvater/Vane county/Ropewalk/index|Ropewalk]] (baron's seat): mussels, oysters and penned fish, the county's larder
- [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/Silvater/Vane county/Gullmoor/index|Gullmoor]] (baronet): sardines and anchovies, and the great gull flocks
