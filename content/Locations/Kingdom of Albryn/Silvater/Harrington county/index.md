# Harrington County
Harrington is the West's cold crown, a scatter of grey-green isles where the wind smells of salt and woodsmoke. Its harbor at Greyhal holds the gate between the coalition and the Kingdom, the great chain lying slack across the water until a ship has paid its due. Inland the county is oats and apples, gorse honey and quarried stone; out on its northern edge the kilns of Ashby breathe their haze over the kelp beds, and the drying racks of Merrow rattle with stockfish beneath the skulls of the Merrowbane's foes.

- [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/Silvater/Harrington county/Elmstead/index|Elmstead]] (baronet): quarry stone and elm wood
- [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/Silvater/Harrington county/Greyhal/index|Greyhal]] (count's seat): the customs harbor, the toll-chain, salt pans and the prison hulk

- [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/Silvater/Harrington county/Wrenhold/index|Wrenhold]] (viscount's seat): supply port for the northern patrols, seabird eggs and guano

- [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/Silvater/Harrington county/Merrow/index|Merrow]] (baron's seat): stockfish, cod and ling, and the merrow skulls of the town hall
- [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/Silvater/Harrington county/Linnet/index|Linnet]] (baronet): flax and sailcloth known up and down the West
