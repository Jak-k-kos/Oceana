# Ship Upgrades
***
***
Most upgrades cannot be placed on small ships. please contact your local harbor master for details on upgrading you ship. market price may or may not reflect the price given by your local harbor masters. for arcane upgrades please visit your nearest mage tower so they may tell you no themselves. terms and conditions apply
## railing
---
## Boarding Nets
Market Value: 8000 SH
Desc: Climbing the ship's sides is difficult terrain. A creature that reaches the rail must pass a DC 13 Strength (Athletics) check or be restrained until it uses an action to cut or wriggle free. Each 10-ft section of net: AC 10, 5 HP.

## Raised Railing
Market Value: 25000 SH
Desc:  Creatures on the top deck have three-quarters cover instead of half. Boarders must climb an extra 10 ft. The cover doesn't apply against high-arc weapons.

## Sheer Rails
Market Value: 18000 SH
Desc: Grappling attempts against this ship are at disadvantage, and the DC to winch it closer goes up by 5.

## Life Netting
Market Value: 8000 SH
Desc: Netting across the railing gives advantage against saving throws against being thrown overboard

## Fire Troughs
Market Value: 10000 SH
Desc: Troughs of sea water and buckets along the rails aid in fighting fires on the ship. when a crew member uses their action to fight a fire, the fire damage is reduced by 2d6 instead of 1d6

## Corvus
Market Value: 22000 SH
Desc: hinged spiked boards are placed onto the railings of your ship. crew members may make an operate crew action to drop it onto any adjacent ship no bigger one size larger than their own using an attack roll against the other ships AC. the ships are considered grappled, until the plank is destroyed the plank is considered normal terrain for boarding and has AC 15 and 40 HP.

## Plating
---
## Spiked Plating
Market Value: 30000 SH
Desc: the sides of your ship count as difficult terrain. Any Creature Climbing the ships sides takes 1d6 piercing DMG for every 5 feet traveled.

## Double Reinforcement
Market Value: 50000 SH
Desc: +10 Damage Threshold. The ship's initiative drops by 2, and it looses the agile trait, if it doesn't have the agile trait, sail crew action dc is increased by 2.

## Iron Straps
Market Value: 40000 SH
Desc: +2 AC. the ships initiative drops by 2 and it looses the agile trait, if it doesn't have the agile trait, it gains cumbersome, if it already has cumbersome sail crew action dc is increased by 2.

## Slick Sheeting
Market Value: 20000 SH
Desc: +3 to initiative. if the ship has the cumbersome trait it looses it, if it doesn't have the cumbersome trait, it gains the agile trait, if it already has the agile trait it gains another +2 to initiative. AC is decreased by 1.

## Reinforced Cutwater
Market Value: 15000 SH
Desc: hazards in the water no longer count as difficult terrain. all damage from terrain based hazards is halved.


## structural
---
## Reinforced Bracing
Market Value: 30000 SH
Desc: a ships maximum speed isn't halved until it hits 1/4 max hull points

## Water Tight Bulkheads
Market Value: 30000 SH
Desc: The ships minimum Hull points is now -3/4 its max HP.

## Chain Pumps
Market Value: 20000 SH
Desc: while one creature uses the operate crew action (DC 10 STR), sinking damage is halved

## Thickened Body
Market Value: 40000 SH
Desc: -5 Damage Threshold, +3 hull dice, increase max hull points to match

## Added Masts
Market Value: 350000 SH
Desc: gain an extra 4 sail stations, increase max speed to match. -5 damage threshold.

## Improved Sails
Market Value: 15000 SH
Desc: Halves the speed penalty from sailing into the wind, doubles speed bonus granted from the wind. Sail DC +2

## Gun Deck Expansion
Market Value: 35000 SH
Desc: The ships cargo space is torn down for more guns. weapons slots are increased by 25%, rounded down (minimum +1). its tonnage is halved and its damage threshold decreases by 5.
## Light Mods
---
## Smugglers Hold
Market Value: 15000 SH
Desc: Create a hold capable of hiding 1/10 the maximum tonnage of the vessel. can only be found with a DC 20 Investigation check. 

## Deck Grip
Market Value: 15000SH
Desc: -2 DC for being knocked prone on your deck. weather conditions only increase the sail dc by 2 instead of 3

## Inspiring Figurehead
Market Value: 60000 SH
Desc: Once per turn, a failed crew action is counted as a success instead.

## False Flags
Market Value: 5000 SH
Desc: a chest of expertly made flags and name boards is added to your ship. while flying a false flag, it requires a DC 15 insight or investigation check to identify the markings as false.
## Observation Well
Market Value: 8000 SH
Desc: A glass dome is installed into the hull of the ship allowing a creature to see 60' underwater in clear water. when doing so the creature gains advantage on perception checks to see things underwater
## Gnomish Far seer
Market Value: 8000 SH
Desc: a gnomish far seer is added to your crows nest giving advantage to all perception and investigations made by creatures in the crows nest using the far seer. greatly increase the range of sight from the crows nest while using the far seer.

## Life Lines
Market Value: 500 SH per lifeline
Desc: Sturdy ropes are tied along the masts and railings. a creature can use an action to tie themselves or another to a life line making them immune to being thrown overboard. while tied down a creature cannot move more than 20' away from the tie down point

## Dazzle Paint 
Market Value: 5000 SH
Desc: The first ship weapon attack from every new enemy ship in a combat is made at disadvantage. only one paint may be used on a ship at a time.

## Dusk Paint
Market Value: 5000SH
Desc: Gain advantage on stealth checks in fog, rain, or darkness. only one paint may be used on a ship at a time.

## Sea Glass Paint
Market Value: 5000SH
Desc: Gain advantage on stealth checks while in broad daylight. only one paint may be used on a ship at a time.

## Dread Seekers Paint
Market Value: 5000SH
Desc: all crew of this ship gain advantage on intimidation checks against non crew members while on or next to this ship, any intimidation checks made using flag code are made with advantage. only one paint may be used on a ship at a time

## Bitter Wash Paint
Market Value: 5000SH
Desc: Creatures that attempt to Bite or use another natural weapon on the ship make a DC 12 Constitution save, taking 3d4 Poision damage on a failed save and half as much on a success. only one paint may be used on a ship at a time

## Harbor Livery Paint
Market Value: 5000SH
Desc: Crew Members gain advantage on persuasion checks while on or next to their boat. only one paint may be used on a ship at a time

## Glow Line Paint
Market Value: 5000SH
Desc: Gain advantage to survival checks to fish while at sea. creatures and ships gain disadvantage while trying to sneak up to the hull of the ship. the ship gains disadvantage to stealth checks. only one paint may be used on a ship at a time

## Hot Rod Paint
Market Value: 500SH
Desc: You'll feel like your going a lot faster with this paint. only one paint may be used on a ship at a time
