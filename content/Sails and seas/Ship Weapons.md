# Weapon Upgrades
***
All ship weapons are martial weapons. Each weapon is mounted on a side of the ship (bow, port, starboard or stern) and can only target things in that side's quadrant, marked by lines drawn at 45 degrees from the ship's corners. The bow and stern can each hold only one weapon, no matter how many slots it uses. Port and starboard can hold as many as fit in the ship's Weapon Slots.

## Ranged Weapons
---
### Cannon
AC: 19
HP: 55
DMG Immunities: Poison, Psychic
Weapon Slots: 1
Market Price: 140,000 SH
Desc: Large object. A gunpowder cannon, the backbone of most ships' firepower. Loading it takes one creature's full action, then another creature can take the Operate Crew Action to fire it.
#### Ammunition:
* Cannonball: Ranged Weapon Attack: range 600/2,400 ft., one target. Hit: 44 (8d10) bludgeoning damage.
* Chain Shot: Ranged Weapon Attack: range 100/150 ft., one target. Hit: 22 (4d10) bludgeoning damage, and one of the target's Sail Stations can't be used until a crew member succeeds on a DC 10 Repair action to fix it. That repair spends a Hull Die but restores no Hull Points.
* Grapeshot: Choose a point within 80 ft. Each creature in a 10 ft. radius of that point takes 14 (4d6) piercing damage, or half as much on a successful DC 13 Dexterity saving throw.
* Smoke Bomb: Fire at a point within 1,000 ft. It bursts into a 30 ft. radius cloud that heavily obscures the area for 1 minute. A medium wind clears it in 4 rounds, a heavy wind in 1 round.

### Long Gun
AC: 19
HP: 50
DMG Immunities: Poison, Psychic
Weapon Slots: 1
Market Price: 120,000 SH
Desc: Large object. A long, narrow-bored cannon, often mounted as a bow or stern chaser. It fires the same ammunition as a cannon. The longer barrel throws everything farther, but the lighter bore hits with less force. Loading it takes one creature's full action, then another creature can take the Operate Crew Action to fire it.
#### Ammunition:
* Cannonball: Ranged Weapon Attack: range 800/3,200 ft., one target. Hit: 33 (6d10) bludgeoning damage.
* Chain Shot: Ranged Weapon Attack: range 200/300 ft., one target. Hit: 22 (4d10) bludgeoning damage, and one of the target's Sail Stations can't be used until a crew member succeeds on a DC 10 Repair action to fix it. That repair spends a Hull Die but restores no Hull Points.
* Grapeshot: Choose a point within 120 ft. Each creature in a 10 ft. radius of that point takes 14 (4d6) piercing damage, or half as much on a successful DC 13 Dexterity saving throw.
* Smoke Bomb: Fire at a point within 1,500 ft. It bursts into a 30 ft. radius cloud that heavily obscures the area for 1 minute. A medium wind clears it in 4 rounds, a heavy wind in 1 round.

### Swivel Gun
AC: 15
HP: 20
DMG Immunities: Poison, Psychic
Weapon Slots: 0 (railing mounted; Small ships up to 2, Medium up to 4, Large up to 6, Huge up to 8)
Market Price: 24,000 SH
Desc: Medium object. A small cannon on a pivot mount set into the rail. It's light enough that one creature can load and fire it with a single Operate Crew Action. It can be mounted on any side, including the bow or stern, without counting as that side's one weapon. It's the only gun most small boats can carry.
#### Ammunition:
* Swivel Shot: Ranged Weapon Attack: range 120/480 ft., one target. Hit: 11 (2d10) bludgeoning damage.
* Swivel Grape: Choose a point within 60 ft. Each creature in a 5 ft. radius of that point takes 7 (2d6) piercing damage, or half as much on a successful DC 12 Dexterity saving throw.

### Ballista
AC: 15
HP: 30
DMG Immunities: Poison, Psychic
Weapon Slots: 1
Market Price: 72,000 SH
Desc: Large object. A giant crossbow that fires heavy bolts, the budget choice for ships that can't afford cannons. Loading and aiming it takes one action before it can be fired with the Operate Crew Action.
#### Ammunition:
* Bolt: Ranged Weapon Attack: range 120/480 ft., one target. Hit: 17 (3d10) piercing damage.
* Flaming Bolt: Ranged Weapon Attack: range 120/480 ft., one target. Hit: A wooden or otherwise flammable ship catches fire. At the start of its turn it takes 7 (2d6) fire damage, and the fire grows by 1d6 each turn after that. See [[Damage and Repair#Fire|Fire]].

### Harpoon Gun
AC: 15
HP: 30
DMG Immunities: Poison, Psychic
Weapon Slots: 1
Market Price: 72,000 SH
Desc: Large object. A heavy barbed harpoon on a ballista-like mount, tied to a coiled line, used for hunting sea monsters and sea dragons. Loading it takes one action before it can be fired with the Operate Crew Action. The line has AC 12 and 20 HP, and a creature can cut it with an action.
#### Ammunition:
* Harpoon: Ranged Weapon Attack: range 90/180 ft., one target. Hit: 16 (3d10) piercing damage. If the target is a creature, it is grappled by the line (escape DC 15) and can't move farther than 180 ft. from the ship. As an action, a crew member can make a DC 15 Strength check to reel a Large or smaller creature 10 ft. closer. A Huge or larger creature can instead drag the ship with it when it moves, at half its speed.

### Catapult
AC: 15
HP: 70
DMG Immunities: Poison, Psychic
Weapon Slots: 2
Market Price: 38,000 SH
Desc: Huge object. A ship-mounted catapult that throws heavy loads in a high arc. Loading it takes one action before it can be fired with the Operate Crew Action.
#### Ammunition:
* Catapult Stone: Ranged Weapon Attack: range 200/800 ft. (can't hit targets within 60 ft.), one target. Hit: 27 (5d10) bludgeoning damage.

### Mortar
AC: 17
HP: 60
DMG Immunities: Poison, Psychic
Weapon Slots: 2 (Large ships can mount 1, Huge ships up to 2)
Market Price: 190,000 SH
Desc: Huge object. A squat, thick-barreled gun on an iron turntable bolted to the top deck, lobbing shells in a steep arc. It isn't mounted on a side: it can target in any direction and doesn't count as the bow or stern weapon. Loading it takes one creature's full action, then another creature can take the Operate Crew Action to fire it. Because its shells come down from above, its attacks ignore cover from rails, walls and Raised Railing. It can't hit targets within 100 ft.
#### Ammunition:
* Mortar Shell: Ranged Weapon Attack: range 400/1,600 ft., one target. Hit: 38 (7d10) bludgeoning damage.
* Burst Shell: Ranged Weapon Attack: range 400/1,600 ft., one target. Hit: 27 (5d10) bludgeoning damage, and each creature on the target's top deck within 15 ft. of the impact takes 14 (4d6) piercing damage, or half as much on a successful DC 13 Dexterity saving throw.

### Grappling Hook
AC: 15
HP: 50
DMG Immunities: Poison, Psychic
Weapon Slots: 1
Market Price: 72,000 SH
Desc: Large object. A heavy four-pronged hook fired from a ballista-like mount, used to stop a ship from getting away.
#### Ammunition:
* Grappling Hook: Ranged Weapon Attack: range 60/120 ft., one target. Hit: 16 (3d10) piercing damage, and the target is grappled until the hook is removed or its chain is broken. The chain has AC 15, 30 HP, a Damage Threshold of 10, and immunity to poison and psychic damage. If the grappled ship is at least one size smaller, a creature can use its action to make a DC 15 Strength check to winch it 5 ft. closer. This can only be done once per round.

### Salamander
AC: 15
HP: 25
DMG Immunities: Poison, Psychic
Weapon Slots: 1 (bow only)
Market Price: 240,000 SH
Desc: Large object. A bronze salamander head on the bow, fed by an oil pump, that sprays a stream of fire. One crew member working the pump fires it with the Operate Crew Action. It has 3 uses, and refueling takes 10 minutes.
#### Ammunition:
* Salamander Fuel (3 uses): A 40 ft. cone of fire. Each creature in it takes 28 (8d6) fire damage, or half as much on a successful DC 14 Dexterity saving throw. A wooden or otherwise flammable ship in the cone doesn't take this damage. Instead it automatically catches fire with no save, taking 7 (2d6) fire damage at the start of its turn. See [[Damage and Repair#Fire|Fire]].

### Dragon Head
AC: 17
HP: 45
DMG Immunities: Poison, Psychic
Weapon Slots: 2 (bow only)
Market Price: 380,000 SH
Desc: Huge object. A larger, cast-iron version of the salamander. Its bigger pumps need two crew members taking the Operate Crew Action to fire it. It has 3 uses, and refueling takes 10 minutes.
#### Ammunition:
* Dragon Head Fuel (3 uses): A 60 ft. cone of fire. Each creature in it takes 42 (12d6) fire damage, or half as much on a successful DC 16 Dexterity saving throw. A wooden or otherwise flammable ship in the cone doesn't take this damage. Instead it automatically catches fire with no save, taking 10 (3d6) fire damage at the start of its turn. See [[Damage and Repair#Fire|Fire]].

## Melee Weapons
---
Melee ship weapons are heavy, two-handed martial weapons unless stated otherwise.

### Naval Ram
AC: -
HP: - (below the waterline, can't be targeted)
DMG Immunities: -
Weapon Slots: 0 (bow only, doesn't count as the bow's one weapon; one per ship)
Market Price: 38,000 SH
Desc: A bronze-sheathed beak fixed to the bow below the waterline, built to punch through enemy hulls. When this ship makes a ram attack, it takes no damage on a hit, and only half the rammed ship's maximum Hull Points on a miss. After a ram, this ship can choose not to be grappled.

### Oar Blade
AC: 15
HP: 25
DMG Immunities: Poison, Psychic
Weapon Slots: 0 (replaces a Sail Station, port or starboard only)
Market Price: 24,000 SH
Desc: Large/Huge object. A reinforced oar with a sharpened blade. When a crew member takes the Sail Crew Action with it, they can also immediately make an attack with it, but the Sail DC with an oar blade is 15. It can also be used as a normal weapon without sailing.
* Melee Weapon Attack: reach equal to half the ship's width (minimum 5 ft.), one target. Hit: 14 (4d6) slashing damage.

### Auroch Ram
AC: 15
HP: 40
DMG Immunities: Poison, Psychic
Weapon Slots: 0 (bow only, counts as the bow's one weapon)
Market Price: 38,000 SH
Desc: A heavy ram with an auroch's head, hidden in a compartment just below the bow deck. Thanks to its pulleys and rigging, a single crew member can swing it.
* Melee Weapon Attack: reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 21 (6d6) bludgeoning damage.
