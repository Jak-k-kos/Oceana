# Ship Upgrades
***
***
some upgrades cannot be placed on small ships. please contact your local harbor master for details on upgrading your ship. market price may or may not reflect the price given by your local harbor masters. for arcane upgrades please visit your nearest mage tower so they may tell you no themselves. terms and conditions apply

**Upgrade slots:** a ship can carry one Railing, one Plating and one Structural upgrade at a time. Light Mods stack freely, though only one paint can be worn at a time.

**Ship size:** upgrades that cover the whole hull list a price for each ship size. The Corvus, Inspiring Figurehead, False Flags, Observation Well, Gnomish Far Seer, Life Lines and the Diving Bell cost the same on any ship.
## Railing
---
### Boarding Nets
Market Value: Medium 48,000 SH | Large 96,000 SH | Huge 192,000 SH
Desc: Climbing the ship's sides is difficult terrain. A creature that reaches the rail must pass a DC 13 Strength (Athletics) check or be restrained until it uses an action to cut or wriggle free. Each 10-ft section of net: AC 10, 5 HP.

### Raised Railing
Market Value: Medium 190,000 SH | Large 380,000 SH | Huge 760,000 SH
Desc:  Creatures on the top deck have three-quarters cover instead of half. Boarders must climb an extra 10 ft. The cover doesn't apply against high-arc weapons.

### Sheer Rails
Market Value: Small 48,000 SH | Medium 95,000 SH | Large 190,000 SH | Huge 380,000 SH *(Small ship compatible)*
Desc: Grappling attempts against this ship are at disadvantage, and the DC to winch it closer goes up by 5.

### Life Netting
Market Value: Small 12,000 SH | Medium 24,000 SH | Large 48,000 SH | Huge 96,000 SH *(Small ship compatible)*
Desc: Netting across the railing gives advantage against saving throws against being thrown overboard

### Fire Troughs
Market Value: Medium 72,000 SH | Large 144,000 SH | Huge 288,000 SH
Desc: Troughs of sea water and buckets along the rails aid in fighting fires on the ship. When a crew member uses their action to fight a fire, it shrinks by 2d6 instead of 1d6.

### Corvus
Market Value: 120,000 SH
Desc: hinged spiked boards are placed onto the railings of your ship. crew members may make an operate crew action to drop it onto any adjacent ship no bigger one size larger than their own using an attack roll against the other ships AC. the ships are considered grappled, until the plank is destroyed the plank is considered normal terrain for boarding and has AC 15 and 40 HP.

Market Value: Small 70,000 SH | Medium 140,000 SH | Large 280,000 SH | Huge 560,000 SH *(Small ship compatible)*
Desc: +3 to initiative. if the ship has the cumbersome trait it loses it, if it doesn't have the cumbersome trait, it gains the agile trait, if it already has the agile trait it gains another +2 to initiative. AC is decreased by 1.

Market Value: Small 36,000 SH | Medium 72,000 SH | Large 144,000 SH | Huge 288,000 SH *(Small ship compatible)*
Desc: hazards in the water no longer count as difficult terrain. all damage from terrain based hazards is halved.

### False Flags

Desc: A glass dome is installed into the hull of the ship allowing a creature to see 60' underwater in clear water. when doing so the creature gains advantage on perception checks to see things underwater
### Gnomish Far seer
Market Value: 72,000 SH *(Small ship compatible)*
Desc: a gnomish far seer is added to your crows nest giving advantage to all perception and investigations made by creatures in the crows nest using the far seer. greatly increase the range of sight from the crows nest while using the far seer.

### Life Lines
Market Value: 480 SH per lifeline *(Small ship compatible)*
Desc: Sturdy ropes are tied along the masts and railings. a creature can use an action to tie themselves or another to a life line making them immune to being thrown overboard. while tied down a creature cannot move more than 20' away from the tie down point

### Diving Bell
Market Value: 140,000 SH
Desc: A heavy bronze bell with glass ports, lowered from a winch on deck. It holds up to 4 Medium creatures, who can breathe normally inside it. The ship must be anchored or holding still to use it. A crew member at the winch can take the Operate Crew Action to lower or raise the bell up to 30 ft, to a maximum depth of 200 ft. Creatures can swim out of the bell to explore and come back inside to breathe. The air lasts 2 hours with 1 creature inside, or 30 minutes with 4. Raising the bell to the surface refreshes it. Medium or larger ships only. Bell: AC 17, 40 HP. Chain: AC 15, 20 HP. If the chain breaks, the bell sinks to the bottom.
