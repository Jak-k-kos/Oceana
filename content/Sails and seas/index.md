# Sails and Seas
When the waters rose, the road became the sea, and the ship became the cart, the horse, the house and the fortress all at once. Every sailor in Oceana lives by the timbers beneath their feet. A sound hull and a willing wind will carry a crew to fortune; a cracked keel or a careless captain will carry them to [[Morvaine]]. Here lies everything a crew needs to know about the ships they sail, the guns they fire and the ways they speak to one another across the waves.
