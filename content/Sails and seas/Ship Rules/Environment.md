The sea is never only water. It is wind and weather, reef and current, fog that swallows a ship whole and cold that turns the rigging to glass. A captain who reads the sea can turn a losing fight into a victory, and a captain who ignores it will be taught otherwise, usually all at once.

# Wind
The wind is the first and most fickle master of every sailing ship. A Sail Powered ship facing the way the wind blows gains extra movement equal to the Wind Speed. A ship facing into the wind loses that much movement instead.

A ship counts as facing with the wind if its bow points the same compass direction the wind is blowing, or one of the two directions beside it. A north wind, for example, carries ships facing north, northeast and northwest.

The winds of Oceana come in four moods:
- **Dead Wind:** not a breath stirs the canvas. A Sail Powered ship has a Speed Unit of 0.
- **Light Wind:** the ordinary breeze most ships sail in. Sail Crew Actions work as normal, and there is no Wind Speed.
- **Medium Wind:** a fine, brisk wind beloved of captains in a hurry, with a Wind Speed anywhere from 0 to 50 ft. Ships running with it gain speed, and the only drawback falls on those sailing against it.
- **Heavy Wind:** squalls and screaming gales, with a Wind Speed anywhere from 60 to 110 ft. They can hurl a ship along at terrible speed, or tear apart a crew too green to handle her.
	- At the end of each of its turns, a Sail Powered ship must have used all of its movement, including any bonus from the wind. If it hasn't, it takes 1 Hull Die of damage for every 10 ft of movement left over.
	- Whenever a crew member fails a Sail Crew Action on a Sail Powered ship, the ship takes bludgeoning damage equal to one of its Hull Dice. This damage ignores Damage Threshold.

# Hazardous Terrain
Rocks breaking the surf, reefs too shallow for a larger hull, and the bones of sunken wrecks lying just beneath the waves have sent more than one proud ship to the bottom. Any solid, immovable thing in the water counts as Hazardous Terrain.

A ship that tries to move into it loses all its remaining movement. It also takes bludgeoning damage equal to one Hull Die for every 5 ft it moved that turn before striking. This damage ignores Damage Threshold.

# Difficult Terrain
Difficult terrain works on the water just as it does on land: every foot moved costs 1 extra foot. At sea it might be a seaweed bed, a coral reef (for smaller ships), or a skin of thin ice.

# Fog
Fog rolls in thick over cold and humid waters, and a whole fleet can vanish inside it. Light fog lightly obscures the area it covers. Heavy fog heavily obscures it.

# Currents
Out on the open sea, the waves are only the wind's echo, and the wind rules above cover them. But rivers, straits and narrow channels run with currents all their own. A current has a direction and a speed set by the DM.
- At ship initiative 10, every creature and object in the current, ships included, is carried that far in its direction.
- A ship moving against the current loses movement equal to the current's speed.

# Extreme Cold
In bitter waters, spray freezes on the rails and ice creeps up the rigging. After a ship spends more than 3 hours below freezing, its decks turn slick and its lines stiff, and the DC for the Sail Crew Action rises by 5.

# Environmental Challenges
Now and then the sea does more than make a voyage hard. It rises up and tries to kill. Sailors tell of these dangers in every harbor tavern, usually with a drink in hand and a glance toward the door.

## Welestia's Tears
The most common storm on the water: grey squalls of slanting rain and sudden gusts, with now and then a crack of lightning. Most blow over leaving nothing worse than a soaked crew. But the old saying holds that the Tears are where the Rage begins, and every captain keeps one eye on the clouds until the sky clears.

## Welestia's Rage
The grief of the storm goddess loosed in full. The sky turns the color of a bruise from one horizon to the other, the sea rises in grey mountains, and there is no edge to sail for and no harbor to run to. A ship caught in the Rage can only hold fast and outlast it. Many of the ships lost near the Widow's Wrath met their end in one.

## The Tempest
A small and savage storm, a knot of black cloud and white water no wider than a harbor that roams the waves like a beast on the hunt. Lightning, breaking seas and sudden squalls fill it, and at its heart lies a strange, still Eye. No deckhand is counted a true sailor until they have weathered one.

## Waterspout
A grey finger of wind and water reaching down from a storm cloud to stir the sea. It wanders where it pleases, drawing ships toward it and flinging anything it touches through the air. Then, as suddenly as it came, it collapses in a roar of falling water.

## The Long Swell
Great, smooth hills of sea rolling out from a storm far beyond the horizon. The sky can be clear and the wind gentle, and still the whole ocean rises and falls. A ship on the crests can see for miles, a ship in the troughs sees nothing but walls of green water, and a ship caught side-on risks being rolled.

## The Maelstrom
A great wheel of water turning where the drowned land falls away into the deep, a funnel of foam spiralling down toward a roaring throat. Its pull grows stronger the longer it turns. A ship that reaches the bottom does not come back up.

## Fire From the Deep
The birth of a new island, one of [[Roktra|Roktra's]] gifts. The sea boils, ash blots out the sun, molten rock rains down, and new land rises around any ship foolish enough to linger. Among the land-born it is a holy thing. To a ship caught beside it, it is the end of the world.

## Red Tide
Some summers the sea begins to bleed. A rust-red bloom spreads for miles, the air above it burns the throat, and everything beneath it dies. Sailors call it [[Caruilla|Caruilla's]] Fever, and they give it a wide berth, because the dying sea always draws its hunters.

## Sea Dragon Run
When the sea dragons move, they move together: thousands of them in a single river of scales and spray, thundering through the open water. The fishermen of [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Delafina/index|Delafina]] ride its edges as their grandparents taught them. To anyone else caught in its path, it is a stampede that stops for nothing.

## Beneath the Keel
It begins with the sea going quiet. A shadow larger than any island slides beneath the hull, so long that it takes whole minutes to pass. Most of the great beasts of the deep are only passing through. A wise crew keeps its guns quiet and lets it go by. A foolish one gives it a reason to look up.
