Ship combat in Oceana uses the Captains & Cannons rules. They sit on top of normal 5e combat, so everything your character can normally do still works. You just also get ways to help run the ship.

- [[Basic Ship Rules]]: what the numbers on a ship mean

- [[Ship Phase]]: how ships move, attack, grapple and ram
- [[Damage and Repair]]: hull points, sinking, repairs and crew losses
- [[Environment]]: storms, whirlpools and winds.

# Order of Combat
Every round has two phases.
- **Character Phase:** Creatures take their turns as in regular combat, and can spend actions on Crew Actions.
- **Ship Phase:** Only ships act, using the movement and attacks their crews earned during the Character Phase. Characters can still use reactions for readied actions.

## Step by Step
1. **Roll initiative.** Characters roll initiative as in regular combat.
2. **Declare environmental challenges.** If the battlefield has one, the DM says what will happen this round and on which initiative counts.
3. **Character Phase.** Everyone takes their turn. The DM runs NPCs as normal, and doesn't have to reveal which Crew Actions enemy sailors take if the players can't see them.
4. **Ship Phase.** Ships act in order of initiative score plus any bonuses, highest first, using the actions their crews earned.
5. **Next round.** Once every ship has acted, the round ends and play returns to step 2. Repeat until combat ends.
