The Character Phase works like a normal round of combat. On your turn you can move, attack, cast spells and do anything else you normally could. Enemies will often be out of range, though, so most of the time you'll want to spend your action on a **Crew Action** instead. The phase ends when everyone has taken a turn, and then the [[Ship Phase]] begins.

# Crew Actions
Crew Actions are special actions you can take while aboard a ship to help run it. Most don't do anything right away. Instead, they add to the pool of movement and attacks your ship gets to use in the Ship Phase.
- Most Crew Actions are ability checks. The DCs below assume smooth sailing; the DM can raise them in rough conditions.
- Failing a Crew Action has no penalty other than wasting your turn.
- The DM chooses whether they or the players roll for NPC crew. If there are too many NPCs to roll for, use the [[#Crew Bonus (Variant Rule)|Crew Bonus]] variant below.

## Sail
Hauling ropes, pulling oars or stoking engines.
- **Check:** DC 10 Strength (Water Vehicles)
- **Success:** The ship gains movement equal to its Speed Unit for the Ship Phase this round.
- **Failure:** The ship gains half its Speed Unit instead.

## Helm
Keeping the ship on course.
- A ship with **Helmsman Required** can only move straight ahead if nobody aboard has taken the Helm action this round.
- Helm usually doesn't need a check. The DM may call for one in difficult waters or for very precise maneuvering: a Strength (Water Vehicles) or Dexterity (Water Vehicles) check, with the DC set by the situation.
- You can take Helm on a ship without Helmsman Required. It isn't needed to turn, but matters if an effect forces the helmsman to make a Water Vehicles check.
- On a ship with **Independent Propulsion**, taking Helm gives the ship its base movement speed.
- Whoever took Helm this round can add their Water Vehicles proficiency to a [[Ship Phase#Ramming|ram attack]].

## Operate
Using any ship equipment that isn't for sailing or steering, most often a ship weapon.
- Name the weapon you're operating when you take the action. You don't have to choose a target yet.
- The weapon has to be ready to use (for example, a ranged weapon must already be loaded).
- Each crew member who operates a weapon adds one attack with that weapon to the ship's pool for the Ship Phase. Attack and damage are rolled when the attack happens in the Ship Phase.
- Other equipment may also need Operate; its description explains how.

## Command
Coordinating the crew: drum beats, flag signals, or relaying orders below decks. Anyone can do this.
- **Check:** DC 10 Charisma
- **Success:** +2 to the ship's initiative in this round's Ship Phase. The bonus resets at the end of the round.
- Any number of characters can take Command in a round, and successes stack.

# Other Actions
## Readying an Action
When you ready an action, you can choose to use it during the Ship Phase instead of the Character Phase. All the normal readied action rules still apply.

## Repair
The Repair action is also taken during the Character Phase. It isn't a Crew Action, so it takes effect immediately. See [[Damage and Repair#Repairing Damage|Repairing Damage]].

## Mapping the Ship
You don't need a map of the ship for these rules. A map can add strategy, but theater of the mind works fine as long as everyone keeps track of how many weapons are in use, how many sailors are on the sails, and roughly where characters are standing.

## Leadership
Nothing in these rules requires a captain to direct everyone. Players decide their own actions; each table can find the level of leadership that suits it.

# Crew Bonus (Variant Rule)
Large ships need more crew than a party can provide. Rather than rolling for every NPC sailor, the DM can fold them into a single **Crew Bonus**. This is recommended when NPC crew outnumber the players 2 to 1.

**Calculating it:** Count the NPC crew being combined and divide by the number of player characters. Any leftover crew aren't combined and act as their own characters. (Example: 12 crew / 4 players = Crew Bonus 3.)

**Effect:** Crew Actions get +1 to the check per point of Crew Bonus, and successful ones are boosted:
- **Sail:** A success grants +1 Speed Unit of movement per point of Crew Bonus; a failure grants +1/2 Speed Unit per point. The ship still can't go over its maximum speed.
- **Operate (ship weapon):** For each point of Crew Bonus, choose another ship weapon aboard identical to yours (including ammunition) that is ready to use and can target the same target. On a hit, roll half that weapon's damage dice (rounded up) and add them to your damage. The chosen weapons count as fired, and any effects of firing them (like ammunition spent) apply.
- **Command:** The initiative bonus increases by 1 per point of Crew Bonus.

**Limits:**
- Only crew who could actually take and succeed at a Crew Action are combined. Crew over the ship's maximum and incapacitated crew don't count.
- Players can ask, round by round, for specific NPCs to stay out of the bonus and act on their own. The DM rolls for those NPCs as normal, and they don't count toward the bonus.
- Only players benefit from the Crew Bonus. NPCs never do.
- Other actions may benefit from the Crew Bonus at the DM's discretion.
