The new age, begun with the first new year after the seas stopped rising.

**Dates:** 2575-Desca-1 onward  
**Preceded by:** [[Aquilla's Wrath]]

## The Seas Stand Still  
On approximately 2574-Macis-8, for the first time in more than two thousand years, the seas stopped rising. The tideline held where it was, and the flood that gave [[Aquilla's Wrath]] its name was over.

The flood had been ended by [[The Tidebreakers]], a band of adventurers on the far side of the world, who fought their way past the sea folk guarding the cauldron of Magnus the Magnificent and destroyed it. Their names are known in every harbor now, and their deeds are already being sung in every tavern. Yet the world still gave the new age the name of its goddess. Many believe it was [[Aquilla]] who finally allowed the flood to end, and that the Tidebreakers could never have succeeded without her mercy. And so the new age has taken her name: the Era of Aquilla's Mercy.

## The First Mercy  
Word of [[The Tidebreakers]]' victory took weeks to cross the world. It did not reach most of the islands until 2574-Calra-12, more than a month after the seas had stopped rising, and that day became the first festival of [[Aquilla's Mercy]], celebrated in harbors and villages across the islands. It is the first holiday [[Aquilla]] has ever had.

## A New Year, a New Age  
By the oldest custom of the record-keepers, an age begins only with the turning of a year, and so the flood's end did not begin the new age at once. For the rest of 2574, the world lived in what sailors call the Turning Year: the seas stood still, but in every chart and ledger the Wrath went on. The Era of Aquilla's Mercy began in earnest at the [[Hearthfire's Blaze]] of 2575, when every town lit its bonfire for a new age as well as a new year.

## An Uncertain Peace  
The seas may have stopped rising, but the world they left behind is still a drowned one, and the Era of Aquilla's Mercy has only just begun.

The sea folk who guarded the cauldron for centuries have watched surface dwellers destroy it and then celebrate in the name of [[Aquilla]]. Their patron, [[Caruilla]], is furious, and though she has sought no vengeance, her sea folk have noticed her anger. Whether the new age will bring peace between the surface and the sea, or something far worse, remains to be seen.
