A night of unveilings and new beginnings beneath the full orange moon of [[Ostin]].

**Date:** Once in each 42-day turn of the orange moon (about twice a year)  
**Honors:** [[Ostin]], the Tinker

## Overview
When the orange moon of [[Ostin|the Tinker]] comes full, the workshops of the islands throw open their doors. By long custom, this is the night when new things are first shown to the world. Inventions are unveiled in the public squares, new workshops open, and new ship designs are set on the water for the first time.

## The Showing
Craftsmen of every kind bring their newest work into the streets under the orange light: a better lock, a stronger hull plank, a lens that sees farther. Crowds wander from one display to the next. A failed unveiling is cheered almost as loudly as a success. Ostin's faithful hold that a failure shown honestly is still a step forward, and many of his own champions are remembered as much for their failures as their triumphs.

## The Artificer's Gift
Only beneath [[Ostin|Ostin's]] full moon can his power be granted, so no one becomes an artificer on any other night. Those who hope to receive the gift spend the night at their own workbench, building, with the shutters open to the moon. Most rise at dawn with nothing but a finished piece of work. A rare few rise as artificers.

## Superstitions
Tools are left out on the windowsill to catch the orange light, and are said to keep a truer edge for it. Starting a project on the night after the Maker's Moon is thought to be bad luck, because the luck of the moon has already been spent on others.
