A yearly festival of thanksgiving before the harvest, held in the name of [[Apiot]].

**Date:** The 5th of Osca  
**Honors:** [[Apiot]], the Green Mother  
**Paired with:** [[Apiot's Promise]]

## Overview  
Just before the harvest is brought in, the islands pause to give thanks for what [[Apiot]] has given. Apiot's Boon is a festival of plenty and gratitude: of laden tables, open doors, and thanks given to the Green Mother and to every mother alike.

## The First Fruits  
No family eats from the new harvest until Apiot has had her share. On the morning of the Boon, every household brings the first fruits of its fields, orchards and sea gardens to Apiot's shrine as an offering. The shrines overflow with grain and fruit, with kelp, roots and flowers, until they look like gardens themselves.

## Saving the Promise  
From those first fruits, the finest seeds are chosen and set aside. They are kept safe through the long winter, and come spring they will be carried back to the shrine to become part of the Promise seed at [[Apiot's Promise]]. So each year's harvest is sown from the very best of the last, and the two festivals are bound together in a single turning of the seasons.

## Emptying the Stores  
To make room for the harvest to come, the last of the old year's stores are brought out, cooked and shared freely. No one hoards on the Boon. Kitchens are busy from dawn, the feasts are cooked over [[Flaris]]'s fire, and the tables are open to anyone who comes, so that the old year's food feeds the whole town before the new year's harvest arrives.

## The Honoring of Mothers  
[[Apiot]] is the patron of mothers, and on the Boon, every mother is thanked for the year's care. Children, grown and small, bring their mothers flowers and the first fruits of their own small gardens, and the first toast of the feast is raised to the mothers of the town.
