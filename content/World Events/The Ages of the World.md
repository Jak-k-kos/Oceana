Every sailor knows the shape of the tale, even if few could tell it straight: the world was dry, then it drowned, and now the drowning has stopped. Here the whole of it is set down in brief, from the shaping of the world to the first bright years of the new age. Each mark along the way opens onto its own tale.

## [[Aquilla's Slumber]]: time out of mind to 532 PAC-Osca-25
- [[The Shaping of the World]]: the gods shape the world, each taking on the aspect of what they made, and crown [[Arkaius]] first among them.
- [[The Golden Age]]: the age of magic, when mages were as common as merchants and the seas lay calm within their shores.

## [[Aquilla's Wrath]]: 531 PAC-Desca-1 to 2574-Osca-25
- 532 PAC: [[The Sinking of the Cauldron]]. The cauldron of Magnus the Magnificent breaks loose, and the flooding begins.
- 410 PAC: [[The Drowning of Vhaldrun]]. The tidal waves begin to wash through a door into Vhaldrun.
- 408 PAC: [[The Eviction]]. The sea rises over the door's threshold, the torrent begins, and the fiends pour out. The Three-Sided War begins.
- 403 PAC: [[The Drowning of Vhaldrun|The door to Vhaldrun]] sinks beneath the waves.
- 399 PAC: [[The Sealing]]. The fiends stop coming, no one knows why, and the flood returns at full fury.
- 238 PAC: [[The Last of the Fiends]]. The Legions of Vhaldrun are broken to their last remnants.
- 107 PAC: Mortimer Greenbe, who will one day be [[Urset]], is born in Veygard.
- 1 PAC: [[The Fall of the Weaver]]. [[Arkaius]] dies.
- 1 PAC: [[Arcanus Collapse]]. [[Urset]] drinks the magic of the world dry before the year is out.
- 1-Desca-1: By the custom of the record-keepers, the years are counted anew.
- 11 to 31: [[The Long Doubt]]. The seas are found to be rising still, and the world argues for twenty years before it accepts it.
- 41: [[The Fall of Veygard]]. The last nation of the old world falls to the sea.
- 1052: [[The Founding of the Coalition]]. A handful of island towns swear to stand together.
- 1325: [[The Fall of the Azure Empire]]. Alaric Albryn tears down the Empire and is crowned the first king of Albryn.
- 1604: [[The Dragon Queen's Usurpation]]. Queen Maelis loses her throne to her brother.
- 1875: [[The Founding of the Black Tide]]. The pirates of the south band together under the first Dread Pirate King.
- 1975: [[The Coming of Powder]]. Orrin Blackwell, champion of [[Ostin]], makes the first powder. The sea folk are driven back, and the first raft cities are built.
- 2365: The crown of the [[Locations/The Black Tide/index|Black Tide]] passes from father to son for the first time.
- 2421 to 2567: [[The Southern Reclamation]]. Under a line of failing kings, the Black Tide is driven back by the Kingdom and the coalition.
- About 2500: [[Locations/Smuggler's Reach/index|Smuggler's Reach]], long cut off, breaks away from the Black Tide.
- 2574-Macis-8: [[The Breaking of the Cauldron]]. [[The Tidebreakers]] slay [[Caruilla|Caruilla's]] champion, destroy the cauldron, and the seas stop rising.
- 2574-Calra-12: The first [[Aquilla's Mercy]] is held.
