The age between the Arcanus Collapse and the coming of the flood.

**Dates:** 0-1-1 to 257-4-8  
**Preceded by:** [[Arcanus Collapse]]  
**Followed by:** [[Aquilla's Wrath]]

## The Dark Age  
The [[Arcanus Collapse]] did not simply take magic from the world. It took nearly everything that had been built upon it.

For ages, magic had done the work that might otherwise have driven mortals to invent. Light, healing, travel, craft and learning had all leaned upon it, and when it vanished, the world was left with little to stand on. Great works fell into ruin, knowledge that had lived only in spellbooks became useless, and what science existed was so bound up with magic that it collapsed alongside it. Mages who had once been masters became beggars and scholars of a dead art, and the people of the world were left to rebuild from almost nothing.

It was a dark age, and the world has never truly recovered. Learning crept forward only slowly, and it is because of those lost centuries that, more than two thousand years later, the world has advanced so little beyond where it began.

Yet for all its hardship, the Slumber is remembered more gently than it deserves. The seas lay calm within their shores, and [[Aquilla]] seemed content to leave the land to those who lived upon it. Only later, looking back across the long and terrible centuries of the flood, did the world name this age Aquilla's Slumber: the years before the sea goddess woke. Beside what came after, even a dark age can seem like a quiet one.

## Magnus the Magnificent  
Near the end of the Slumber, a mage named Magnus the Magnificent set out to solve one of the oldest troubles of mortal life. He would end the want of water forever. With what magic he could still command, he forged a great cauldron that would pour fresh water without end, so that no village would ever again go thirsty and no field would ever again wither in drought.

Something went wrong. What exactly happened has been lost, but before anyone realized the danger, the cauldron had slipped beneath the waves, still pouring, endlessly, into the sea.

## The End of the Slumber  
On 257-4-8, the age came to an end, though no one living at the time knew it. The waters had begun to rise. They rose so slowly that no one noticed for generations, and by the time the world understood what was happening, [[Aquilla's Wrath]] had already begun.
