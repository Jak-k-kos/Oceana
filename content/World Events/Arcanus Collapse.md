The day the magic of the world was drunk dry, and the event from which all years are now counted.

**Date:** Year 0  
**Preceded by:** The age of magic, now counted in years PAC (Pre Arcanus Collapse)  
**Followed by:** [[Aquilla's Slumber]]

## The Age Before  
Before the Collapse, magic ran through the world like rivers through a valley. [[Arkaius]], the Weaver, held the domain of magic and the throne of the gods, and under his hand wonders were common things. Mages were as plentiful as merchants, enchanted goods were sold in the marketplace, and an archmage was a respected figure in any great court. Those years are now reckoned backward from the Collapse, and called PAC: the years Pre Arcanus Collapse.

## The Fall of the Weaver  
In 1 PAC, [[Arkaius]] died. How a god can die, no one knows, and who struck him down has never been discovered. His champion fell on the very same day.

As the Weaver's soul unraveled, a mortal mage named Mortimer Greenbe, imprisoned for half a century in a warded tower of the kingdom of Veygard, gathered up its fraying threads and bound them to himself. In that moment he rose as [[Urset]], the new god of magic. A few threads slipped through his grasp and passed to the three moons, the children of [[Arkaius]] and [[Welestia]], but the rest were his.

## The Collapse  
Urset's first act as a god was to draw the magic of the world into himself, as much as he could hold, for his greed had no end. Across the world, the rivers of magic ran dry in a single stroke. Enchantments failed, magic items guttered out like candles in the wind, and the great mages of the age found themselves cut off from the power they had spent their lives mastering. Most never cast a spell again.

So complete was the change that the world began to count its years anew. The Collapse became year 0, and every year since has been counted forward from it.

## Aftermath  
Soon after the Collapse, Urset sought to claim the widowed [[Welestia]] as his bride. Her answer was a storm that left him burned and crippled, and [[Ellehwin]], newly seated at the head of the pantheon, sentenced him to the lower Halls of Divinity, where he remains to this day, hoarding the magic he stole.

Yet magic did not vanish entirely. A little still trickles into the world:

- **Mages** draw what power they can from [[Urset]] himself, bargaining for every spell.
- **The three moons** hold a sliver of their father's soul, and grant a measure of power to a rare few.
- **Bards** learned from [[Lyandris]] how to sing a thread of magic loose from Urset's hoard.
- **Dragons** steal their magic back with the aid of [[Koruk]].
- **A rare few rangers, druids and artificers** have found ways of their own to steal from the hoard.

Magic items from the age before still exist, but they are rare beyond measure, and most are worth a fortune.
