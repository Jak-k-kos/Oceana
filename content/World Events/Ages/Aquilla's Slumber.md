## The Years Before the Waking
Aquilla's Slumber is the oldest age of the world, and the gentlest. It began with [[The Shaping of the World]], when the gods gave the world its form and crowned [[Arkaius]] first among them, and it flowered into [[The Golden Age]], when magic ran through the world like rivers through a valley and wonders were sold in every marketplace.

Through all those ages, [[Aquilla]] lay quiet, her waters hemmed within the shores [[Roktra]] had raised. No one living in those days would have thought to name the age for her. Only later, looking back across the long and terrible centuries of the flood, did the world call it her Slumber: the years before the sea goddess woke.

## The End of the Slumber
The Slumber ended with [[The Sinking of the Cauldron]] in 532 PAC. By the oldest custom of the record-keepers, an age begins and ends only with the turning of a year, and so the Slumber is reckoned to have closed with the last night of 532 PAC, and [[Aquilla's Wrath]] to have begun with the dawn of 531 PAC.
