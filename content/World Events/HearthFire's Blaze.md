A yearly festival of community and celebration, held in the name of [[Flaris]].

**Date:** The 1st of Desca
**Honors:** [[Flaris]], the Hearthfather  
**Follows:** [[Hearthfire's Glow]]

## Overview  
If [[Hearthfire's Glow]] is a night for the family, Hearthfire's Blaze is a day for everyone. On the first day of every year, the islands turn outward, and each town gathers as one to welcome the year to come with fire, feasting and the grandest boasts it can muster.

## One Fire From Many  
At dawn, every family uncovers the hearth coals banked on the night of the Glow and carries a few of them to the heart of town. There, one by one, the coals are added to a great pile of wood, and from all of them together a single bonfire is kindled. The town's fire is made from every home's hearth, and so the whole town begins the new year sharing one flame.

## Lighthouses and Lanterns  
As the bonfire catches, the town's lighthouse is put out for a single moment, then relit from the new bonfire, so that the first light the sea sees in the new year comes from the hearths of the whole town. Sailors carry flame from the bonfire back to their ships as well, and every ship's lantern for [[Flaris]] is relit from it before the year's first voyage.

## The Boasts  
When the fire is roaring, the boasting begins. One by one, townsfolk step up before the bonfire with a drink in hand and declare, as loudly and as grandly as they can, what they will accomplish in the year to come.

No boast is ever meant to be kept. The grander and more impossible, the better. One might swear to beat [[Tarrakus]] himself at his own war games, another to win the hand of a god, to breach the mountain with their bare hands, or to face the Dread Pirate King and send him to the bottom of the sea. The crowd cheers each one, and each boast is grander than the last, not to win anything, but because the Blaze deserves nothing less.

The children always go first, and their boasts are often the most beloved of the day.

When a boast is spoken, the boaster throws a twig or a scrap of kindling into the bonfire. The fire takes the boast, sealing it forever, never to be kept and never to be forgotten. The very best boasts live on as town legends, retold around the bonfire every Blaze for years to come.

## The Celebration  
The rest of the day belongs to the celebration. Long tables are set out around the bonfire, food and drink are shared freely, and music, dancing and games carry on well into the night. It is a day for the whole community to stand together, warmed by one fire, and to welcome whatever the new year may bring.
