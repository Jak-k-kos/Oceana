A yearly festival of sowing and new beginnings, held in the name of [[Apiot]].

**Date:** The 15th of Macis  
**Honors:** [[Apiot]], the Green Mother  
**Paired with:** [[Apiot's Boon]]

## Overview  
Just before the fields are sown, the islands gather to ask [[Apiot]] for a good harvest, and to make her a promise in return. Apiot's Promise is a quiet, hopeful festival, full of seeds and soil and small vows. Where [[Apiot's Boon]] gives thanks for what has been grown, the Promise is about everything that has yet to be.

## The Promise Seed  
On the morning of the Promise, every household brings a handful of seed to Apiot's shrine to be blessed. Much of it is the very seed set aside at the last [[Apiot's Boon]], the finest of the year's first fruits, kept safe through the winter for this day.

From every family's handful, a little is mixed together into a single store called the Promise seed. It is the first seed sown each year, planted in the town's common field, so that the whole town's harvest begins from one shared planting.

## The Promises  
Before the sowing begins, each farmer makes a promise to the land. They are small promises, and real ones: to let a tired field rest, to mend a broken wall, to tend the soil with care. In return, it is said, Apiot promises a harvest.

These are not the grand, impossible boasts of [[Hearthfire's Blaze]]. A promise made to Apiot is meant to be kept, and few would dare to break one.

## A Seed for Every Child  
Families with a child born since the last Promise plant that child's seed on this day, often a tree or a hardy vine, so that the plant and the child may grow up side by side. On many islands, the oldest trees are known by the names of the children they were planted for, long after those children have grown old themselves.

## The Blessing of Mothers  
Every woman who has become a mother since the last Promise is blessed on this day. On her first Promise as a mother, she is brought before Apiot's shrine, honored by the whole town and given small gifts, so that every mother receives the Green Mother's blessing once in her life.

## The Sea Gardens  
Since the flood, [[Apiot]] has turned her hand to the sea, and her festival has followed her there. On the day of the Promise, kelp-tenders and reef gardeners plant their first beds of the year alongside the farmers in the fields, so that the land and the sea are sown together.
