The year a queen of Albryn lost her throne to her brother.

**Date:** 1604-Osca-7  
**Part of:** [[Aquilla's Wrath]]  
**Preceded by:** [[The Fall of the Azure Empire]]  
**Followed by:** [[The Founding of the Black Tide]]

## A Slipping Crown
Some three centuries after the founding, Queen Maelis of Albryn found her favor slipping only a few years into her reign. Seeking to win it back, and to bring her kingdom closer to the [[Locations/The Draconic Conclave/index|Draconic Conclave]] besides, she took a dragon for her husband. It was a bold gamble, and it won her neither. The Conclave gave Albryn nothing, and the court liked her even less.

## The Brother's Crown
Her brother took the throne from her. It was not the first crown to change hands that way in Albryn's history, nor would it be the last, and the realm went on much as before. Every king and queen of Albryn since has descended from him. The child of the dragon marriage was cut out of the succession, and from that child came House Brassmoor, the only dragonborn house among the nobility of Albryn.
