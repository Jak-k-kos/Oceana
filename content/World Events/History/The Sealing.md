## The Last to Come
For nine years the fiends poured up out of the deep. Then, in 399 PAC, they stopped. No more came, not one, ever again.

## Why
No one knows why. Some say the door itself broke, torn apart by the weight of the sea pressing through it. Some say the land around it shattered and collapsed, burying the doorway beneath the rubble. Others say the water above it simply grew too deep, and that no fiend could survive the long climb from the door to the surface. Only the sea folk could know the truth, for the door lies deep within their waters, and none of them has ever said.

## The Flood Returns
What is known is this: in the very same year the fiends stopped coming, the flood returned. The rising that had slowed for so long leapt back to its full and terrible pace, as if Vhaldrun had at last drunk all it could hold, and the drowning of the world began again in earnest.

## Stranded
The fiends already in the world were stranded here, and those left behind were stranded there. The Three-Sided War would go on for more than a century and a half, but from that day on, no reinforcements would ever come.
