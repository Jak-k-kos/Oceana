The age when the land learned to answer the sea with thunder.

**Date:** 1975-Calra-16

## The First Thunder
For nineteen centuries after the [[Arcanus Collapse]], the land dwellers had held the sea folk back with spear and sail and little else. Then, some six hundred years ago, Orrin Blackwell, champion of [[Ostin]], made the first powder. From his workshop it spread from forge to forge and shipyard to shipyard, and the craftsmen who made the first guns and cannons, the first shells and the first mines, all swore they had felt Ostin's hand guiding theirs. Blackwell's name is still carved above the doors of powder magazines across the islands, half in honor and half for luck.

## The Sea Folk Driven Back
Fire was the one gift the sea could never share. The sea folk had numbers beyond counting and great beasts of the deep to fight for them, but their magic had died with the rest of the world's, and they could not make powder beneath the waves. They had no answer to it. Cannons tore through their war-beasts, shot and shell scattered their raiding bands, and for the first time in living memory the sea folk gave ground. The shallows that had once been highlands were won back, mile by mile, and the routes between islands grew safer than they had been in a thousand years.

## The First Raft Cities
With the shallows held, the land dwellers did something they had never dared before. In the sheltered waters at the feet of the Azure Peaks, they lashed great platforms together and anchored them to the drowned land below, and they built towns upon them. These were the first raft cities, floating streets and markets and harbors where there had been only open water. Today they ride the waves across the [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/index|Kingdom]] and beyond.
