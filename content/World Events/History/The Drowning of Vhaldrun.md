## The Door in the Plains
Far from these islands, in lowland plains long since lost beneath the waters of the sea folk, there stood a door to Vhaldrun, the burning realm of all things demonic.

## The Waves at the Door
For more than a century the flood crept toward it. Now and then, in the worst of the storms, a great wave would reach the door and spill a little of the sea across its threshold before drawing back. By 410 PAC the waves no longer came now and then. They came with every tide, washing through the door and pouring into the dark, and the fiends of Vhaldrun began, at last, to take notice.

## The Torrent
In 408 PAC the sea itself rose over the threshold, and the waves became a torrent. Vhaldrun drank it in like a cellar beneath a burst cistern, and above, the rising of the world slowed. For a few bright years the scholars of the age dared to hope the flood might be ending. It was not. Vhaldrun was simply filling. So slowly did the sea climb now that it was 403 PAC before the waters closed over the door's roof, and the doorway became a drain at the bottom of the sea, roaring downward into the dark.
