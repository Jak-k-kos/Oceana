## The Last Bastion
Of all the great nations that stood before the flood, Veygard alone still stood after it. For five hundred years it held against the rising sea, raising walls and wards against the tidal waves, taking in refugees by the thousand and meeting the sea folk on its shores. Its strength had always rested on its mages, and above all on one: Mortimer Greenbe, its Head Magos, the most gifted mage of his age, who served the crown even from the warded tower where it kept him.

## The Wards Go Dark
Then Mortimer rose as [[Urset]], and the [[Arcanus Collapse]] took the magic of the world. In a single year, every ward that had held back the sea guttered out, and every mage on Veygard's walls became an ordinary man or woman with a staff. The kingdom that had sheltered the world for five centuries now had nothing left to shelter itself.

## The Fall
It lasted forty years more, by stubbornness and stone alone. Then, in the year 41, the sea and the sea folk came together, and Veygard was gone. Its people scattered across the islands of the world, and Veygard itself is remembered now chiefly as the birthplace of a god.
