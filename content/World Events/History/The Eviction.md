The year the drowning of Vhaldrun drove out its fiends, and the Three-Sided War began.

**Date:** 408 PAC-Macis-17  
**Part of:** [[Aquilla's Wrath]]  
**Preceded by:** [[The Drowning of Vhaldrun]]

## Driven Out
When the sea rose over the threshold and the torrent began, the water in Vhaldrun climbed fast enough to drive its fiends from their homes. They came out in scattered bands at first, then in whole companies, then in their thousands. Even when the door sank beneath the waves in 403 PAC, still they came, swimming up out of the drowned doorway, through the black water and into a drowning world that had never wanted them.

## The Three-Sided War
What followed was a war with three sides and no allies. The sea folk, swelling with every drowned mile, fought to claim the new deep. The land dwellers fought to keep what little ground remained. And the fiends fought everyone.

## The Legions of Vhaldrun
The fiends did not fight as a rabble. The devils were the generals and the demons were their armies, brutish, unthinking things driven forward by cunning masters who had schemed and commanded for longer than any mortal nation had stood. For long years the Legions of Vhaldrun were the terror of the world, and the land dwellers and the sea folk alike bled before them.
