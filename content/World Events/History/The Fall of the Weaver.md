## The Silent Day
In 1 PAC, [[Arkaius]], first among the gods, died. How a god may die at all is a mystery that has never been answered, and the hand that struck him down, if hand it was, has never been found.

## The Unraveling
As the Weaver's soul came undone, it should have passed to [[Morvaine]], as every soul must. It never reached her. Far away in Veygard, a mage named Mortimer Greenbe, imprisoned for half a century in a warded tower, gathered up its fraying threads and bound them to himself, and rose as [[Urset]], the new god of magic. A few threads slipped through his grasping fingers and drifted up to the three moons, so that a little of their father lives on in each of them. The rest were his, and before the year was out he would drink the magic of the world dry.

## The Widow
[[Welestia]] had been the storm, and [[Arkaius]] the stillness at its heart. With the eye gone, there was nothing left to hold her. Her grief broke over the world in storms such as no one had ever seen, and they have never truly ended. It is from her mourning that the [[Locations/The Widow's Wrath/index|Widow's Wrath]] takes its name.

## A New Throne
The throne of the gods passed to [[Ellehwin]], eldest of the moons, for of all the gods she was the only one every other would suffer to sit there. She has never stopped searching for the one who killed her father.

## Whispers of the Guilty
The rumors of his murder have never died, though not one has ever been proven.

- **[[Welestia]]**, for her terrible temper and her more terrible grief.
- **[[Urset]]**, who gained the most.
- **[[Morvaine]]**, for who else could kill a god but death herself?
- **[[Koruk]]**, who warred with the Weaver for ages.
- **[[Aquilla]] and [[Caruilla]]**, for the cauldron poured in their waters and filled their seas. Some say one of them, or both together, struck him down to keep it pouring.
- **[[Tarrakus]]**, whom only the most cynical dare to name.
- **Arkaius himself.** A rare few wonder whether the Weaver chose his own ending.
