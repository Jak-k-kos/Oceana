How the gods shaped the world, and how the world shaped the gods.

**Era:** [[Aquilla's Slumber]]  
**Followed by:** [[The Golden Age]]

## Before the Beginning
Where the gods came from, not even the gods will say. The oldest tales begin with the world already waiting, empty and unformed, and the gods already there to shape it.

## The Shaping
Each god took up a piece of the world and made it. [[Roktra]] raised its bones, piling the mountains high and carving the valleys deep. [[Aquilla]] poured out its waters, and [[Caruilla]], wishing only to be near the one she had chosen as her sister, filled them with every fin and shell that swims. [[Apiot]] sowed the first seeds, and [[Welestia]] stirred the skies into wind and rain. [[Ruthala]] set the beasts running across the land, and from that same wild clay she shaped the land-born peoples, for mortals, she held, are only beasts who learned to speak. [[Flaris]] kindled its fire, [[Koruk]] forged the dragons from fire and stone and boundless pride, [[Lyandris]] gave it a voice, [[Fennara]] gave it chance, [[Tarrakus]] gave it the cold arithmetic of war, and [[Morvaine]] gave every road its ending. And through it all, [[Arkaius]] spun the threads of magic, running them unseen through flame and wave, stone and sky, binding every other god's work into one.

In the shaping, each god took on the aspect of what they had made. The sea made [[Aquilla]] cold and unchanging, and the storm made [[Welestia]] wild. That is the oldest truth of the heavens: the gods made the world, and the world made the gods in turn.

## The Peoples of the World
The land-born made their homes in [[Roktra|Roktra's]] stone, and the old earth god came to love them as his own. Beneath the waves, [[Caruilla]] shaped the sea folk, the only mortals she has ever truly loved. And from [[Morvaine|Morvaine's]] Pool of Life came the souls to fill them all.

## The First Among Gods
Of all the gods, only [[Arkaius]] held a domain that touched every other, and for that, as much as for his wisdom, they crowned him first among them. It was he who spoke the First Law: that no god may lay a hand upon the world beyond their own domain. And so the world was given to mortals, to make of it what they would.
