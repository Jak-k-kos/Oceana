The year the pirates of the south stopped fighting one another and began fighting everyone else.

**Date:** 1875-Macis-2  
**Part of:** [[Aquilla's Wrath]]  
**Preceded by:** [[The Dragon Queen's Usurpation]]

## A Thousand Captains
For centuries the pirates of the southern seas had been many and scattered: a thousand captains, each with a ship, a crew and a grudge, preying on merchants and on each other in equal measure. They were a danger to any lone ship, but never to a kingdom.

## The First King
In 1875, one of them changed that. Whether by blade, bribe or sheer force of will, the tales disagree, a single captain brought the greatest of the pirate lords to heel and bound them into one fleet beneath one flag. They called themselves the [[Locations/The Black Tide/index|Black Tide]], and they called their master the Dread Pirate King.

## The Code of the Tide
The Black Tide was meant to be something the world had never seen: a nation without nobles. No one would rule by birth or by wealth. The finest captains would be raised to dread pirate lords, and the lords would choose the finest among them to be king. When a king died or failed, the lords would choose again. Every hand aboard every ship, from the king to the lowest deckhand, was owed a fair share of all the Tide took and all it made. To the pirates who swore to it, it was freedom. To everyone else, it was the most dangerous idea on the seas.

## The Hidden South
From that day on, the Black Tide was no rabble but a nation of its own. Its islands are its most jealously kept secret. No chart in the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]] or the [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/index|Kingdom]] shows where they lie, and few who have found them have sailed home again.
