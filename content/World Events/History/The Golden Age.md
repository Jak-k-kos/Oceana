The age of magic, when wonders were common things and the seas lay calm within their shores.

**Era:** [[Aquilla's Slumber]]  
**Preceded by:** [[The Shaping of the World]]  
**Followed by:** [[The Sinking of the Cauldron]]

## Rivers of Magic
In the Golden Age, magic ran through the world like rivers through a valley. Mages were as plentiful as merchants, enchanted goods were sold in every marketplace, and an archmage was an honored figure in any great court. Light, healing, travel and craft all leaned upon magic, and the world had little need to invent anything else.

## The Gods Among Them
[[Arkaius]] ruled the halls of the gods with a gentle and steady hand. He gave his heart to [[Welestia]], the storm, and he was the eye at its heart. For love of him she sent soft rains to the fields and fair winds to the sails. From their union came the three moons, [[Ellehwin]], [[Ostin]] and [[Winstra]], the only gods ever born of gods.

Not every god was so gentle. [[Koruk]] was as savage as the chromatic dragons who basked in his favor, and his children darkened the skies in burning swarms. Again and again he set himself against [[Arkaius]], though never once did he win.

## The Calm Sea
Through all those long ages, [[Aquilla]] lay quiet, her waters hemmed within the shores [[Roktra]] had raised. The sea folk kept to the deep, and the peoples of the land rarely gave them a thought. Only later, looking back across the long and terrible centuries of the flood, did the world name this age [[Aquilla's Slumber]]: the years before the sea goddess woke.
