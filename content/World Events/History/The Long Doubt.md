## The One Mercy
When the [[Arcanus Collapse]] drained the world of magic, it took nearly everything with it. Yet in the wreckage, people found one small comfort. Magnus's cauldron had been a thing of magic, and surely it had died with the rest. Whatever else the Collapse had stolen, the flood, at least, was over.

## The Tide Marks
It was not over. Around the year 11, harbormasters began to notice that the tide marks cut into their quay walls were creeping higher, a finger's width here and a thumb's there. It was nothing like the old torrent, so slow that a lifetime might see only a few feet of it, but it was there, and it did not stop.

## Twenty Years of Argument
For twenty years the world argued. Some called it the settling of the waters after the torrent, some blamed the tides or the moons, and some simply refused to believe it. Every year the marks climbed a little higher, and every year the arguments grew a little quieter. By the year 31 no one could deny it any longer. The cauldron was still pouring.

## How?
No one knows how it survived. Some say [[Urset]] himself feeds it with his stolen magic. Others whisper of a bargain between [[Urset]] and [[Caruilla]], or between [[Urset]] and [[Aquilla]]. A few claim [[Caruilla|Caruilla's]] champions have poured their own divine power into it, generation after generation. No surface dweller knows the truth, and no sea folk has ever cared to tell them.
