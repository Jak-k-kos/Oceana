## The Closed Gates
In those days the Azure Peaks were ruled by the Azure Empire, a proud realm of high walls and ancient claims. The Peaks took their name from a little blue flower that once covered their slopes every spring, though it has long since vanished from the world. As the lands below the Peaks drowned, their people climbed toward the mountains in their thousands, begging for refuge. The young [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]] had no room left to take them in. And the Empire shut its gates.

## The Tribes
The tribes of the drowning lands did not die quietly. They gathered beneath the Empire's walls, refugees and warriors alike, and they tore the Azure Empire down. When it was over, they crowned the leader who had brought it down, Alaric Albryn, and so began House Albryn and the kingdom that bears its name.

## The Founding Houses
Every general and every person of note in that war was raised to the nobility, and their blood still runs through the noble houses of Albryn today: human and orc, elf and drow, dwarf, goliath and tiefling. The kingdom they built remembered the gates that had been shut against them, and among its oldest laws is that a king may bow to no master.
