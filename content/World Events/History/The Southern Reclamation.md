The long years in which the Black Tide, under a line of failing kings, was driven back across the south.

## The Tide That Comes and Goes
For as long as there has been a [[Locations/The Black Tide/index|Black Tide]], its borders have ebbed and flowed. A bold king would win a stretch of water, a strong [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/index|Kingdom]] or a stubborn [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]] would win it back, and the long push and pull of the south went on, generation after generation.

## The Crown Made Heir
Some two hundred and ten years ago, a Dread Pirate King handed his crown to his son, and the dread pirate lords, grown fat and comfortable on their spoils, let him. It did not seem a disaster at first. The son ruled well enough, and so did his heir after him. But the crown that had always gone to the strongest now went to whoever happened to be born to it, and the lords who should have stopped it grew lazier with every generation. The rot set in slowly. By just over a century and a half ago, the Black Tide had kings who could not win, and it began to lose.

## The Long Retreat
It lost for nearly a hundred and fifty years. The Kingdom and the coalition pressed south together, island by island and raft city by raft city, and the Black Tide could not stop them. Sweep after sweep of its waters fell, and its kings did little more than watch.

## The Reach Breaks Away
As the Kingdom drove deeper, the Black Tide's far eastern waters were cut off from their king. For years [[Locations/Smuggler's Reach/index|Smuggler's Reach]] kept faith with a throne that could not reach it, and could not help it. Some seventy-five years ago it stopped bothering. The Reach broke away from the Black Tide and splintered into a smattering of pirate bands and little city-states, each with its own captain, council or crime lord, bound together by nothing but a shared love of profit and a shared hatred of anyone who would rule them.

## The End of the Line
The last of the failing kings was [[Dread Pirate King Gromon Spritroar|Gromon Spritroar]], who was feared across the south and lost ground all the same. Ten years ago, one of his own lords, [[Dread Pirate King Vask|Vask]], put the stagnant lords who had allowed the rot to the sword and took the crown. Within two years the retreat had stopped. The Black Tide has not lost a mile of water since, and the Kingdom and the coalition have both noticed.
