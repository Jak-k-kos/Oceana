## Islands Alone
A thousand years after the [[Arcanus Collapse]], the slow flood was still climbing. Hills became islands, islands became rocks, and the people upon them were left stranded and alone. The sea folk pressed in from the deep. And on the islands themselves, bandits who had once robbed the roads took to boats, and began to become pirates.

## The Pact
In 1052, a handful of island towns swore to stand together. They would fight the sea folk side by side, hunt the pirates wherever they hid, and open their doors to one another in hard times. From that first pact came the laws every member island still keeps today: answer the draft, honor guest-right, keep the rules of war set down by [[Tarrakus]], give no shelter to piracy, and seek mediation before war.

## The Azure Plains
The founders looked out at the sea that both divided and joined them, so wide and blue and flat beneath the sky, and called it the Azure Plains. The name stuck to the alliance itself. It began small and grew, island by island, for centuries. It stopped growing only when the expanding [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/index|Kingdom of Albryn]] reached its borders and there were no free islands left to join.
