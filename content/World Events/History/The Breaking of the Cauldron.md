**Date:** 2574-Macis-8

## Three Thousand Years of Trying
For more than three thousand years, the cauldron poured. And for more than three thousand years, the bravest and the most foolish of the surface world went looking for it. Countless expeditions sailed out to find it and end the flood. Every one of them failed. Most never came back at all.

## The Tidebreakers
[[The Tidebreakers]] succeeded where all the rest had failed. Each of them was a once-in-a-millennium prodigy of battle, and together they were something the world had never seen. They had a guide no one before them had ever had: Nerith of the Deep, a triton who had turned against his own people to lead them to the cauldron. Some whisper that one of the band is the hidden champion of [[Tarrakus]], though no one has ever proven it.

## The Champion of the Deep
At the cauldron they found its guardian waiting: the champion of [[Caruilla]] herself. What passed between them is already more legend than history, and with every telling the battle grows grander. But when it was over, Caruilla's champion lay dead, and the cauldron was broken at last.

## The Seas Stand Still
On approximately 2574-Macis-8, for the first time in more than three thousand years, the seas stopped rising. Word took more than a month to cross the world, and when it reached the islands on 2574-Calra-12, harbors erupted in the first festival of [[Aquilla's Mercy]].

## The Silence Below
No one knows how [[Caruilla]] took the death of her champion, or what she means to do about it. If she has chosen a new champion, no one above the waves has heard of it. Some hope she is too easily distracted to hold a grudge for long. Others fear the worst. No one knows.
