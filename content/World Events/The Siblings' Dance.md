A night when all three moons come full together, and the children of [[Arkaius]] dance across the sky as one.

**Date:** When [[Ellehwin]], [[Ostin]] and [[Winstra]] are all full on the same night  
**Honors:** [[Ellehwin]], [[Ostin]] and [[Winstra]], the three moons

## Overview
The three moons are rarely of one mind. [[Ellehwin]] would keep the world as it is, [[Ostin]] would remake it, and [[Winstra]] would rather be off exploring its edges. Their quarrels are as old as the moons themselves. But once in a generation, all three come full on the same night, and the green, orange and purple moons hang together in the sky. On that night, it is said, the siblings set their quarrels aside and dance. It is a festival of reconciliation, and across the world people follow their example.

## The Truce of the Moons
All hold to the dancing moons. Older than any nation still afloat, the faith asks only one thing of the night: that no blade be drawn and no cannon fired until the moons part. It has never needed a treaty to be kept.

The islands of the [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/index|coalition]] set aside their feuds. The ships of the [[Locations/Kingdom of Albryn/index|Kingdom]] and the [[Locations/The Black Tide/index|Black Tide]] lie still on the water, and even the pirates of [[Locations/Smuggler's Reach/index|Smuggler's Reach]] keep their guns quiet. Sailors tell of enemy crews rowing out to meet in the middle of contested waters, sharing drink and songs beneath the three moons, then rowing home at dawn to take up the fight again.

Rivals share a table, and quarrels that have lasted for years are often settled before morning. Families scattered across the islands make long voyages to be together for it. Above all, the night belongs to brothers and sisters: estranged siblings are expected to come home, and few dare to refuse.

Lanterns are hung in threes, one green, one orange and one purple. In the squares, people dance in rings of three under the moons until morning.

## The Dance-Born
Children born on the night of the Siblings' Dance are called dance-born, and they are thought to carry a little of all three moons: the stubbornness of [[Ellehwin]], the clever hands of [[Ostin]] and the restless heart of [[Winstra]]. Families count it a great blessing. Dance-born are said to be lucky, and some claim they are more often chosen by the moons than anyone else.

## The Faithful Few
Each moon holds a sliver of [[Arkaius]], and on this one night all three shine together. The small mourning cults that still keep his memory believe the Siblings' Dance is the one night when the dead god can hear them. Their candles burn in every window they can find, and for one night in a generation their numbers swell with the curious and the grieving.

## Superstitions
Old mages swear that magic seeps back into the world on the night of the Siblings' Dance, the threads of [[Arkaius]] almost whole again for a few short hours. No one has ever proven it. It is also said that [[Urset]] hates this night above all others, and that his faithful keep to their locked rooms until the moons part.
