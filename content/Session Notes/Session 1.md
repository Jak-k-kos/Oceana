# Calra 12th, 2574
In-game date: 2574-Carla-12
Rouge is told of her true mother from Tabatha, heart broken she runs off. Meanwhile, Scuby is caught playing around making his own instruments when he is caught by his mother. Being scolded by her for her relationship with Mr. Ferguson and his _silly dreams_ of playing music when Rouge bursts through the trees crying. After telling one another the stories of their motherly troubles Scuby helps her new necklace on rouge. Shady runs into Tryknin and his father in the forest as they are bringing the logs into town for the festival. Shady and Tryknin find Rouge and Scuby along the beach. As they unite, they head into town to find Silk. Silk presents some clearly stolen fish, claiming it's a new tradition to leave free fish out. Enjoying their snack they head to Emilio, Silk's adoptive father, to take on a task of retrieving a letter from the harbor master. On our way we talk to the fish merchant Jameson to distract him so Silk can steal us more fish.
Finding our way to Tobias, the harbor master, we learn that there hasn't been any gulls all week. This is extra strange considering the important letter to the governor should've come by now. 

We learn that Belna is the main trading partner with Insul.

We head to a strange ship that we see in the harbor where Kurt the gnome lets us onto the boat to climb the mast to try and see the incoming Guls, Shady see's a golden eagle, and a white and red spot in the grass to the south.

We leave the mysterious ship to investigate, the black tabaxi captain watches us leave.

As we approach we find a knife wing gull that has been killed, a messenger tube tied to it's leg, but the note inside is ruined by the blood. 
shady investigates and discovers that the bird was killed by something with large talons. Shady tells the group of the large golden eagle. Scuby recognizes the seal of a familiar trading company that his family often sells to.

Tryknin spots a gold and brown dot flying across the sky, landing nearby. Seeing the eagle, we follow it to a nest that we discover is full of the bodies of several gulls.  We head off to find Tam, Scuby's sister, to ask if she has read anything about birds like this eagle. WE sneak into the Wellfleet home. She tells us she has an ecology book, and shows us a page about the eagle. It is found in the eastern side of the kingdom, Esmerna is an island that has a druid family that trains all the messenger birds. 

We steal a book on poisons from Silk's brother, using it to find a poisonous flower to try and kill the eagle. We poison a fish and leave it in the nest after investigating the messages tied to the gulls leg, but none are readable. 

We set off to the festival. As Aquilas mercy begins, Emilio holds a town speech, reiterating the history of the flooding of the planet, and the wars that followed. 

Mr. Ferguson gives Scuby an accordion with his name engraved on it, and invites him to play for everyone at the festival. While enjoying the music, Dahlia asks Shady to dance. Tryknin speaks with his dad, where he learns that his father once knew someone who had an eagle similar to the one that we had seen earlier. While performing, Scuby hears a strange beating noise. Indicating he has heard something strange. Motioning for Rouge to check it out, she goes to the door. Looking out over the town, she sees the town is ablaze, massive pirate ships have filled the harbor. Running inside she alerts the town. Tryknin's father seems to know something about the attack, and instructs Tryknin to flee with his friends to the woods. Mr. Ferguson tells us to go to the lookout tower and send off the red flares, saying it will alert back up to come to the rescue of the island. As we flee, Tryknin sees his home ablaze, surrounded by pirates. While we head to the tower we find the path blocked by two Pirates. 

After slaying and looting the two pirates and heading to the tower, we are surrounded by a veritable army of pirates, led by a human in a purple coat, who introduces himself as Captain Desoto, flanked by the same black tabaxi that we met on the strange merchant ship, who we learn is his right hand man. The captain shoots everyone except Tryknin. We find ourselves bleeding black blood, as we fall to the ground, the captain addresses Tryknin, taunting him. The captain offers a challenge to Tryknin, if he can land a single hit against him he will allow him to live. In a flourish of blades, Tryknin finds his neck split open, having merely cut a small gash in the captains coat. We lie on the ground, bleeding out. Within moments we fade into death. 

# Calra 13th, 2574
In-game date: 2574-Carla-13
After what feels like an eternity, we find ourselves standing on a black sand beach, a mysterious ship sailing in the waters. Approaching us is a tall women with a skull like face, the goddess # Morvaine. She tells us that it is not our time, before we suddenly awake to find our island burned and ravished.
