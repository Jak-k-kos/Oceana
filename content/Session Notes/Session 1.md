# Calra 12th, 2574
In-game date: 2574-Calra-12
[[Rouge Bellball|Rouge]] is told of her true mother from [[Tabatha Bellball|Tabatha]], heart broken she runs off. Meanwhile, [[Scubby Wellfleet|Scubby]] is playing around making his own instruments when he is caught by [[Vhallen Wellfleet|his mother]]. He is scolded by her for his relationship with [[Mr. Ferguson]] and his _silly dreams_ of playing music. After [[Vhallen Wellfleet|Vhallen]] leaves, [[Rouge Bellball|Rouge]] bursts through the trees crying. After telling one another the stories of their motherly troubles [[Scubby Wellfleet|Scubby]] helps put her new necklace on [[Rouge Bellball|Rouge]]. [[Shady Bellball|Shady]] runs into [[Tryknin Goldenroar|Tryknin]] and [[Gaknin Goldenroar|his father]] in the forest as they are bringing the logs into town for [[Aquilla's Mercy|the festival]]. [[Shady Bellball|Shady]] and [[Tryknin Goldenroar|Tryknin]] find [[Rouge Bellball|Rouge]] and [[Scubby Wellfleet|Scubby]] along the beach. As they unite, they head into town to find [[Silk Faranz|Silk]]. [[Silk Faranz|Silk]] presents some clearly stolen fish, claiming it's a new tradition to leave free fish out. Enjoying their snack they head to [[Emilio Faranz|Emilio]], [[Silk Faranz|Silk]]'s adoptive father, to take on a task of retrieving a letter from the harbor master. On our way we talk to the fish merchant Jameson to distract him so [[Silk Faranz|Silk]] can steal us more fish.
Finding our way to Tobias, the harbor master, we learn that there haven't been any [[Knife-Wing Gull|gulls]] all week. This is extra strange considering the important letter to the governor should've come by now.

We learn that [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Belna/index|Belna]] is the main trading partner with [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Insul/index|Insul]].

We head to a strange ship that we see in the harbor where Kurt the gnome lets us onto the boat to climb the mast to try and see the incoming [[Knife-Wing Gull|gulls]]. [[Shady Bellball|Shady]] sees a [[Goldmantle Eagle|golden eagle]], and a white and red spot in the grass to the south.

We leave the mysterious ship to investigate, the [[Pitch|black tabaxi captain]] watches us leave.

As we approach we find a [[Knife-Wing Gull|knife-wing gull]] that has been killed, a messenger tube tied to its leg, but the note inside is ruined by the blood.
[[Shady Bellball|Shady]] investigates and discovers that the bird was killed by something with large talons. [[Shady Bellball|Shady]] tells the group of the large [[Goldmantle Eagle|golden eagle]]. [[Scubby Wellfleet|Scubby]] recognizes the seal of a familiar trading company that his family often sells to.

[[Tryknin Goldenroar|Tryknin]] spots a gold and brown dot flying across the sky, landing nearby. Seeing [[Goldmantle Eagle|the eagle]], we follow it to a nest that we discover is full of the bodies of several [[Knife-Wing Gull|gulls]]. We head off to find [[Tamra 'Tam' Wellfleet|Tam]], [[Scubby Wellfleet|Scubby]]'s sister, to ask if she has read anything about birds like this [[Goldmantle Eagle|eagle]]. We sneak into the Wellfleet home. She tells us she has an ecology book, and shows us a page about [[Goldmantle Eagle|the eagle]]. It is found in the eastern side of the kingdom, [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Esmerna/index|Esmerna]] is an island that has a druid family that trains all the messenger birds.

We steal a book on poisons from [[Silk Faranz|Silk]]'s [[Edward Faranz|brother]], using it to find a poisonous flower to try and kill [[Goldmantle Eagle|the eagle]]. We poison a fish and leave it in the nest after investigating the messages tied to the [[Knife-Wing Gull|gulls]]' legs, but none are readable.

We set off to [[Aquilla's Mercy|the festival]]. As [[Aquilla's Mercy]] begins, [[Emilio Faranz|Emilio]] holds a town speech, reiterating the history of the flooding of the planet, and the wars that followed.

[[Mr. Ferguson]] gives [[Scubby Wellfleet|Scubby]] an accordion with his name engraved on it, and invites him to play for everyone at [[Aquilla's Mercy|the festival]]. While enjoying the music, [[Dahlia Fiore|Dahlia]] asks [[Shady Bellball|Shady]] to dance. [[Tryknin Goldenroar|Tryknin]] speaks with [[Gaknin Goldenroar|his dad]], where he learns that [[Gaknin Goldenroar|his father]] once knew someone who had an [[Goldmantle Eagle|eagle]] similar to the one that we had seen earlier. While performing, [[Scubby Wellfleet|Scubby]] hears a strange beating noise. Indicating he has heard something strange. Motioning for [[Rouge Bellball|Rouge]] to check it out, she goes to the door. Looking out over the town, she sees the town is ablaze, massive pirate ships have filled the harbor. Running inside she alerts the town. [[Tryknin Goldenroar|Tryknin]]'s [[Gaknin Goldenroar|father]] seems to know something about the attack, and instructs [[Tryknin Goldenroar|Tryknin]] to flee with his friends to the woods. [[Mr. Ferguson]] tells us to go to the lookout tower and send off the red flares, saying it will alert back up to come to the rescue of [[Locations/Azure Plains Coalition (APC)/Insul/index|the island]]. As we flee, [[Tryknin Goldenroar|Tryknin]] sees his home ablaze, surrounded by pirates. While we head to the tower we find the path blocked by two pirates.

After slaying and looting the two pirates and heading to the tower, we are surrounded by a veritable army of pirates, led by a human in a purple coat, who introduces himself as [[De'Soto|Captain De'Soto]], flanked by the same [[Pitch|black tabaxi]] that we met on the strange merchant ship, who we learn is [[Pitch|his right hand man]]. [[De'Soto|The captain]] shoots everyone except [[Tryknin Goldenroar|Tryknin]]. We find ourselves bleeding black blood, as we fall to the ground, [[De'Soto|the captain]] addresses [[Tryknin Goldenroar|Tryknin]], taunting him. [[De'Soto|The captain]] offers a challenge to [[Tryknin Goldenroar|Tryknin]], if he can land a single hit against him he will allow him to live. In a flourish of blades, [[Tryknin Goldenroar|Tryknin]] finds his neck split open, having merely cut a small gash in [[De'Soto|the captain]]'s coat. We lie on the ground, bleeding out. Within moments we fade into death.
